For a generation of Kenyan television viewers, Regina Re remains frozen in time as the sharp, confident host who signed off every evening quiz with her signature “toodles.” But the former face of *Who’s Smarter Now?* is now navigating a starkly different reality far away from studio lights, held alongside three other individuals as homicide detectives piece together the death of her 86-year-old mother.

Dorita Lasinder Macharia was found dead on Friday, October 30, 2026, at her home in Nairobi’s Loresho estate. Investigators state that the octogenarian sustained a deep cut on her neck, triggering a high-level police inquiry into the circumstances surrounding the incident.

During court proceedings before a Nairobi court, investigators laid out preliminary findings that shift the focus from a standard wellness check to a complex family tragedy. Detectives told the court that a disagreement had allegedly occurred between Dorita and her granddaughter, Thandie Malaika Wanjiku Gitao, prior to the fatal incident. 

Beyond the initial altercation, police submissions detailed allegations involving Regina and her brother, Antony Jimmy Macharia. According to investigators, the two siblings were found at the Loresho residence apparently attempting to clean the room where their mother had lost her life.

Alongside Regina, Antony, and Thandie, a fourth individual—Thandie’s boyfriend, Immanuel Waigwe Munyeki—is also being held by law enforcement as part of the joint inquiry.

Following the presentation of preliminary evidence, the court granted detectives a 10-day window to hold all four suspects in custody. The extended detention allows police to complete forensic examinations, gather witness statements, and retrieve CCTV footage from the property.

The matter is officially scheduled to return to court for mention on November 12, 2026.
