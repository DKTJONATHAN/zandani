Fourteen years into devolved governance, the Senate is moving to eliminate a persistent operational blind spot: the lack of a centralized, real-time tracking tool for decisions taken on the floors of Kenya's 47 county assemblies.

The State Department for Devolution opened competitive bidding on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, seeking an individual software consultant to design and deploy an online database that will systematically catalog and manage resolutions passed across all devolved legislatures.

Financed through the World Bank-backed Second Kenya Devolution Support Programme (KDSP-II), the digital registry is designed to anchor the Senate’s supervisory and representational powers directly to primary records from the grassroots.

### Bridging the Article 96 Information Gap

Under Article 96 of the Constitution, the Senate bears the explicit mandate to protect the interests of counties and safeguard the devolution architecture. In practice, however, tracking the sheer volume of motions, vetting approvals, policy sanctions, and local regulations adopted by individual assemblies has largely relied on fragmented reporting, ad hoc committee summons, and post-facto gazette notices.

![Senate sitting](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/senate_5.jpg.webp?itok=gtnamuDd)

According to the Devolution department's procurement briefing, the central database is intended to establish uniform access to county legislative records, enabling lawmakers in Nairobi to assess local legislative trends, cross-county policy coordination, and statutory compliance as they happen.

The system will also create a shared public and institutional archive, opening decentralized assembly decisions to wider scrutiny by intergovernmental bodies and local stakeholders.

### Backed by KDSP-II Governance Pillars

The automation drive falls under the broader framework of the KDSP-II facility, a multi-agency initiative focused on institutional performance across sub-national administrative units.

The programme operates across three central pillars:

![The National Assembly, during the National Land Commission (Amendment) Bill sitting in 2023](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/parliament_0.jpeg.webp?itok=jTUIMYAx)

* **Sustainable financing and expenditure management**, an intervention stream where the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) supports measures to tighten own-source revenue mobilization and county expenditure controls.
* **Intergovernmental coordination and human resources**, which enlists KRA and national agencies to strengthen joint inter-tier governance, operational efficiency, and county workforce structures.
* **Oversight, accountability, and project standards**, a regulatory window aimed at enforcing project risk screening, climate change risk profiling, and the formalization of community-led project management committees.

By tying the resolution database to KDSP-II, the Devolution department is positioning county legislative outputs as critical compliance data rather than isolated ward-level politics.

### Bidding Requirements and Selection Timeline

The consultancy is open strictly to individual technical experts rather than corporate firms. Interested software architects and database specialists must provide an up-to-date Curriculum Vitae, statutory professional registrations or licensing documentation, and verified client testimonials demonstrating prior delivery of comparable enterprise public-sector platforms.

Submissions close at 11:00 am on November 12, 2026. Applicants may tender their applications electronically or deposit physical dossiers into the tender box located on the fifth floor of Teleposta Towers along Kenyatta Avenue in Nairobi.

Once contracted, the consultant's platform will face its first major test: successfully integrating disparate assembly record systems across diverse county technical setups to produce an unfragmented legislative ledger for Parliament.
