NAIROBI - Content creator Lydia Wanjiru has undergone a second Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL) procedure, marking another significant milestone in her ongoing series of body enhancement surgeries. The influencer shared footage from her hospital room late this week, showing her interacting with medical staff and engaging in mandatory post-operative movement.

In updates posted to her social media platforms, Wanjiru confirmed she is stable and currently focused on the initial healing phase of the operation. This procedure follows a recent liposuction treatment on her arms, which she also documented extensively for her followers.

Brazilian Butt Lift surgery involves the extraction of adipose tissue from specific body areas, which is then processed and injected into the buttocks to modify shape and volume. Medical practitioners typically emphasize that combining multiple cosmetic procedures within a short timeframe necessitates strict adherence to recovery protocols to manage risks of complications.

Wanjiru, who rose to prominence by sharing lifestyle, travel, and personal development content, has consistently utilized her platform to offer transparency regarding her cosmetic choices. Her decision to document the current recovery process continues a pattern of sharing intimate details of her beauty regimen with her viewership.

The report of her latest surgery arrives as speculation continues to surround her private life, particularly following recent sightings of the influencer with her former partner, Frank Doso. While Wanjiru has frequently addressed her body modifications, she has offered limited commentary on the nature of her recent public appearances with Doso, despite previous assertions that a reconciliation was unlikely.
