When Milimani Senior Principal Magistrate Teresia Nyangena ordered a suspected cybercriminal remanded for 36 days on Thursday, October 1, the ruling brought an abrupt end to a multi-county law enforcement hunt triggered by a breakdown in bail compliance.

The suspect, facing charges over the unauthorized digital extraction of Ksh 96 million from two local financial institutions, had previously secured his liberty by posting a combined cash bail of Ksh 2 million. That financial guarantee—representing roughly two percent of the total funds alleged to have been diverted—proved insufficient to ensure his appearance in court, leading to three consecutive missed trial dates and an urgent public appeal by state detectives.

### Mechanics of the Intrusion

The prosecution’s case against the suspect centers on two coordinated system breaches executed in June 2025. According to court filings, the suspect bypassed standard verification infrastructure to gain unauthorized access to computer systems belonging to a commercial bank and a savings and credit co-operative (sacco).

![Undated image of the Milimani Law Courts in Nairobi with an insert of the Kenyan notes](https://i.ibb.co/G34yKsrW/6df1958376d8.webp)

By routing transactions outside normal payment approval flows, he allegedly siphoned Ksh 44 million from one institution and Ksh 52 million from the second before internal monitoring flagged the missing funds.

The Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) formally charged him on March 3 and March 12, 2026, under the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act No. 5 of 2018. The charges include Access with Intent to Commit a Further Offence under Section 15(1) and Computer Fraud under Section 26(1)(b).

### From Cash Bail to Inter-County Chase

Despite the scale of the alleged financial loss, the court initially granted the accused cash bail set at Ksh 1.5 million for one file and Ksh 500,000 for the second. Once released, however, the suspect failed to present himself for three scheduled court hearings.

![DCI Headquarters, Nairobi](https://i.ibb.co/b58RxQMn/3de7fd1dac03.webp)

The repeated absences prompted Milimani Law Courts to issue a warrant for his arrest, forcing state investigators to pivot from routine prosecution to an active pursuit across the Rift Valley.

On September 21, the DCI published the suspect's photograph on its public communication channels, identifying him as a resident of Baringo County and appealing to citizens for intelligence on his whereabouts. The search ended days later in Kericho, where officers executed a dramatic arrest following tips from the public.

### Custody Restored Pending November Review

Brought back to Nairobi under police escort, the suspect appeared before Magistrate Nyangena on Thursday under the executed arrest warrant. Evaluating his track record of absconding while on release, the court canceled his bail terms and ordered him held in custody until November 5.

The 36-day remand places the suspect in state custody while prosecution teams and court clerks realign the trial schedule for both pending cybercrime files ahead of the November 5 status session.
