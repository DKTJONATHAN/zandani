Nairobi, Sunday, September 13, 2026 - Diamond Platnumz and Zuchu formally introduced their newborn daughter to the public today during a high-profile Maulid ceremony held to commemorate the child’s forty-day milestone. The event, attended by an exclusive guest list of family and industry peers, served as the official debut for the infant, identified as Sandrah. The WCB Wasafi management team shared a brief welcome message via official channels, confirming the baby’s nickname, SJ, and marking the conclusion of the traditional celebratory period.

The celebration commenced midday at a private estate, adhering to traditional Maulid customs. Guests were restricted from capturing candid photography during the initial proceedings, allowing the couple to maintain control over the infant's first public presentation. Following the ritual prayers, the parents presented the child to attendees before the official social media announcement was synchronized across the label’s platforms.

**Confirmation**
Representatives for WCB Wasafi confirmed the child is healthy and the family is currently observing the remainder of the 40-day custom in private.

**Numbers**
* 40: The number of days passed since the child's birth, marking the traditional milestone.
* 2: The number of central public figures involved in the partnership now centering their family narrative.
* 1: The number of public aliases, "SJ," confirmed for the infant.
