Five months after the Environment and Land Court threw out its environmental approval for cutting statutory corners, the State Department for Housing and Urban Development has been forced back to regulatory ground zero in Lang'ata.

The National Environment Management Authority (NEMA) has formally opened a 30-day public review for the proposed Southlands affordable housing project in Mugumo-ini Ward. Rather than advancing the contested development as a single clearance, authorities have carved the 7,128-unit high-density scheme into five discrete Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) study reports, according to Kenya Gazette notices published on October 23, 2026.

The regulatory restart represents a direct institutional response to a judicial halt delivered in May 2026. The Environment and Land Court quashed an environmental license that NEMA had issued on December 16, 2025, halting active construction on the grounds that the initial impact evaluation was fatally flawed, neglected surrounding residents' direct environmental concerns, and bypassed mandatory public participation benchmarks.

### Segmented Footprint Across Five Lots

The revived filings reveal how the national government intends to distribute the massive urban footprint across Mugumo-ini. The total portfolio of 7,128 residential units has been split across five separate planning lots.

![President William Ruto takes a selfie in front of the Pioneer Affordable Housing Estate sign in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, October 18, 2026.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/ruto-selfie-affordable-housing-eldoret-october-2026.jpg.webp?itok=4bBsqrNp)

Four of the designated parcels are slated to host 1,386 units each, with each lot accommodating 14 multi-storey blocks rising to ground plus eight upper floors. The fifth lot will absorb the remaining 1,584 units under a similar high-rise configuration.

The state's filings outline a comprehensive mix of social and affordable housing categories. The social tier will provide one-room, two-room, and three-room units, while the affordable housing tier incorporates studio, two-bedroom, and three-bedroom apartments.

Auxiliary infrastructure cataloged across the five study reports includes dedicated internal access roads, municipal water connection networks, centralized bulk water storage reservoirs, commercial shopping stalls, surface parking zones, and outdoor children's recreation spaces.

### Court-Mandated Safeguards and Social Impact

Unlike the fast-tracked approvals annulled by the bench earlier this year, the newly submitted dossiers directly confront the physical pressures of placing thousands of households into an established urban ward.

![Ruto Affordable Housing](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/ruto%20ii%20affordable%20housing.jpeg.webp?itok=lZLudQLy)

The assessment reports explicitly list construction-generated dust, noise pollution, structural ground vibration, and localized traffic gridlock among the primary environmental stresses that neighboring communities face. Crucially, the filings incorporate specific baseline commitments intended to resolve the human displacement questions that fueled the initial legal challenge.

Under the proposed mitigation measures, the project proponents are required to verify a formal register of persons affected by the development and deliver agreed institutional support before clearing any portions of the site. The filings further mandate the establishment of operational community grievance channels, sustained engagement with resident groups throughout the construction life cycle, and ring-fenced protections for vulnerable demographics, including the elderly, children, persons with disabilities, and female-headed households.

### 30-Day Window to Test Compliance

The publication of the five gazette notices under Section 59 of the Environmental Management and Co-ordination Act effectively places the burden of proof back on the public and affected neighborhood associations.

NEMA has opened a statutory 30-day window from the October 23 publication date for members of the public to submit written memoranda or deliver oral presentations on the project's viability and mitigation adequacy.

Full physical copies of the five ESIA reports have been deposited for public inspection at NEMA’s national headquarters along Popo Road, off Mombasa Road, as well as the Nairobi City County Director of Environment’s offices. Formal objections and technical representations are being directed to NEMA Director-General via dgnema@nema.go.ke, requiring specific project reference numbers as the authority weighs whether the restructured plan satisfies the strict terms laid down by the court.
