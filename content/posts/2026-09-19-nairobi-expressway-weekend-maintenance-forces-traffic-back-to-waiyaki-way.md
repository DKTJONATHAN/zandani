The Nairobi Expressway’s elevated deck will be off-limits for city-bound traffic starting tonight, as operators move to conduct maintenance work on the overpass. The closure, which begins at 8 p.m. on Saturday, September 19, forces all motorists traveling from the James Gichuru Junction toward the Central Business District to exit the expressway and navigate the ground-level lanes of Waiyaki Way.

MOJA Expressway Company has set a 40-hour window for the works, with the overpass expected to remain closed until Monday, September 21, at 12 p.m. While the company maintains that the Westlands entrance will remain operational, the diversion essentially funnels high-speed traffic back into the congested surface-level arteries of Westlands.

For commuters, this shift represents a return to the bottleneck conditions that have plagued the area recently. On September 9, a similar closure at the ABC Place-Red Hill Road junction caused significant gridlock, with motorists reporting long delays as vehicles were forced to merge onto Waiyaki Way’s already saturated lanes. The reliance on Waiyaki Way as an emergency pressure valve for the expressway continues to test the capacity of the older road network.

Despite the closure of the upper deck, the company confirmed that traffic exiting the expressway toward the overpass will resume normal flow by 5 p.m. today, providing a small window of reprieve before the full shutdown of the city-bound lane begins later this evening.

Traffic marshals are expected to be stationed along the affected sections to manage the transition. However, with the weekend typically seeing high volumes of traffic moving through the Westlands corridor, authorities are advising road users to adjust their travel schedules to account for the increased density on the lower road.

This maintenance cycle highlights the ongoing challenge of managing a dual-layer road system where the failure or scheduled work on the elevated deck creates an immediate, cascading effect on the city's primary ground-level transit routes.
