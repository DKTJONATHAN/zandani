The Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) has declared a comprehensive 14-hour closure of the Nairobi Southern Bypass, temporarily handing the critical transit link over to runners in the 2026 Standard Chartered Nairobi Marathon while diverting general vehicular traffic onto alternative urban arteries.

In a public notice issued on Monday, October 19, the state highways regulator confirmed that the total vehicular exclusion will run along the entire stretch of the dual carriageway between the Ole Sereni Interchange on Mombasa Road and Kikuyu Town.

"The Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) wishes to notify the Public that there will be a temporary closure of Nairobi Southern Bypass from Ole Sereni Interchange to Kikuyu Town on Saturday, October 24, 2026 at Midnight to Sunday October 25, 2026 at 2.00pm," the agency stated.

"This is to allow for the 2026 Standard Chartered Nairobi Marathon," KeNHA added.

![Southern Bypass Nairobi](https://i.ibb.co/WWhRgHCF/edf45030d74b.webp)

### The Operational Detour Map

With the highway cordoned off through the early hours of Sunday afternoon, the closure strips motorists of the primary peripheral expressway designed to bypass Nairobi’s central business district and interior estates. To mitigate anticipated disruptions, the authority outlined specific rerouting directives tailored to commuter origins across Kiambu and Nairobi counties.

Motorists heading from Kikuyu toward Mombasa Road are instructed to bypass the corridor entirely and use Waiyaki Way. Drivers traveling between Kikuyu and Karen have been assigned Dagoretti Road as their direct diversion route.

Traffic moving from Karen toward Mombasa Road will be filtered through Lang'ata Road. For commuters traveling out of Dagoretti toward the Mombasa Road corridor, the authority designated alternative passage via Waiyaki Way, or alternatively, through Ngong Road connecting via Mbagathi Way.

Commercial and private movement moving out of the Industrial Area toward the Kikuyu hub will also absorb a broader diversion circuit. KeNHA directed those vehicles to pick up Mombasa Road, connect through Uhuru Highway, and join Waiyaki Way to complete their westward journey.

### Access Corridors and Police Enforcement

![Stanchart Marathon](https://i.ibb.co/FksrvP4J/94a4a26ae290.webp)

Local access routes surrounding the entry point at the southern apex will also face controlled adjustments. Motorists approaching from Likoni Road destined for Mombasa Road have been directed to navigate via Enterprise Road.

For drivers heading from Mombasa Road toward the hospitality facilities near the interchange, KeNHA noted that access to Emaar Ole Sereni will be routed strictly through the Nextgen Mall access corridor.

"Motorists are advised to use Mombasa Road (A8) from Ole Sereni Interchange through Uhuru Highway, and vice versa, as the alternative route," the notice stated.

To ensure traffic compliance and keep alternative paths moving, the authority confirmed that a coordinated ground operation will govern all major junctions along the detour routes.

"All road users are further advised to follow the traffic signs and guidance as directed by traffic marshals and Police," KeNHA stated, cautioning drivers to align their movement schedules with the Sunday 2:00 pm reopening mark.
