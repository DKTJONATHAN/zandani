Embakasi East MP Babu Owino has framed his 2027 Nairobi gubernatorial ambitions as an uncompromised solo movement, publicly shutting down reports that former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua urged him to step aside for a Senate run.

Addressing the political chatter across his digital platforms, the lawmaker dismissed allegations of backdoor negotiations, choosing instead to frame the capital’s upcoming executive contest around his own identity.

“My unworthy opponents have given up and are now banking on fake news. *Hii Nairobi ilienda kitambo sana*,” Babu stated, adding that as far as he is concerned, “the Gubernatorial race is between Babu Owino vs Paul Ongili.”

The response follows persistent political reports alleging that Gachagua held private discussions designed to streamline opposition tickets within Nairobi County ahead of the 2027 General Election. According to those leaked details, Gachagua allegedly favored Embakasi North MP James Gakuya to spearhead the gubernatorial ticket under a Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) banner, while proposing that Babu shift his sights to the Senate seat to avoid splitting opposition votes against incumbent Governor Johnson Sakaja.

Babu, however, flatly rejected the notion of taking directives from behind-the-scenes political brokers. Reaffirming his determination to unseat Sakaja, the Embakasi East representative confirmed he will run for governor on his newly rebranded Mwananchi Party platform.

Rather than fitting into pre-arranged coalition arrangements, Babu is constructing his campaign around a grassroots ward assembly strategy. He emphasized plans to field Mwananchi Party candidates across Nairobi’s 85 electoral wards, aiming to build a reliable numerical majority inside the Nairobi County Assembly to safeguard his executive agenda should he capture City Hall.

The public denial highlights the friction surrounding attempts by senior opposition figures to negotiate unified slates for high-stakes urban contests. Managing competing personal brands and established electoral ambitions remains a major hurdle for strategists seeking to present a single front against the ruling administration.

For now, Babu maintains that his campaign structure is moving forward without external political clearance, leaving opposition dealmakers to recalculate their city strategy without his compliance.
