Stepping straight from the high-security guest lists of a cross-border wedding into local congregational life, Charlene Ruto and Isaya Yunge made their first public appearance as a married couple from the back pews of an African Inland Church in Mwanza.

Two days after formalizing their union at the Malaika Beach Hotel on November 6—an event drawing high-ranking political figures from both Kenya and Tanzania, including President William Ruto and First Lady Rachel Ruto—the newlyweds arrived at AIC Nyanguge aboard a black Mercedes-Benz G-Class SUV.

Rather than heading for the reserved front-row seating typically assigned to presidential family members and distinguished visitors, Yunge walked his wife into the sanctuary and settled the couple toward the rear of the hall.

During the Sunday service on November 8, Yunge spent portions of the gathering engrossed in a Bible while seated beside Charlene. The bride wore a white dress featuring black polka dots, paired with a white handbag, while her husband wore a light jacket, white shirt, and beige trousers.

Toward the end of the service, local church leadership invited the couple to the front of the sanctuary, where members of the congregation gathered around them to offer prayers for their marriage.

The Mwanza service marks the opening public engagement for the couple following their wedding celebrations on Lake Victoria's shores.
