Mama Ida Odinga stepped onto the red dust of Migwena Cultural Grounds on Thursday morning with her children Raila Odinga Jnr, Rosemary, and Winnie Odinga flanking her steps, anchoring the Odinga household's return to Bondo for the first anniversary of the late former Prime Minister's passing.

The family’s arrival in Siaya County marked the culmination of private household preparations that began behind closed gates at the family’s Opoda Farm estate. Before stepping out into the public glare at Migwena, Mama Ida opened the doors of the ancestral residence to host Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna and delegates from his Linda Mwananchi movement, setting an intimate, close-circle tone ahead of the massive public gathering.

### The Morning at Opoda Farm

The private reception at Opoda Farm positioned Sifuna’s delegation at the center of the morning’s family proceedings. While thousands of supporters began filling the terraces and open spaces of Migwena Cultural Grounds, the Odinga siblings and their mother used the farmhouse perimeter to receive close political confidants ahead of the formal religious and cultural protocols.

Winnie, Rosemary, and Raila Jnr accompanied their mother as the household shifted from the quiet enclave of Opoda to the sprawling civic arena in central Bondo. The movement of the entire family unit underscored a unified public appearance, reaffirming the household’s central place in Siaya's socio-political calendar twelve months after losing its patriarch.

### VIP Roster and the Empty Chairs

The anniversary roster at Migwena highlighted a defined circle of seasoned political companions traveling to Bondo. Former President Uhuru Kenyatta and Wiper Democratic Movement leader Kalonzo Musyoka headed the roster of expected dignitaries traveling west to join the family on the dais.

Both leaders, bound to the former Prime Minister through years of coalition pacts and shared electoral campaigns, reaffirmed their long-standing ties to the Opoda household by making the memorial journey.

Yet the event’s protocol ledger was defined just as sharply by the seats that remained vacant. Key figures from the national administration and regional leadership were not expected to attend the Bondo ceremony. President William Ruto was among those confirmed off the guest attendance list.

Also missing from the Migwena lineup were Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi—a veteran stalwart of Odinga’s political apparatus now serving in the national executive—and Homa Bay Governor Gladys Wanga. Their non-attendance drew a distinct operational boundary around who stepped into the Migwena VIP enclosure for the one-year commemoration.

### Honoring the Patriarch at Migwena

At Migwena Cultural Grounds, the gathering took on the full visual weight of a national cultural homage. Thousands of attendees occupied the grounds to reflect on Odinga’s lifetime of political organizing, constitutional agitation, and advocacy for institutional reforms across Kenya.

With Mama Ida seated beside her children and long-time political allies, the memorial proceedings combined deep personal mourning with broad public remembrance. The family's joint appearance in Bondo provided a definitive snapshot of the Odinga estate managing its public duties, closing out the formal one-year mourning cycle with its traditional inner circle firmly in place.
