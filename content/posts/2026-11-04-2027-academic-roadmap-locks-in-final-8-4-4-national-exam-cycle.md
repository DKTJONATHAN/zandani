The Ministry of Education has set the timeline for the final phase-out of the 8:4:4 education system, issuing a 2027 academic calendar that pairs the historic last sitting of the Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) with four parallel Competency-Based Education (CBE) national assessments.

Under an administrative circular signed by Basic Education Principal Secretary John Ololtuaa and distributed to regional, county, and sub-county education directors, all pre-primary, primary, junior, senior, and secondary institutions will open for Term One on January 4, 2027.

The directive establishes the operational cadence for a transitional year in national education, balancing term lengths against a heavily structured examination schedule in the final quarter of 2027.

### Staggered Closing Dates and Extended Breaks

![Secondary School students sitting for their Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) examinations.](https://i.ibb.co/vCKqyQPF/069256a5e835.webp)

Term One will run for 14 weeks for pre-primary, primary, and junior secondary learners, closing on April 9, 2027. Senior and secondary schools will operate on a slightly shorter 13-week schedule, concluding on April 2.

Learners across all tiers will take a nine-day half-term break between February 19 and February 28, 2027. A three-week holiday in April will follow before schools resume for Term Two on May 3.

The second term covers 13 weeks for all levels, running through July 30, 2027. Midterm breaks for Term Two are scheduled from June 9 to June 20, leading into a four-week August holiday from July 31 to August 29.

### Compressed Term Three Built Around Exams

Term Three opens on August 30, 2027, capped at 10 weeks with no midterm break to preserve an uninterrupted period for national testing.

The final term serves as the administrative bridge between two educational eras, hosting five distinct national evaluation tracks across November:

![Basic Education PS John Ololtuaa during a past event](https://i.ibb.co/9H6WVYLd/3177f4eb09b8.webp)

* **November 1 – November 5, 2027:** A five-day assessment period dedicated to primary and junior CBE evaluations, including the Kenya Primary School Education Assessment (KPSEA), Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA), Kenya Intermediate Level Education Assessment (KILEA), and Kenya Pre-Vocational Level Education Assessment (KPLEA).
* **November 8 – November 26, 2027:** A three-week national examination window dedicated exclusively to the KCSE.

The November 2027 KCSE sitting represents the final cohort tested under the 8:4:4 curriculum framework established four decades ago.

### Pathway to Full CBE Implementation

Following the conclusion of examinations, learners will enter a two-month end-of-year holiday running from November 6, 2027, to January 3, 2028.

When basic education institutions reopen for the 2028 academic year, the system will operate entirely under Competency-Based Education across all grade levels, marking the final structural retirement of the 8:4:4 system.
