NAIROBI - Content creator Mjaka Mfine has publicly dismissed recent speculation regarding her romantic life, asserting that she has never pursued a relationship with a fellow public figure or entertainment personality.

Speaking during an interview with radio host Alex Mwakideu, Mjaka stated that she consciously avoids romantic entanglements within the entertainment industry to maintain a necessary level of privacy.

“I don't think mimi nishai date content creator ama mtu anajulikana Kenya. Sitaki kujiweka kwa hio maisha. Huyu mwenye niko na yeye wacha abaki pahali ako. If you are a public figure, haiwezi,” Mjaka told Mwakideu.

According to the creator, the complications inherent in high-profile dating, particularly the constant public discourse surrounding couples in the spotlight, remain incompatible with her lifestyle. She emphasized that her current partner does not occupy a space in the public eye, reinforcing a deliberate choice to shield her personal relationships from online speculation.

While Mjaka’s remarks have effectively distanced her from rumors concerning her professional peers, the timing of the interview follows persistent fan chatter surrounding her relationship with creator Mokaya. Although she did not reference him directly, her broad dismissal of dating within the Kenyan spotlight acts as an indirect response to observers who have long speculated about the nature of their association.
