Eugene Wamalwa just made Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna the most aggressively courted man in opposition politics, going on live radio to offer his own political crown to seal the deal.

In an extraordinary broadcast pitch, the Democratic Action Party of Kenya (DAP-K) leader declared he is prepared to abandon his personal 2027 State House bid and step down from the party’s helm if Sifuna agrees to make DAP-K his new operational base.

Wamalwa did not hedge his pitch behind quiet committee backchannels. Speaking on air, he laid out a complete political surrender of his personal campaign ambitions to provide Sifuna with an immediate, nationally structured ticket.

“If he comes to the DAP-K, I can shelve my ambition and ask party leadership for Sifuna. Why not?” Wamalwa said. “As we speak, I am the presidential candidate, cleared, for DAP-K. Why not? If he comes and asks if I could support him to be the DAP-K presidential candidate, I’ll say why not?”

### Capitalising on a Registration Headache

Wamalwa’s open-mic overture lands at a delicate juncture for Sifuna, whose departure from the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) left him anchoring the fledgling Linda Mwananchi formation alongside allied lawmakers. That movement has spent weeks wrestling with bureaucratic gatekeepers to secure a recognized political outfit.

The push hit a brick wall at the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties when a blueprint to take over the United Democratic Party (UDP) and rebrand it as the Ukombozi People’s Party collapsed. The Registrar rejected the proposed moniker, ruling that the "Ukombozi" name closely mirrored an already reserved political identity.

Sensing an opening, Wamalwa has stepped into the breach to remind Sifuna that he does not need to build an outfit from scratch when established machinery sits ready to be handed over.

“Apart from UDP, even DAP-K is also available for Sifuna,” Wamalwa noted, while also reassuring listeners that the vintage UDP outfit remains viable even without the blocked cosmetic makeover. “The UDP will be used, but not Ukombozi.”

### A Crowded Field of Suitors

The public concession adds a theatrical twist to the high-stakes jostling among opposition principals, who are eager to lock down Sifuna’s grassroots capital in Nairobi and Western Kenya. Wamalwa’s willingness to surrender DAP-K’s presidential ticket puts pressure on parallel plans being drafted by rival camps.

Trans Nzoia Governor George Natembeya has firmly pointed to UDP as the primary mechanism Sifuna will employ, maintaining that internal discussions regarding the vehicle's mechanics remain on course. 

Simultaneously, allies of Wiper leader Kalonzo Musyoka have been structuring an alternative path altogether, sketching out a potential Kalonzo-Sifuna joint presidential ticket that has been flagged for debate at an upcoming leadership retreat.

For Wamalwa, however, offering to demote himself from presidential contender to chief backer is the boldest public wager yet in the ongoing opposition reshuffle.

Whether Sifuna accepts the keys to DAP-K or takes his chances with an alternative outfit may not remain a mystery for long. Vihiga Senator Godfrey Osotsi has indicated that the Linda Mwananchi team has already secured a functional party vehicle and intends to unveil the exact platform Sifuna will step onto in a matter of weeks.
