A synchronized utility pause is hitting some of Nairobi’s most exclusive diplomatic missions, five-star hospitality landmarks, and high-net-worth residential addresses. On Wednesday, September 30, 2026, Kenya Power is executing scheduled network maintenance that directly cuts through the capital's premium corridors.

The scheduled interruptions, largely running from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., will force major international entities and elite residential squares to shift to alternative power sources or alter their daily operational flows.

### The Diplomatic and Riverside Luxury Strip

At the center of the Wednesday maintenance list is the leafy Riverside enclave. The outage targets Riverside Drive, Riverside Gardens, Mzima Springs Road, Riverside Lane, and parts of Tende Drive. 

This specific pocket serves as a heavy-hitting hub for diplomatic representations and premium multi-million-shilling apartments. Among the high-profile installations affected are:

*   The German Embassy
*   The Uganda High Commission
*   International Organization for Migration (IOM) Mzima Springs
*   Riverside Square and One Riverside
*   Millennium Apartments and Key Stone Office Park
*   Strathmore High School and Mandari Kindergarten

### Westlands and Nyerere Road Corporate Hubs

Further up in the Westlands commercial district, another major node of corporate offices and retail spaces faces the same 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. window. The maintenance sweeps across Raphta Road, Lantana Road, Church Road, Ring Road Westlands, and East Church Road. 

This zone holds key corporate spaces and consumer hubs, including Oracle's offices, Rehema House, Kipro Centre, Consolata Church, and the Chandarana supermarket outlet on Raphta Road. 

Simultaneously, the luxury hospitality sector is feeling the pinch. The five-star Serena Hotel, situated along Nyerere Road near Central Park, is tucked into an outage zone that also encompasses Mamlaka Road and Upper State House Road, putting premium accommodation and conferencing facilities on high-generator alert for the day.

Down on Mombasa Road, a separate commercial and industrial cluster is set for a similar eight-hour pause. Key corporate footprints in this zone include the Rural Electrification and Renewable Energy Corporation (REREC) offices, beauty manufacturer Nice & Lovely, Manchester Knight, Viraj Complex, Auto Express, and the Total Petrol Station.

### Regional VIP and Industrial Interruptions

Outside the capital, the maintenance schedule hits several targeted administrative, judicial, and industrial assets in the counties.

In Nyeri, the maintenance team is focusing on the areas surrounding the Major Seminary from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. This interruption directly touches the private estate of Judge Mukunya, alongside Karson Estate, Gachengo Family, and the Catholic Hostels. Further rural maintenance in Miiri and Kiangai will run from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., impacting the Kiangai and Thunguri Coffee Factories.

In Kisumu, the utility firm has marked out Tido and Cibo for maintenance between 9:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. This move temporarily unplugs Kibos Industries, a major manufacturing and agro-processing asset in the region, alongside White Coal and Tamu.

Other regional administrative centers facing disruptions between 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. include Embu’s Gachoka area—which shuts power to the Embu Airstrip, Don Bosco, Jeremiah Nyagah Technical, and the local District Officer's offices—and the entire town of Kehancha in Migori County, extending through Ntimaru and the Angatta Sugar neighborhood.

With the maintenance window spanning the entire working day, affected institutions and high-end residences are left to navigate the eight-to-nine-hour gap using standby diesel generators and alternative energy systems.
