Raila Odinga Junior has taken to a video message recorded from Tanzania to deliver traditional Swahili marital counsel directly to Tanzanian entrepreneur Isaya Yunge following his high-profile wedding to Charlene Ruto.

Stepping into the cross-border commentary surrounding the union between the daughter of President William Ruto and the Tanzanian businessman, Junior drew on classical coastal metaphors to offer advice on spousal care, respect, and dignity.

Addressing the groom directly, Junior anchored his tribute in a Swahili proverb aimed at outlining a husband's responsibility to shield his wife from public disregard or carelessness.

“Kwa ndugu yangu Isaya, kumbuka: mwanamke si samaki wa kuanikwa juani, wala si ua la kuvurugwa!” Junior said in the message.

The imagery—admonishing against leaving a woman exposed like fish left to dry under a harsh sun, or trampling her like a fragile bloom—framed marriage as a sanctuary of protection rather than public exposure.

### A Joint Message from Tanzania

The advice came as part of a broader joint message sent by Junior and his wife, Yvonne, who extended formal blessings to the newlyweds as they establish their marital life across Kenya and Tanzania.

“From Mama Yvonne and myself, once again to the newlyweds, Mr and Mrs Yunge,” Junior stated. “May God bless your marriage with love, wisdom, happiness and a lifetime of beautiful memories. Kwa ndugu yangu Isaya, mahaba yake hazina.”

The video backdrop in Tanzania carried its own geographic resonance, given Yunge's established business footprint in the country and the couple's recent wedding celebrations that spanned both nations.

### Cross-Border Resonances

The public message from the son of Kenya's long-time opposition leader to the son-in-law of the sitting President added a distinct cultural note to the ongoing regional interest in the marriage.

Rather than offering conventional political courtliness, Junior focused exclusively on the domestic duties of marriage, emphasizing honor and emotional security within the Swahili linguistic tradition.

The marital guidance follows a series of post-wedding appearances and public declarations by both Yunge and Charlene as they complete their transition into married life across East Africa.
