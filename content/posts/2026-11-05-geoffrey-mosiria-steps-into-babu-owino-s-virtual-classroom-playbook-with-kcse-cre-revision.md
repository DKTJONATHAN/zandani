Former Nairobi County Chief Officer Geoffrey Mosiria is trading standard political messaging for chalk-and-talk digital streaming, rolling out a virtual Christian Religious Education revision session for candidates sitting the ongoing 2026 Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education examinations.

Taking to social media, Mosiria directed students preparing for Thursday's papers to log on with their Bibles ready, specifically highlighting the Old Testament. In his public statement, he distanced himself from illegal examination shortcuts while urging candidates to rely on their own preparation and faith.

“Tupatane revision CRE paper kesho jioni na kila candidate abebe Bible old testament! Mimi sina leakage niko na imani mtapita tu, bora ujiamini na uweke mungu mbele!” Mosiria posted online ahead of the session.

The virtual classroom strategy bears a striking resemblance to the digital revision brand built by Embakasi East MP Babu Owino. Owino recently revived his free national examination revision classes on October 23, walking students through complex numerical concepts for KCSE Mathematics Papers 1 and 2. Owino first popularized the online tutorial format back in 2020 during nationwide school closures prompted by the COVID-19 pandemic, routinely bringing the sessions back whenever national exams roll around.

While Mosiria’s intervention brings a new subject into the mix of political figures offering academic support during exam season, the timing and execution carry clear political undertones. Both Mosiria and Babu Owino are widely anticipated to lock horns in the contest for the Embakasi East parliamentary seat in the upcoming 2027 General Election. 

By stepping into the virtual revision space, Mosiria is directly contesting the digital turf that Owino has leveraged for years to maintain direct visibility among students, young voters, and households across the country as the 2027 electoral calendar approaches.
