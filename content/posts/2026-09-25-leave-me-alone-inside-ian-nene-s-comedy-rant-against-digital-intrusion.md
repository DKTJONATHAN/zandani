“Leave me alone, leave me alone, don’t ask me questions, I ain’t even got my number on your phone.”

That was the direct, rhythmically delivered verdict from Ian Nene—better known to a generation of Kenyan television viewers as Almasi from the Citizen TV show *Machachari*—in a selfie-style Instagram video shared on Thursday, September 24, 2026. Rather than issuing a formal press statement or ignoring the prying messages entirely, the former actor turned his plea for privacy into a full-scale comedy skit, complete with a bold “Public service announcement” graphic and a prompt for viewers to forward the clip to anyone needing a similar lesson in boundaries.

The self-shot video captured Nene rapping and speaking directly to the camera, balancing humor with an unambiguous message to social media users who continue digging into his personal affairs. While his followers flooded the comments section with laughter, suggesting the clip become a ringtone or even a national anthem, the lighthearted performance underscores a heavier, ongoing reality of navigating digital fame from thousands of miles away.

Since stepping away from the local acting scene, Nene’s life has taken a distinct trajectory far removed from the screens that made him a household name. He relocated to the United Kingdom to pursue his studies, a transition that coincided with a significant spiritual pivot. Nene has previously detailed spending more than three years living a monastic life and immersing himself in spiritual teachings—a path sparked by deeply personal circumstances following his mother’s diagnosis with stage-four breast cancer, which led him to question his faith and seek answers outside the public eye.

That deliberate retreat into spirituality and life abroad has done little to sever the public's fascination with his movements. The pressure of maintaining a digital presence while managing public scrutiny came into sharper focus in August 2026, when his former *Machachari* co-star MaDVD revealed that relentless online criticism and commentary have made Nene deeply hesitant about returning to Kenya.

By packaging his demand for personal space into a viral rap rather than a defensive post, Nene chose a route of tactical detachment. Whether the playful warning permanently halts the flood of invasive questions remains to be seen, but for now, the former child star has made it clear that his personal life operates strictly off the grid.
