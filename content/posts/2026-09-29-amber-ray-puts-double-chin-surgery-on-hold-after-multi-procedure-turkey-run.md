Amber Ray’s overseas cosmetic transformation has hit a temporary medical wall, with her surgeon ordering a strict physical pause before she can tackle her double chin.

Speaking at Natalie Githinji’s Endometriosis Warriors event at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC), the content creator explained that her plans for a final facial procedure had to be shelved. According to Amber, her medical team drew the line against stacking too many operations together, insisting her system needed time to stabilize.

“I still want to do my double chin. I wanted to do it, but the doctor told me he can’t do many surgeries at the same time, so my body needs to be given a break,” she told attendees. She noted that once the mandatory recovery window closes, she intends to revisit the procedure.

The pause follows a high-stakes trip to Turkey in September, where the influencer underwent a series of simultaneous procedures and documented the journey for her digital followers. Her itinerary in Turkish clinics went far beyond standard touch-ups, covering breast augmentation, Lipo 360 for midsection fat reduction, and a subsequent fat transfer to her calves recommended by her surgeon.

Despite the physical toll of undergoing three major operations at once, Amber reported an unusually smooth healing process. Beyond body contouring, her transformation included a major dental overhaul. She revealed she had work done on 21 teeth, with each tooth costing approximately KSh 44,000—bringing the total bill for the dental procedure alone to about KSh 924,000.

The medical directives also forced a shift in her daily routine. Amber disclosed that she stopped consuming alcohol for three weeks as part of her post-surgery protocol, alongside monitoring her diet to protect her healing progress and safeguard the investment made in her new look.

With her body still recovering from the Turkey trip, the timeline for the double-chin procedure remains dependent on her medical team's clearance.
