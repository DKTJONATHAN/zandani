Detectives from Gigiri Police Division have brought initial relief to pedestrian commuters and business operators across Nairobi’s Westlands-Parklands commercial belt after tracking down and detaining a key suspect behind an armed daylight mugging captured on camera last week.

The apprehension on Wednesday, October 21, capped a six-day intelligence-led pursuit initiated after the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) reviewed circulating footage of the street-level attack. Investigators established that during the scuffle, the assailant dropped a firearm on the tarmac before fleeing with victim valuables, handing state prosecutors a critical physical exhibit ahead of court proceedings.

“Detectives from Gigiri have arrested a suspect captured in a viral video alongside his accomplices violently attacking a member of the public and robbing him of his valuables before fleeing the scene in Parklands, Nairobi,” the directorate confirmed in an operational brief.

![Screengrabs from a viral video showing suspects violently attacking and robbing a member of the public in Parklands, Nairobi, October 15, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/TxLfyX7v/0b2887cc2c93.webp)

### Material Exhibit Anchors Custody

The recovery of the abandoned firearm transformed the case from an anonymous digital outcry into a concrete ballistics and evidentiary prosecution. The incident, recorded by onlookers and building security feeds, began circulating across digital platforms on October 15, triggering alarm among suburban residents over brazen, open-air armed ambushes.

While the suspect remained on the run across the capital for nearly a week, Gigiri-based plainclothes officers tracked his movements using informant networks and operational leads. DCI confirmed that the retrieved weapon remains secured in police armouries as an official exhibit, which will form the centerpiece of charges alongside the digital footage once criminal processing concludes.

Police confirmed the detained suspect is being held in custody while formal arraignment documents are finalised.

![DCI headquarters off Kiambu Road, Nairobi](https://i.ibb.co/QWdDYwx/8daa6f231163.webp)

### Commercial Corridors on Alert

The breakthrough addresses growing anxiety across the Parklands and wider Westlands zone, where private establishments and pedestrian routes have faced a string of high-profile armed intrusions.

In July, the directorate mounted a parallel forensic and witness inquiry after a four-man gang staged an armed raid on a restaurant along General Mathenge Road. In that incident, four attackers operating on two motorcycles fired live warning shots into the air to subdue dining patrons before making off with personal valuables and retreating across escape paths leading directly into Parklands.

While Wednesday’s arrest removes one identified assailant from the streets, police acknowledged that the threat has not been fully eliminated. Investigators from Gigiri are currently tracking additional intelligence leads to locate and detain the remaining accomplices seen participating in the October 15 mugging, who remain on the run.
