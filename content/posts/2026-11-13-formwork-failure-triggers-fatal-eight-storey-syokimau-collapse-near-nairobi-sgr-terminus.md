Active concreting work on the upper floors of an eight-storey building under construction in Syokimau ended in disaster on Friday morning, November 13, 2026, after a sudden formwork failure sent structural sections crashing down near the Nairobi Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) Terminus.

The partial collapse along Mombasa Road trapped construction workers beneath heavy debris, triggering an immediate multi-agency emergency deployment to the site on the outskirts of Nairobi. Preliminary security and disaster reports indicate that at least five people are feared dead as search teams continue to comb through shattered concrete.

### Formwork Break During Upper-Floor Pouring

Site operational reports indicate that the structure gave way while workers were engaged in active concreting on the top levels. The temporary formwork supporting the poured concrete failed, causing upper-floor sections to collapse downward and trapping laborers working in the immediate drop zone.

Emergency responders arriving at the Syokimau site managed to extract three survivors from the rubble during the initial hours of the operation. All three were stabilized before being rushed to hospital for medical treatment. Authorities have not yet disclosed the severity of their injuries.

The Kenya Red Cross confirmed that its response personnel were working alongside municipal emergency crews and security teams to track down one worker who remains unaccounted for in the rubble.

“Three people have been rescued and taken to hospital after a building under construction collapsed in Syokimau. Search and rescue operations are ongoing for one person reported missing,” the Kenya Red Cross stated in a site update.

### Pattern of Structural Failures

The Syokimau collapse highlights recurring safety vulnerabilities during high-rise concrete work across the Nairobi metropolitan area. The incident comes just weeks after a similar structural failure was reported in Eastleigh, raising further questions regarding quality checks and temporary support structures during heavy concrete pours.

Multi-agency teams remain at the Syokimau location near the SGR Terminus, with heavy machinery deployed to clear structural debris while search operations continue for the missing worker.
