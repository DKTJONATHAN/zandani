Storefronts from Nairobi’s commercial strips to Kitui’s administrative blocks faced an abrupt pause on Thursday, October 15, 2026, as Kenya Power deployed a sweeping maintenance grid shutdown across five counties.

The scheduled outages, hitting critical zones in Nairobi, Kajiado, Nyeri, Kiambu, and Kitui, forced local enterprises, schools, and health facilities to either fire up costly backup generators or suspend operations during peak morning and afternoon business hours. For most of the affected corridors, the utility's maintenance window stretched steadily from 9 am to 5 pm, while select sectors in Nairobi, Kajiado, and Kitui experienced total current cuts starting as early as 8 am.

The wide geographic sweep laid bare the daily vulnerability of urban and semi-urban commercial ventures relying on uninterrupted grid stability. Salons, digital hubs, cold-storage vendors, and medical institutions within the targeted zones had to navigate the workday without reliable power, altering service delivery for hundreds of residents who depend on these local service nodes.

While Kenya Power issued advance advisories urging businesses and households to make alternative arrangements, the sudden loss of daytime power translates into immediate revenue hits for informal traders and small enterprises operating on tight daily margins. The utility noted that power restoration could either precede the 5 pm deadline or stretch past it depending on the actual progress of the engineering teams on site.

Residents and business owners in the affected regions were left monitoring switchboards as maintenance crews worked through the day's designated grid circuits.
