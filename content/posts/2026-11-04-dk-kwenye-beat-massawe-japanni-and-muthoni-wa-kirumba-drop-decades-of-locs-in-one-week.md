When gospel musician DK Kwenye Beat reached for the clippers to lay down 15 years of signature dreadlocks, he was not just following a personal whim. His clean-cut reveal followed hard on the heels of a sudden, back-to-back shedding wave across Kenya’s prominent radio corridors.

The unravelling began on Wednesday, October 28, 2026. Massawe Japanni, mid-morning host of *Bustani la Massawe* on Radio Jambo, stepped out with a fully shaved head, publishing step-by-step images documenting her severed brown and reddish locs threaded with white beads. Eschewing any narrative of distress, she marked the pivot with a direct declaration on her official Instagram account: "Not a crisis. Not a phase. Just growth."

![DK Kwenye Beat // Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/1GD2htmM/12ecfab55c10.webp)

Within days, the wave crossed frequencies to Kameme FM. Broadcaster Muthoni wa Kirumba, a fixture of the station's mid-morning lineup *Canjamuka* alongside comedian Muthee Kihenjo, announced she was parting ways with the dreadlocks that had defined her public image for 18 years of her 12-year tenure at the broadcaster. Holding her cut locs up for the camera, she remarked that her hair had reached "a legal age" and was being sent "to campus" before declaring the start of a new chapter on November 2, 2026.

DK Kwenye Beat formalized the trio's transformation shortly after, framing his own 15-year haircut as a calculated spiritual milestone rather than a casual trim. Citing biblical parallels to Abraham leaving the familiar, the gospel artist stated that his previous look had fulfilled its operational lifespan.

"What was once part of my journey has served its purpose," DK noted in his video announcement, pointing toward a fresh apostolic direction and new level of ministry.

The swift succession of heavy-hitting media figures dropping decades of distinct personal branding within a seven-day window drew immediate online scrutiny. Followers flooded respective comment sections under handles like @massawejapanni with supportive acknowledgements—including welcoming messages into the new look—while other users met Muthoni’s sudden reveal with skepticism, openly questioning the authenticity of the transition photographs before details of the physical cuts were fully laid bare.
