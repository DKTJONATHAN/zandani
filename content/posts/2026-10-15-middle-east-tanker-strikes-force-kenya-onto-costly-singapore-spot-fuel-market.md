A wave of attacks targeting commercial oil tankers in the Gulf has severed Kenya’s regular Middle Eastern supply routes, forcing domestic oil marketers into the premium Asian spot market and doubling the landed cost of refined fuel.

Testifying before the National Assembly’s Special Funds Accounts Committee on Thursday, Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA) Director-General Edward Mwirigi confirmed that local procurement has pivoted toward Singapore after strikes on Saudi and regional fuel vessels disrupted scheduled shipments to East Africa.

The emergency rerouting carried an immediate financial penalty. Super petrol acquisition costs surged from a baseline of $600 per metric tonne to $1,300 in September—a 116 percent escalation that filtered directly into pump prices published on October 14.

### The Cost of Diverted Cargoes

Under EPRA’s latest pricing cycle, Nairobi pump prices jumped to record highs for 2026. Super petrol reached Ksh226.96 per litre, diesel rose to Ksh227.76, and kerosene climbed to Ksh219.87.

![EPRA Director General Edward Mwirigi Kinyua during the EPRA event on October 14.](https://i.ibb.co/mVhV0MQB/c05fe0cacc06.webp)

Mwirigi told lawmakers that Singapore’s petroleum market carries significantly higher product premiums and shipping charges than traditional Gulf export terminals, but alternative options narrowed rapidly once maritime corridors became hostile.

“September has been the worst month ever since this war broke out," Mwirigi told the committee, reiterating disclosures made a day earlier to the press. "Super petrol has reached USD 1,300 per metric tonne from the initial USD $600 per metric tonne."

Lawmakers pushed the regulator on why domestic consumers were kept in the dark about the offshore supply collapse, demanding specific records on reports that two fuel vessels contracted under regional supply chains had been bombed. While Mwirigi confirmed targeted attacks on Saudi-linked infrastructure, he maintained that Kenya’s pricing structure remains competitive relative to landlocked neighbors relying on the same logistical corridor through the Port of Mombasa.

### Scrutiny Over Broken Stabilization Guarantees

The testimony placed the energy regulator in a difficult political corridor inside Parliament. Committee members pressed EPRA to square the sudden price shock with public commitments made by President William Ruto in May 2026, when the executive pledged that fuel prices would remain insulated from extreme swings.

![Matatus during rush hour at the Fig Tree bus stop along Thika Super Highway, November 12, 2019.](https://i.ibb.co/21Q9CBLt/55acde7ee4bc.webp)

Instead, the October review wiped out months of marginal price relief, leaving the state’s price moderation framework overwhelmed by the sheer scale of global procurement spikes.

The administrative price increases also arrive without the buffer of a full state subsidy. The sole active fiscal cushion remains legislative: the National Assembly passed the Tax Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026, sponsored by Majority Leader Kimani Ichung’wah, which keeps the Value Added Tax on petroleum products pegged at 8 percent for an additional three months, warding off an automatic reversion to the standard 16 percent rate.

### Street-Level Squeeze

While the 8 percent VAT retention prevented an even steeper escalation at the pump, it has done little to calm downstream sectors that operate on razor-thin operating margins.

Public service vehicle operators began recalculating operational sheets within hours of EPRA's announcement. Matatu owners have signalled imminent fare adjustments of up to 10 percent on urban and inter-county routes, warning that diesel at Ksh227.76 per litre makes current passenger rates untenable.

With Mwirigi cautioning the committee that pump prices could climb even higher in subsequent pricing cycles if Gulf security deteriorates further, the next pressure test will arrive in mid-November, when EPRA calculates import consignments currently loading across Asian and Middle Eastern terminals.
