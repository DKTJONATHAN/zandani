Martha Karua is framing her return to the presidential ballot through a distinct organizational shift, formally entering the 2027 race under the People’s Liberation Party (PLP) banner.

The former Justice Minister issued her declaration on Friday, November 28, 2026, positioning the announcement not as a final destination but as the launch of a broader national mission. Her campaign framework rests explicitly on demands for accountable leadership, equal access to economic opportunities, strict adherence to the rule of law, and institutional respect for citizen dignity.

“A declaration marks a beginning, not a destination. I am running for President of the Republic of Kenya in 2027, and I do so with a deep sense of responsibility and an unwavering belief in the promise of our nation,” Karua stated in her official release.

By anchoring her bid within the PLP, Karua enters an early, crowded political landscape where party leaders and independent figures are actively carving out positioning strategies ahead of the 2027 General Election. Her entry introduces a familiar reformist voice back to the top-tier ballot, testing a dedicated party structure as early political realignments begin to crystallize across the country.

Rather than framing the campaign around elite coalition mathematics, Karua used her announcement to direct attention outward toward grassroots voter engagement. She urged Kenyans to take direct ownership of the upcoming electoral cycle, emphasizing that public participation remains central to shifting the country's governance trajectory.

“The road ahead is ours to walk,” Karua noted, calling for collective civic involvement as the national campaign calendar inches closer. “The future is ours to shape.”
