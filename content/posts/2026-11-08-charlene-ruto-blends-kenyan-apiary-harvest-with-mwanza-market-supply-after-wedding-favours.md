Charlene Ruto turned her personal passion for apiculture into a central feature of her Tanzanian wedding favors, transporting honey harvested from her own Kenyan bee farms to the shores of Lake Victoria before having to execute a local sourcing run in Mwanza town when the supply ran short.

The gift package was conceived to give invited guests at the lakeside celebration a tangible taste of both Kenya and Tanzania. While preparing for the November 6, 2026 white wedding at Malaika Beach Resort, Charlene paired jars of her personal harvest with traditional Tanzanian printed cloth.

"Part of the gifts we are giving all my guests for my wedding is honey from Kenya," Charlene explained in video footage recorded during gift assembly. "They're going to get a kanga, beautiful kanga from Tanzania. And we also wanted them to have something from Kenya."

![Charlene Ruto // Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/vx28Hh7Y/8a7e03fd94d2.webp)

For Charlene, who has spent recent years expanding her agricultural footprint across Kenya, using her own apiaries was meant as a personal note of gratitude for guests traveling to Mwanza.

"When thinking of what to gift my wedding guests it was inevitable to remind them how their sweet presence meant the world to me, thus the idea of honey!" she shared on social media. "Transported honey all the way from my bee farms in Kenya to share with them the sweetness of my motherland."

However, assembly line reality set in when the pre-packed jars carried across the border proved insufficient for the full guest list. Rather than scale back the package, Charlene sourced additional stock directly from local vendors within Mwanza town.

"However, I hadn't packed enough na vile iliisha I had to buy some from Mwanza town, very glad to have supported a local honey producer while at it!" she noted. "My guests got to experience the sweetness of Kenya and Tanzania!"

The Mwanza white wedding represented the second leg of a high-profile, cross-border nuptial timeline uniting the Kenyan presidential family with Tanzanian tech entrepreneur Isaya Yunge, who was born and raised in the lakeside city.

The couple formally kicked off their wedding celebrations three months earlier on August 8, 2026, with a traditional Kalenjin engagement ceremony (*Koito*) held at Intonna Heritage Farm in Kilgoris, Narok County.

For the weekend white wedding on the shores of Lake Victoria, Charlene was escorted down the aisle by her parents, Kenyan President William Ruto and First Lady Mama Rachel Ruto. The event brought together extended family, regional political leaders, and invited guests from across East Africa.

With the completion of the Mwanza ceremony, the couple concluded months of public attention following their August engagement, leaving guests with a gift set that physically reflected the cross-border nature of their household.
