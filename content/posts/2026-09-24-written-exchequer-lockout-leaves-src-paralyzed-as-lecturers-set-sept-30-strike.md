A statutory paper trail failure between the Salaries and Remuneration Commission, the Ministry of Education, and the National Treasury has shattered negotiations for public university academic staff, triggering a seven-day nationwide strike notice set to shut down campuses on September 30.

While the headline announcement sets a midnight deadline for public university lecturers to down tools, the collapse of the talks exposes a deep institutional bottleneck within the government's wage-budgeting pipeline. According to disclosures by the Universities Academic Staff Union (UASU), the bargaining machinery unraveled when the remuneration commission admitted it was legally powerless to act.

![Lecturers singing solidarity songs after holding a press conference in October, 2025.](https://i.ibb.co/s9NCpnc8/5a0f32b5ec34.webp)

During a formal engagement on Monday, September 21, the Salaries and Remuneration Commission (SRC) informed university sector unions that neither the Ministry of Education nor the National Treasury had provided a written commitment guaranteeing that funding for the 2025–2029 Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) would be drawn from the National Exchequer.

Without that written financial commitment, the commission stated it could not issue the constitutional advice required to authorize university councils to present a financial counter-proposal. The revelation directly undermined months of negotiations conducted at Machakos University since last year, revealing that university negotiators were operating without the statutory clearance necessary to tabulate binding salary figures.

UASU Secretary-General Constantine Wasonga confirmed on Thursday, September 24, that the union will enforce a total work stoppage starting midnight on Friday, September 30. The union cited the state's persistent failure to honour the Return-to-Work Formula signed on November 5, 2025, alongside the ongoing absence of a counter-proposal for the 2025–2029 bargaining cycle.

![UASU lecturers](https://i.ibb.co/3YBn3jzR/60ea37216c8c.webp)

What heightens frustration among university staff is the disparity across the wider civil service. While other public sector unions have successfully finalized and signed their 2025–2029 CBAs, the academic workforce in public universities remains marooned in an administrative loop between spending units and the central bank of state funding.

The strike call follows an earlier warning issued on August 14, when UASU cautioned that the long-overdue agreement—originally slated for signing in November 2025—was driving public higher education toward disruption. With university councils unable to table verified monetary offers without SRC’s approval, the burden of preventing a full shutdown shifts back to Treasury and Education officials.

Unless the National Treasury issues a formal exchequer commitment to unlock SRC's constitutional clearance before September 30, lecture halls across all public universities will close, leaving academic calendars hanging on an unresolved inter-ministerial memorandum.
