For years, millions of Safaricom subscribers have watched their accumulated Bonga Points sit quietly in their accounts, often redeemed only during low-stakes airtime top-ups or gadget upgrades. That closed-loop loyalty ecosystem is shifting as betting platforms find direct ways to convert idle digital currency into active wagering capital.

Under its latest operational rollout, Pepeta has integrated Safaricom's loyalty framework directly into its deposit pipeline, allowing users to bypass traditional mobile money balances by routing points straight through Paybill 779900. The mechanism bridges standard telco perks with live wagering, offering a practical alternative for players looking to engage without dipping into their primary cash wallets.

![How to deposit on Pepeta](https://i.ibb.co/wFh8FLZW/f1d486997e92.webp)

Beyond loyalty points, the platform’s deposit architecture leans heavily on telco partnerships to maintain user engagement. Through the 'Dandia Data Dailee' promotion running through December 31, 2026, users placing qualifying sportsbook stakes of KSh 50 or more on cash balances unlock up to 150MB of free mobile data. By bundling internet bundles directly into the wagering cycle—regardless of whether a bet wins or loses—the strategy targets the high cost of mobile data that often limits casual online browsing.

On the transaction front, the platform accommodates both Safaricom and Airtel networks while introducing structural safety nets for match outcomes. Its 2UP early payout feature is designed to neutralize late-game equalizers by settling bets instantly the moment a selected football side secures a two-goal cushion during regular play, protecting payouts from subsequent comebacks or abandoned fixtures.

![Dandia na Pepeta](https://i.ibb.co/ZpSQpqzm/a307d72c594a.webp)

With hourly withdrawal thresholds capped at KSh 70,000 and accessible across web, USSD via *457#, and SMS channels, the platform highlights how digital gaming brands are embedding themselves deeper into Kenya's existing mobile telecommunications infrastructure.
