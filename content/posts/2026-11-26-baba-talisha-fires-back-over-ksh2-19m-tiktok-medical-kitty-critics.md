Faustine Lukale, widely known to digital audiences as Baba Talisha, has drawn a hard line against online speculators questioning the integrity of the multi-million-shilling medical fund raised to finance his upcoming eye treatment in India.

In a strongly worded statement released on Thursday, November 26, 2026, the digital influencer and photographer dismissed what he termed deliberate misinformation aimed at casting doubt on the well-wishers who rallied to his aid. Maintaining that he refuses to engage with fabricated online drama, Baba Talisha stressed that the individuals who backed his medical appeal are fully aware of how the process was handled.

“You can spread false information, twist narratives and create stories just to make people talk and question the intentions of those who stood by me. But one thing you cannot change is the truth,” the creator wrote, adding that he remains focused on his recovery rather than social media noise.

The friction follows a high-profile five-hour TikTok fundraising drive held on Monday, November 23, 2026. During the live stream, fellow digital creators, corporate partners, business associates, and fans raised Ksh2.19 million, comfortably eclipsing his initial Ksh1.6 million target. Coupled with an earlier collection of Ksh323,000, the total medical kitty climbed to approximately Ksh2.52 million.

The financial push became necessary after Baba Talisha contracted Acanthamoeba Keratitis, a rare and aggressive corneal infection that severely damaged his eyesight. Though he underwent an emergency therapeutic cornea transplant locally on November 21, 2026, attending specialists advised urgent specialized ophthalmic care abroad. According to prior disclosures by his wife, businesswoman Wanjiru C. Wanjiru, popularly known as Miss Wanjey, the family’s medical insurance policy was entirely depleted by his initial hospital bills.

With the funds secured and the online noise addressed, the creator is now preparing for his departure to India for advanced medical intervention.
