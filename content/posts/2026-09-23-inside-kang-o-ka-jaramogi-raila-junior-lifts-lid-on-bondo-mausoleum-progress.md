Polished marble flooring and extensive interior tiling have taken shape inside the mausoleum rising at Kang’o Ka Jaramogi, marking a major structural leap for the memorial site in Bondo, Siaya County.

Raila Odinga Junior offered a first detailed look at the ongoing construction through a digital walkthrough shared on his TikTok account. Unlike casual social media updates, the footage captures a formal inspection rhythm. Raila Junior is seen navigating the active site flanked by aides dressed in formal attire, dedicated security personnel, and site supervisors wearing standard safety gear.

The visual documentation charts the transition of the family’s ancestral compound from active building zones to an advanced institutional memorial. Beyond the interior spaces, the recording follows the delegation as they step out into the wider grounds, where Raila Junior pauses to exchange greetings with local residents and construction personnel stationed at the property.

The memorial site rests within the historic heart of the Odinga family estate, named after Kenya’s first Vice President, Jaramogi Oginga Odinga. The property serves as the final resting place of the late former Prime Minister and long-serving opposition leader, cementing the estate's dual role as a private family homestead and a landmark of national political heritage.

Structural framing and finishing work continue at the Siaya County compound as finishing touches advance toward project completion.
