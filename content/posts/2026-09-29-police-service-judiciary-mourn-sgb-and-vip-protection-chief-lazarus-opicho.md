Statements from the National Police Service and the Judiciary have confirmed the death of Senior Assistant Inspector General of Police (SAIG) Lazarus Opicho, the commander heading the Security of Government Buildings (SGB) and VIP Protection Unit within the Administration Police Service.

Opicho passed away on Monday night, September 28, 2026, at the Aga Khan University Hospital in Nairobi following a brief illness, leaving a sudden operational void at the helm of Kenya's critical government infrastructure security apparatus.

Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja issued a formal tribute to the fallen commander, characterizing him as an exemplary and dedicated public servant whose disciplined guidance within the police hierarchy shaped sensitive protective operations across state installations.

Chief Justice Martha Koome underscored the institutional reach of Opicho’s career, crediting him with creating the specialized security apparatus that shields judicial officers nationwide.

![Senior Assistant Inspector General of Police Lazarus Opicho](https://i.ibb.co/b5ZWp0q4/450178cd1700.webp)

“I have received with profound shock and sadness the news of the passing of SAIG Lazarus Opicho, who, until his demise, served as the Commander of the Security of Government Buildings and VIP Protection Unit,” Koome stated. “On behalf of the Judiciary, and on my own behalf, I extend my heartfelt condolences to the leadership and entire fraternity of the National Police Service, his family, friends and colleagues on this untimely loss.”

### Formative Footprint Across Key State Units

Opicho’s operational influence extended across several specialized deployments over more than a decade. In 2021, the police command appointed him to establish and lead the Judiciary Police Unit as its pioneer commandant, an assignment tasked with structuring dedicated security rings around court precincts, judges, and judicial staff.

His track record in strategic protection previously included service as a Police Superintendent detailed to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in 2009, where he directed security protocols and specialized investigations for diplomatic engagements.

![Administration Police Constables during a pass-out parade at Administration Police Training College, Embakasi, Nairobi, January 11, 2024.](https://i.ibb.co/fd13Cq0Y/2c8ee3776a91.webp)

In August 2024, an administrative reshuffle within the Administration Police Service elevated Opicho to commandant of the SGB and VIP Protection Unit. In that role, he directed operational deployments securing vital installations, national executive offices, visiting foreign dignitaries, and high-ranking public figures under state protection.

### Career Elevation and Credentials

Opicho earned promotion from Assistant Inspector General to the rank of Senior Assistant Inspector General of Police in 2025, retaining his command over the SGB and VIP protection formations.

A graduate of the National Defence College holding the NDC (K) credential, Opicho was also a recipient of the Head of State Commendation (HSC) for distinguished public service.

The National Police Service command is expected to announce interim leadership arrangements for the SGB and VIP Protection Unit alongside formal funeral arrangements.
