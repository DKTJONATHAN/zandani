The Ministry of Sports has initiated phased ticket sales for the January 5, 2027 clash between Bayern Munich and Ajax Amsterdam, turning the high-profile European friendly into the primary operational and commercial test run for the newly completed 60,000-seat Raila Odinga International Stadium.

Ticket access opened at 6:00 pm on Thursday through the official Talanta Sports City portal, establishing a floor price of Ksh2,500 for general seating alongside premium corporate hospitality packages and skybox tiers.

The fixture serves as the formal opening of the Talanta Sports City centerpiece, designed to stress-test crowd flow, digital access gates, and hospitality services six months before Kenya co-hosts the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations alongside Uganda and Tanzania.

### Access Controls and Tiered Sales

![Sports and Entertainment Cabinet Secretary Salim Mvurya, during the Talanta Stadium inspection on August 19.](https://i.ibb.co/Z50cZHw/39900b10e3d4.webp)

Sports Cabinet Secretary Salim Mvurya confirmed that ticketing would be restricted to the dedicated portal, www.TalantaSportsCity.com, in an effort to curb black-market reselling and fraudulent third-party portals.

"The tickets will be released in phases across multiple seating categories, with prices starting from as low as Kshs. 2,500, reflecting our commitment to making this exceptional sporting experience accessible to as many Kenyans as possible," Mvurya said.

Organizers have separated the inventory into regular stadium seating and exclusive corporate hospitality packages, routing private skybox bookings through a dedicated concierge service. Alongside the match, the venue will host a 12-hour fan festival featuring local and international musical performances, cultural showcases, and hospitality lounges designed to maintain active crowd presence outside standard 90-minute operations.

### Contractual First-Team Guarantees

To counter public skepticism common to post-season and mid-season exhibition tours, match organizers secured written commitments from both clubs on October 28 binding them to field full-strength senior squads.

![An image of the Talanta Stadium under construction on June 23, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/5gpJ3j8w/e9c4f0fe746f.webp)

The agreements explicitly bar Bayern Munich and Ajax from deploying youth academy squads or exhibition "legends" rosters, ensuring that marquee stars travel to Nairobi during their domestic mid-winter competitive breaks. 

The presence of European first-team players is central to testing security screening protocols, pitch-surface performance, and broadcast cabling under international match conditions matching Confederation of African Football (CAF) standards.

### Financing Scrutiny Ahead of AFCON 2027

The fixture comes amid public debate over the costs required to lure top-tier European clubs to East Africa. In response to questions surrounding state expenditure, Mvurya emphasized that the exhibition is funded through private-sector commercial partnerships rather than exchequer allocations.

The operational performance of the Raila Odinga International Stadium on January 5 will serve as an audit for Kenya’s infrastructure readiness. With AFCON PAMOJA 2027 set to kick off on June 19, 2027, the January friendly provides venue managers their only full-capacity dry run to assess ticketing integrity, traffic management along the corridor, and inner-bowl operations before continental tournament inspectors arrive.
