The Kenya Environmental Health and Public Health Practitioners Union (KEPHUPU) has instructed its members to resume duty within 24 hours, providing immediate operational relief to unmanned border screening points and county disease surveillance desks facing mounting Ebola threats.

Announcing the decision in Kitui County, KEPHUPU Secretary General Brown Ashira confirmed that the union had suspended its 94-day nationwide industrial action for a strictly monitored 30-day negotiation window following an intervention by Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale.

"We want to ask all our members at the border points, at all the county governments here in Kitui, we want to ask all our members to resume duty in the next 24 hours as we give the duration of 30 days of negotiation time to be able to see our issues being addressed," Ashira stated.

The swift return order prioritises national biosecurity, with the union openly conceding that escalating regional Ebola infections made an extended withdrawal of surveillance personnel untenable. Public health officers serve as the primary defensive perimeter at international entry points and decentralized county monitoring networks, screening cross-border travellers and isolating suspected contagion before outbreaks penetrate local communities.

"In the wake of Ebola, we want to make sure that our population are protected, safeguarded by the well-endowed PHOs who know what their work is in terms of prevention," Ashira added.

### The Kitui Blueprint and Outstanding Grievances

The suspension was formalized after the union signed an initial return-to-work formula with the County Government of Kitui, creating a local operational template that KEPHUPU expects the remaining 46 devolved administrations to duplicate during the month-long conciliation period.

However, the operational truce leaves an expansive ledger of unresolved structural demands on the bargaining table. Central to KEPHUPU's grievances is the long-delayed formal government recognition of the union, alongside the conclusion, signing, and registration of its Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA).

The union is also pushing for:

* Nationwide recruitment of additional public health officers to ease chronic understaffing.
* Formal posting and regularized stipend disbursement for eligible interns.
* Absorption of contractual and Universal Health Coverage (UHC) staff into permanent and pensionable terms.
* Payment of dedicated risk allowances tailored to frontline outbreak response.
* Halting the administrative transfer of public health personnel and diagnostic assets to the Kenya National Public Health Institute without independent budgets or stakeholder consultation.

### Intergovernmental Deadlock Over UHC Billions

While CS Duale's direct mediation unlocked the 30-day suspension, the broader labor dispute remains entangled in an ongoing fiscal turf war between the national executive and county leadership over healthcare human resources.

CS Duale has previously pointed fingers at county governments, accusing devolved administrations of deliberately dragging their feet on absorbing more than 7,400 UHC personnel into permanent county payrolls. According to the Health Ministry, the national treasury set aside Ksh8.6 billion specifically to facilitate that workforce transition, with the CS terming county bureaucracies the primary impediment to stable public care delivery.

County chiefs reject that diagnosis. Former Council of Governors Health Committee Chairperson Ahmed Abdullahi has previously accused the Ministry of Health of repeatedly reneging on intergovernmental pacts, arguing that the ministry's failure to honor its financial and policy commitments continues to spark cyclical industrial action across the medical sector.

With public health officers now returning to frontier screening booths and sub-county clinics, the clock on the 30-day window has begun. If the national government and county service boards fail to translate Duale's intervention into registered agreements by late November, the country's fragile disease surveillance network faces an abrupt return to full shutdown.
