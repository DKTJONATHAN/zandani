Kenyan singer and songwriter Sofiya Nzau is trading digital streaming dominance for passport stamps, locking in a sprawling 2026/27 world tour that reaches across Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia.

Recognized as one of East Africa’s most streamed artists on platforms like Spotify and Apple Music, Nzau’s newly revealed routing skips predictable Western European capitals in favor of a striking mix of emerging international markets and home-ground stops.

The international leg kicks off with a diverse sequence of cities, bringing her live set to Batumi, Krasnodar, Kyrgyzstan, Amsterdam, Nairobi, and Lagos. That initial run balances local performance demands with stops in Eastern Europe and Central Asia, before the artist loops back through continental music capitals.

Once the African and European dates wrap, the tour's second phase pushes deep into the Southern Hemisphere and Southeast Asia. Scheduled performances running into February 2027 will take Nzau to Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, Bali, Bangkok, and Auckland.

The ambitious itinerary highlights a broader shift among contemporary Kenyan acts securing direct footing on international stages, testing whether heavy digital consumption in foreign markets translates into physical ticket sales.

For local followers, the home leg in Nairobi provides a direct chance to catch the globally-profiled artist on familiar ground. Management indicates that the current schedule remains a baseline, with additional international dates expected to roll out as the multi-continent tour progresses through 2026.
