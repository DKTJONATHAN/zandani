Deputy President Kithure Kindiki has ordered United Democratic Alliance officials in Embu to conduct an immediate audit of property destroyed during chaotic scenes at Nembure Stadium on Saturday, where running battles forced his emergency evacuation and left a trail of smouldering vehicles.

### What we know
- Kindiki’s rushed retreat from Embu exposes raw fault lines and persistent grassroots resistance within the ruling party's core Mt. Kenya backyard.
- Excluding local youth factions at the perimeter gates continues to trigger swift, retaliatory street violence targeting political delegations.
- Torching private motors and a journalist's vehicle signals a deliberate shift from routine political heckling to destructive arson.
- Directing county operatives to tally losses effectively shifts crisis management onto local coordinators while security chiefs scramble for answers.

The Nembure rally unraveled shortly after midday when security marshals blocked scores of local youth from entering the venue. Within minutes, the perimeter turned into a flashpoint as stone-throwing groups clashed with anti-riot police, who fired volleys of tear gas to maintain a buffer around the main podium. As security details realised the perimeter was breached, they bundled the Deputy President into his motorcade and sped away toward the Embu-Meru highway.

The aftermath on the tarmac outside the stadium bore the brunt of the confrontation. Eyewitnesses recounted that at least 10 vehicles parked along the approach roads were set ablaze by roving bands of demonstrators. Among the charred shells was an unmarked car belonging to a local media correspondent who had been dispatched to cover the governing party's regional mobilization drive. Firefighters arriving under armed escort struggled to salvage anything from the smoking wrecks.

Addressing the fallout hours later, Kindiki instructed Embu UDA chairpersons to establish direct tallies of the wreckage and draft support measures for the victims. The Deputy President characterised the clashes as a sponsored attempt to bully the party through intimidation, warning that law enforcement agencies had been instructed to track down and prosecute the financiers of the melee. He insisted that the party would not abandon policy-centered politics in the face of manufactured unrest.

On the ground in Nembure, the street-level assessment paints a more turbulent picture of unresolved local rivalries over party access and regional supremacy. While the administration frames the clash as isolated thuggery, the sheer speed at which local frustration curdled into coordinated arson highlights how volatile routine party gatherings have become across the lower eastern corridor. For residents living along the stadium access routes, the weekend ended with closed storefronts, acrid rubber smoke, and mounting questions over how routine political meetings turn into open-air battlefields.
