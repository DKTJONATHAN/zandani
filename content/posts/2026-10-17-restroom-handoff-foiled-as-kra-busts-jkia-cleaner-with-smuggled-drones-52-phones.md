A scheme to bypass international passenger screening at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport unraveled on Saturday after customs officers intercepted a terminal cleaner attempting to carry two backpacks packed with undeclared electronics out of the arrivals hall.

The operation by the Kenya Revenue Authority exposed an airside handoff designed to slip commercial cargo past border barriers without paying duty or securing statutory aviation clearances.

### The Restroom Transfer

According to customs officials, the operation came to light during routine passenger clearance when border agents spotted a female airport cleaner heading toward a terminal exit while carrying two heavy black backpacks. When the worker realized she had drawn the attention of customs personnel, she broke into a run, prompting officers to intercept and detain her on site.

During questioning, the cleaner stated that an incoming female passenger had approached her inside the airport restrooms shortly after deplaning from an international flight. The traveler allegedly handed over both backpacks and directed the cleaner to bypass the central customs clearance buffer with the bags.

![A past image of the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport with an insert of a drone.](https://i.ibb.co/Hpf3rdYr/06c1932d9d85.webp)

Customs investigators subsequently fed both packs through a computed tomography (CT) baggage scanner, which flagged dense electronic components inside the fabric lining. 

Officers tracked down the arriving traveler within the terminal. Border records examined during the encounter identified her as a frequent traveler. She acknowledged handing the baggage to the cleaning staff member in the restrooms, leading officers to conduct a joint physical inspection in the presence of both women.

### Unlicensed Avionics and Mobile Cargo

The unzipping of the intercepted backpacks yielded a substantial commercial haul: 52 refurbished mobile phones alongside three consumer drones, flight controllers, spare batteries, chargers, travel adapters, and USB cables. 

Inventory documentation filed by customs listed the impounded aircraft as two DJI Mini 3 Pro units and one DJI Air 2S model. All items were transferred under armed guard to the customs warehouse at JKIA, where they remain secured as formal documentation proceeds.

While refurbished electronics frequently target downtown retail outlets without payment of requisite duties, the presence of unmanned aircraft immediately elevated the infraction from a revenue recovery issue to an aviation compliance breach.

![Some of the devices recovered by the DCI during a raid that yielded over 500 stolen smartphones and motherboards in Kisii, September 17, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/RTgQBJ0g/8ad517c52830.webp)

"The importation of drones into Kenya is restricted and requires the relevant authorisation from the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority," KRA said in an enforcement brief. "Travellers and importers are therefore required to establish and comply with the applicable regulatory requirements before bringing drones into the country."

Under statutory Kenyan civil aviation regulations, unmanned aircraft systems cannot clear borders without pre-clearance certificates, import permits issued by the aviation regulator, and security verification.

### Mounting Scrutiny on Airport Corridors

Saturday's interception points to heightened vigilance against inside runners at JKIA, where smugglers have periodically leaned on outsourced terminal workers who hold airport passes to leapfrog screening corridors.

The seizure follows another high-value bust at the terminal on October 1, when customs officers uncovered a shipment of 40 undeclared iPhones valued at approximately Ksh 8.2 million in the baggage of an inbound passenger from Dubai.

With both women detained and the undeclared electronics in state storage, customs authorities have initiated administrative and investigative procedures to determine whether charges will extend to conspiracy to evade customs controls alongside the baseline regulatory violations.
