Kabi Wa Jesus has turned his family’s latest real estate venture into the visual center of his current digital broadcasting drive, publishing fresh progress updates of a property build that has now moved past its foundational structure.

On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, the creator posted a series of aerial and ground-level photographs on Facebook, offering followers a clear look at the structural work underway. Alongside the images, Kabi addressed the operational and personal friction involved in managing a major build, taking a candid stance on the realities of the project.

“To be honest, it hasn’t been an easy journey, but through it all, God has been faithful,” he wrote. “Every step, every challenge and every little milestone has been a testimony of His grace.”

### Turning Construction Friction Into Digital Content

The property update forms part of Kabi’s ongoing *50 Days of Thanksgiving* series, a structured online rollout through which he has been sharing daily reflections on his personal life, family milestones, and faith. Rather than keeping the structural update confined to static social media images, he used the post to prompt engagement from his audience, asking followers to share the personal goals they were working toward in their own lives.

He also confirmed that the site will serve as the backdrop for upcoming long-form video content. A comprehensive walkthrough showing the layout and structural details is set to air on the family's main YouTube channel in an upcoming episode.

### The Property Portfolio Timeline

This ongoing build marks a secondary chapter in the couple’s documented real estate journey. In 2021, Kabi first introduced his wife, Milly Wa Jesus, to a vacant construction lot, publicly outlining their plans to put down roots in a permanent family home. That initial venture moved rapidly through its building phases, culminating in 2022 when the couple published a full tour of the finished house, detailing its interior design, customized living spaces, and modern features for their audience.

The current 2026 build highlights how the family continues to integrate major life updates directly into their content distribution strategy. By anchoring his multi-week thanksgiving campaign around real-time site updates, Kabi bridges personal real estate investments with audience retention across his primary social platforms.

Further structural details and interior plans are expected to be disclosed when the next full episode drops on the Wa Jesus Family YouTube channel as part of the continuous series.
