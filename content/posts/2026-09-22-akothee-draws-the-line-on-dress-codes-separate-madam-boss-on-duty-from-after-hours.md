Singer and businesswoman Akothee has drawn a sharp line between her civic assignments and her private nightlife, telling online critics that completing a county task for Homa Bay Governor Gladys Wanga does not place her off-duty wardrobe under public management.

The entertainer addressed the uproar on Facebook after photos showing her alongside Luo leaders in an outfit that exposed her legs triggered complaints from commentators demanding a formal apology for alleged disrespect.

Akothee made it clear that her contractual obligations ended the moment she formally submitted her work to the county chief.

### Delivering the Brief, Then Dropping the Briefing

According to the musician, her day began under formal protocol, executing an official assignment she had been appointed to carry out by Governor Wanga. Once the project had been handed over to the county government, however, she considered her professional responsibilities concluded.

Her evening plans, she argued, belonged entirely to her personal life rather than civic protocol.

“There was no memo saying, ‘Akothee, kindly report to nightlife wearing a bedsheet,’” she wrote, dismissing the expectation that she ought to carry formal office decorum into social venues.

Addressing the scrutiny over her appearance around political figures, she rejected the suggestion that an entertainer's presence in political circles requires adopting permanent conservative dress.

“There was NO DRESS CODE, so I dressed MY BODY — the same body God gave me, including these long legs that have apparently now become a county emergency,” she said.

### The Boundary Between 'Madam Boss' and the Performer

For Akothee, the backlash exposed a recurring inability among observers to distinguish between a contracted professional delivering on a brief and an independent creative spending her leisure time.

Her corporate and philanthropic persona, built under the "Madam Boss" moniker, has frequently seen her partner with state agencies, county administrations, and commercial brands. Yet she insisted that those partnerships do not buy full-time ownership over her individual expression.

“Please learn to separate Madam Boss on duty from Madam Boss after working hours,” she stated, urging the public to evaluate her performance on delivered output rather than evening leisure attire.

### No Apology on the Table

Calls for her to issue a statement expressing regret for the appearance were shut down without compromise.

“Apology? FORGET IT! I did my job. I handed over. Then I became AKOTHEE THE ENTERTAINER,” she wrote.

Signing off the post as “AKOTHEE THE GREAT,” the entrepreneur maintained that public commentary will not dictate how she navigates her time once official duties are completed, leaving the debate firmly in the hands of critics while she moves on to her next engagement.
