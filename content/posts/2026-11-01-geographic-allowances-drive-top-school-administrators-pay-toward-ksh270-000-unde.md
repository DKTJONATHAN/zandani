Geographic posting has emerged as the decisive factor determining take-home earnings for Kenya's public school heads under the newly activated phase of the 2025–2029 Collective Bargaining Agreement.

While the Teachers Service Commission (TSC) sets basic remuneration through a structured scale of job grades, the addition of cluster-based housing and hardship allowances creates an earnings spread of nearly Ksh40,000 among administrators holding identical ranks, according to details in a circular dated July 16, 2026.

The revised terms, which took effect retroactively on July 1, 2026, govern the second phase of the four-stage national labor pact. Under the structure, Chief Principals designated under Job Grade D5 and T-Scale 15 earn basic monthly salaries ranging between Ksh133,351 and Ksh164,977, depending on their established salary points.

### The Geographic Spread at the Apex

For top administrators stationed in Nairobi, compensation reaches a monthly gross of Ksh230,977 when a basic salary of Ksh164,977 is combined with a Ksh50,000 house allowance and a Ksh16,000 commuter allowance.

![A section of Teachers Service Commission Headquarters (TSC) building in Upperhill, Nairobi. Wednesday, March 4, 2020.](https://i.ibb.co/F4GdGSVv/a07931e46ef2.webp)

That compensation ceiling shifts sharply for equivalent heads serving in gazetted hardship areas. The circular provides a monthly hardship allowance of Ksh38,100 for Grade D5 personnel, pushing potential gross monthly earnings to Ksh269,077.

By contrast, institutional heads operating outside designated hardship zones and away from major urban hubs face lower housing subsidies. Chief Principals stationed in the second municipal cluster receive Ksh35,000 in monthly house allowance, while those deployed to other rural and semi-urban stations receive Ksh25,000—exactly half the rate remitted to colleagues in the capital.

### Scales for Senior Principals and Principals

The administrative tiers directly below Chief Principal reflect similar regional structuring.

Senior Principals classified under Job Grade D4 receive basic salaries of between Ksh120,016 and Ksh148,480. Principals in Job Grade D3 draw basic pay ranging from Ksh107,634 to Ksh131,405.

![TSC CEO EVALEEN MITEI](https://i.ibb.co/Rf62cqY/38d44f80c7ba.webp)

Both D3 and D4 administrators receive a flat monthly commuter allowance of Ksh14,000. Their housing allocations follow a uniform three-tier grid: Ksh45,000 in Nairobi, Ksh28,000 across cluster-two urban centers, and Ksh21,000 in all other administrative zones.

Beyond basic salaries and monthly operational stipends, administrators in Grades D3, D4, and D5 are entitled to an annual leave allowance of Ksh10,000, disbursed once each calendar year. The schedule also maintains a disability guide allowance of Ksh20,000 per month for qualifying personnel.

### Transition Rules and Scope

The commission confirmed that existing managerial titles will remain unaltered under the revised scales, with regular annual pay increments proceeding in accordance with the Code of Regulations for Teachers (2015). Administrators transitioning to the higher scales preserve their established incremental anniversary dates.

The circular draws a strict boundary around eligibility: the July 1 adjustments apply exclusively to educators who were on permanent and pensionable service as of that date, expressly excluding intern teachers from the Phase Two adjustments.

The terms stem from the multi-year collective agreement signed on July 18, 2025, between the commission and three sector unions: the Kenya National Union of Teachers (KNUT), the Kenya Union of Post Primary Education Teachers (KUPPET), and the Kenya Union of Special Needs Education Teachers (KUSNET). Two further annual adjustment stages remain before the agreement concludes its four-year cycle in 2029.
