Former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua has found a fresh political sibling in Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna, warmly christening him “my cousin” as he throws his full weight behind the newly unveiled Equitable Party.

In a spirited public statement, Gachagua didn't just offer standard opposition solidarity; he leaned heavily into personal branding. By framing Sifuna’s new political outfit as a crucial vehicle for the 2027 General Election, the ousted former second-in-command signaled a deliberate alignment with the combative Nairobi legislator.

The public endorsement comes as Gachagua continues to chart his own political path following his high-profile impeachment in October 2024. By attaching his considerable political footprint to Sifuna’s fresh party launch, Gachagua is actively working to anchor himself within the core architecture of the anti-incumbent coalition.

Going beyond simple well-wishes, Gachagua outlined a demanding blueprint for the newly minted outfit. He urged Sifuna to move with immediate speed to market the Equitable Party nationwide, putting out a call for rigorous candidate recruitment across every elective seat to unseat lawmakers loyal to President William Ruto.

Striking a characteristically combative tone, Gachagua warned that President Ruto's parliamentary allies face an unforgiving electorate in 2027, declaring that supporters will use the new party machinery to ensure “no escape route” for the current regime.

Whether the affectionate “cousin” label translates into a durable electoral pact or merely underscores the fluid dynamics of opposition realignments remains to be seen as both figures position themselves ahead of the 2027 ballot.
