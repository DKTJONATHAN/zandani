A roadside surveillance operation along the Maua-Makutano Road near Kemu in Meru County resulted in the arrest of two traffic police officers and the immediate impoundment of a Toyota Fielder suspected to belong to one of the suspects.

Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission (EACC) operatives deployed to the corridor executed the targeted sting following persistent complaints from motorists traversing the route. During the operation, investigators recovered Ksh23,000 in mixed denominations from the officers.

![A photo of traffic police officers controlling vehicles along a road, with an inset showing money spread on a table.](https://i.ibb.co/Kc7Rpw2B/3e6afd57ab92.webp)

Unlike traditional desk-based inquiries, the intervention focused directly on active roadside checkpoints where drivers face routine cash demands. The impounded vehicle and the cash have been secured as material evidence in the ongoing inquiry.

Following the field apprehension, the two suspects were transferred to the commission's Upper Eastern Regional Office in Isiolo for formal statement recording and processing ahead of further legal steps.

![TRAFFIC POLICE](https://i.ibb.co/1Y91DH9T/d10c42fd484d.webp)

The operation forms part of a wider enforcement push by the anti-graft agency targeting frontline collection points on transit networks. Earlier on Friday, a parallel EACC operation netted an NTSA clerk who had allegedly collected Ksh8,000 out of a demanded Ksh18,000 facilitation fee for a driving licence before being released on a Ksh50,000 cash bail.

Investigators have not yet disclosed whether the officers will face immediate court arraignment in Isiolo or if further arrests are expected as forensic analysis of the recovered cash and vehicle continues.
