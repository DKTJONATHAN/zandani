**NAIROBI, Kenya** - Politician Morara Kebaso has publicly confirmed his marital status, disclosing that he is currently in a polygamous marriage with two wives. The statement, delivered during an interview on Friday with the Kisii-based vernacular outlet Etaya Radio, comes amid ongoing public interest regarding the activist's private life.

Addressing listeners, Kebaso stated, "Someone asked if I’m married. Yes, I am a married man, I have two wives." He further elaborated on his family structure, specifically naming his children and confirming the identities of his spouses. During the broadcast, he referred to his second wife, Pauline, and reiterated his commitment to his household, describing himself as a "family man."

The confirmation follows a period of heightened social media discourse regarding Kebaso’s personal affairs. His former partner, Mercy Makini, previously cited a desire to seek personal peace and escape domestic issues as the reason for her decision to end their relationship earlier this year.

Kebaso’s remarks on Etaya Radio serve as his latest public address regarding his domestic life since the separation from Makini gained traction in the public sphere. He has yet to provide further details regarding his current living arrangements, noting only that he is awaiting further developments regarding his second wife's public role.
