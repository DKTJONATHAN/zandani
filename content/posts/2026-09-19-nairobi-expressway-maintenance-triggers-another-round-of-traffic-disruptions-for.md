Nairobi commuters are bracing for another weekend of traffic snarl-ups as the MOJA Expressway Company announced the temporary closure of a key section of the Nairobi Expressway for maintenance. The move, set to divert thousands of vehicles onto already congested public roads, highlights a growing pattern of disruptions linked to the privately-operated toll highway.

From Saturday, September 19, 2026, at 8 pm, until Monday, September 21, at 12 pm, the elevated overpass deck of the Expressway will be inaccessible to motorists travelling from the James Gichuru Junction towards the Central Business District (CBD). During this 40-hour period, affected drivers will be redirected to Waiyaki Way to continue their journeys.

MOJA Expressway Company, the private firm managing the Expressway, confirmed the closure in a statement released on Friday, September 18. While the Nairobi Westlands Entrance to the Expressway will remain operational, and motorists exiting towards the overpass will resume normal operations from Saturday at 5 pm, the primary diversion is expected to significantly impact traffic flow along the busy Westlands routes.

The upcoming maintenance follows closely on the heels of a similar incident just days prior. On September 9, motorists experienced severe delays along Waiyaki Way after the closure of the overpass at the ABC Place-Red Hill Road Junction. Many attributed the resulting traffic gridlock to vehicles diverting from the Nairobi Expressway, underscoring the ripple effect that disruptions on the toll road can have on the city's broader transport infrastructure.

The repeated need for maintenance closures, and the subsequent channeling of Expressway traffic onto alternative routes, presents an ongoing challenge for Nairobi's urban planning and daily commuters. While the Expressway was designed to alleviate congestion, its operational requirements, including scheduled maintenance, appear to be shifting the burden to other critical arteries like Waiyaki Way.

MOJA Expressway has advised drivers to plan their journeys carefully, allow for extended travel times, and exercise caution. Traffic marshals will be stationed along the affected routes to guide motorists. The company extended an apology for any inconvenience caused, acknowledging the impact on daily travel.
