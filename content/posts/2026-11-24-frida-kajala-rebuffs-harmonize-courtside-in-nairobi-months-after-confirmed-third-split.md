Tanzanian musician Harmonize found himself at the centre of a tense courtside moment in Nairobi after his ex-girlfriend, actress Frida Kajala, explicitly declined his attempt to hug her during an international basketball tournament.

The public encounter, captured in video footage circulated on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, took place in the spectator seating of the 2027 Road to the Basketball Africa League (BAL) East Division Elite 16 qualifiers. Harmonize had approached Kajala’s row, leaning forward to pull her into an embrace. Kajala immediately recoiled, stepping back from the singer, refusing the physical contact, and standing up to turn her head away.

After being rebuffed, Harmonize shifted his path to greet nearby spectators sitting along the aisle. As he walked past Kajala without making eye contact, he was caught on camera saying, “You acting funny,” before moving further down the row.

### Courtside Drama at the Road to BAL

Both public figures had traveled to Kenya's capital for the week-long regional basketball showcase, which runs from November 23 to November 29, 2026. Harmonize was present in the arena to support Tanzanian basketball club Dar City, who opened their tournament run with a 96-76 victory against Mozambique’s Ferroviario da Beira.

While the singer took up a visible position supporting the Dar es Salaam outfit during their match, his attempt to engage Kajala turned their shared presence in the arena into the focus of attention off the court.

### A Rollercoaster Timeline

The awkward exchange in Nairobi marks the pair's first high-profile public run-in since Harmonize confirmed the termination of their relationship in September 2026. The split marked the third formal separation for the couple, whose high-profile romance originally began around 2020.

Their relationship history has been marked by distinct phases of public reconciliation and distance. Earlier in 2026, Harmonize proposed to Kajala, signaling an intention to marry that was widely publicized across regional entertainment platforms. However, the engagement broke down within months, leading to the September confirmation that they had ended their partnership.

Kajala's refusal to engage courtside signals a firm post-breakup boundary, even as both high-profile figures continue attending the same regional sports fixtures in Nairobi through the remainder of the November tournament schedule.
