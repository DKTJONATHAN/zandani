NAIROBI - Content creator and forex trader Chuka Kenyan Prince has officially transitioned his public romantic pursuit of influencer Sheryl Gabriella into a musical project, releasing a new track titled "Bado Nakupenda."

The song’s lyrics explicitly reference the rejection Chuka previously disclosed, with the artist singing, “Sheryl Gabriella nilisema nakupenda ukanikataa… hata unikatae bado nakupenda.” This release follows a series of viral social media posts, including a video where the creator held a photograph of Gabriella while professing his feelings, an act that drew significant attention across digital platforms.

Public response to the song has been varied. While some followers have engaged with the track as a lighthearted continuation of his online persona, others have questioned the appropriateness of continuing to pursue an individual who has already declined his advances.

Commenting on the release, one social media user challenged the sincerity of the gesture, suggesting, “Chora tattoo ya jina lake kwa kifua ndio tuamini,” while others have used the platform to compare his public antics to the theatrical style often seen in regional music industries.

Chuka, who has previously gained notoriety for viral moments involving extravagant displays at nightclubs and satirical news broadcasts, maintains that the song is a genuine reflection of his sentiments. As of Wednesday, Sheryl Gabriella has not issued a public response to the song or the renewed attention surrounding her name.
