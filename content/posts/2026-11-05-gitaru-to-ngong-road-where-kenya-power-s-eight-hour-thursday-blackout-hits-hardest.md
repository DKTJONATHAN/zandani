Traders operating along key urban commercial strips and residential pockets in Nairobi and satellite counties are rearranging their operations this Thursday as Kenya Power rolls out a widespread maintenance schedule.

The scheduled interruptions, which began at 9:00 am, are slated to run for up to eight hours in selected zones, with restoration anticipated between 4:00 pm and 5:00 pm depending on the progress of engineering teams on the ground.

In the capital and its immediate western approaches, the disruption hits critical traffic and commercial nodes. Motorists and business owners along Ngong Road and Dagoretti Corner find themselves navigating the daytime hours without mains electricity. Further out along the western circuit, Gitaru Shopping Centre and its surrounding business community face a similar halt in supply, impacting local retail shops, cyber cafes, and light commercial workshops that depend on steady daytime power.

The utility firm's Thursday bulletin also touches pockets across six counties, drawing in selected areas within Kiambu, Kisii, Mombasa, Kilifi, and parts of the wider Nairobi metropolitan zone, including Marimani and Bwagamoyo.

According to the utility provider, the temporary outages are part of periodic grid enhancement works designed to improve overall supply stability and network reliability. However, for small-scale enterprises operating on tight daily margins in busy centers like Gitaru and Dagoretti Corner, an eight-hour window without power translates directly to lost trade hours and alternative fuel expenses for those running backup generators.

Customers in the affected zones have had to adjust their schedules ahead of the grid shutdowns, with normal supply expected to resume progressively as maintenance teams complete their designated line repairs by late afternoon.
