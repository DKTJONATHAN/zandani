Kevin Bahati has turned to live television accounting to reset the public narrative around his month-long "Bahati’s Big Day" campaign, rejecting viral claims of a Ksh 100 million payout and disclosing that the event raised approximately Ksh 10 million for his philanthropic foundation.

Speaking on NTV’s *Fixing The Nation* program on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, the musician and 2027 political hopeful addressed mounting public pushback over allegations that he misled followers into expecting a 10th-anniversary white wedding ceremony with his wife, Diana Marua. Bahati insisted he never explicitly promised a wedding, describing the promotional campaign as an intentional artistic hook designed to command nationwide attention.

### The Ksh 1M Price Tag and Visibility Rollout

Addressing the widely discussed Ksh 1 million entrance tag printed on event invitations, Bahati explained that the figure was a strategic marketing mechanism to build exclusivity and demand rather than an unyielding cover charge. Guests, he noted, ultimately contributed varying amounts based on their individual capacity.

The publicity campaign also incorporated featured visits to small business owners, including neighborhood barbershops and beauty salons. According to Bahati, those promotional clips recorded over three million views per video, leveraging his personal reach to grant local enterprises broader visibility while building momentum for the main event.

### Disputing the Ksh 100M Rumors

The artist pushed back directly against online reports claiming the high-profile gathering collected upwards of Ksh 100 million from attendees, putting the verified total collected at roughly Ksh 10 million.

Bahati emphasized that the money was routed directly to *Bahati Cares*, his initiative aimed at supporting vulnerable households across the country, rather than being absorbed into his personal or corporate accounts. He described the venture as a structured corporate social responsibility drive aimed at leveraging high-net-worth relationships for community support.

### Donor Vetting and Political Ambitions

During the broadcast, show hosts questioned Bahati on the source and vetting of donor funds, particularly given his active political profile looking toward the 2027 general election. The lineup of attendees included top political figures and Cabinet Secretaries.

Bahati defended the financial integrity of the contributions, asserting that the money originated from legitimate business owners and established well-wishers. He pointed to the corporate turnout and political presence as validation of his executive reach and organizing capacity.

With the financial disclosures now on record, the focus around "Bahati’s Big Day" moves from promotional speculation to the actual disbursement tracking of the Ksh 10 million pool under *Bahati Cares* as Bahati continues shaping his platform for 2027.
