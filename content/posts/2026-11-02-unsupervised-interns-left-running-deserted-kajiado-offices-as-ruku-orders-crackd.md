Government offices across multiple regional hubs are being kept afloat by junior interns operating entirely without senior guidance, an impromptu administrative inspection has revealed.

Public Service Cabinet Secretary Geoffrey Ruku made an unannounced visit to government workstations in Kajiado County on Monday, arriving after 9 a.m. to verify reports of mounting laxity. The inspection found the majority of permanent public servants missing from their desks.

![Public Service CS Geoffrey Ruku during an impromptu visit to Kajiado County Government offices on November 2.](https://i.ibb.co/4wNd2Cd8/9b0233314c16.webp)

Of the few individuals who had reported for duty, approximately 90 per cent were government interns left to manage official operations without senior administrative oversight. Ruku indicated that this heavy reliance on unguided trainees is not an isolated Kajiado anomaly, noting that similar inspections in Nakuru, Kisumu, Mombasa, and Nyeri uncovered identical patterns where interns showed higher punctuality than their absentee supervisors.

The absence extended directly to the administrative machinery tasked with enforcing workplace compliance. Human resource managers responsible for tracking attendance and compiling daily operational reports were also found missing from their offices during the Monday sweep.

The Ministry of Public Service has signaled immediate disciplinary measures, starting with penalizing two local officers in Kajiado and initiating punitive steps against other senior staff members found culpable of abandoning their posts.

![Public Service Commission House](https://i.ibb.co/XdFk1Ly/e8f6130daceb.webp)

With the festive season approaching, the ministry reiterated that official Christmas leave requires formal approval and departmental scheduling rather than an automatic nationwide shutdown. Government offices observe gazetted public holidays on December 25 and December 26, meaning normal administrative workflows remain active under standard public service schedules.

Unlike civil service offices, public sector staffing rules for educators follow a separate calendar overseen by the Ministry of Education. While Term Three instruction concluded on October 23, teachers tied to national examinations continue to maintain active duty through the ongoing testing period.
