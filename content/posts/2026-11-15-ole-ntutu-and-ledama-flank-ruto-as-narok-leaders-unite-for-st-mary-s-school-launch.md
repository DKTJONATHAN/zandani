Narok Governor Patrick Ole Ntutu and Senator Ledama Olekina set aside partisan divides on Sunday, stepping onto the dais side by side to welcome President William Ruto for the official commissioning of St. Mary’s Pre-School and its new administrative wing.

The high-profile joint appearance brought together the county's executive leadership and its fiery legislative voice in a rare display of unified hosting during the Head of State’s ongoing development tour of Narok County. Standing shoulder-to-shoulder with senior national education officials and local leaders, Ole Ntutu and Olekina anchored the local delegation receiving the presidential entourage.

The newly opened early childhood facility and administrative block represent a targeted push to upgrade educational infrastructure in the region, aimed at improving learning conditions for students and streamlining administrative workflows for teachers. For the surrounding community, the joint backing from both county leadership and national government figures signaled a rare moment of localized consensus on institutional development.

Following the ribbon-cutting ceremony and inspection of the school grounds, the presidential party shifted venues to the nearby St. Mary’s Girls High School compound. There, President Ruto joined Ole Ntutu, Olekina, regional leaders, and local congregants for a Sunday church service and afternoon fellowship.

The dual engagements formed the core of the President's weekend itinerary in Narok, blending grassroots project inspections with high-level stakeholder meetings across the county.
