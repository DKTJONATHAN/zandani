Solomon Waigwa has formally drawn the curtain on months of cross-border matrimonial ceremonies, officially welcoming First Daughter Charlene Ruto into the Yunge family after she exchanged vows with Tanzanian entrepreneur Isaya Yunge in Mwanza.

Waigwa, who has been central to Isaya’s personal and public life as his adoptive father, issued a direct personal blessing to the newlyweds over the weekend following their lavish white wedding ceremony on Friday, November 6. Stepping forward as the family patriarch, Waigwa framed the union as both a milestone of faith and the successful completion of an intricate cross-border courtship.

“My Son, I love you. Congratulations to you and your beautiful wife from the Lord above,” Waigwa wrote, sharing moments captured from the celebrations. Turning his focus directly to his new daughter-in-law, he added: “Charlene, welcome to the family; this is the day that the Lord has made!”

### The Bond Behind the Delegation

![Jaymo Ule Msee Announces 20-Episode Comeback Series](https://i.ibb.co/Xxg59vvC/0c4124bea04f.webp)

Waigwa’s prominent role throughout the high-profile union reflects a deeply rooted relationship with Isaya. Having raised and guided the entrepreneur through adoption, Waigwa has often spoken about their connection being anchored in deliberate love and parental responsibility rather than biological circumstance.

That paternal responsibility took centre stage ahead of Friday’s vows. Waigwa personally organized and headed the groom’s delegation out of Nairobi, flying family members across the border to the Tanzanian port city on Lake Victoria.

The traveling party assembled by Waigwa included Isaya’s mother, sister, sister-in-law, and extended relatives, ensuring the groom’s household had full representation at the lakeside venue before the vows were administered.

### From Kilgoris to the Lakeside Altar

Friday’s ceremony in Mwanza was the second major cultural milestone where Waigwa anchored the groom’s side of the aisle.

Back on August 8, Waigwa led the delegation that traveled to President William Ruto’s Intonna Heritage Farm in Kilgoris, Narok County, for the formal Kalenjin *Koito* ceremony. The traditional rites sealed the initial agreement between the two families, during which Waigwa publicly expressed his deep gratitude for being able to stand alongside Isaya as his father through such consequential negotiations.

Between the August traditional settlement and the November nuptials, the wedding journey involved months of coordinated family milestones. First Lady Rachel Ruto hosted a Mother’s Blessing ceremony alongside Charlene’s bridal shower, paving the runway for the bilateral celebration.

### Sealing the Union

The culmination on Friday brought together high-profile dignitaries and political figures from both Kenya and Tanzania. President William Ruto personally walked Charlene down the aisle at the scenic lakeside venue, placing her hand into Isaya’s before the couple exchanged their vows in the presence of the First Lady and both families.

With the state protocols, private ceremonies, and cross-border journeys now concluded, Waigwa’s post-wedding blessing marks the practical transition from ceremonial pomp to private home life. For Charlene, who has spent the year balancing public youth advocacy with her personal countdown to marriage, the warm reception from the groom's patriarch confirms her permanent entry into the Yunge fold.
