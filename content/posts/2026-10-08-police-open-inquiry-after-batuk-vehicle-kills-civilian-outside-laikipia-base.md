The National Police Service (NPS) has taken control of an inquiry into a fatal road collision outside a British military installation in Laikipia County, after a heavy vehicle belonging to the British Army Training Unit Kenya (BATUK) struck and killed a local resident on Thursday, October 8.

The British Army confirmed the fatality in an afternoon statement, disclosing that the incident occurred on a road segment immediately adjacent to one of its active training grounds near Nanyuki. While military medical personnel were dispatched from inside the base perimeter to render emergency assistance, the civilian died from injuries sustained at the scene.

### Information Embargo During Police Probe

BATUK has instituted a total factual embargo regarding the circumstances of the crash, withholding the victim’s identity, age, gender, and exact location. The unit also declined to reveal the specific vehicle type, the identity or nationality of the driver, or the direction of travel at the moment of impact.

![BATUK TRUCK](https://i.ibb.co/jP5tysxp/cd240492f9de.webp)

Military officials stated that no further details would be released while civilian authorities conduct their formal investigation.

"We are aware of a fatal road traffic incident outside of one of our training areas involving a local national and a British Army vehicle," BATUK noted in its official dispatch. "The British Army stood up our emergency medical response to the incident and will continue to work with the Kenyan National Police who are investigating."

### Shared Roads and Civil Jurisdiction

While foreign military units operate under bilateral defense agreements within designated firing ranges and tactical camps across Laikipia, movements on public feeder roads connecting these bases fall under Kenyan civil and criminal jurisdiction. 

![Batuk](https://i.ibb.co/n80SrqnF/8fd749b5479b.webp)

When military hardware collides with civilian traffic outside base gates, primary responsibility for scene reconstruction, speed estimation, witness interviews, and mechanical inspections lies squarely with the traffic division of the National Police Service.

BATUK extended formal condolences to the victim’s family as law enforcement officers secured the crash site.

### Pending Investigation Findings

The case now rests on the findings of local police traffic investigators in Laikipia, whose report will determine driver liability and dictate whether formal charges or diplomatic administrative channels will govern the legal aftermath of the crash.
