A brewing battle over the Orange Democratic Movement’s candidate selection rules has spilled into the open in Nairobi, after Raila Odinga Junior demanded an end to direct party tickets while throwing his weight behind Lang’ata MP Phelix Odiwuor, popularly known as Jalang’o, for the county’s Senate seat.

The endorsement directly cuts across the ambitions of his sister, Winnie Odinga, who announced her own intention to run for Nairobi Senator just 48 hours earlier during the first anniversary commemoration of their father's death on October 15.

Speaking at a Nairobi gathering attended by Jalang’o and Nairobi Woman Representative Esther Passaris on Friday, Junior made it clear that family ties would not translate into automatic party clearance for the capital’s high-stakes races.

“I would like to thank Jalas for offering himself for the Senatorial position. I would like to assure you that we (as ODM) are behind you; we are supporting you,” Junior said. “Baba left us inside ODM, and that is where we are, and our brother belongs to ODM, and I am solidly behind these two.”

![Raila Junior](https://i.ibb.co/LXbhCbZt/ca5895aee43b.webp)

### The Push Against Direct Nominations

Junior tied his endorsement to a broader structural demand: ODM must subject every 2027 hopeful to competitive grassroots nominations rather than issuing boardroom certificates.

To anchor that stance, Junior confirmed he will personally enter the primary ring to contest the Kibra parliamentary seat—the historic electoral bedrock represented by the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga for two decades before the constituency was split.

By insisting that all contenders earn their tickets through balloting, Junior is attempting to dismantle the traditional practice of negotiated direct tickets that has historically defined ODM’s metropolitan selections. For Winnie Odinga, an East African Legislative Assembly lawmaker who cited incumbent Senator Edwin Sifuna’s presidential ambitions as creating “space in Nairobi,” the intervention removes any expectation of an uncontested party coronation in the capital.

![Winnie Odinga](https://i.ibb.co/ds7Spxgt/2056db333739.webp)

“Let it not be a rumour that as Edwin Sifuna searches for higher offices, I see there is some space in Nairobi, and that is why I have decided I will vie for Senator of Nairobi County,” Winnie had stated at the memorial.

### Capital Realignment

The competing ambitions put ODM’s metropolitan machine under immediate friction. Jalang’o, who secured Lang’ata in 2022 under an ODM ticket, had already set his sights on the citywide Senate race to replace Sifuna, whose stated bid for the presidency vacates one of the party’s most reliable metropolitan platforms.

With Junior throwing his support behind Jalang'o and entering the Kibra contest, the Odinga political lineage enters the 2027 electoral cycle without a single coordinated family slate. 

Whether ODM's National Executive Committee can maintain an orderly, competitive nomination exercise in Nairobi without triggering deep internal fractures now rests on how the party manages two competing Odinga household camps vying for the same primary ballot.
