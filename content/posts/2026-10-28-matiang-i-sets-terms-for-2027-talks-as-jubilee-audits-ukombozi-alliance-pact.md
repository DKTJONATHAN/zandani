The Jubilee Party has moved to institutionalise its 2027 election strategy, establishing a formal negotiating panel to vet prospective opposition pacts while filling executive gaps in its party machinery.

The restructuring marks Party Leader Fred Matiang’i’s first major executive rollout since taking over formal stewardship of the former ruling outfit. Approved by the National Executive Committee (NEC) on Wednesday, the measures set clear procedural boundaries for how Jubilee will handle competing coalition overtures over the next political cycle.

At the core of the strategy is the newly formed Inter-Party Coalition Building Committee, a three-member team tasked with reviewing coalition frameworks before Jubilee signs any binding electoral agreements. The panel will be chaired by former Murang’a Senator Kembi Gitura, working alongside Secretary General Moitalel Ole Kenta and veteran administrator Janet Ong’era.

### Scrutiny of the Ukombozi Deed

The Gitura-led team has been handed immediate operational oversight over the draft Deed of Agreement for the Ukombozi Alliance (TUA), an emerging political coalition vehicle. Rather than issuing a blanket endorsement, Jubilee's NEC directed the committee to examine the draft text in detail and propose formal amendments to protect party interests before any ratification.

![Jubilee Party leader Fred Matiang'i addressing the press at the party headquarters along Kiambu Road in Nairobi on August 2026.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/fred-matiang-i_0.jpg.webp?itok=uezBkZ3v)

The committee's brief also extends to opening structured talks with other opposition parties, assessing potential joint tickets, and proposing models for parliamentary and grassroots cooperation heading into the 2027 polls.

According to party officials, the mandate reflects a deliberate shift away from ad-hoc political arrangements toward binding, vetted pacts. While opening the door to new configurations, the NEC reiterated that Jubilee remains an active constituent member of the Azimio la Umoja One Kenya Alliance, creating a dual-track approach where existing coalition ties are balanced against fresh opposition realignments.

“The National Executive Committee reaffirmed the Party's commitment to constructive dialogue, cooperation and the pursuit of a common agenda for the transformation of our country,” Ole Kenta said regarding the talks.

### Filling Secretariat Vacancies

Alongside the coalition mandate, Matiang’i made strategic appointments to steady the party’s administrative organs ahead of planned regional mobilization.

![Kioni Matiang'i Jubilee](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/kioni-matiang-i-jubilee.jpg.webp?itok=SiYXIdrh)

Lucy Naisiae Kashu has been installed as the interim chairperson of the Jubilee Party Women's Caucus, while Madina Yunis Mahat takes over as the interim Deputy National Organising Secretary. Both positions had remained vacant during the party's recent organizational disputes and leadership reshuffles.

The personnel picks, subsequently ratified by the NEC, provide operational cover for party mobilization drives as Jubilee attempts to reactivate grassroots networks that had fractured following the 2022 general election.

### The Transition Factor

The internal reorganization arrives weeks after former President Uhuru Kenyatta formally relinquished Jubilee Party leadership to Matiang’i. That transition was prompted by statutory provisions that limit retired heads of state from retaining formal executive offices in political parties, an issue that had generated sustained legal challenges from rival factions.

Before stepping aside, Kenyatta urged Jubilee’s rank and file to back Matiang’i as the party’s 2027 presidential flag bearer. Wednesday’s committee appointments demonstrate Matiang’i exercising that delegated authority, constructing an inner negotiating architecture composed of figures with long-standing parliamentary and administrative experience.

The Gitura committee is scheduled to begin technical reviews of the Ukombozi draft deed before presenting its final recommendations and proposed revisions back to the NEC for binding determination.
