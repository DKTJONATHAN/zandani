Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna has bypassed the administrative hurdle of establishing a new political outfit from the ground up, securing The Equitable Party (TEP) through an executive restructuring deal that hands him both the party’s leadership and its 2027 presidential ticket.

The handover, confirmed in Nairobi on Sunday, October 4, shifts the machinery of an existing registered party into the hands of the Linda Mwananchi Movement (LMM) patron, giving his nascent national campaign immediate legal footing ahead of the next General Election.

Rather than navigating the protracted verification processes required by the Registrar of Political Parties to register a fresh entity, Sifuna’s entry relies on a negotiated absorption. TEP Party Leader Gilbert Marava announced that the party's current executive organs will make way for the lawmaker, who will take the helm as the party's apex official.

![Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna during a past event](https://i.ibb.co/bjXZSF3t/82289922a331.webp)

"On our end, we have decided to restructure, and in the process, those who registered will stay, and we will bring in our brothers," Marava said during the Nairobi unveiling. "Sifuna is going to run for president, and he will be the next leader of The Equitable Party."

### The Restructuring Model

The arrangement outlined by Marava establishes a dual-track transition: retaining existing party members on the register while formalizing an influx of Sifuna’s political network under the TEP umbrella. 

By commandeering an operational party structure, Sifuna resolves the critical logistical challenge that frequently complicates exploratory civic campaigns. While his Linda Mwananchi Movement has served as an advocacy vehicle, it lacked statutory recognition as an electoral entity capable of sponsoring candidates. Merging those ground networks into TEP converts an advocacy front into a fully fledged party apparatus.

![Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna during a past event](https://i.ibb.co/ZzxMSXM3/ae53f0aa4b0f.webp)

The restructuring effectively clears a path for Sifuna to position himself at the center of the 2027 presidential matrix on an independent ticket, insulating his ambition from coalition gatekeeping.

### Toward Formal Transition

The immediate operational focus shifts to the formalization of the party’s changes. For the handover to become legally binding under election laws, TEP must file its restructured leadership register, constitution revisions, and executive changes with regulatory authorities.

With Marava publicly yielding the party leadership, the transition places Sifuna in direct control of TEP’s candidate nomination instruments, establishing an autonomous base as he begins building out a national campaign infrastructure toward 2027.
