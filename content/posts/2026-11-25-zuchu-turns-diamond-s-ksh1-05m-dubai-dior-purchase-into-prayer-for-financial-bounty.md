Zuchu responded to Diamond Platnumz’s latest high-value gesture—a Ksh1.05 million Lady Dior handbag bought in Dubai—by publicly invoking a prayer for his wealth to multiply, framing the seven-figure designer acquisition as an act of generosity deserving spiritual replenishment.

The Wasafi signee showcased the exotic python-skin accessory on her Instagram page following a weekend shopping trip in Dubai, where the WCB boss spent AED 30,000 (approximately Ksh1,049,786 or Tsh21.6 million) on the high-end French fashion piece. 

Addressing Diamond directly in her message, Zuchu paired the luxury reveal with a specific blessing for his continued financial expansion.

“Thank you, Ba Mtu Allah Akurejeshee Pakubwa Ulipotoa … Akuongezee Riziki Usikaukiwe Amin,” she wrote, asking for his provision to remain endless following the substantial outlay.

The purchase was independently corroborated by Tanzanian entertainer and longtime Wasafi affiliate Baba Levo, who accompanied Diamond during the Dubai trip over the weekend. Baba Levo confirmed being present when the Bongo Flava icon completed the transaction, adding direct verification to the transaction details circulating across regional digital platforms.

The luxury splurge comes as Diamond and Zuchu maintain their position as one of East Africa’s most heavily tracked celebrity pairings. Beyond their shared roster at WCB Wasafi, the two have established a joint personal footprint that includes formalizing their union through an Islamic marriage ceremony (*nikah*) and welcoming a child together.

High-profile gifting has remained a persistent feature of their public relationship, with previous personal milestones routinely accompanied by visible displays of high-end acquisitions. However, Zuchu’s decision to explicitly tie the Ksh1.05 million Dior piece to a public prayer for financial multiplication underlines how the couple continues to blend high-stakes personal luxury with strategic public messaging.

As both artists move through late 2026 with active release schedules and commercial commitments, the Dubai purchase reasserts WCB’s preference for pairing international luxury acquisitions with highly visible personal updates.
