NAIROBI - Activist and politician Morara Kebaso has issued a formal public apology following weeks of acrimonious disputes involving his first wife, Mercy Makini, signaling a shift toward ending the publicized domestic conflict.

In a statement posted to his official social media channels on Sunday, September 13, Kebaso acknowledged his role in the escalating tensions. While he did not explicitly identify the individuals involved, the message served as a direct address to the controversies that have defined his personal life throughout September 2026.

"A leader should never be too proud to apologize," Kebaso wrote. He noted that although he possessed material he could have utilized to defend his position and prolong the public discourse, he opted for restraint. He stated that his recent period of silence was intended to facilitate self-accountability and de-escalate the situation.

The fallout originated earlier this month when Kebaso publicly introduced a second wife. Following the announcement, Makini issued a series of statements rejecting the polygamous arrangement and levelling allegations of domestic violence and child neglect against the politician. These claims concern a period following her departure from the matrimonial home in March 2026.

Prior to Sunday's apology, Kebaso had actively contested those allegations, asserting that he had consistently provided financial support and oversaw property construction for Makini’s family.

The politician concluded his Sunday statement by calling for an end to the public feud, encouraging all parties to move forward. As of Monday, September 14, there has been no public response from Makini regarding the apology.
