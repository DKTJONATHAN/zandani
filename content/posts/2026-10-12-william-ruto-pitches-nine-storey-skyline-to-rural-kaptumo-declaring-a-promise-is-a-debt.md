William Ruto arrived in Nandi County with an ambitious urban makeover pitch for the countryside, telling local crowds that the sleepy trading centre of Kaptumo is about to get a nine-storey residential tower.

Taking the stage during his working tour across the county, the President placed his Affordable Housing Programme at the centre of his public appeal, framing high-density apartment living not as an urban luxury reserved for major cities, but as an immediate reality for rural outposts.

For Ruto, bringing a multistory concrete development to Kaptumo was the ultimate calling card for his administration's modernization push.

“Without this national program, a modern development of this scale would not be in Kaptumo today,” Ruto told the gathering, pointing to the nine-storey residential block as a direct engine for local youth employment and contemporary community living.

### The Kaptumo High-Rise Ambition

The Kaptumo housing development represents one of the most striking visual shifts the President is selling to grassroots communities. Instead of the low-slung commercial strips that traditionally define tea-growing centres across the region, Ruto sketched out a vision where high-rise architecture brings modern amenities directly to village doorsteps.

Beyond offering young locals construction jobs, the President framed the project as proof that his flagship housing initiative can reach deep into the interior, bypassing the conventional thinking that confines multi-storey blocks to regional capitals.

His message in Nandi leaned heavily on personal accountability to the voters who backed his rise to power, positioning each project as a personal score settled with the electorate.

“A promise is a debt, and I am in Nandi County today to deliver on our development pledges,” Ruto said.

### Aldai Grabs the Lion’s Share of Sh25 Billion Roads

The high-rise housing pledge was anchored by a massive transport funding package, with Ruto announcing a KSh25 billion commitment to build 350 kilometres of tarmac across 14 separate road corridors in the county.

Aldai Constituency emerged as the clear focal point of the infrastructure blueprint. Six of the 14 gazetted road projects are mapped directly inside Aldai, aimed at overhauling the local transport network to facilitate mobility and open up agricultural commerce across neighboring trading hubs.

For residents accustomed to seasonal mud and rugged terrain, the 350-kilometre road upgrade plan was framed as an economic corridor designed to link rural farms to national supply chains without the punishing transit delays of the past.

### The May 2027 Grid Countdown

Ruto also brought a hard timeline to the county’s power supply, announcing a KSh2.1 billion kitty earmarked to hook 23,000 additional Nandi households into the national power grid.

Setting a firm deadline for the power project, the President declared that the connections must be completed before May 2027. The last-mile power expansion, he noted, is designed to fuel small businesses, power agro-processing, and light up villages that have spent years waiting on utility infrastructure.

Throughout the stopover, the President's public rhythm reflected his favored grassroots playbook: pairing bread-and-butter utility upgrades with bold architectural targets. 

By the time his motorcade moved on, Ruto had left Kaptumo with an image rarely associated with rural Nandi—the promise of a nine-storey skyline rising above the tea bushes, backed by a strict May 2027 utility clock.
