Mike Sonko stepped directly into Ukambani's 2027 political calculations on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, targeting the inner circle surrounding Wiper Patriotic Front leader Kalonzo Musyoka immediately after completing consultations with two of the region's heavyweight political figures.

The former Nairobi governor, who currently leads the National Economic Development Party (NEDP), took to his official social media channels to warn that self-serving advice from opposition handlers risks isolating the Wiper chief as major coalition realignments gather momentum.

Sonko’s public intervention followed a rapid sequence of high-level meetings. He first held talks with former Machakos Senator Johnson Muthama before conferring with Labour Cabinet Secretary Dr. Alfred Mutua, a pivotal state operative and key ally of President William Ruto in Lower Eastern.

### Targeting the Inner Circle

Writing on his Facebook page shortly after the meetings wrapped up, Sonko openly questioned the advice being fed to Kalonzo by surrounding political aides, warning that the ground beneath the opposition camp is actively shifting.

“Some bogus noise makers are misleading Kalonzo without understanding the political reality,” Sonko stated. “Let them know that the one-term option is also on the table. We are carefully weighing both political options.”

His remarks marked a sharp public challenge to the narrative that the Wiper leadership holds an exclusive lock on the region's direction heading into the 2027 contest.

### High-Stakes Consultations

By convening with both Muthama and Dr. Mutua in a single day, Sonko highlighted an emerging dialogue between key regional figures across opposing political divides. Mutua has consistently served as the administration's chief anchor in Ukambani, while Muthama remains a veteran negotiator with deep structural ties in regional political organizing.

Sonko framed the cross-factional sit-downs not as casual political chatter, but as an active evaluation of terms for regional empowerment ahead of the election cycle.

“Our final decision when we all come to the table will be guided by the interests of our people, unity, and the future of our country,” Sonko emphasized. “We stand for unity, not division, or empty political games.”

### Shifting Lower Eastern Realities

While Sonko stopped short of declaring a definitive deal, his public posturing makes explicit that working directly with President Ruto's administration remains a live strategic option under active consideration by key Lower Eastern leaders.

With political figures across Ukambani re-evaluating their coalition options early, Sonko's warning signals that key power brokers are actively testing alternative paths well before formal party lineups are finalized for 2027.
