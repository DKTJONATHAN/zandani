Geoffrey Mosiria is drawing a hard line through Nairobi's shifting political alignments, cautioning Senator Edwin Sifuna that any attempt to patch things up with Embakasi East MP Babu Owino could spell doom for his career.

Speaking through a social media post and accompanying voice notes on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, the Nairobi City County Chief Officer for Environment argued that political betrayals cannot be wiped away by hasty truces. According to Mosiria, extending an olive branch to a rival who has previously broken ranks simply hands them a renewed platform to covertly undermine and dismantle allies from within.

Leaning into a blunt street-level evaluation, Mosiria declared, “Msaliti ni msaliti. Never give a second chance to someone who once betrayed you,” insisting that a Judas figure retains that identity regardless of changing political winds.

To back up his caution, Mosiria pointed to a broader historical precedent within opposition ranks. He argued that former Prime Minister Raila Odinga repeatedly stumbled in his bids for the presidency because he consistently welcomed past detractors back into his inner political circle. In Mosiria's view, repeating that cycle at the county level would leave Sifuna vulnerable as factions realign.

The friction highlights underlying tensions simmering within the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM). As camps position themselves for the 2027 General Election, the struggle for dominance across Nairobi County has turned interpersonal relationships into high-stakes currency, leaving little room for public reconciliations without friction from within the party's ranks.
