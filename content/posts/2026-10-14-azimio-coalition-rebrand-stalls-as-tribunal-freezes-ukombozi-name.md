Azimio La Umoja’s planned transition into The Ukombozi Alliance has hit a regulatory wall after the Political Parties Dispute Tribunal halted all administrative steps to register or gazette the coalition's new identity.

The interim directive, issued on Tuesday, October 13, restrains the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties from approving, gazetting, or processing the name change pending a full inter partes hearing. The freeze targets an administrative rebrand that Wiper Party leader Kalonzo Musyoka had publicly presented just days earlier as the coalition’s vehicle to absorb fresh political formations ahead of the 2027 General Election.

### The Administrative Injunction

In court papers, the tribunal certified the application as urgent and barred the registrar from advancing the registration of Azimio under the moniker "The Ukombozi Alliance" (TUA).

![Wiper Leader Kalonzo Musyoka speaking at a rally in Mombasa on October 10, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/6cV0Bb3J/b72d60b0de5a.webp)

"Pending the inter partes hearing and determination of this Application, an interim order be issued restraining the 1st Respondent, whether by itself, its officers, servants or agents, from approving, gazetting, registering or taking further administrative steps towards the registration of the 2nd Respondent under the name 'The Ukombozi Alliance (TUA)'," the tribunal directed.

The challenge was brought by a petitioner contending that the statutory reservation for a similar identity—the Ukombozi Peoples Party (UPP)—preceded the coalition's paperwork, legally precluding the registrar from granting the name to the Azimio alliance.

The tribunal ordered the ORPP and the Wiper Patriotic Front to file their formal responses within seven days.

### A Contested Moniker

The court freeze exposes an intensifying bureaucratic scramble over the "Ukombozi" label across competing opposition circles. 

Beyond Musyoka's coalition manoeuvres, the moniker has circulated within Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna's Linda Mwananchi camp. The ORPP had previously rejected an application to rebrand the United Democratic Party (UDP) as the Ukombozi People's Party, citing statutory similarities with an earlier filing. In a letter dated September 8, the registrar noted that another applicant had formally reserved the name "Ukombozi Alliance" on July 20, 2026.

![Azimio Chairperson and former President Uhuru Kenyatta with Wiper party leader Kalonzo Musyoka during a past event](https://i.ibb.co/HLP8n4yW/f6db9e651b76.webp)

Sifuna’s allies have contested that timeline, maintaining they had lodged proposals to adopt the Ukombozi brand as early as March 2025, long before the wider Azimio coalition initiated its re-alignment.

### Coalition Runway on Hold

The legal halt complicates the opposition's organizational calendar. Azimio's leadership had sought to use the new identity to draw a line under previous coalition friction and construct an expanded platform to contest President William Ruto's administration.

Instead of rolling out unified regional operations under the new banner, the coalition must now litigate the basic statutory mechanics of its naming rights.

The Political Parties Dispute Tribunal scheduled the matter for mention on October 29, 2026, when it will evaluate compliance with its interim directives and set timelines for the full substantive hearing.
