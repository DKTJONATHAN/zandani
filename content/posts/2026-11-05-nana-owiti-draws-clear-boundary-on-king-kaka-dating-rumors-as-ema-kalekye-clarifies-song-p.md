Media personality Nana Owiti has taken a firm stance on emotional maturity and personal progression, dismissing social media friction following viral rumors linking her former partner, musician King Kaka, to digital creator Ema Kalekye.

Addressing the claims during a phone interview with Milele FM host Ankali Ray, Owiti made it clear that she harbors no resentment over her former partner’s personal life, framing life after a breakup around continuous self-improvement rather than past attachments.

"Life lazima imove on," Owiti told Ankali Ray when asked about the circulating reports. "Very happy for him, kama hiyo ni ukweli si iko sawa, kila mtu lazima asonge hatuwezi shinda mahali pamoja, hivyo ndio maisha iko."

Owiti emphasized that maintaining personal space and focusing on daily self-advancement takes precedence over dwelling on closed chapters.

"Mimi napenda maturity, kila mtu aishi maisha yake vile inafaa," she shared. "Tumewekwa hapa kila siku you have to be better than yesterday, so maisha lazima isonge."

The online discourse gained momentum after King Kaka shared a social media video featuring Kalekye listening to his latest love ballad. Online sleuths quickly seized on the post, pointing to playful caption banter in which the rapper referred to the content creator using the phrase "Morio wa mine."

The resulting online chatter prompted Kalekye to publicly separate professional music distribution from private romance. Shutting down the romantic allegations, Kalekye explained that the interaction was strictly a routine song promotion effort built around digital creator engagement.

"Just to clear the air, I think this is being blown out of proportion. It was simply a song repost, nothing more," Kalekye stated.

King Kaka and Owiti officially separated in 2024 after several years together. The two continue to maintain a co-parenting relationship for their two children as both navigate independent media and creative careers.
