Kenya’s annual year-end road safety drive has shifted from standalone highway roadblocks into an integrated enforcement net, as Inspector General of Police Douglas Kanja ordered field commanders to tie physical traffic operations directly to digital transport surveillance networks.

Speaking during an annual thanksgiving and prayer service for uniformed disciplined services at the Holy Family Minor Basilica on Saturday, Kanja directed police to execute a multi-agency crackdown targeting unroadworthy motor vehicles, motorcycles, drunk drivers, speeding motorists, and overloaded public transport. Rather than running police inspections in isolation, Kanja instructed service commanders to coordinate operational logistics across government transport desks to handle the surge in commercial and personal travel over the coming weeks.

The service-wide order formally connects frontline police impoundment authority with the National Transport and Safety Authority's ongoing *Msimu Bila Ajali, Usalama Barabarani* campaign, which launched two weeks ago across critical national transit spines. 

### The Digital Inspection Net

![kanja-motorists.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/fdrp8fzh/5afa885c826e.webp)

Under the joint operational structure, field officers will draw on real-time data streaming into NTSA’s Intelligent Road Safety Management System. Technical teams are monitoring speed limiters installed in commercial and public transport vehicles to determine whether onboard units are functional and continuously transmitting operational speed records, rather than relying exclusively on handheld radar guns at static checkpoints.

That digital filter directly informs which vehicles are pulled aside for physical roadworthiness examinations. Officers have been instructed to verify inspection stickers, driver licences, PSV badges, and route permits while enforcing strict compliance with passenger manifests and axle weight caps on long-distance routes.

Motorcycles and public service vehicles face specific scrutiny under Kanja’s directive, with commanders instructed to impound unroadworthy units outright rather than issuing warnings. The inclusion of bodaboda operations reflects sustained pressure to bring rural and urban commercial two-wheelers into the formal safety compliance net before holiday traffic peaks.

### Night Patrols and Transit Corridors

![A police officer attempts to arrest a bodaboda rider](https://i.ibb.co/NgfrZt4j/4902f87b8a0c.webp)

The coordinated framework places its heaviest physical presence on late-evening and overnight patrol windows—the operational hours NTSA has flagged as carrying the sharpest spike in fatal crashes. Joint patrol teams are tasked with maintaining active sobriety checks and hazardous-driving monitoring along major inter-county highways, while expanding surveillance to cover localized festive gatherings and family venues where road safety discipline often lapses.

"As we move into the final weeks of the year, we anticipate increased road traffic and heightened commercial activities; we expect you to curtail drunkenness and dangerous driving, speeding, overloading, and unroadworthy motor vehicles and motorcycles," Kanja instructed commanders.

The police chief also urged pedestrians and private motorists to exercise defensive road practices as passenger volumes climb nationwide.

With NTSA's 24-hour monitoring units deployed and police commands under orders to seize non-compliant vehicles, the operational test over the coming weeks will rest on how effectively field units sustain high-friction night operations without creating gridlock along high-density transport arteries.
