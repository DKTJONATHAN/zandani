Wambo Ashley has marked the formal establishment of her independent household with holy water and priestly vestments, inviting her brother—an ordained Catholic priest—to conduct a full blessing of her new property as she steps out on her own.

The digital creator shared footage on Thursday, October 8, 2026, documenting the clerical ritual inside the residence. Rather than opting for an elaborate housewarming party or commercial reveal, Ashley leaned into familial tradition, capturing her brother performing the traditional Catholic prayers throughout the home’s open corridors.

“POV: Your brother is a Catholic Father. So you can’t make a single move without getting blessed. We thank God for him,” Ashley noted alongside the video documentation of the ceremony.

### From Bare Concrete to Bespoke Renovation

The house blessing arrives weeks after Ashley first unveiled the empty footprint of the property to her audience. That initial walkthrough captured an unfurnished, stripped-down space, which she earmarked for substantial interior remodeling.

Chief among her planned structural modifications is a complete overhaul of the kitchen area. The physical transition into the property follows months of quiet personal recalibration as she constructs a permanent base separated from her former joint domestic life.

Having an in-house member of the clergy anchor her entry into the property provides a personal anchor for the move. In Catholic tradition, a domestic blessing is not merely ceremonial décor; it is intended to demarcate a home as a sanctuary, dedicating every room before regular daily life and furnishings take over.

### The Four-Year Partnership Closes

The establishment of the new home represents the most visible physical separation between Ashley and fellow creator Nicholas Kioko since the pair officially confirmed their split on September 14, 2026.

While the public announcement landed in mid-September, the actual dissolution of their four-year romantic partnership took place months earlier in private. In their coordinated statement, the former couple disclosed that they had already spent three months living apart before addressing their followers, using that window to adjust away from online scrutiny.

“After four years together, we have mutually decided to go our separate ways. We separated three months ago and have taken time to process and accept this decision,” the joint statement read.

During their four years together, the duo had built an interconnected digital footprint, sharing collaborative vlogs, lifestyle milestones, and family routines that drew a dedicated audience across social platforms.

### Co-Parenting Priorities

Even as Ashley establishes her own residence, the boundaries of her ongoing connection to Kioko remain firmly fixed around their three young children—a set of twin boys and a daughter.

Both creators stressed during their September announcement that their shared parenting responsibilities would remain intact despite running two distinct households.

“While our relationship has come to an end, we remain committed to raising and loving our three amazing children,” they affirmed, while appealing for public patience during the transition.

With the priestly blessings concluded and the initial construction work underway in the kitchen, Ashley’s next milestones will centre on completing the structural renovations and fully moving her young family into the sanctified space.
