A legislative overhaul assented to by President William Ruto has restructured the high command of the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), stripping the traditional ex-officio tag from key state officers and directly integrating maritime trade administration into national tax oversight.

The enactment of the Kenya Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026, signed at Eldoret State Lodge on Friday, October 16, creates an institutional seat on the tax agency’s board of directors for the Managing Director of the Kenya Ports Authority (KPA), currently Captain William Ruto.

The administrative fusion is engineered to eliminate operational daylight between the agency controlling the country's maritime gateways and the authority charged with assessing and collecting customs duties.

### Ending Ex-Officio Tenure

Beyond the arrival of the port chief, the statute carries significant implications for the internal power balance of the KRA board, which is chaired by former Laikipia Governor Ndiritu Muriithi.

![President William Ruto assenting to the Tax Laws (Amendment) Bill and the Kenya Revenue Authority (Amendment) Bill 2026 at Eldoret State Lodge, Uasin-Gishu County, October 16, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/5xhNVbHK/90a47d5c2008.webp)

The law formally scraps the ex-officio designation historically attached to ex-officio seats. The structural alteration recalibrates the legal terms of service, tenure protections, and termination procedures for the Principal Secretary to the National Treasury, the Attorney General, and the KRA Commissioner General.

By converting these roles into substantive board positions, the legislation brings government policy representation and internal executive management into full statutory parity with independent directors.

### Synchronizing Customs and Berths

Parliamentary briefs prepared during the bill's review underline that maritime cargo processing and customs enforcement had outgrown their segregated corporate frameworks. According to records prepared by National Assembly Clerk Samuel Njoroge, the formal merger of port logistics with tax oversight targets friction along clearance corridors.

Integrating the KPA leadership into board-level strategy is designed to establish unified oversight over customs manifests, vessel berthing priorities, container offloading bottlenecks, and cargo tracking systems.

“This is an efficiency measure due to the co-dependent nature of the operations of both the Kenya Revenue Authority and the Kenya Ports Authority,” the National Assembly noted in its justification for the changes.

![President William Ruto assenting to the Tax Laws (Amendment) Bill and the Kenya Revenue Authority (Amendment) Bill 2026 at Eldoret State Lodge, Uasin-Gishu County, October 16, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/7x6qGtsq/55fccf7ea68b.webp)

Customs entry points account for a major slice of state revenue, where misdeclarations, transit cargo diversion, and clearance disputes routinely disrupt Treasury targets and commercial shipping schedules.

### Fast-Track Legislative Passage

The governance changes moved through the legislature within days. The bill was sponsored on the floor by National Assembly Majority Leader Kimani Ichung’wah, clearing its final reading on October 7 before being submitted to State House for presidential assent nine days later.

The restructured board overseen by Muriithi operates alongside Commissioner General Adan Mohamed, who serves as the authority’s chief executive. Legal counsel and government compliance are anchored by Attorney General Dorcas Oduor, alongside National Treasury representative Lawrence Kibet, who acts as the alternate director to the Treasury Principal Secretary.

Corporate governance and board administration continue under Paul Matuku, Commissioner of Legal and Board Services, alongside non-executive independent directors Hadi Sheikh Abdullahi, Richard Boro Ndung’u, Lydia Cherono Rono, Risper Olick, Ashif Kassam, and Shimaka Leonard.

With the statute now gazetted into law, the immediate operational test shifts to Mombasa, where unified tracking systems and joint operational protocols between KRA customs officers and KPA dock managers must now be harmonized under the amended governance mandate.
