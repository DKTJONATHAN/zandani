Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja’s migration to the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) was not a sudden partisan rupture, but the formal conclusion of a pre-arranged cross-party operational pact sanctioned at the highest levels of executive power.

Speaking during an interview on Ramogi TV on Tuesday, November 3, Sakaja revealed that his transition from the ruling United Democratic Alliance (UDA) was explicitly negotiated with President William Ruto and the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga long before his public reception into ODM on November 2.

The disclosure details how political engineering between the country’s top leadership blurred standard party boundaries in the capital, allowing Sakaja to execute internal ODM election tasks while officially remaining a member of the ruling party.

### Executing Directives Across Party Lines

![ODM officials led by DPL Simba Arati and Junet Mohammed welcomes Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja to ODM party after ditching UDA on November 2, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/yms1MGT1/10db6493f7b8.webp)

According to Sakaja, the foundation of his party shift was laid in early 2025 during discussions tied to Raila’s campaign for the African Union Commission (AUC) chairmanship. During that period, executive leadership established a working arrangement that positioned Sakaja to align with ODM’s internal machinery ahead of his planned 2027 re-election bid.

"The conversation of moving to ODM is a conversation that we had with Baba and with President William Ruto," Sakaja said, recalling that Ruto instructed him to work directly alongside Raila prior to diplomatic engagements in Addis Ababa.

Following those instructions, Sakaja began actively participating in internal ODM party operations in Nairobi. This included receiving and carrying out explicit operational orders regarding the management and support of ODM’s grassroots branch elections within the capital.

"So I was given instructions on what to do, how to support, which I did," Sakaja stated, noting that local party actors in Nairobi were aware of his administrative involvement during the party's election cycle.

### From Soft Alignment to Formal Reception

While the timing of Sakaja's formal defection was kept fluid throughout 2026, his political trajectory had become increasingly visible. In August 2026, the governor publicly acknowledged that he was conducting consultations over his party standing as pressure mounted from key constituency bases in Nairobi.

![Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja speaking after defecting to ODM on November 2, with an inset of Ruto during a past UDA delegates meeting](https://i.ibb.co/rGJR0q0G/87b6bdb834d5.webp)

By late October, Sakaja was participating directly in ODM public platforms, including a major rally at Jacaranda grounds on October 31. His formal reception into the party took place on November 2, where he was officially welcomed by ODM Party Chairperson Gladys Wanga, Deputy Party Leader Simba Arati, and National Assembly Minority Leader Junet Mohammed.

Raila had previously signaled public support for Sakaja’s county leadership during his AUC campaign bid, directing party members to support the governor's re-election ambitions.

### Realignment in the Capital

Sakaja’s explicit confirmation that his party switch was conducted with President Ruto's advance approval shifts the focus to how Nairobi's county assembly and executive leadership will navigate the new party configuration.

Having won the county's top seat on a UDA ticket in 2022, Sakaja now anchors his 2027 defense entirely within the ODM party framework. The formalization of his move leaves UDA without its primary executive officeholder in the capital, while solidifying ODM's administrative grip on the city-county political structure.
