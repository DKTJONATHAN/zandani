Raymond Omosa, the self-styled forex trader known online as Kenyan Prince, has leveraged his recent relocation to the United Arab Emirates as a platform for institutional defiance, publicly daring United States President Donald Trump and global police agency Interpol to attempt an arrest in Dubai.

Speaking from his base in the Gulf, Omosa dismissed international law enforcement bodies as ineffective while asserting that his foreign residency status and self-reported commercial legitimacy render him immune to legal pursuit.

"Interpol are cowards. I dare Interpol: if they have muscles and they are not cowards, they should come for me here. I am chilling in Dubai," Omosa stated in a broadcast to his followers.

He extended his challenge directly to Washington, questioning why American authorities had taken no action against his operations despite ongoing public discussions surrounding financial investigations.
