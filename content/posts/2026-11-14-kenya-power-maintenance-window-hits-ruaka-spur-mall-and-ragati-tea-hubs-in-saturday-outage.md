Key commercial hubs, leisure destinations, and tea processing facilities across Kiambu and Nyeri counties are set for extended power interruptions on Saturday, November 14, 2026, as Kenya Power conducts scheduled grid maintenance.

The planned maintenance cycle will freeze mains power for up to nine hours across major urban centers in Kiambu—including Ruaka town's primary retail strip, Spur Mall, and Ruiru Golf Club—while halting power supply to tea processing operations at Ragati Tea Factory in Nyeri County.

### Kiambu Commercial Corridors Facing Nine-Hour Freeze

In Kiambu County, the power utility has scheduled outages running from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, affecting two major economic corridors.

The first major zone covers the fast-growing Ruiru Kimbo and Murera Coffee belt. Business operations, residential estates, and institutional facilities situated within this radius will spend eight hours off the grid. Key commercial and institutional centers affected include Spur Mall, the Nairobi Institute of Business Studies (NIBS), Ruiru Golf Club, and the Toll Station area.

The interruption extends deep into surrounding residential and civic infrastructure, taking in Mugutha, Ruiru KU Model School, DC Kwa Kairu, GSU Ruiru, Mona Park, Mulberry, Kwa Chief, Wankan, Paul Mwai, Happy Church, Courtesy Beach, and Green Ridge. Schooling and administrative points such as Mugutha Secondary School, Murera Primary School, St. Martins Catholic Church, Mugutha Police, and the Judea, Neema, River Line, and Dam Ridge estates are also listed within the maintenance boundary, alongside Ruera Dam, Makaburi Village, Wa Kariuki, Apollos, and establishments directly behind Spur Mall.

Simultaneously, Kenya Power will interrupt supply across Ruaka, one of Kiambu's most densely populated commercial and residential suburbs. Between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM, the outage will hit major retail anchors and essential service providers along the main transit routes. Affected locations include Ruaka Town, Kyuna Road, Joyland, Gertrude Hospital, Quickmart Ruaka, Cleanshelf Ruaka, Total Service Station, and Gikwaru Drive.

### Nyeri Maintenance Hits Ragati Tea Hub and Telco Infrastructure

Further north in Nyeri County, the maintenance exercise starts earlier, running from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the Mt Kenya Region.

The principal operational hub affected is the Ragati Tea Factory, alongside surrounding agricultural and trading zones in Ihwagi and Ragati. The nine-hour shutdown will impact small-scale tea processing, local markets, and public institutions across the district.

Specific locations listed for the Saturday morning outage in Nyeri include Ihwagi Market, Ihwagi Coffee Factory, Ihwagi Secondary School, Gathehu Village, Gatondo Secondary School, Kariki TBC, Kiawaiguru TBC, and Gitemaini TBC. Trading centers and educational facilities along the belt—such as Itundu Market, Kangiri TBC, Gachugu-ina TBC, Kiarage TBC, Giakaibei Market, Giakaibei Secondary School, Ndumanu Village, Kanjuri High School, Kanjuri Market, Giagathege TBC, Kiunjugi TBC, Kariambi TBC, Gitima-ini Market, Kiangengi Market, and Kagochi Market—will similarly operate off the grid.

The Nyeri interruption also touches critical connectivity infrastructure, with Safaricom Boosters and Airtel Boosters listed within the planned maintenance envelope around Ragati Forest Station, Kianjiru-ini TBC, and the One KM radius.

Kenya Power has advised commercial operators, institutional administrators, and residents within the designated maintenance corridors in both counties to adjust their operational schedules ahead of the planned Saturday morning shut-off.
