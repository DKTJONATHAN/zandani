A multi-county manhunt that tracked a fugitive farmhand from Nyeri to Kisii County has ended in a cell death and an independent custodial inquiry, putting the spotlight on transit holding procedures at Narumoru Police Station.

The Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) outlined the transit timeline of Dennis Aremo Osiemo, who was found hanging from ventilation grills inside a police cell on Sunday, September 20—just two days after detectives apprehended him hundreds of kilometers away in Kisii County.

![Narumoru Police post](https://i.ibb.co/sdm0hFZz/1b0275bbc5a9.webp)

The case originated from a July 2, 2026, assault incident in Nyeri where Osiemo, a former farmhand, allegedly attacked his former employer. According to investigators, the victim sustained severe stab wounds and spent nine days admitted to Tumutumu Hospital. Frustrated by the initial handling of the matter, the complainant escalated her concerns to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecution (ODPP) in Nanyuki, which subsequently ordered detectives to take over the investigation.

Following the directive, Kieni East detectives tracked Osiemo to his hiding place in Kisii County, executing an arrest on September 18. Rather than being returned directly to Narumoru, the suspect was initially booked into Keroka Police Station as local officers coordinated his transfer.

Osiemo was moved to Narumoru Police Station on September 19, where investigators state he was processed, had his statement recorded, and was observed to be in normal condition ahead of a scheduled Monday arraignment. Instead, officers discovered his body hanging inside the cell on Sunday morning.

![DCI](https://i.ibb.co/kVrrFKnh/26f9df06eda3.webp)

The Independent Policing Oversight Authority (IPOA) has since taken charge of the inquiry into the circumstances surrounding the death. The crime scene was processed in the presence of IPOA representatives, senior police commanders, and a relative of the deceased.

The body remains at the Narumoru Mortuary awaiting a postmortem examination as investigators work to reconstruct the final hours of the suspect's detention.
