Strict state examination rules that freeze candidate rolls months ahead of testing have forced the High Court to broker a joint operational compromise at Gatoto Community Primary School, insulating 303 national assessment candidates from an entrenched management row.

Under a consent order adopted on Wednesday, the court established a dual-custody arrangement that allows accredited officials back on the premises starting October 1, ensuring national assessments proceed without conceding ground on the school's substantive leadership dispute.

### The Nominal Roll Lock

The court's intervention followed technical evidence from Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC) Chief Executive David Njeng’ere, who cautioned that the state examination framework cannot accommodate eleventh-hour leadership changes.

![KNEC CEO Dr David Njengere after opening exam container at Kamukunji Sub County on December 5, 2022](https://i.ibb.co/4RnY421J/c20287afbea9.webp)

According to KNEC, the 303 candidates across the Kenya Primary School Education Assessment (KPSEA) and Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA) were formally registered between February 16 and March 31. Once that statutory window shut, candidate identities and school centre codes were permanently locked into the council’s national nominal roll.

Njeng’ere told the court that altering the institutional registration data mid-stream would fatally compromise the chain of custody and integrity of the national records. , national assessment papers for all 303 learners have already been printed and remain held under secure custody in KNEC warehouses awaiting transit to distribution containers.

### A Court-Enforced Operational Truce

To bridge the gap between KNEC’s immutable administrative roll and the rival faction currently occupying the school, the court sanctioned a structured coexistence protocol.

Centre manager Margaret Kimani Kabugi—the administrator formally registered in KNEC’s national database—alongside seven accredited personnel, has been granted unfettered entry to Gatoto Community Primary School from October 1 through October 30 for pre-examination staging.

The consent explicitly orders the on-site leadership to step aside from examination logistics, instructing the current management, staff, and their agents against blocking or interfering with the accredited council team.

![exams boys](https://i.ibb.co/7x6htqzw/c2a8942dbdea.webp)

"The said eight staff members shall work in hand with the Petitioners’ staff members for the success of all candidates," the court consent outlined, setting clear operational boundaries for both camps.

### Security and Examination Timelines

The court order extends full perimeter and administrative clearance to KNEC assessment supervisors, invigilators, quality assurance monitors, and armed police escorts deployed during the assessment cycle.

Preparatory logistics run throughout the first three weeks of October, leading up to the official examination rollout scheduled for October 26.

While the wider staff access window covers preparation through the end of October, Kabugi's individual mandate as centre manager remains legally active on-site until November 7 to oversee the return of sealed scripts to state custody. The underlying legal battle over the permanent control and proprietorship of Gatoto Community Primary School remains pending before the courts.
