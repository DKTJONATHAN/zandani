On the exact day the United Patriotic Movement (UPM) formally collected its certificate of full registration from state regulators, the emerging political entity made a stark declaration of organizational flexibility: it is prepared to set aside its newly acquired party brand or yield its flagship gubernatorial candidate to facilitate Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna’s presidential framework.

Speaking on Friday, October 2, Kakamega Senator Boni Khalwale—who currently sits in the Senate on a United Democratic Alliance (UDA) ticket—announced that his 2027 campaign for Kakamega Governor will be mounted on whichever political party platform Sifuna chooses to adopt.

Khalwale stated that UPM’s founding members and its interim National Executive Committee (NEC) had reached a formal consensus to subordinate their own party certification to Sifuna’s political decision, reflecting an unconventional approach to party building in Kenya's electoral landscape.

“I want it to be clear that Edwin Sifuna’s hands are free to announce any party,” Khalwale said. “If he announces that he will run on the UPM ticket, these founder members and the interim NEC have resolved that they will welcome him with both hands so that he flies the presidential flag of Linda Mwananchi on UPM.”

![Senator Boni Khalwale addressing residents in Likuyani Constituency on Tuesday April 5, 2022](https://i.ibb.co/HTMYbcDq/83cf89ab8820.webp)

Khalwale added that if Sifuna selects a different registered vehicle, UPM’s leadership would set aside their registration certificate and deploy Khalwale directly into Sifuna's party structure.

“They have also resolved that, should he announce a party other than UPM, they will then shelve the UPM certificate and wholeheartedly support Sifuna and donate me to run in his party as the Governor of Kakamega in 2027,” Khalwale said.

### Certificate Pickup and Regulatory Demands

The declaration coincided directly with the administrative completion of UPM’s registration process at the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties (ORPP).

Registrar J.C. Lorionokou confirmed Friday that UPM’s certificate of full registration had been officially processed on June 26, but had remained pending collection at the agency's offices until its founding members arrived to pick it up.

Upon handing over the documentation, the registrar’s office formally notified UPM’s leadership of the statutory compliance mandates governing fully registered parties ahead of the 2027 general election. These include maintaining updated membership registers, adhering to internal governance rules, establishing clear conflict resolution mechanisms, and filing mandatory financial disclosures.

### The Linda Mwananchi Coalition Architecture

Khalwale’s willingness to tie his gubernatorial nomination to Sifuna’s choices marks a notable shift in Kakamega’s political alignments and underscores the evolving structure of the Linda Mwananchi movement.

On September 26, principals of the Linda Mwananchi movement officially named Sifuna as their coalition leader and designated presidential candidate. The umbrella grouping brings together various political figures and political outfits, including Embakasi East MP Babu Owino’s The Mwananchi Party.

While Linda Mwananchi operates as a broad political coalition seeking formal alignments with like-minded parties, Sifuna has yet to announce the specific party ticket on which he will run.

That decision is expected in early October, when Sifuna is scheduled to publicly unveil his party choice—a reveal that will now immediately dictate both the organizational trajectory of the newly certified UPM and the official ticket for Khalwale’s Kakamega gubernatorial campaign.
