Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) Secretary General Catherine Omanyo has drawn a hard line against digital rumor-mongers, calling out internet trolls who turned a routine medical checkup for party leader Dr. Oburu Oginga into an online obituary watch.

Speaking live on Radio Maisha, Omanyo delivered a blunt reality check to social media commentators who had prematurely written off the veteran politician. With Dr. Oburu slated to walk out of the hospital as early as Tuesday afternoon or Wednesday, October 14, 2026, the Busia County Woman Representative did not mince words about the dark side of high-profile medical leaves.

“I recently read comments from people who thought he had passed away. He hasn’t; he is still here and doing well,” Omanyo stated on air. Turning her focus to those wishing the worst on the veteran leader, she added a sharp warning: “They should realize that sometimes you might wish for someone else to die, only for you to go first. In this life, you never know.”

According to Omanyo, the public panic was entirely a byproduct of Dr. Oburu's towering political profile rather than any medical crisis. She explained that while ordinary citizens navigate high blood pressure and routine ailments quietly, a party leader's hospital visit instantly triggers a cascade of  alerts and wild speculation.

“We all get sick sometimes… It is just that because he is a party leader, his illness becomes . But it is normal; he might have had a bit of high blood pressure here and there,” she noted.

The recovery timeline also served up an unexpected endorsement for local medical infrastructure. While Dr. Oburu initially traveled to the United Kingdom for professional medical consultations, Omanyo pointedly credited Kenyan specialists with steering the core of his physical recovery.

“Our doctors are excellent; even though he went to England, it was here in Kenya that he made the most significant recovery. Our Kenyan doctors are truly capable, and I thank them very much,” she said.

With hospital discharge formalities underway, the focus shifts back to the political landscape as the party leader prepares to return home from his medical stay.
