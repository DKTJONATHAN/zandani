The Office of the Registrar of Political Parties (ORPP) faces a seven-day deadline to issue a fresh determination on the reservation of the political name *Linda Mwananchi*, following a decision by the Political Parties Dispute Tribunal (PPDT) that declared the original rejection unconstitutional.

In a virtual ruling delivered on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the tribunal held that the registrar acted without legal justification when it blocked the reservation and formal registration of the outfit, which is linked to Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna.

The tribunal's decision turns on administrative compliance, finding that the registrar breached statutory procedures outlined in the Political Parties Act by denying the name reservation without anchored legal grounds.

### Limits on Administrative Discretion

Under the Political Parties Act, the registrar holds the responsibility of vetting proposed political party names to ensure they do not mirror existing entities, violate public morality, or incite ethnic hatred. However, the tribunal found that the ORPP failed to demonstrate how *Linda Mwananchi* crossed those explicit statutory thresholds.

By ruling the refusal unlawful, the tribunal reinforced boundaries on state gatekeeping, signaling that administrative agencies cannot exercise unguided discretion when citizens attempt to reserve party titles or launch formal political movements.

### The Road Ahead for ORPP

The dispute originated when proponents of the movement submitted an application seeking to secure *Linda Mwananchi* as a reserved party name. The registrar subsequently turned down the application, effectively locking the group out of the initial stage required toward official political party status.

With the rejection now formally set aside, the ORPP is required under the tribunal’s orders to re-evaluate the reservation request within seven days. The registrar must either approve the name or issue a legally grounded statutory justification if any compliance defects exist, leaving the movement's registration trajectory tied directly to the upcoming weekly deadline.
