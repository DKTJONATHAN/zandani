The financial incentives driving illicit fuel harvesting into sharp relief emerged this week after Kenya Pipeline Company (KPC) security teams intercepted a clandestine tap along a coastal transmission corridor just as motorists absorb record retail prices.

Working during routine surveillance on Monday, November 2, pipeline maintenance and emergency response units tracked and secured an unauthorized connection on the line linking Pumping Station 14 to Pumping Station 15 in Mombasa County. KPC General Manager Dinah Kirwa confirmed that monitoring teams neutralized the bypass before any petroleum products could be drawn off, resulting in zero product loss.

![A collage of an illegal fuel connection in Mombasa uncovered by KPC on November 2, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/mCS5T6nZ/96000bbf316a.webp)

The physical breach has since been handed over to the Chaani Police Station, where investigators have opened a formal inquiry. Authorities are examining the mechanics of the subterranean tap and are expected to determine whether personnel stationed at the facilities aided or compromised the security perimeter.

The interception occurs during a sensitive pricing window for Kenyan consumers. Pump prices across the country remain heavily inflated following the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority’s October 14 adjustment cycle, which pushed petrol to Ksh226.96, diesel to Ksh227.76, and kerosene to Ksh219.87 per litre. The sharp increases—attributed by the Ministry of Energy to supply disruptions tied to Middle East conflicts—have intensified profit margins for illicit market actors operating along bulk transit routes.

![Kenya Pipeline Company (KPC) Limited](https://i.ibb.co/BH1DZsY4/bf503b692dce.webp)

While KPC's rapid electronic surveillance and physical patrols successfully neutralized this particular breach, the incident underscores the persistent vulnerability of downstream infrastructure when retail commodity prices spike. Police investigations into the Chaani connection remain active as the current EPRA pricing cycle counts down toward its November 14 reset.
