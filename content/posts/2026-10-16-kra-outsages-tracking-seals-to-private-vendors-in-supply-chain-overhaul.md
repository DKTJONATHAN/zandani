Importers, exporters, and logistics firms operating through Kenyan customs must contract private technology providers before October 26, 2026, as the Kenya Revenue Authority terminates its direct provision of electronic cargo tracking seals.

Under a public notice issued by the tax authority, the transition establishes a Multi-Vendor User-Owned e-Seal Framework within the Regional Electronic Cargo Tracking System (RECTS). Under the incoming model, businesses moving dry cargo and motor vehicles under customs control will no longer rely on state-supplied devices. Instead, they must procure monitoring units directly through commercial agreements with private vendors authorized by the state.

![An Image of a cargo clearance officer supervising clearance at Mombasa port](https://i.ibb.co/FjgrNgg/11825c4400a8.webp)

The October 26 cutoff applies immediately to dry freight and vehicular transit. Existing KRA-owned seals deployed across those categories will be deactivated, leaving monitoring to be conducted exclusively through vendor-supplied hardware. For wet cargo, including petroleum and fuel products, the authority has adopted a phased wind-down rather than a simultaneous hard stop, with current KRA-managed fuel seals being progressively retired.

The structural shift moves the operational burden of device acquisition and vendor management onto commercial supply chain operators. Importers and transporters retain the discretion to select their preferred service provider from an approved registry published on the tax authority's official portal.

![customs KRA NYS](https://i.ibb.co/KzbD4Ggn/357d8e892eda.webp)

Stakeholders seeking authorization lists or clarification on vendor compliance have been directed to contact the Cargo Monitoring Unit via email or through offices located on the 13th floor of Times Tower in Nairobi.

The overhaul follows an earlier regulatory tightening introduced in August 2026, which mandated that all exporters shipping goods to Kenya secure reference codes via an Advance Cargo Declaration (ACD) before dispatching consignments.
