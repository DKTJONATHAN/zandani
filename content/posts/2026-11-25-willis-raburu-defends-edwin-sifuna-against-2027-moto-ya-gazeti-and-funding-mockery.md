Willis Raburu has turned the ammunition used by critics against Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna’s 2027 presidential ambitions right back around, arguing that personal and financial mockery is proof of genuine political traction.

Speaking during an appearance on comedian Dr Ofweneke’s show, Raburu pulled back the curtain on the specific class-based and financial arguments being deployed by supporters of President William Ruto, known locally as Tutam. Rather than viewing the attacks as detrimental, Raburu argued that opposition chatter signals that the senator's profile is causing discomfort among rivals.

“Walianza kusema moto ya gazeti, wakasema shiny, oooh, he does not have money to do it,” Raburu noted, detailing the specific financial and image-based digs aimed at undermining Sifuna’s candidacy.

The media personality, who has publicly thrown his weight behind Sifuna and urged the opposition to hand him a presidential ticket, cautioned young politicians against letting resource-based intimidation sideline their ambitions. Sifuna’s presidential bid received formal backing in September 2026 when the Linda Mwananchi movement endorsed him as its flagbearer.

Beyond his vocal support for Sifuna, Raburu has actively looped into youth-led political mobilization networks across Nairobi. On November 19, 2026, he publicly backed Embakasi East MP Babu Owino’s recruitment drive for The Mwananchi Party, welcoming young aspirants to jump into the ring.

Raburu is himself navigating the shifting political landscape ahead of the 2027 General Election, having declared his intention to contest the Kisumu senatorial seat.
