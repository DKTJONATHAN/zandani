Content creator and amateur boxer Mbavu Destroyer is moving his online platform from fitness challenges and ring bouts into environmental endurance, partnering with record-holding activist Truphena Muthoni to anchor a massive national tree-planting initiative.

The pair revealed their coalition alongside Australian environmentalist Jimi Cohen in a joint social media broadcast. The roll-out pairs high-profile viral stunts with a long-term mobilization campaign, using individual world-record attempts to build digital momentum for a broader ecological target.

The project starts with an immediate physical challenge in Kenya by Jimi Cohen, who is staging an individual world record attempt to plant more trees within a single 24-hour window than any person in history.

"Tomorrow I'll be planting more trees in a day than any human has ever planted right here in Kenya because in Kenya anything is possible,” Cohen stated during the announcement.

### Staging Endurance for Environmental Targets

That solo marathon serves as the promotional launching pad for the coalition's main objective: a nationwide effort scheduled for April 22, 2027, coinciding with World Earth Day. On that date, the group aims to coordinate local communities across Kenyan counties to plant one billion trees within a single 24-hour window.

“A new GUINNESS WORLD RECORD! Then @truphena.muthonii will be launching a country-wide tree planting day to get 1B Trees planted next Earth Day,” Mbavu Destroyer confirmed on his channels.

For Muthoni, 22, the initiative extends a deliberate strategy of using high-intensity physical endurance to capture public attention for climate causes. She previously gained national recognition after setting a Guinness World Record for hugging a tree continuously for 72 hours, an effort that earned her the Head of State Commendation medal from President William Ruto.

Muthoni has also applied speed-planting formats to marine ecosystems, having spearheaded a coastal campaign that saw 150,000 mangrove seedlings planted in just one hour.

"We're going to plant the most trees planted by humanity. 1 billion trees in only 24 hours in Kenya,” Muthoni said.

### Bridging Creator Audiences and County Action

Integrating Mbavu Destroyer into the campaign signals a deliberate attempt to translate social media metrics into physical participation. Known for energetic workout videos, boxing content, and youth-focused physical stunts, his role centers on converting digital followers into grassroots volunteers ahead of the 2027 target.

The organizers pointed to Kenya’s international standing in climate and environmental justice initiatives as the key reason for selecting the country as the base for the global record attempt. Local participation across all 47 counties will dictate whether the one-billion milestone moves from a viral campaign pitch to a verified ecological result.

With Cohen's solo attempt kicking off the sequence, the team plans to run targeted community mobilization drives across the country over the next several months leading into World Earth Day 2027.
