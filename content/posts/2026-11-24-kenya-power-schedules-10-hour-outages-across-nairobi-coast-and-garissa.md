Kenya Power has lined up sweeping electricity interruptions across five counties, setting up a disruptive Tuesday for households, businesses, and essential service providers alike. 

The scheduled maintenance exercise, slated for Tuesday, November 24, 2026, will see select regions plunged into darkness for up to 10 hours as technical teams carry out routine infrastructure servicing.

The countrywide scope of the Tuesday bulletin touches disparate economic hubs and residential zones, stretching from the coastal tourist corridors of Mombasa and Kwale to northern administrative centers in Garissa, alongside high-density neighborhoods in Nairobi and Kiambu.

In the coastal circuit, parts of Mombasa County—including Jamhuri Road, Green Wood Drive, and Mama Ngina Drive—face an interruption window running from 9am to 4pm. Down the coast in Kwale County, the outages will hit Tiwi, Musufini, and critical infrastructure at Tiwi Water Boreholes between 9am and 5pm, directly impacting local water supply operations.

Moving inland to Garissa County, residents and commercial entities in Modika, C.A.K. Quba, Mwangaza, and Sumeya are scheduled to lose grid power from 9am to 5pm. Similarly, property owners and traders in Kiambu County, specifically around Ondiri, Muguga Complex, and Kamangu, will experience a concurrent five-hour blackout window during the same daytime hours.

The utility firm has directed all consumers located within the targeted outage zones to formulate alternative logistical arrangements for power-dependent operations while technicians execute the maintenance works.
