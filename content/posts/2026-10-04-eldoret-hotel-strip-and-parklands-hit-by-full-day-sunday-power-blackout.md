Sunday leisure spots, major hotel properties, and busy retail corridors across five counties face an eight-hour daytime power pause as Kenya Power rolls out scheduled network maintenance on October 4, 2026.

The utility firm's planned shutdown targets key commercial and urban residential zones in Nairobi, Uasin Gishu, Kiambu, Kisumu, and Mombasa, with the vast majority of interruptions running from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

### Eldoret Commercial and Hospitality Core Off the Grid

In Uasin Gishu County, the maintenance schedule strikes right at the commercial and administrative heart of Eldoret town around the GK Prison precinct. The eight-hour outage cuts power to premier hospitality addresses including Sirikwa Hotel, Goshen Hotel, and Cicada Hotel, directly disrupting Sunday dining, events, and accommodation facilities.

The interruption also hits major retail and civic nodes within Eldoret. Outlets and offices housed inside Zion Mall and MUSCO Towers will navigate the shutdown alongside the County Government of Uasin Gishu offices. The outage area extends along Eastern Avenue, Komora, St. Matthews ACK church, and Moi Girls’ High School, alongside Eldoret GK Prison and neighboring residential premises.

### Parklands Enclaves and Pipeline Industrial Belt

Nairobi County experiences two distinct maintenance operations starting at 9:00 am. In the northern suburbs, a residential and commercial pocket of Parklands will spend eight hours without mains electricity. The affected strip spans 4th Parklands, Masari Road, KU Lane, Ngao Road, Suswa Road, and Wangapala Road.

The Parklands cutoff also covers Diamond 1 and 2, KPLC Staff Quarters, the Presidential Escort facility, and adjacent estates. 

On the other side of the capital, an industrial and commercial cluster behind Kenya Pipeline along the railway line faces an identical 9:00 am to 5:00 pm interruption. That shutdown takes Rubis Depot, Eastlands College, Uzuri Foods, and Cera Coating offline, alongside facilities on Mogadishu Road, Olkarau Road, Pate Road, Runyenjes Road, and sections of Nanyuki Road.

### Heavy Industry and Market Centers Across Kiambu and Mombasa

Kiambu County bears one of the widest footprints of the Sunday schedule. The utility has scheduled work across an expansive manufacturing, institutional, and residential zone in Thika between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm. 

The list of affected manufacturing facilities includes Kenblest, Carton Experts, Retec Factory, Kenya Leather, Avoil Industries, Kiganjo Alternative Energy, alongside Magic Stone Quarry and Budhia Quarry. Institutional setups inside the outage zone include Thika Barracks, MKU Pavilion, Makongeni Police Station, KCB Makongeni, St. Mulumba Catholic Church, Mulumba Hospital, Kenyatta Secondary School, Carter Oakhill School, and Glorious Fountain Academy.

Residential hubs in Kiambu slated for the outage include Kisii Estate, Landless Estate, Buffalo Estate, Kabati, Kwa Magu, Nyacaba, Kamenu, Malaba, Muthara, and Kimuchu, as well as trade centers at Muguga, Gatuanyaga, Komo, and Munyu markets.

At the Coast, Mombasa County's scheduled maintenance focuses on the Shimanzi logistics and maritime corridor from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Key energy and transport installations affected include East Africa Terminals Ltd, Vivo Energy Kenya, East African Storage, Ola Energy, and Salvochem. The outage encompasses the entire Mahindi Station, the whole of Kismayu Road, part of Shimanzi Road, Kitui Flour Mills Makande, and Bandari Clinic.

### Western Milling and Institutional Belt

In western Kenya, maintenance runs from 10:00 am to 5:00 pm, affecting regional agro-processing hubs and academic centers. The scheduled line work cuts supply to Shoka Maize Millers, Mudete Tea Factory, and Kitigu Goldmine.

Health and educational institutions within the western circuit, including Sabatia Eye Hospital, Kaimosi University, and Iregi Teachers Training College (Iregi TIC), fall under the seven-hour interruption. Major open-air trading points along the corridor—including Mbale Market, Serem Market, Majengo Market, Gambogi Market, and Chavakali Market, alongside Makhokho—are also set for the outage.

Kenya Power stated that the planned interruptions allow technical teams to conduct routine repairs and upgrades on distribution infrastructure. The utility urged customers across the affected pockets to arrange backup solutions early and warned residents to treat all electrical points as live throughout the maintenance window.
