Kirinyaga Governor Anne Waiguru is refusing to treat the deputy presidency as a quiet political appointment, stepping out publicly to demand that President William Ruto’s 2027 running mate slot be thrown open to competitive selection across the political landscape.

Rather than waiting on behind-the-scenes negotiations, the two-term Kirinyaga governor is putting her administrative credentials directly into the public square. Waiguru maintains that every executive and legislative seat in Kenya—from county assembly members and governors all the way to the president—must face the voters every five years, and she insists the office of the Deputy President should follow the exact same democratic standard.

### A Call for Competitive Ticket Selection

Addressing the public, Waiguru moved swiftly to counter speculation that her national ambitions are fueled by personal rivalries or political friction with colleagues. Instead, she framed her interest in the seat as a standard exercise of constitutional choice, arguing that any established leader has the right to present themselves for national executive leadership without being misconstrued as fighting individual battles.

She emphasized that in a healthy democracy, leadership opportunities should remain open to rigorous public evaluation. By calling for competitive selection, Waiguru is advocating for a framework where candidates for the running mate position demonstrate their executive readiness before the electorate rather than relying on automatic incumbency or closed-door party consensus.

### Resume Flex and Regional Demographics

As part of her pitch to serve as President Ruto’s running mate in the 2027 General Election, Waiguru is centering her extensive background in government. She is pointing directly to her dual track record in public administration, highlighting her foundational tenure as Devolution Cabinet Secretary alongside her leadership experience as a two-term county governor navigating sub-national governance in Kirinyaga.

Her campaign strategy also leans heavily into the strategic political math of the Mt Kenya region. Waiguru hails from Mt Kenya West, a voting bloc widely recognized as holding a substantially larger share of the electorate compared to Mt Kenya East, which is the home region of incumbent Deputy President Kithure Kindiki. By emphasizing those regional numbers in her public pitch, Waiguru is bringing demographic weight into the core of her bid.

Beyond regional electoral calculations, political observers note that Waiguru’s public declaration carries a strong appeal for female voters across the nation. As one of Kenya’s most prominent female governors, her pursuit of the second-highest executive post offers a high-visibility rallying point for women voters, while positioning her as a potential future contender for the presidency itself.

By asserting that the running mate slot should be a transparent contest grounded in administrative experience and voter numbers, Waiguru has effectively set the terms of her bid. Her public play ensures that the race for Ruto's 2027 running mate will remain one of the most closely watched developments as Kenya's political landscape continues to evolve.
