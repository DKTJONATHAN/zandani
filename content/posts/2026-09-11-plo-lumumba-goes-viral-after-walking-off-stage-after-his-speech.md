**KISUMU** - Legal scholar and orator Prof. P.L.O. Lumumba exited the stage abruptly at the Mama Grace Onyango Cultural and Social Centre on Friday, following a request by event organizers to suspend his keynote address to facilitate the arrival of Kisumu Governor Anyang’ Nyong’o.

The interruption occurred during the 18th TICH Annual Scientific Conference, where Prof. Lumumba was speaking on the challenges facing African access to research materials. As organizers moved to pause the session for the governor’s entry, Prof. Lumumba paused his delivery, citing his discomfort with the disruption of intellectual proceedings in favor of political protocol.

"Does political arrival hold more value than substantive intellectual discourse?" the professor posed to the audience before stepping away from the microphone and vacating the stage. Witnesses at the venue noted that the scholar exited the hall while the governor was being formally received by the event organizers.

The incident has since garnered significant traction on social media, prompting a wider conversation regarding the prioritization of VIPs at public functions. Supporters of the scholar’s actions argue that academic forums should remain insulated from political pageantry, suggesting that late-arriving officials ought to occupy their seats without interrupting ongoing programs.

Conversely, observers familiar with local governance protocols have maintained that regional leaders are often entitled to formal acknowledgments within their jurisdictions. These sources argue that the incident reflects a long-standing tension between established political etiquette and the preservation of academic decorum in Kenya. Neither the office of the Governor nor the organizers of the 18th TICH Annual Scientific Conference have issued a formal statement regarding the disruption as of Friday evening.
