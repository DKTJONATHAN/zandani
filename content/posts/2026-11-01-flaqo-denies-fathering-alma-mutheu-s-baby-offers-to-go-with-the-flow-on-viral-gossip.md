Flaqo Raz has drawn a firm line against paternity claims linking him to fellow content creator Alma Mutheu, while simultaneously offering an open door to the digital buzz surrounding the story.

Addressing the claims during a phone interview with Milele FM presenter Ankali Ray, the multi-character sketch comic insisted his past relationship with Alma never stepped beyond the camera lens. At the same time, Flaqo took an unusually detached stance toward the viral claims, noting that if the internet insists on linking them, he has no problem converting the chatter into personal reach.

"Hiyo ball siyo yangu," Flaqo said during the radio exchange. "Watu wakiamua kuamini pia naenda na flow. We were working with Alma before, I swear we haven't done anything apart from work."

### The BNN Spark

The claims gathered steam after entertainment gossip platform BNN Premium published posts alleging Flaqo and Alma had quietly pursued a "situationship" away from their public content collaborations. The online narrative quickly morphed across social feeds into speculation over whether the comedian was responsible for Alma's expected child.

Alma, who has carved out an audience across Kenyan digital circuits with comedy skits and lifestyle appearances, had recently become the subject of pregnancy discussions among her followers. The claim that Flaqo was intimately involved offered fresh fodder for comment sections eager to connect high-profile creators behind the scenes.

Rather than responding with legal warnings or public indignation, Flaqo treated the radio cross-examination with the easy cynicism typical of veteran digital creators. While he was unequivocal that no physical intimacy took place during their video shoots, he made no effort to plead with skeptics to drop the subject.
