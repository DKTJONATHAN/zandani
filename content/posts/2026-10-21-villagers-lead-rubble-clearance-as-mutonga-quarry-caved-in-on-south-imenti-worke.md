Neighbours and surviving stonecutters were forced into the role of primary emergency responders on Wednesday morning, hauling heavy rock by hand after a massive cliff-face collapse buried workers at Mutonga quarry in South Imenti.

The incident, occurring along the border zone between Meru and Tharaka Nithi counties, triggered an urgent scramble among residents living near the extraction pits in Igoji before specialized disaster machinery could access the remote terrain.

### The Morning Collapse and Emergency Alert

The rockfall struck mid-morning as crews were carrying out routine stone extraction. Meru County Special Programmes Director Lena Kiambi confirmed the disaster publicly at approximately 9:20am, indicating that county-level emergency personnel had been mobilized to the site.

![Images from the scene as residents joined recue efforts at Mutonga Quarry in Igoji, Meru County, following a fatal collapse, October 21, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/G3kXGWfx/f86e59741a74.webp)

"Mutonga, Igoji quarry accident. Rescue mission enroute," Kiambi announced as county units began navigating the route to the peripheral ward.

By the time official rescue units were dispatched, local residents had already descended into the pit. Using basic hand tools and bare hands, community members began prising apart fractured rock slabs to locate muffled calls beneath the mound.

### Survivor Accounts and Casualty Estimates

The immediate community intervention yielded at least one critical extraction. Villagers managed to pull one severely injured worker from beneath the debris, transferring him to a nearby medical facility for emergency stabilization. Another quarryman managed to scramble clear of the collapsing wall unhurt.

![A collage showing fallen rocks and rescue efforts at Mutonga Quarry in Igoji, Meru County, following a fatal collapse, October 21, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/pjfr7bNL/974013796a36.webp)

Accounts from the uninjured survivor indicated that at least six colleagues remained unaccounted for and were suspected to be wedged under the primary fall of rock. Unofficial preliminary reports from the area placed the potential death toll at six, though county disaster officials withheld formal fatality figures pending the clearing of the main debris field.

### Frontier Extraction and Structural Hazards

The Mutonga site serves as a vital economic engine for youth across Igoji and neighbouring settlements across the Tharaka Nithi boundary, supplying foundational building stone to regional construction markets. However, the manual method of undercut extraction—where labourers chip away at cliff bases without heavy stabilizing equipment—has repeatedly exposed workers to sudden structural shearing.

Wednesday’s cave-in immediately revived persistent scrutiny regarding occupational safeguards and geotechnical monitoring at artisanal extraction sites scattered across the Mount Kenya slopes.

Meru County authorities and local administrators have launched inquiries to determine what destabilized the rock face, while earth-moving machinery joins the community cordon to account for the remaining trapped workers.
