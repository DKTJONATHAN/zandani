Interior and National Administration Principal Secretary Dr. Raymond Omollo stepped onto the sands of Uhendo Beach in West Sakwa to confront a persistent cross-border grievance—Ugandan security harassment of Kenyan fishermen on Lake Victoria—while regional lawmakers used the platform to deliver a sharp political message to the Orange Democratic Movement.

Addressing a crowded consultative meeting alongside fisherfolk from Usigu, Rarieda, and Bondo sub-counties, Dr. Omollo detailed a two-pronged strategy involving high-level diplomatic channels and direct operational upgrades for local enforcement.

"Mkazungumzia mambo ya usalama baharini. Najua tumekua na tatizo na majirani wetu apa Uganda," Dr. Omollo told the gathering, confirming that the Ministry of Foreign Affairs has been formally tasked with negotiating a permanent cessation of lake disruptions. "Tumekubaliana na hawa viongozi wa usalama, tutaongeza idadi ya maafisa wetu. Kenya Coast Guard Service wako hapa. Tutawaongezea zile vifaa zenye zinahitajika."

The security overhaul promises fresh operational backing for the Kenya Coast Guard Service, with the state committing to secure funding aimed at ending maritime incursions. Alongside the security pledges, the meeting spotlighted President William Ruto's ongoing investments in the regional blue economy, including nine modern fish landing sites, specialized safety equipment, and specialized training frameworks.

Yet, the beach-front consultation quickly bridged maritime security and national party politics. Gem MP Elisha Odhiambo and Bondo MP Gideon Ochanda seized the podium to address internal political realignments, urging the ODM leadership to grant President William Ruto unhindered political space to consolidate cross-community partnerships.

Mr. Odhiambo publicly dismissed recent calls by factions within ODM demanding a strict 50-50 power-sharing arrangement with the current administration, arguing that such ultimatums are impractical and threaten to alienate crucial regional alliances.

The high-stakes session was convened alongside Nyanza Regional Commissioner Onesmus Kyatha and Siaya County Commissioner Norbert Komora, aligning grassroots administrative machinery with national political signaling along the Lake Victoria corridor.
