Reverend Kathy Kiuna has grounded her latest luxury travel flex in theology, framing a private jet flight to Tanzania as an unexplainable manifestation of spiritual favour. 

Documenting her travel to attend the high-profile wedding of President William Ruto’s daughter, Charlene Ruto, and Tanzanian entrepreneur Isaya Yunge, the preacher released night-flight footage showing the high-end cabin amenities reserved for her entourage.

“Favour is just that. You can’t explain it. It is what it is,” Kiuna wrote alongside the video posted on Tuesday, November 10, 2026.

The footage offered a step-by-step look into the travel arrangements, starting with Kiuna filming herself on the tarmac before boarding the white-and-blue aircraft. Inside, the spacious cabin featured beige leather upholstery, polished brown wooden tables, and structured seating configurations designed for long-distance comfort.

Passengers were served fresh fruit platters and light refreshments during the evening flight across the border. Kiuna recorded several moments from her seat, capturing the calm cabin atmosphere as the aircraft headed toward northern Tanzania.

The flight was not a solo journey. Kiuna was accompanied by her daughter, Vanessa Kiuna, as well as Reverend Julian Kyula, the founder of Rhema Feast, along with a select cohort of guests invited to the cross-border wedding festivities.

The delegation flew to Mwanza to witness Charlene Ruto and Isaya Yunge exchange vows. The couple held their wedding ceremony on Friday, November 6, 2026, drawing dignitaries, business figures, and church leaders from both Kenya and Tanzania.

The Mwanza ceremony capped off a multi-stage marital timeline for the couple, following earlier traditional rites in Kilgoris and Church testimonies in Ngorongoro. Visuals from the Malaika Beach Resort venue and surrounding events have circulated widely across regional social media platforms since the weekend.

For Kiuna, the flight served as both a lifestyle update and a public testimony, positioning the chartered passage to Mwanza as a personal milestone of divine backing.
