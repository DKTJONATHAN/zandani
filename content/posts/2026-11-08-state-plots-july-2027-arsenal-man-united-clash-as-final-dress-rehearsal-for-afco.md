The government is mapping out a second European club showcase at the 60,000-capacity Raila Odinga International Stadium, setting up a proposed July 2027 exhibition between Manchester United and Arsenal as the final operational dry run ahead of the Africa Cup of Nations.

Speaking on Sunday, November 8, Interior Cabinet Secretary Kipchumba Murkomen disclosed that the state intends to bring the two English Premier League giants to Nairobi between June and July 2027. The window sits squarely on the eve of the 2027 continental tournament, which Kenya will stage in a joint hosting arrangement with Uganda and Tanzania.

"Next year the president will bring Arsenal and Manchester United to play in Nairobi in July 2027," Murkomen stated, reaffirming an ambition first signaled by President William Ruto before recent stadium agreements were finalized.

The proposed clash would serve as the secondary milestone in an aggressive commissioning schedule aimed at positioning the newly completed arena as a regional sports tourism anchor. The opening test takes place six months earlier, on Tuesday, January 5, 2027, when German powerhouse Bayern Munich faces the Netherlands’ AFC Ajax in the stadium’s inaugural match.

### Operational Staging and Crowd Logistics

State planners are using the confirmed January opener to establish baseline crowd control, perimeter security, and ticketing operations for the arena, which is now entering its concluding construction phases. 

Entry prices for the Bayern Munich and Ajax fixture have been pegged from Ksh2,500, with Murkomen projecting substantial international passenger arrivals into Nairobi ahead of matchday.

"We have 5,000 Europeans coming to Kenya to watch the match. We have people from Nigeria and Egypt who are also coming to watch the game," Murkomen said, pointing to regional and international demand.

![Interior CS Kipchumba Murkomen during a church service in Ikolomani, Kakamega on November 8, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/6SpNFnF/82c8262391f9.webp)

To accommodate international travel packages, organizers have built a 12-hour fan festival around the January fixture, integrating international musical performances, concierge management, and cultural exhibitions. Both Bayern and Ajax have contractually committed to fielding full senior squads rather than academy or ceremonial legends squads, establishing a high benchmark for subsequent invitations.

### The Path to an EPL Fixture

Securing Manchester United and Arsenal would present a considerably steeper logistical and commercial undertaking. Both clubs command massive domestic followings across East Africa, but their pre-season tours are typically locked into long-term commercial circuits across North America and Asia.

While the January fixture against continental European sides relies on mid-season winter breaks, a June-to-July slot matches the standard European pre-season preparation cycle. Staging a high-intensity derby between two Premier League sides in Nairobi would place unprecedented scrutiny on pitch maintenance, high-capacity transport links around the stadium, and hospitality logistics immediately before AFCON delegations arrive.

For state officials, testing the Raila Odinga International Stadium under maximum capacity twice within seven months provides clear technical telemetry on the venue’s readiness. The unresolved question is whether formal contract negotiations with the English clubs will match the public political commitments before global pre-season schedules are locked in.
