Nairobi forex trader Raymond Omosa, widely known online as Kenyan Prince, has set a tight personal asset deadline ahead of his planned nuptials, declaring that he will acquire a private helicopter before walking down the aisle in 2027.

Appearing on Milele FM, the content creator tied his wedding timeline with long-term partner Stephanie Ngunjiri to the acquisition of the aircraft, placing a firm one-year target on the purchase.

"If I will have to buy a chopper before marrying Stephanie, it will take me one year. You see in 2027, I am buying a chopper. Let’s not say by when, but 2027 is the year," Omosa declared during the broadcast.

### Strictly Personal Air Mobility

Unlike standard commercial charter investments typical among regional business figures, Omosa clarified that the aircraft will be reserved exclusively for personal leisure rather than commercial hire or corporate transport.
