Eric Omondi has tallied the numbers on his latest public mobilization, confirming that contributions for 17-year-old Bryson Makokha have crossed the Ksh 5.6 million mark.

Taking to social media on Thursday, November 12, 2026, the comedian updated supporters on the milestone achieved through his Sisi Kwa Sisi initiative. The public drive kicked off after a video circulated online showing the teenager in tears while attempting to sell bananas by the roadside in Lukhuna, Chwele location, Bungoma County. 

The widely shared clip brought national focus to the daily friction Makokha faced. Passersby routinely avoided purchasing his produce due to the visible physical effects of his rare, chronic skin condition, laying bare the severe economic and social stigma he navigated while trying to earn an income.

Rather than stopping at public sympathy, the fund's mobilization model was structured around direct contributions sent straight to the teenager's father. According to the initiative's operational outline, the accumulated total of Ksh 5,665,000 is partitioned into three distinct needs: securing specialized medical intervention for the skin condition, returning the teenager to his studies, and establishing a sustainable commercial venture to secure the family's long-term livelihood.

The swift turnaround from a roadside clip of distress to a multi-million-shilling intervention highlights the mobilization speed of Omondi’s grassroots digital campaigns, shifting the family's immediate survival struggle into a funded medical and economic reset.
