Public health monitors tracking Kenya’s Ebola containment effort are racing against a closing 21-day window after a primary contact in isolation tested positive for the Bundibugyo strain.

The 65-year-old woman, who had been confined to a quarantine facility since October 6 as a close relative and primary contact of the country's initial patients, developed a fever, sore throat, and malaise while under observation. Health officials confirmed that isolation protocols were triggered immediately, with subsequent laboratory tests returning positive results for the virus.

![A monitor reviewing results of airport thermal scanners at the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), October 6, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/YTPLTXzr/a77e82fbca18.webp)

Director-General for Health Dr. Patrick Amoth noted that the patient has been transferred to the Infectious Disease Unit for specialized supportive care. Meanwhile, the second patient diagnosed in the outbreak remains in stable condition and is responding to treatment.

The confirmation of the third case highlights the delicate mechanics of the ongoing containment effort, which hinges entirely on the 21-day monitoring cycle that began during the first week of October. As the fortnight draws to a close, health authorities report that 127 of the 130 identified primary contacts—representing 97.7 per cent—have been successfully traced and secured.

![A photo collage of a test tube labelled ebola and Nairobi in the background, October 14, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/RkfD1KJ3/6827c675cd4e.webp)

However, surveillance teams continue efforts to locate the remaining three individuals before the statutory monitoring period lapses. While both the World Health Organization and Africa CDC emphasize that rigorous contact tracing remains the primary defense against wider transmission, the Ministry of Health maintains that the general public faces a low risk due to the absence of documented community spread.

Authorities have cautioned that additional positive results could still emerge from within the quarantined cohort before their respective isolation windows formally expire later this month.
