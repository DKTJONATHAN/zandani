One year after the death of gospel musician Betty Bayo, her widower Hiram Gitau, known to the public as Tash, has opened up about the heavy domestic reality of keeping their blended family together in the public eye.

Betty passed away on November 10, 2025, throwing a tight-knit household into sudden grief. Writing on his official Facebook page on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, Tash marked the grim milestone by admitting that navigating the daily routine without her remains an uphill task.

“Today makes exactly one year since you left us, yet it still feels like it all happened just yesterday. To tell you the truth, life has been hard without you, and the gap you left can never be filled,” Tash shared in his memorial post.

Behind the emotional tribute lies a specific family structure that has evolved over the past twelve months. Betty left behind two children, Sky Victor and Danny, from her previous marriage to Pastor Victor Kanyari. Following her wedding to Tash in December 2021, the gospel minister had stepped in as an active parental figure in the children's lives.

When Betty died, the children elected to remain under Tash’s direct care. Rather than fracturing the household, the transition prompted a collaborative co-parenting arrangement with Pastor Kanyari, who has publicly acknowledged Tash's steadfast dedication to the children's upbringing.

In his anniversary statement, Tash renewed his personal pledge to provide for Sky and Danny, ensuring that the void left by their mother's passing is cushioned by stable care. He concluded his message by thanking Betty for her years as a partner, mother, and confidante.

“Thank you for all the beautiful memories we shared. You were the best wife and best friend anyone could ever ask for. I love you so much, Darling. Even in death, nothing will ever take your place… Continue resting in peace, my darling wife.”

The family continues to manage its public profile and private grief as the children remain settled under Tash's supervision.
