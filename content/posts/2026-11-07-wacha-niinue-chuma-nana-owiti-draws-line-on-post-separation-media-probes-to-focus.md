When media presenter Ankali Ray reached out to Nana Owiti seeking commentary on her personal life following her separation from rapper King Kaka, the media personality immediately established a firm boundary between public speculation and her personal priorities.

Rather than providing a detailed breakdown of her romantic status or indulging queries on whether she had moved on, Owiti firmly redirected the exchange. Pointing out the shift in the line of questioning, she reminded the presenter that the call had been initiated for an entirely different topic before shutting down the relationship inquiry.

“Umenipia juu ya shug(h)li nyingine, achana na hii ingine. Me niko sawa, usijali, niko na watoto,” Owiti stated during the interaction.

The exchange illustrated a tactical media deflection from the mother of two, who made it clear that her attention is fully anchored on parenting her children and managing her own affairs, rather than participating in ongoing public commentary regarding her past relationship.

### Intentional Boundaries and Family Support

Beyond deflecting relationship probes, Owiti used the conversation to outline the support system keeping her grounded. Rather than expressing a need for new romantic connections or external validation, she highlighted the support network she has actively built around herself.

Addressing listeners and fans who might be concerned about her personal state, Owiti explained that the affection she receives from her immediate family and a carefully curated circle of close friends provides all the emotional stability she requires.

“Shukran, napendwa na familia na marafiki nimewachagua, iyo love inatosha, usijali,” she added, stressing the intentionality behind her social network.

Her remarks pointed to a clear assertion of independence: that personal fulfillment after a high-profile separation does not depend on being attached to a partner, but rather on cultivating a safe environment with handpicked confidants.

### Reframing the Narrative Around Fitness

Away from queries about her emotional life, Owiti made it evident that her present energy is directed toward self-improvement, specifically through physical training.

When pressed on how she is spending her time moving forward, she pivoted straight to her fitness regimen, effectively bringing the line of questioning to a close on her own terms.

“Wacha niinue chuma mimi, I need to better myself,” she said, choosing to emphasize strength training and personal betterment over relationship commentary.

By focusing on lifting weights and physical self-improvement, Owiti reframed her public narrative away from post-breakup commentary and onto active self-care. The exchange sends a direct signal on how she intends to handle future public inquiries—keeping personal relationship matters strictly off the broadcast mic while continuing her daily routine of raising her children, working out, and leaning on her core inner circle.
