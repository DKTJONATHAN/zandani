Embakasi East MP Babu Owino has spent his career mastering the art of high-decibel street politics, but his latest move borrows from the quietest corners of constitutional academia.

On Saturday, November 7, 2026, the lawmaker unveiled former two-term Makueni Governor Prof. Kivutha Kibwana as the official patron of the Mwananchi Party. The appointment hands the fiery legislator an elder statesman whose calm public demeanor stands in stark contrast to Babu’s combative, arena-filling personal brand.

Announcing the alliance, Babu leaned heavily into Kibwana’s institutional pedigree, deliberately framing the former law dean and human rights veteran as the guiding compass of an outfit aiming to expand well beyond its Eastern Kenya roots.

"Professor Kivutha Kibwana is a distinguished Professor of Law and a passionate defender of human rights," Babu stated. "He was ranked among Kenya’s best-performing Governors and left a remarkable legacy through his transformative leadership and service to the people of Makueni County. The Mwananchi Party is honoured and privileged to have him as our patron."

### The Marriage of Opposites

On paper, the pairing brings together two entirely different political generations and temperaments. 

Babu, serving as party leader, built his public profile through the cut-and-thrust of university union battles, fiery social media addresses, and aggressive populist mobilization across Nairobi’s sprawling eastern wards. Kibwana, by contrast, established his reputation in legal academia, participating in the arduous constitutional reform struggles of the 1990s before steering Makueni County between 2013 and 2022.

During his decade at the helm in Wote, Kibwana gained national renown for pioneering a localized public participation framework, prioritizing basic healthcare, sand dams, and agro-processing while studiously avoiding the flashy motorcades favored by his county-chief peers. 

By placing Kibwana at the top of the party masthead, Babu secures a policy-heavy mentor tasked with providing strategic counsel, designing party doctrine, and grooming candidates as the party steps up nationwide recruitment ahead of the 2027 General Election.

### Repurposing Muungano for a Capital Run

The patron appointment also completes a pivotal chapter in the administrative makeover of Babu’s political home. 

The outfit previously operated as the Muungano Party, an Eastern Kenya-centric vehicle historically associated with grassroots civic mobilization. On September 10, 2026, the party formally cleared a rebranding through the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties, shedding its regional moniker to re-emerge as the Mwananchi Party.

With Kibwana’s formal blessing, Babu has effectively bridged the party’s past identity with his aggressive forward campaign. The Embakasi East legislator has spent the past several months touring outside his parliamentary borders, staging targeted membership enlistment events designed to transform the revamped party into a competitive springboard for an anticipated 2027 run for Nairobi Governor.

Securing Kibwana gives that campaign a layer of policy seriousness designed to disarm skeptics who view Babu purely through the lens of political agitprop. As Mwananchi Party begins charting ticket allocations and ward-level recruitment desks, Babu enters the 2027 chess match with an unlikely ally by his side: a scholarly veteran providing the quiet blueprint while the party leader commands the microphone.
