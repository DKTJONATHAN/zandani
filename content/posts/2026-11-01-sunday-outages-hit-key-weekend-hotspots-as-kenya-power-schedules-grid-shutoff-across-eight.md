Kenya Power’s scheduled Sunday maintenance on November 1, 2026, is set to test the backup power systems of major weekend hospitality hubs, shopping malls, and residential estates across Nairobi and seven surrounding counties.

While routine grid updates are regular occurrences, the timing and location of the eight-county exercise hit directly at prime weekend commercial zones. High-profile resorts along the Northern Bypass, busy retail hubs in Kiambu and Machakos, and coastal retreat strips in Kilifi face uninterrupted outages lasting between three and nine hours.

### Kiambu and Machakos Retail Corridors Face Full-Day Cut

In Kiambu County, two major circuits will be offline from 9:00 am until 5:00 pm. The interruption covers the commercial and residential stretch along the Northern Bypass and Kigwa Road, directly affecting the Elysian Resort, Sabis School, Four Ways, Laymark Apartments, Kencom Estate, and Muhasibu Estate. Surrounding service spots, including Rubis and Shell stations on the Bypass, will also run on independent backup power.

A secondary Kiambu circuit stretching across Gatuanyaga, Makongeni, and Kiganjo will experience a simultaneous eight-hour outage. This section encompasses key consumer destinations such as Ananas Mall, Retec Factory, Avoil Industries, Kenblest, and Retec Factory, alongside nearby residential clusters in Kisii Estate, Kamenu, Landless, and Kimuchu.

Across county lines in Machakos, the Athi River industrial and retail corridor will see power cut from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. The scheduled maintenance reaches major retail and leisure operations including Signature Mall, Tamarind, Sabaki Estate, Greatwall Gardens, and London Distillers. Nearby commercial properties including Saphire Business Park, Kims Business Park, and Nova School are also slated for the full-day disruption.

Further east in Machakos, Matuu Town and its surrounding commercial centers—including Kanyonyo Market, Kivandini Market, and Kithyoko Market—will be without power from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, affecting local hospitality venues and essential service posts such as the Kanyonyo Border Police Unit and Police Hospital.

### Coastal Hospitality Strip Plunged Into Seven-Hour Window

In Kilifi County, the maintenance window targets the North Coast leisure corridor from 9:00 am to 4:00 pm. The affected areas include established leisure and lodging spots such as Mawenzi Resort, Porini Restaurant, Mtwapa Gardens, Destiny Inn, White Peacock, and Bliss Villas.

The coastal shutdown also impacts local commercial setups, including Glacier Foods, Mzuri Sweets I, Mvita Oils, Ashut Plastics, Ashton EPZ, Bestline Garments, and Petrocity Kanamai. Educational and institutional premises in the sector, such as the Northcoast Medical College, Holistic Education Trust, and KALRO facilities, fall within the same seven-hour outage boundary.

In Nairobi, the utility has targeted part of Parklands for maintenance. The shutoff affects sections of Masari Road, 2nd and 3rd Parklands, Baywaters, Doctors Plaza, and nearby commercial establishments.

### Broad Reach Across Eastern and Western Counties

Beyond the central leisure and commercial corridors, the Sunday schedule extends deeply into eastern and western rural hubs:

* **Kitui County:** A massive nine-hour shutdown running from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm covers Mwingi Town, Kyuso, Tseikuru, Mutito, Nguni, Mathuki, and surrounding market centers. Key public facilities caught in the outage include Mwingi Hospital and the Kiambere Water Supply scheme.
* **Kisumu County:** Two separate five-county maintenance windows from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm will affect Sondu (including Bethlehem Hospital) and Miwani (covering Miwani Boys, Gondi Oraro, and Nyangeta Police).
* **Siaya County:** Sirembe Market, Sirembe Secondary School, Ndegwe schools, and Aluor educational institutions face planned interruptions starting at 9:00 am.
* **Bungoma County:** A shorter three-hour maintenance window from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm is scheduled for Mabanga and Ndengelewa.

Kenya Power advised commercial operators and residents in all designated zones to adjust operations accordingly while technical teams carry out the routine grid updates.
