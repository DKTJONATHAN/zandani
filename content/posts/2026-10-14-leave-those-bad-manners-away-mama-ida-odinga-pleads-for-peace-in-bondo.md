Mama Ida Odinga stepped onto the turf of the Migwena Cultural and Sports Grounds in Bondo on Tuesday with one non-negotiable demand: anyone arriving with hired political hostility must leave those habits outside the gate.

Flanked by Siaya Governor James Orengo during an on-site inspection of the main venue for Thursday’s public commemoration, the matriarch of the Odinga family sought to strip the looming first anniversary of late Prime Minister Raila Odinga of the partisan theatre that often trails major gatherings in the region.

Raila passed away on October 15, 2025, at the age of 80 while undergoing treatment in India, ending an era that steered national politics for half a century. But as county planners prepare to receive tens of thousands of guests in his ancestral backyard, internal rivalries and fears of hired crowds have threatened to sour the solemnity.

### The Plea to Drop 'Bad Manners'

Walking the grounds where Thursday’s mass will take place, Ida addressed the political tensions simmering beneath the surface, reminding arriving delegations of the broad coalition her husband built throughout his life.

“Baba belonged to many people, belongs to many denominations, belonged to many parties,” Ida said. “I would plead to Kenyans that we give our visitors who have come the correct image of what Baba stood for.”

Her appeal landed just as Orengo stepped up the microphone to put suspected mobilizers on notice. Rebuffing behind-the-scenes attempts to hijack the occasion, Orengo invoked Ida's direct instructions while taking sharp aim at national actors.

“The biggest sponsors of goons in Kenya is the state, the national government, and persons and agencies working with the national government,” Orengo claimed during the tour. “I urge them, please keep them away from Raila’s great and big event. Even Mama Ida has pleaded that we leave those bad manners away from such an occasion.”

### Babu Owino Flags Heckling Plot

The calls for calm follow explicit warnings from Embakasi East MP Babu Owino, who publicly alleged that political adversaries had bankrolled disruptive factions specifically tasked with harassing him and disrupting his attendance at the Bondo service.

With political temperatures running high over Raila's political succession, the memorial lineup represents a powder keg of competing interests. While Babu Owino raised concerns over paid crowds lying in wait, regional leadership used Tuesday's final site check to draw firm red lines around conduct inside the perimeter.

County organizers are mindful that the event will draw national figures from opposing political formations who rarely share a platform without verbal barbs flying.

### Screening 20,000 Guests at Migwena

Beyond podium etiquette, Siaya County teams and national security agencies are bracing for a logistical squeeze. Over 20,000 mourners, including foreign dignitaries, diplomats, and senior government functionaries, are slated to descend on the Migwena venue.

Planners have mapped out emergency medical zones, heavy crowd-control cordons, and public health gates that include precautionary Ebola screening points in line with recent regional advisories.

The Thursday gathering at Migwena grounds follows the state-backed dedication of Raila’s mausoleum at Kang’o ka Jaramogi, an event recently led by President William Ruto.

For Ida and the local steering committee, the final hours before the gates open will center on ensuring that respect for Raila’s memory overshadows the friction of those hoping to turn his anniversary into a political brawl.
