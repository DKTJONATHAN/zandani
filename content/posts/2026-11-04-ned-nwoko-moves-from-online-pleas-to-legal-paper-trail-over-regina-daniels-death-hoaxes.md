Nigerian Senator Prince Ned Nwoko has drawn a sharp line between routine digital commentary and actionable liability, warning that online publishers peddling false health reports about his wife, Nollywood actress Regina Daniels, will face court action.

In a public statement addressing fabricated death announcements and misleading stories circulating across social platforms, the senator insisted that the threshold for tolerance had been breached. Rather than issuing another standard public appeal for discretion, Nwoko revealed that his office is actively logging specific publications to build a formal evidentiary file for legal channels.

“We are documenting these publications, and anyone found responsible will be held accountable through the appropriate legal channels,” Nwoko stated. “There is a limit to what should be tolerated, and that limit has been reached.”

The escalation follows a period of intense public scrutiny regarding Daniels’ personal life and physical well-being. On October 10, 2026, the actress marked her 25th birthday by releasing a video showing her in a wheelchair, where she detailed how a minor condition had developed into severe mobility challenges.

While the video brought her ongoing medical battles into public view against a backdrop of persistent speculation surrounding her marriage, Nwoko maintained that her recovery remains his primary concern. The senator emphasized that the fabricated narratives being traded for online engagement are inflicting unnecessary distress not only on the actress but also on their children.

“I am particularly concerned about the circulation of false and disturbing reports about her, including fabricated death announcements, misleading stories and other hurtful content,” Nwoko noted. “These things are not harmless social media engagement.”

Urging commentators to step back from judging or exploiting her health journey, the senator reiterated that his focus is entirely on securing an environment where Daniels can concentrate on rehabilitation without being subjected to continuous digital harassment.

As the documentation of targeted blog posts and social media channels continues, the legal threat establishes a rare concrete pushback by a prominent Nigerian public figure against unverified platform monetization surrounding personal health crises.
