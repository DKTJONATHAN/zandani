Brenda is no longer content with simply defending her version of events; she is actively directing the interrogation.

Speaking during an October 20, 2026 phone call with popular digital content creator 2Mbili, the former partner of Kenyan rapper Stevo Simple Boy sought to flip the prevailing public narrative surrounding their infant son, Dragon Junior. Rather than portraying the recent DNA test results as a heartbreaking surprise that shattered their relationship, Brenda maintained that the musician had been fully briefed on the child's biological status long before laboratory paperwork ever entered the public domain.

More significantly, Brenda attempted to deputise 2Mbili in real time. Instead of offering passive denials to an online audience, she challenged the presenter to confront Stevo directly with a specific, pointed question.

“Na Stevo nilikuwa nimeshamwambia kitambo. Wacha ukimuliza sasa hivi umulize Stevo, ‘Brenda aliwahi kuambia mtoto si wako?’ Atakuambia,” she told 2Mbili during the recorded conversation.

### The Claim of a Quiet Domestic Understanding

Brenda’s repeated assertion—“Nilikuwa nimemwambia kitambo”—targets the very foundation of the public discourse that has surrounded the couple. Over recent weeks, the online spectacle positioned Stevo as an unsuspecting partner discovering paternity exclusion in full view of cameras.

According to Brenda, that depiction obscures a prior, private understanding. She claimed that she had approached Stevo behind closed doors to voice her doubts and disclose the truth about Dragon Junior’s paternity. Following that private conversation, Brenda insisted, the two reached a mutual agreement: despite the absence of a biological link, they would continue raising the boy together as a shared domestic responsibility.

By presenting their arrangement as an established, settled pact, Brenda’s remarks attempt to recalibrate how fans and commentators evaluate the rapper’s subsequent public posture. If her account holds true, the formal medical testing did not reveal a hidden secret, but rather weaponised an existing domestic reality.

### The Disruption of Outside Intermediaries

During the same phone exchange, Brenda pointed toward third-party involvement as the true catalyst for the domestic breakdown. She recounted the specific moment when physical documents arrived at their residence, identifying an associate named Oscar as the person who brought the test results home.

According to Brenda, Oscar's physical arrival with the paperwork immediately triggered sharp tension within the household, transforming a previously managed domestic situation into an acute confrontation. The introduction of third parties, she indicated, effectively dismantled the quiet consensus she claims she and Stevo had forged in private.

Her detailed description of Oscar’s arrival shifts attention away from the scientific findings and toward the immediate circle surrounding the recording artist. In Brenda’s framing, the breakdown was accelerated not by biology, but by the physical presentation of results within their domestic space by outside associates.

### An Unverified Narrative in the Creator Economy

While Brenda’s statements offer an alternative timeline to the ongoing saga, her claims remain entirely unverified through independent channels. 

Stevo Simple Boy has not publicly corroborated her assertions regarding prior disclosure, nor has he confirmed that any mutual co-parenting agreement was in place before the laboratory findings surfaced. Oscar, cited by Brenda as the catalyst for the immediate household fallout, has similarly not provided a public account of his involvement.

What Brenda’s phone appearance accomplishes, however, is a strategic reorientation of Kenya’s celebrity interview circuit. By openly demanding that creators like 2Mbili act as fact-checkers rather than mere sounding boards, she has placed the ball firmly back in the rapper's court. Until Stevo answers the specific question she laid out, the dispute over who knew what—and when they knew it—remains the central fault line in the fallout.
