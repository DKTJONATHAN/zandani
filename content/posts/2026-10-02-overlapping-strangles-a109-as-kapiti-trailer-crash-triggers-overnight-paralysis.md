Aggressive lane indiscipline and widespread overlapping transformed a two-trailer collision into an immovable overnight paralysis along the Nairobi–Mombasa Highway, leaving the northern transport corridor choked for more than ten consecutive hours.

According to an operational advisory released Friday morning by the Long Distance Drivers and Conductors Association (LoDDCA), undisciplined maneuvers by impatient drivers directly frustrated initial efforts to open passage, turning what began as a localized crash site into an impenetrable barrier across three critical transport segments.

“Kapiti Traffic Update: After last night's accident, traffic has now stretched from Malili to Machakos junction and from Athi River past Machakos junction along A109,” the drivers’ lobby stated, flagging that unauthorized lane departures had multiplied the crisis.

### Dual Blockade at Kapiti Plains

The prolonged gridlock originated shortly after 9:00 p.m. on Thursday evening at Kapiti Plains, an expansive stretch of the A109 known for heavy nighttime haulage. Two heavy commercial trailers traveling in opposite directions collided head-on.

![A collage of motorists stuck in traffic along the Mombasa–Nairobi Highway near Konza following a late-night accident in Kapiti Plains, October 2, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/Cpmc7Gt4/74f0e9294fbc.webp)

The force of the impact splayed both freight vehicles across the road, sealing off both the Nairobi-bound and Mombasa-bound lanes. Because the highway along that stretch carries high volumes of heavy goods vehicles alongside long-distance passenger buses, the total obstruction immediately halted through-traffic in both directions.

Rather than queuing to allow recovery teams and emergency responders room to maneuver, oncoming vehicles began fanning out into the shoulders and oncoming tracks. By midnight, multiple lines of vehicles had formed abreast, completely eliminating the lateral space necessary for heavy towing equipment to reach the wrecked trailers.

### The Anatomy of a Corridor Lock

The resulting gridlock swiftly expanded outward from the Kapiti core, developing into a complex multi-node bottleneck that defied conventional clearing efforts.

By dawn on Friday, the tailbacks had divided into two massive, stationary columns:

![A collage of motorists stuck in traffic along the Mombasa–Nairobi Highway near Konza following a late-night accident in Kapiti Plains, October 2, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/tMXZ4Jh7/70e546751d9c.webp)

* **The Upcountry Sector:** Traffic backed up south of the collision point, creating a dense, uninterrupted line running from Malili toward the Machakos turn-off.
* **The Metropolis Approach:** North of the incident, congestion stretched outward from Athi River, pushing completely past Machakos Junction toward the Konza technopolis interchange.

Commercial truckers, cross-border cargo transporters, and public service commuter coaches spent the entire night immobilized, with engines cut and fuel reserves drained by idling systems.

### Unresolved Movement Along the A109

By daylight on Friday, movement along the vital arterial remained severely crippled. The presence of overlapping vehicles three to four abreast along unpaved shoulders meant that even when partial clearance around the wreckage was attempted, opposing streams could not navigate past one another.

The prolonged delay carries direct economic costs for logistics operators and long-haul transporters reliant on the Mombasa–Nairobi pipeline, which handles the bulk of import and export freight moving between the port and the regional hinterland.

Clearance operations were expected to extend well into Friday as transit wardens and road teams worked to untangle the knotted secondary lines at Machakos Junction before the primary crash wreckage at Kapiti could be fully removed from the carriageway.
