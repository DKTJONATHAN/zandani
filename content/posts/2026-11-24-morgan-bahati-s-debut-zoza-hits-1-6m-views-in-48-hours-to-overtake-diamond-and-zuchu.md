Morgan Bahati has made an aggressive digital entrance into the East African music scene, driving his debut single *Zoza (Nikifika 18)* directly to the number one position on YouTube’s Kenyan trending chart within two days of its release.

The song, which dropped on November 20, 2026, accumulated approximately 1.6 million views across its first 48 hours on the platform. Published directly onto the high-traffic Bahati Kenya YouTube channel, the record converted established audience traffic into immediate streaming momentum, placing the newcomer at the summit of local digital content consumption.

### Dislodging the Tanzanian Collaboration

The rapid climb of *Zoza (Nikifika 18)* pushed the debut single past *Mvua Ya Baraka*, the high-profile joint release by Tanzanian hitmakers Zuchu and Diamond Platnumz. Before Morgan’s release altered the chart order, the Tanzanian stars had held a strong grip on Kenya's trending chart after amassing millions of views across the region.

Dislodging a collaborative project from two of East Africa’s most established chart-toppers represents a notable early milestone for a debut effort. Zuchu and Diamond Platnumz entered the week with deep catalogues and large subscriber bases, making the sudden shift at the top of the chart a rare displacement by a first-time recording artist.

### Channel Velocity and Digital Impact

The track’s initial performance underlines the role of platform distribution in modern song rollouts. By launching *Zoza (Nikifika 18)* through the verified Bahati Kenya channel infrastructure, the release gained immediate placement across active subscriber feeds, accelerating its view counts past typical debut trajectories.
