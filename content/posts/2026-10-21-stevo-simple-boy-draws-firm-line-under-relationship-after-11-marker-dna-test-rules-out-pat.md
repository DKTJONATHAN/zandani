Stephen Otieno sat across from Dr. Peter Ngumba to receive the clinical document that would irrevocably alter his domestic life. Rather than letting rumors fester across social feeds, the musician known to millions as Stevo Simple Boy chose to document the consultation at Microafia Healthcare, listening in silence as the practitioner methodically dismantled his legal and biological ties to the infant known as Dragon Junior.

Dr. Ngumba’s medical breakdown left no room for ambiguity. Reviewing the laboratory profile compiled from biological material submitted under the designations Stephen Otieno Senior and Stephen Otieno Junior, the doctor cited irreconcilable genetic discrepancies across the board.

“Based on the DNA profile generated from the samples submitted of Stephen Otieno Senior and Stephen Otieno Junior, Stephen Otieno, you are not the biological father,” Dr. Ngumba explained during the recorded session. “Mismatches were observed in 11 samples tested.”

### A Documented Consultation and a Measured Exit

Armed with the laboratory verdict, the Kibera-raised recording artist wasted little time establishing a definitive boundary. On Tuesday, October 20, 2026, Stevo issued a formal communication confirming an immediate and permanent separation from his partner, Brenda.

While personal paternity disclosures in the local entertainment circuit frequently devolve into messy online squabbles, Otieno maintained an intentionally restrained posture. His public announcement avoided recrimination, seeking instead to formalize the end of the union without theatrical animosity.

“Following the DNA test results, I wish to officially announce that Brenda and I are no longer together as a couple,” Stevo stated. “The results have been difficult to process, but I have chosen to handle this matter with dignity and respect for everyone involved.”

The rapper went further to offer formal credit to the medical facility that managed the procedure, acknowledging the clinic's role in delivering clarity amid months of private uncertainty. “I sincerely appreciate Microafia Healthcare for conducting the DNA test and providing the results,” he added.

### Shielding the Music from Personal Fallout

Throughout his public career, Stevo Simple Boy has navigated acute public curiosity regarding his romantic liaisons and financial dealings. The decision to release the medical findings alongside an orderly separation notice represents a calculated effort to arrest speculation before it could eclipse his commercial output.

Recognizing the media storm typical of paternity disputes among public figures, the rapper closed his statement with an explicit appeal for distance while he recalibrates his private affairs. Yet even in the immediate wake of the laboratory findings, he insisted that his professional calendar would remain uninterrupted.

“I ask my fans and the public to respect my privacy as I navigate this chapter of my life,” Otieno said. “My focus remains on my music, my career, and building a better future.”

By laying the clinical evidence bare and announcing the dissolution of his partnership in the same breath, Otieno has effectively closed the door on the domestic chapter. His immediate schedule now pivots squarely back to the recording studio, leaving the forensic certainty of 11 mismatched markers to serve as the final word on the relationship.
