A run on over-the-counter cetirizine driven by unverified social media claims has begun disrupting pharmacy shelves across Kenya, triggering price spikes, localized inventory shortages, and an executive push to monitor drug distribution chains down to the individual counter.

The Pharmaceutical Society of Kenya (PSK) issued an advisory on Thursday, November 5, warning that widespread retail availability of the antihistamine does not guarantee safety when diverted from its manufactured purpose. The alert follows online campaigns advising women to insert oral cetirizine tablets vaginally under the false premise that the medication suppresses libido and tightens mucosal tissue.

Beyond the acute clinical dangers of the practice, the sudden surge in off-label demand has quickly spilled into the commercial supply chain. Community pharmacies are reporting stock depletions and opportunistic markups, directly penalizing chronic allergy patients who rely on the low-cost medication for daily management of hives, allergic rhinitis, and respiratory inflammation.

### Commercial Distortion at the Counter

![Cetirizine](https://i.ibb.co/Z6MSZM7S/69dc9b973699.webp)

In its formal statement, the PSK confirmed that several retail outlets have exploited the spike in foot traffic by inflating prices or reporting exhausted supplies. The professional lobby cautioned licensed chemists against predatory pricing, urging dispensing staff to safeguard baseline inventory for patients presenting with legitimate therapeutic indications.

"The society disclosed that some pharmacies had reported increased demand, higher prices and shortages of cetirizine, urging outlets to maintain ethical pricing and ensure allergy patients could still access the medicine," the PSK stated.

Cetirizine is formulated strictly as an oral tablet designed to inhibit histamine receptors throughout the body, mitigating systemic allergic responses such as sneezing, watery eyes, and dermatological flare-ups. It carries no clinical approval or pharmacological formulation for mucosal or intracavity administration.

Medical practitioners have moved quickly to counter the biological premises circulating online. The society clarified that the drying sensation produced by antihistamines stems from fluid reduction, not anatomical restructuring or pelvic muscle constriction. Far from offering physical benefits, introducing solid tablet binders and active oral compounds into the vaginal canal strips natural lubrication, creates severe friction, and leaves delicate epithelial tissue vulnerable to abrasions, chemical inflammation, and secondary bacterial infections.

### Surveillance Mechanisms Under Review

The retail disruption has escalated regulatory scrutiny inside Afya House. Speaking during a health stakeholders’ forum on Wednesday, November 4, Principal Secretary for Medical Services Ouma Oluga warned that unmonitored over-the-counter runs point to wider vulnerabilities in national drug stewardship.

Oluga indicated that the Ministry of Health is actively evaluating administrative mechanisms to track medicine purchases across the distribution pipeline. The proposed framework would monitor product movement from primary manufacturers and import warehouses through commercial wholesalers, retail chemists, and end-point consumers.

If enacted, such digital supply tracking would represent a sharp transition for Kenya’s high-street pharmacy sector, where basic antihistamines and common maintenance drugs have historically circulated with minimal point-of-sale friction.

For now, the PSK has directed individuals who have experimented with vaginal insertion to cease use immediately and seek professional clinical care if they display warning signs, including pelvic pain, localized burning, abnormal bleeding, discharge, or systemic fever. Health workers have been instructed to receive affected patients without administrative or social judgment to contain preventable complications before regulatory enforcement tightens at retail counters.
