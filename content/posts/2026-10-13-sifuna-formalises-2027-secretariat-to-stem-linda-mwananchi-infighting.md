Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna has moved to institutionalise his 2027 State House bid, assembling a formal presidential campaign secretariat to stabilise the Linda Mwananchi Movement following a disruptive spell of internal wrangling.

The restructuring, ratified during a high-level leadership review on Monday, October 12, signals an operational shift away from spontaneous public rallies toward a disciplined, bureaucratic campaign structure. To steer that pivot, the movement appointed former Jubilee Party Deputy Organising Secretary Pauline Njoroge as interim head of communications, placing veteran establishment expertise at the centre of Sifuna’s nascent political machinery.

### Transition From Rallies to Institutional Machinery

The move to set up an administrative hub follows an abrupt suspension of Linda Mwananchi’s countrywide tour. Despite drawing solid crowds during regional outings, the political outfit was forced to pause grassroots engagements amid reports of escalating internal disputes.

![Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna during the unveiling of TEP as the political party to vie for the presidency in 2027](https://i.ibb.co/cSyxdtvL/386a028e4e5c.webp)

Rather than pushing ahead with unstructured roadshows, the leadership opted to retreat indoors to calibrate the organisation’s framework. According to a movement notice issued Tuesday, October 13, the leadership's deliberations concentrated on party rebranding and laying the procedural bedrock of the presidential campaign secretariat.

Sifuna also consolidated his electoral identity by formally adopting The Equitable Party (TEP) as his designated political vehicle, moving to insulate his candidacy from factional volatility within the broader Linda Mwananchi tent.

### Seasoned Operatives at the Digital Helm

Njoroge’s appointment brings tested campaign pedigree to an operation seeking to transition from protest politics to executive positioning. She resigned from Jubilee’s National Executive Committee on July 21 after a 14-year tenure, immediately aligning herself with Sifuna’s presidential quest.

![Pauline Njoroge, standing alongside Embakasi East MP Babu Owino, amongst other leaders, during a Linda Mwananchi Rally in Taita Taveta County on July 18.](https://i.ibb.co/pjjSpMNf/702362597ac6.webp)

Her resume includes strategic roles in past presidential election cycles and a senior communications assignment on the secretariat of the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga during his 2025 African Union Commission chairperson candidacy.

Alongside Njoroge, the movement named Fredah Onduso, Abigail Gikonyo, and Charity Waweru to lead its dedicated digital, media, and publicity arm. The three strategists have spent months driving Sifuna’s narrative across online networks, and their formal induction integrates online advocacy directly into the campaign’s official decision-making chain.

### The Operational Road to 2027

By putting an organised media and administrative team in place before resuming public rallies, Sifuna is seeking to establish central clearance over campaignmessaging and ground operations.

Linda Mwananchi confirmed that additional appointments across other functional departments will follow as the campaign fleshes out its full national footprint. The immediate operational test will rest on whether the secretariat can unify divergent internal factions and construct a cohesive national outreach schedule ahead of the 2027 General Election.
