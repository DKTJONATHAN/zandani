Safaricom users across Kenya can now register, deposit, play, and withdraw completely offline by dialing *457# as gaming platform Pepeta rolls out its holiday acquisition strategy.

The campaign, titled the Bado Tunapepeta Festive Giveaway, runs from November 11 through December 31, 2026. Rather than relying solely on smartphone-dependent apps, the platform has integrated direct USSD and Cashia wallet routing to capture users who face data bundle limitations during the festive travel and holiday period.

Participation requires a minimum stake of Sh30 on qualifying Aviator cash bets, which automatically entries players into daily and weekly leaderboards alongside physical prize draws. The operational structure removes traditional withdrawal penalties, offering zero fees on payouts with a low minimum limit of Sh50.

Beyond digital wallets and USSD strings, the platform has partnered with M-Pesa via Paybill 779900 to handle transaction traffic. New registrants are channeled into welcome bonuses that include initial Aviator offers and Aviatrix free bets upon sign-up.

The physical inventory attached to the campaign spans multiple consumer electronics and household goods categories. Grand prize draws include 65-inch Smart TVs, PlayStation 5 consoles, high-output sound bars, double-door refrigerators, gas and electric standing cookers, microwave ovens, electric kettles, and steam iron boxes.

Simultaneously, the daily leaderboard rewards players achieving the highest multipliers between 00:00 and 23:59 hours with cash payouts of up to Sh10,000 daily, alongside hundreds of smaller Sh500 and Sh200 freebets. Weekly payouts running Thursday through Friday target players accumulating the most qualifying bets at odds of 2.0x or higher, distributing cash sums up to Sh50,000 alongside physical commodities. In the event of a tie, the platform defaults to the player with the higher cumulative cash stake.

Betting participation remains restricted to individuals aged 18 and over under standard regulatory guidelines.
