Internal Security Principal Secretary Raymond Omollo chose a public empowerment forum in Embu County to drop a direct home-turf challenge, placing his colleague, Youth Affairs Principal Secretary Pius Moria, at the center of the local youth agenda.

Sharing the platform with Moria, Omollo framed the Youth Affairs PS as Embu’s direct link to national policy, telling residents that the county has no rationale for lagging behind in government-funded technical and vocational training.

“It would never make sense for your own son, Pius, to lead the national charge in uplifting Kenyan youth while young people right here in Embu miss out on schooling and skills training,” Omollo told the rally.

### Redefining the Political Role of Youth

During his address, Omollo argued that national planning has intentionally shifted away from using young people as political background figures and toward structured socio-economic planning.

He noted that the current administration has placed education, job creation, and healthcare at the middle of its long-term strategy.

“For the first time in our nation’s history, we have a leader who recognizes that our young people are not meant to simply trail behind politicians—we must intentionally plan for their education, their employment, and their healthcare,” Omollo stated.

### Expanding TVET Market-Ready Training

Omollo commended Moria for steering structural reforms within the Technical and Vocational Education and Training (TVET) sector, which aim to supply young Kenyans with practical, market-aligned skill sets.

Yet Omollo underscored that nationwide policy gains require aggressive local adoption to produce tangible community results.

By spotlighting Moria’s role on his home turf, Omollo pressed Embu families and regional leaders to push young people into nearby TVET institutions, positioning enrollment as a direct measure of local benefit from top-level government appointments.

Omollo maintained that the government’s commitment to expanding access across all counties remains active, calling on Embu youth to take full advantage of the technical programs to secure economic independence.
