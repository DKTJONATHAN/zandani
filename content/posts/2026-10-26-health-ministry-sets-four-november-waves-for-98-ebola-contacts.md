Kenya’s public health authorities have established a four-phase exit calendar beginning November 3 to clear the remaining 98 monitored Ebola contacts, anchoring their containment strategy on laboratory data that confirms all analyzed cases belong to a single imported viral strain.

This operational framework follows the discharge on Monday of an initial cohort of 20 individuals who completed their mandatory 21-day quarantine without developing symptoms. Director-General for Health Patrick Amoth confirmed that all 20 individuals met clinical release criteria after remaining completely asymptomatic throughout the virus's maximum incubation window.

### Genomic Sequencing Confirms Single DRC Lineage

![Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale, during a meeting at Afya House, with an inset of an Ebola Virus Bundibugyo Strain under a microscope.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/aden-duale-ebola-virus.jpg.webp?itok=0ojPUx92)

Laboratory tests conducted on initial patient samples have linked three sequenced cases directly to the Bundibugyo virus lineage originating in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). While genomic analysis remains underway for six additional diagnostic samples, current laboratory findings show that transmission has remained strictly confined to individuals connected to the primary imported case—a Kenyan national who arrived from the DRC through Uganda undetected on October 3.

Ministry of Health metrics show national figures standing at nine confirmed cases. With two recorded fatalities, the case fatality rate currently sits at 22.2 per cent. The remaining seven patients are receiving specialized care in isolation units, where clinicians report their conditions as stable.

### Staggered Discharges for 98 Remaining Contacts

To prevent monitoring lapses and manage community reintegration, health authorities are staggering the release of the remaining 98 quarantined individuals into four distinct waves rather than conducting a single blanket exit. The first wave under this phase will begin exiting surveillance facilities on November 3, with subsequent groups following as each individual reaches the mandatory 21-day post-exposure benchmark.

![A photo collage of a test tube labelled ebola and Nairobi in the background, October 14, 2026.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/ebola-nairobi-october-2026.jpg.webp?itok=lDxNh9FY)

To mitigate social friction and mental fatigue associated with prolonged isolation, mental health professionals have been assigned to provide psychosocial counseling to returning individuals, their families, and frontline healthcare personnel. Official directives issued to local administrative structures specifically instruct communities against discriminating against individuals returning from quarantine units.

### The 21-Day Clearance Window

Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale stated that if current contact suppression protocols hold without any new unlinked transmission chains emerging, Kenya will meet the World Health Organization’s 42-day standard—representing two full 21-day incubation cycles—to declare the outbreak fully resolved.

With laboratory workers finalizing genomic sequencing on the remaining six diagnostic samples and the secondary contact cohort entering its final monitoring phase, the ministry's immediate focus remains on executing the November 3 discharge wave while preserving strict zero-spillover controls across all isolation centers.
