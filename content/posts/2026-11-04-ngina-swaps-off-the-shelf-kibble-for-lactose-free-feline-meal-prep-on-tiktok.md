Content creator Ngina has taken her audience behind the scenes of her domestic routine, documenting a step-by-step kitchen assembly for her cat's dinner on TikTok. Rather than relying solely on packaged commercial pet food, the creator held her feline companion while preparing a customized meal from scratch, bringing specialized pet care into her daily digital video updates.

The clip posted to her account captures the food preparation process on her kitchen counter. In the video, Ngina mixes prepared ingredients inside a dedicated bowl alongside specialized components, including lactose-free milk selected specifically for feline digestion. A bottle labeled ‘Cooking With Love’ features prominently during the assembly, adding a personalized element to the evening routine.

The setup marks a clear departure from standard pet feeding habits across many households, where pre-packaged commercial kibble or basic table scraps remain the default. By highlighting dietary adjustments like lactose-free dairy—addressing the natural digestive sensitivity cats have to standard cow's milk—Ngina's routine brings focus to nutritional intentionality in everyday pet care.

Her video highlights a broader shift within Kenyan urban households, where domestic animals are increasingly integrated into daily family dynamics and creator content. As companion animals take on more central roles in home life, digital focus has gradually expanded beyond basic pet ownership to include deliberate dietary planning, comfort management, and public discussions surrounding animal welfare standards.
