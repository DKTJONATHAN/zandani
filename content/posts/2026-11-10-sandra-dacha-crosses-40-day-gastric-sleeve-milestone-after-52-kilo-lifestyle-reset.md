Sandra Dacha has reached the 40-day mark of her recovery following Vertical Sleeve Gastrectomy surgery, showcasing early physical changes after permanently removing approximately 75 percent of her stomach.

The comedian and former *Auntie Boss!* actress, widely known for playing Silprosa, framed the milestone not as an isolated quick fix, but as the second phase of an intensive personal health overhaul. Before stepping into the operating room on September 30, 2026, Dacha had already engineered a major physical transition through non-surgical methods, reducing her weight from 152 kilograms to 100 kilograms through strict lifestyle modifications.

Evaluating her progress six weeks post-operation, Dacha confirmed that the structural changes to her digestive system have yielded immediate visual results.

"40 days post-op and the difference is differencing!" Dacha stated. "Today marks 40 days since I underwent Vertical Sleeve Gastrectomy, popularly known as Gastric Sleeve surgery. What a journey it has been! The changes in my body are already so visible, and I honestly couldn’t be happier."

### The Shift to Surgical Intervention

The decision to undergo bariatric surgery followed weeks of public speculation after Dacha posted video footage showing herself dressed in a surgical gown being wheeled into an operating theatre. The unannounced clip prompted widespread concern across her fan base before she formally clarified the medical procedure.

Reflecting on the psychological weight of entering the surgical unit, Dacha acknowledged the vulnerability behind the public persona.

"That emotional moment was real. I was scared, overwhelmed, hopeful and uncertain about what lay ahead," she revealed. "It was a big decision, and I knew I was stepping into a new chapter of my life. This is more than just losing weight—it’s about reclaiming my confidence, prioritising my health and embracing a new chapter of my life."

### Clinical Mechanics of the Sleeve

![Former KTN Child Actor Junior Reappears in US as Truck Driver](https://i.ibb.co/GQBNs5fj/d00196e9e55c.webp)

Vertical Sleeve Gastrectomy (VSG) is a permanent, restrictive bariatric procedure in which surgeons resect 75% to 80% of the stomach along its greater curvature. The remaining tissue is stapled into a vertical, banana-shaped sleeve.

The procedure alters appetite regulation through two distinct mechanisms:
* **Mechanical restriction:** The significantly smaller stomach pouch physically limits food intake during meals, causing patients to experience early satiety.
* **Hormonal reduction:** Removing the outer curvature of the stomach drastically cuts the production of ghrelin, the primary hormone responsible for triggering hunger pangs.

For Dacha, combining this physiological shift with her prior 52-kilogram baseline loss forms the foundation for sustained weight management.

Looking beyond the initial recovery window, the actress emphasized that navigating the post-op phase requires ongoing discipline rather than immediate finality.

"40 days down, a lifetime of transformation ahead," Dacha said. "One day at a time, one kilo at a time."
