Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna announced a broad executive redistribution for the Linda Mwananchi Movement Party on Friday, October 30, structuring the nascent outfit around four deputy party leaders and distributing frontline operational desks to cross-coalition political figures.

Speaking at the formal unveiling of the party's national secretariat in Nairobi, Sifuna outlined an administrative architecture designed to balance regional constituencies before the slate faces ratification at an upcoming National Delegates Conference (NDC).

Under the proposed hierarchy, former Meru Governor Kawira Mwangaza and Mavoko MP Patrick Makau secure two of the four deputy party leader slots. Sifuna also assigned a deputy position to former Law Society of Kenya (LSK) President Faith Odhiambo, leaving a fourth deputy mantle open for a designated representative from the Coast region.

"We will be proposing that we structure the party so that we have a party leader and four deputies," Sifuna stated during the secretariat launch. "For now, we have resolved that Kawira Mwangaza has decided to be one of the party leaders."

### Strategic Desks Handed to Regional Allies

The organizational engine of the outfit places Vihiga Senator Godfrey Osotsi at the helm as national chairperson, backed by former Makadara legislator Reuben Ndolo as his national deputy. 

The administrative hub went to Suba South MP Caroli Omondi, who was proposed as Secretary General, paired with political commentator Pauline Njoroge as his deputy. The treasury docket was allocated to Kisii Senator Richard Onyonka, who will be deputised by Abigael Gikonyo.

The strategic alignment also incorporates figures crossing over from established partisan platforms. Former Narok Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) chairperson Torome Nalengoyo was fronted to steer the minority affairs docket, while Kari Karani was assigned the Organising Secretary portfolio and Francy Kadide took over the Secretary of Legal Affairs desk.

![Senator Edwin Sifuna poses for a photo with LSK President Faith Odhiambo on Friday, October 30, 2026.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/sifuna-and-faith-odhiambo.jpg.webp?itok=v2I6ag2r)

### Electoral Machinery and League Alignments

Beyond internal party governance, Sifuna explicitly tied the new lineup to forthcoming electoral ambitions, tapping Kakamega Senator Boni Khalwale as Secretary for Political Affairs while affirming that Khalwale will mount his bid for the Kakamega gubernatorial seat under the Linda Mwananchi banner.

The party’s demographic desks were designated to Caroline Mayuzu, who takes charge of the women’s league, and Fridah Onduso, proposed to lead the youth league. External liaison networks were assigned to party co-founder Eunice Juma as secretary for diaspora affairs and Gilbert Shanga as secretary for international affairs.

The entire executive roster now heads to the Linda Mwananchi Movement’s National Delegates Conference, where delegates will vote on whether to adopt the multi-deputy constitution and substantively confirm the proposed slate.
