Trisha Khalid has officially stepped into the primary co-hosting chair for Season 5 of *Hello Mr Right*, taking over the microphone alongside veteran anchor Dr. Ofweneke as the Kenyan dating reality show prepares its next round of speed dates and chemistry tests.

The formal handoff reconfigures the show's main stage presentation, placing the actress, model, and digital creator in the seat previously held by internet personality Diana Marua (Diana Bahati). The shift brings a fresh on-screen dynamic to the long-running production, which built its audience around unscripted romantic negotiations and debate over contemporary dating standards.

Dr. Ofweneke formally unveiled Khalid as his new on-stage partner on Tuesday, October 6, 2026, confirming that production is moving forward with the revised talent lineup for the upcoming season.

"Officially presenting to you my co-host for *Hello Mr Right* Season 5, Trisha Khalid," Ofweneke announced as the show's production team cleared the studio floor for initial taping preparation.

### Strategic Anchor Shift

The anchor room change comes as the production team signals an intentional push toward higher drama and sharper banter on set. In an official welcome statement, the show's producers explicitly pointed toward an elevated energy level for the fifth cycle.

"Hii Season 5 si itawaka moto jamani! Congratulations, Trisha Khalid! Welcome to the *Hello Mr Right* Season 5 family. We can’t wait for all the drama!" the show's production desk stated.

Resident relationship expert Getrude Mungai, who remains a central analytical voice on the panel, framed Khalid’s arrival as a key structural reset for how dating conversations will play out on camera.

"Hello Mr Right Kenya Season 5 is here! I’m so excited to welcome Trisha Khalid as the new host of the show," Mungai shared following the announcement. "Trisha, karibu sana to the *Hello Mr Right* family."

Mungai noted that the pairing of Khalid and Ofweneke is expected to drive sharper chemistry and direct exchanges during contestant speed-dating rounds, where singles face immediate elimination or endorsement based on stage interactions.

### Navigating the Speed-Dating Stage

Over four completed seasons, *Hello Mr Right* has established itself as a central platform for Kenyan relationship discourse, using rapid-fire introduction rounds, compatibility questionnaires, and staged pressure tests to evaluate romantic prospects.

Khalid’s entry marks another evolution in the co-host role, following Diana Marua’s tenure in the previous edition. While Marua brought her established lifestyle vlogging footprint to the set, Khalid enters with a background spanning screen acting, commercial modeling, and lifestyle content creation.

The co-host in the *Hello Mr Right* format serves as both a counterweight to Dr. Ofweneke’s high-energy comedic moderation and a direct advocate for female contestants navigating the stage. The role requires quick improvisation as single participants reveal personal preferences, financial expectations, and deal-breakers in real time.

With production now officially underway for Season 5, the new hosting team of Dr. Ofweneke and Trisha Khalid is set to guide the roster of single participants through the show's signature gauntlet of chemistry evaluations and open discussions.
