Parents discharging newborns from hospitals contracted by the Social Health Authority (SHA) will soon bypass physical paperwork under a digital birth registration rollout scheduled for completion within three months.

Immigration and Citizen Services Principal Secretary Belio Kipsang confirmed that the Interior Ministry has finalized arrangements with the Ministry of Health to integrate hospital management systems with civil registration databases. The transition aims to eliminate traditional paper notifications that have long frustrated families who risk losing critical documents before securing formal birth certificates.

![Immigration PS Dr. Belio Kipsang, during an event on Tuesday, December 16, 2025](https://i.ibb.co/6JLnH1RT/1106c09b9ad1.webp)

“We already finalised our engagement with the Ministry of Health, and we shall be rolling out across all our hospitals that benefit from the SHA,” Kipsang said on Tuesday, October 6. “So everybody who benefits from SHA will be benefiting from the process of E-notification.”

The upcoming system captures birth details directly at health facilities, transmitting data electronically to civil registration authorities. This digital handshake forms the foundation for issuing a Unique Personal Identifier (UPI), a core component of the state’s broader push to connect identity records across administrative sectors.

![Photo collage of a birth certificate on a table and people lining up to access government services](https://i.ibb.co/Lzp950hj/1f53fb903b7e.webp)

Beyond hospital wards, the Ministry of Education is cleaning and digitizing student databases to merge them with the UPI framework. According to the Immigration department, once learners are successfully integrated into the system, individuals will have direct digital access to download and print their birth certificates without visiting Huduma Kenya or civil registry offices.

The administrative overhaul places a tight operational deadline on health facilities, which must synchronize their internal record-keeping with state civil databases before the December rollout concludes.
