According to a statutory intake circular issued by the Public Service Commission on Tuesday, recent university and technical college graduates across the built-environment professions stand to secure 6,000 structured workplace placements under the national Affordable Housing Programme.

The deployment, commissioned through the Ministry of Lands, Public Works, Housing and Urban Development's State Department for Housing and Urban Development, establishes the second cohort of technical interns intended to supervise, design, and manage housing developments across the 47 counties.

### Quotas Across Degree and Technical Cadres

Rather than restricting recruitment exclusively to university degree holders, the commission’s distribution schedule allocates substantial room for middle-level technical qualification holders. Out of the 6,000 openings, over 850 positions are reserved directly for diploma holders seeking entry into the construction trades.

![Public Service Commission House](https://i.ibb.co/zWK3MgbZ/e3b7a9608d4b.webp)

Graduates holding bachelor's degrees take the largest individual shares across professional disciplines:

* **Construction Management:** 500 vacancies
* **Architecture:** 475 vacancies
* **Mechanical Engineering:** 350 vacancies
* **Quantity Surveying:** 300 vacancies
* **Structural Engineering:** 300 vacancies
* **Health and Safety:** 300 vacancies

Diploma-level applicants have been allocated 330 openings for architectural assistants, 300 vacancies for quantity surveying assistants, and 225 slots for structural assistants. Additional vacancies span civil engineering, electrical engineering, land surveying, valuation, and urban planning cadres.

### Five-Year Graduation Threshold and Service Conditions

Under the guidelines confirmed by the commission, prospective recruits face tight baseline criteria designed to capture early-career entrants while locking out career interns. Candidates must hold a diploma or degree conferred in 2020 or later by an accredited institution, possess demonstrated computer proficiency, and show no prior record of having undertaken an equivalent state-funded internship.

![Kenyans queue for Jobs in Nairobi.](https://i.ibb.co/6c540prZ/7be4a390fe57.webp)

Successful candidates will be contracted under a 12-month, non-renewable term. During this year-long engagement, recruits will carry out field duties designated by supervising resident engineers and project officers alongside structured mentorship modules.

The interns will draw a consolidated monthly stipend pegged to prevailing state rates for public service interns, culminating in formal certificates of completion upon the conclusion of the 12 months.

### Tight November Application Window

The commission confirmed that all submissions must be filed digitally through its recruitment portal, setting a hard deadline of Monday, November 9, 2026, for candidates seeking inclusion in the second cohort.

The housing intake coincides with a parallel recruitment drive at the Independent Policing Oversight Authority (IPOA). The police civilian oversight body announced 41 permanent and pensionable openings across its operational units, including four investigations officers, three legal officers, three complaints management officers, and eight office assistants, alongside auxiliary vacancies for security officers, drivers, and clerical staff.
