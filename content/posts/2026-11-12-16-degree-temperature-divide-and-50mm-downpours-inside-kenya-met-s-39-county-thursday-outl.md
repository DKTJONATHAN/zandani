A 16-degree Celsius daytime temperature divide and localized downpours measuring up to 50 millimeters define Thursday’s weather across Kenya, as the Kenya Meteorological Department issues rainfall advisories covering Nairobi and 38 neighboring counties.

While Turkana experiences scorching daytime highs of 37 degrees Celsius, high-altitude stretches in Nyandarua and Samburu face chilly peaks of 21 degrees Celsius alongside heavy afternoon thunderstorms moving across the Rift Valley and Northern Kenya.

### Capital Shift and Central Cloud Cover

In Nairobi, early morning conditions presented a mix of sunny intervals and isolated light rain before an expected afternoon transition toward moderate downpours and electrical storms over several parts of the capital.

Surrounding regions across the Central Highlands remained under dense cloud cover through the morning hours, mirroring conditions in Eastern Kenya where Meru, Tharaka-Nithi, Embu, and Machakos recorded overcast skies and early light showers.

### Rift Valley Downpour Intensity

The most intense precipitation is forecast to strike the Rift Valley as afternoon heat builds. Trans Nzoia is targeted for heavy rainfall, with meteorological tracking classifying expected totals between 21 and 50 millimeters accompanied by frequent thunderstorms.

Neighboring agricultural hubs—including Nakuru, Narok, Kericho, and Baringo—are set to experience moderate afternoon showers and thunderstorms. Uasin Gishu and Nandi are expected to record lighter afternoon precipitation.

### Northern Belt and Lake Basin Activity

Northern Kenya faced early wet conditions, with Wajir, Mandera, Marsabit, and Turkana logging steady rainfall measured between 5 and 20 millimeters alongside early morning thunderstorms. Wet conditions across Wajir and Mandera are projected to persist throughout the afternoon.

Similar early-day volatility extended across the Lake Victoria Basin and Western Kenya. Homa Bay, Migori, Kisii, Nyamira, Bungoma, Kakamega, and Vihiga logged morning sunny spells interspersed with light rain and localized thunder.

### Coastal Strip Exception

By contrast, the Coastal strip remains the primary bright exception across the nation. Mombasa, Kwale, Kilifi, and Lamu logged clear sunny intervals throughout the morning, while Tana River recorded isolated light showers amidst extended sunny spells.

The only notable coastal breakdown is scheduled for Taita Taveta, where light showers and localized thunderstorms are anticipated near the base of Mount Kilimanjaro as afternoon weather systems consolidate.
