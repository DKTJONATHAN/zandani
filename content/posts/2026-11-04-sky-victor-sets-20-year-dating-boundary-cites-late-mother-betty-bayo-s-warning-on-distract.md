Teenage content creator Sky Victor is relying on explicit advice inherited from her late mother, gospel singer Betty Bayo, to navigate her rising public profile and structure her personal boundaries.

Addressing recurring inquiries regarding her relationship status, the daughter of Bayo and preacher Victor Kanyari reiterated that romantic relationships remain completely off her agenda. Instead, she is holding fast to maternal instructions issued before her mother's passing, treating those conversations as an active blueprint for her daily life.

"And even my mum has always been telling me boys will waste your time. And I am listening to her even when she is not here," Sky explained while reflecting on her choices.
