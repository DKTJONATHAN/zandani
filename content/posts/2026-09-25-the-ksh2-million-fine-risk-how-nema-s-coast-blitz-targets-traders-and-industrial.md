Commercial operators across the Coast region are confronting a sharp tightening of environmental controls as the National Environment Management Authority (NEMA) combines street-level arrests with mandatory digital licensing audits.

The multi-agency operation, launched on Friday, September 25, exposes small-scale distributors of contraband plastic flat bags to severe statutory penalties while placing industrial users on notice to regularise their packaging operations online.

### Crackdown at the Coast

During the Coast operation, multi-sector teams detained five traders found in possession of prohibited plastic carrier and flat bags. The enforcement sweep targeted supply points and retail stalls that continue to distribute banned single-use plastics across coastal urban centers.

![A photo showing packaging materials inside a factory.](https://i.ibb.co/mC81NvCV/38db6c6e4a10.webp)

Simultaneously, environmental inspectors conducted audits on local factories and processing facilities licensed to use plastic materials for specific industrial packaging. Non-compliant firms discovered operating outside approved environmental parameters were directed to immediately submit applications through NEMA's online licensing portal.

While Kenya maintains a strict ban on general plastic carrier and flat bags, statutory exceptions exist for specialized industrial packaging, provided manufacturers obtain formal authorization and adhere to disposal protocols.

### The Exposure Under EMCA Section 144

At the center of NEMA's renewed blitz is the strict penalty structure set out under Section 144 of the Environmental Management and Coordination Act (EMCA) Cap 387. The law makes no legal distinction between large-scale importers and small-scale possessors of unapproved flat bags.

Under Section 144, anyone who manufactures, imports, exports, uses, sells, offers for sale, or possesses banned plastic carrier or flat bags without a valid licence faces:

* Statutory fines ranging between Ksh2 million and Ksh4 million.
* Imprisonment for a term of no less than one year and up to four years.
* A combination of both the monetary fine and custodial sentence.

![NEMA environmental inspectors and Police Unit undertaking surveillance and enforcement on noise pollution in Nairobi's Kilimani area, October 24, 2024.](https://i.ibb.co/chCY4H3R/404ce21d4029.webp)

This legal framework establishes one of the highest statutory fine ratios for retail contraband in the region, designed to render the illicit plastic bag trade commercially unviable.

### The Industrial Portal Ultimatum

Beyond market sweeps, NEMA's operational shift targets industrial packagers who rely on regulatory exemptions. Facilities utilizing flat bags for internal factory processes, bulk agriculture, or primary goods protection must maintain active certification.

Unlicensed commercial users have been instructed to utilize NEMA’s digital portal to bring their operations into compliance before further enforcement raids occur. The regulator noted that operating an industrial packaging line without active portal documentation constitutes an immediate offense under EMCA.

NEMA confirmed that the Coast blitz marks the beginning of an expanded nationwide surveillance campaign. Commercial distributors and industrial operators across other regions face ongoing spot-checks as authorities attempt to eliminate remaining distribution networks for non-compliant packaging.
