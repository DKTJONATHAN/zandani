Kiharu Member of Parliament Ndindi Nyoro has thrown down a personal challenge to President William Ruto, offering himself up as the Head of State’s political sparring partner while demanding an end to public attacks on the country's senior elder statesmen.

Speaking during a Sunday church service in Kisii County on November 15, 2026, the People’s Party of Kenya (PPK) leader framed President Ruto’s recent rally speeches as an unnecessary assault on retired and ousted leaders, urging him to redirect his rhetoric toward a younger generation of politicians.

Nyoro took direct issue with the language being used against top political figures from the pulpit and political podiums, singling out the treatment of former President Uhuru Kenyatta, former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua, and former Interior Cabinet Secretary Dr. Fred Matiang’i.

“Stop disrespecting President Uhuru Kenyatta, stop disrespecting Rigathi Gachagua, stop disrespecting Fred Matiang’i,” Nyoro stated. “These are senior figures in the political scene who deserve respect. You chased Gachagua away, leave him alone.”

### Shielding the Elders on Home Turf

Addressing the congregation, the Kiharu lawmaker argued that as the country’s designated father of the nation, President Ruto ought to maintain presidential decorum during public addresses rather than deploying derogatory remarks against retired or sidelined leaders.

Instead of trading barbs with senior figures, Nyoro challenged the President to take on younger active politicians—specifically putting his own name forward as an available target for executive political exchanges.

The public rebuke was delivered alongside a notable show of cross-party solidarity. Nyoro shared the platform in Kisii with Jubilee Party Leader Dr. Fred Matiang’i and Siaya Governor James Orengo, turning the church visit into a joint public showcase.

Taking advantage of being in Matiang’i’s home territory, Nyoro used his time at the podium to pitch the former Interior CS to the local congregation, telling Gusii region residents that Matiang’i possesses the administrative track record and capability required to lead Kenya.

### Opposition 2027 Lineup Takes Shape

Beyond Nyoro’s direct challenge to the presidency, the Kisii gathering provided a stage for opposition leaders to outline their collective strategic moves ahead of the next general election cycle.

Siaya Governor James Orengo confirmed to the congregation that various opposition factions are actively consolidating their ranks, with ongoing discussions centered on fielding a single, unified presidential candidate to challenge President Ruto in 2027.

Nyoro’s posture in Kisii marks a deliberate shift into broader national coalition-building, aligning his PPK platform with established opposition heavyweights while stepping up as an outspoken buffer between the current administration and its political predecessors.
