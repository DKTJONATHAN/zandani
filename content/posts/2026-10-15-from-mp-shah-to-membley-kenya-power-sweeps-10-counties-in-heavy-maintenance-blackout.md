Kenya Power rolled out an expansive electricity maintenance sweep across ten counties on Wednesday, October 14, 2026, temporarily disconnecting key medical corridors, expansive gated communities, and vital agricultural plants for up to nine hours.

The scheduled utility maintenance, running between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm, reached far beyond ordinary residential suburbs. The shutdown interrupted power across commercial and institutional lifelines in Nairobi, Kiambu, Kajiado, Kericho, Nyeri, Meru, Murang’a, Embu, Kirinyaga, and Kitui counties, forcing facilities from private referral hospitals to cooperative coffee mills to manage extended periods off the national grid.

### Urban Medical Corridors and Gated Suburbs

In Nairobi, the utility’s maintenance crews isolated major segments of Parklands and the Thika Superhighway commuter zone. The shutdown hit the busy Parklands medical and commercial belt, taking in MP Shah Hospital, Parklands Police Station, the Mayfair area, and adjacent properties along Forest Road, Ojijo Road, Mpaka Road, Crescent Road, and Taarifa Road.

On the city’s northern edge, power was switched off around Safaripark, Roasters, and Mukima Drive 8. Across the county line in Kiambu, the blackout enveloped the entirety of Membley Estate alongside Fort Jesus Ruiru, Jack Ridge School, Wataalam, Githunguri Ranch, Gitambaya, and regional educational centers including Wood Creek and Marion School near Kamiti Corner and Mugumo.

Kajiado County faced disruptions concentrated around Ngong Town, severing grid access for residents and businesses across Lemiso, Gichagi, Abadarengere, Olemontet, Booster Road, and institutional facilities at KHIBT, stretching into Kimuka, Saikeri, and Maroroi.

### Pressure on Health Facilities and Regional Cash Crops

The maintenance operation created significant exposure for rural health infrastructure and agricultural processors across Central Kenya. In Nyeri County’s Karatina hub, the power cut switched off mains electricity to both Jamii Hospital and Karatina Sub-County Hospital, while affecting municipal water pumping operations at Mawasco and primary communication infrastructure hosting Safaricom and Airtel transmission boosters in Saigon and Ragati.

Nyeri’s agricultural processing nodes around Kirimara Coffee Factory and the Kirimara Water Pump were similarly shut down during the daytime cycle. 

In neighboring Kirinyaga County, the outage centered on the Kutus administrative and commercial strip. The cut struck the Kirinyaga County Headquarters in Kutus, the Kirinyaga Coffee Mills, and trading centers across Kimicha, Spenza, Kiaga, and sections of Kagio and Gakindu.

Embu County absorbed comparable industrial disruptions in the Runyenjes area, where the maintenance schedule suspended power to the Runyenjes Kenya Co-operative Creameries (KCC) Plant, the Kang’ondu Coffee Factory, the Runyenjes District Officer’s offices, and Gikuuri Health Centre alongside local telecom booster installations at Kang'ondu and Gichera.

### Rift Valley and Eastern Distribution Networks

The utility's technical teams executed parallel maintenance across Rift Valley farming nodes and Eastern trading centers. In Kericho County, the line disconnection covered Koitaburot, West Valley, Kejiriet, Kipsitet Girls Secondary School, and market facilities at Kipsitet and Koyaibei.

Meru County’s northern transit and agricultural belt saw extensive switch-offs touching Mutuati, Laare, Tuuru Mission, Kambi Kairanya, Antubetwe Kiongo, and Karama Market. In Murang’a County, power cuts interrupted daily trade through Mukurwe wa Nyagathanga, Kahigaini, Gaither, Kiambugi Market, and Gaturi CCM. Further east in Kitui County, lines were decommissioned across rural networks in Mwambiu, Nyaani, Kalua, Ikoo, Mutito, Itiko, and Masasini.

Kenya Power advised affected domestic and commercial accounts across the ten counties to monitor their connection status and log service requests through its automated USSD code, *977#, as field teams completed the scheduled transformer servicing and line reinforcement work across the regional networks.
