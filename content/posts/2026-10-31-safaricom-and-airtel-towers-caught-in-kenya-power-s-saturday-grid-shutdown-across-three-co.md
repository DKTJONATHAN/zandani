When the lights go out for routine grid repairs this Saturday, October 31, 2026, the silence won't just affect coffee factories and residential kitchens—it will hit the digital infrastructure keeping regional mobile users connected.

While routine power interruptions are a standard feature of utility maintenance across the country, Saturday’s gazetted schedule lays bare the acute dependence of corporate telecommunications networks on the national grid. In Nyeri County, where outages are scheduled to run from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, major Safaricom and Airtel booster stations sit directly inside the blackout zone.

The disruption in Nyeri sweeps through Miiri and Kiangai markets, pulling the plug on local landmarks including Miiri Coffee Factory, Kiangai Coffee Factory, Thunguri Coffee Factory, and surrounding residential pockets like Kianyange, Karerahungu, and Kiahiti villages. Alongside these agricultural and trading nodes, the mobile network booster stations servicing the area will go dark for the nine-hour window, testing the backup generator capacity of Kenya's premier telecommunications operators.

Meanwhile, the utility's engineering teams have lined up parallel interventions in southern and western regions. In Kajiado County, two distinct zones in Ngong face lengthy interruptions during the same 8:00 am to 5:00 pm timeframe. Residents and businesses spanning Gichagi, Police Ngong, Lemiso, Maragara Road, Kimuka, Saikeri, and the corridor near Ngong Hills, Manyatta, and Kengen will experience total supply cuts.

Further west in Siaya County, a shorter but complete blackout is slated for the Wagwer area from 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. The interruption directly impacts institutional learning centers, cutting power to Wagwer Primary School, Wagwer Secondary School, and adjacent power consumers.

For mobile subscribers relying on the affected towers in Nyeri, the Saturday maintenance schedule serves as a reminder of how deeply telecom uptime remains tied to scheduled grid rehabilitation. Power is expected to return progressively as maintenance works clear across the three counties once the respective operational windows close.
