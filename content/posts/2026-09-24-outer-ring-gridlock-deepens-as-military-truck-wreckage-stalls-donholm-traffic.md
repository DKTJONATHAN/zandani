Morning commuters headed toward Nairobi’s central business district experienced severe gridlock on Thursday after a collision involving a Kenya Defence Forces truck and a passenger service vehicle blocked a critical section of Outer Ring Road.

The crash occurred at approximately 9 a.m. near the Shell petrol station at the Donholm exit, a major interchange linking Jogoo Road and Manyanja Road. According to witnesses at the scene, the military vehicle collided with an Utimo Sacco matatu operating on the Umoja route, leaving the army truck's passenger-side door wrenched from its hinges and trapping personnel inside the cabin.

![Traffic piles up after a KDF truck was involved in an accident along Outer Ring Road, September 24, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/0pcF6RGG/1451ff2c3ad3.webp)

Footage from the site showed fellow soldiers responding to the emergency before medical and rescue teams arrived, attempting to extricate colleagues seated in the front row. The stalled military vehicle remained stationary on the tarmac for an extended period, preventing normal traffic flow along the multi-lane corridor.

Motorists approaching from Allsops and Kangundo Road faced compounding delays as traffic officers and stranded drivers sought alternative routes around the bottleneck. Public transport operators reported that the blockage paralyzed movement along the vital eastern bypass artery for hours during peak morning movement.

![Screengrabs from the scene as a KDF officer tries to rescue his colleagues after their truck was involved in an accident along Outer Ring Road, September 24, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/GvwQV5vr/1502f2e9f9ae.webp)

By Thursday afternoon, neither the military nor traffic authorities had issued a formal statement detailing the cause of the collision or confirming whether any casualties had been recorded from either the truck or the matatu. 

The incident follows a separate highway collision along the Nairobi-Mombasa highway near Taru and Mackinnon Road, which left multiple passengers injured aboard a Dreamline bus after a collision with a trailer, underlining a volatile week for major transport corridors across the country.
