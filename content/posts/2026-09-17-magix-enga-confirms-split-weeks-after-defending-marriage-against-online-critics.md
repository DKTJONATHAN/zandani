Music producer Magix Enga has confirmed he is single, ending a brief marriage to his wife, Jojo, that was characterized by public defiance against rumors of instability.

The announcement, made via a video shared on Thursday, September 17, represents a swift pivot for the producer. Only two weeks ago, on September 3, Enga was actively shielding Jojo from social media criticism. At that time, he labeled critics as hypocrites, claiming that individuals who insulted his wife in public were the same ones privately soliciting him for financial help.

This split follows a civil marriage ceremony held in Nakuru earlier this year. While the union appeared solid in carefully curated social media posts, it had been under scrutiny since at least June. When reports of a separation first surfaced three months ago, Enga dismissed them entirely, insisting the marriage remained intact.

The relationship had recently taken a more domestic turn in the public eye. Earlier this month, Jojo made headlines by publicly inviting the mothers of Enga’s other children to bring them to their home, suggesting a plan to raise the children together in a blended family setting.

Enga’s latest confirmation of his single status provides no specific details regarding the cause of the breakup or the exact date the separation occurred. The producer has not addressed whether the recent public pressure or the complexities of their blended family arrangements contributed to the decision.

The reversal leaves the status of the Nakuru civil union settled in the eyes of the public, even as the specific incidents leading to the sudden change remain undisclosed.
