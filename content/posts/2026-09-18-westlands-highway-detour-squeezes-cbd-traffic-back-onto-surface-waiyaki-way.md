Motorists travelling toward Nairobi’s Central Business District along the Westlands corridor face a mandatory detour starting Saturday evening, as maintenance crews seal off the elevated overpass deck connecting James Gichuru Junction to the lower road network.

The temporary closure, scheduled by Moja Expressway Company to run from 8:00 PM on Saturday, September 19 through 12:00 PM on Monday, September 21, will push all inbound surface traffic off the overpass deck and directly onto surface lanes along Waiyaki Way.

Crucially for toll users, the disruption is isolated to the overpass bridge deck itself. The primary Westlands Entrance to the Nairobi Expressway will remain operational throughout the maintenance window. Meanwhile, vehicles exiting the expressway toward the overpass are set to resume normal movement earlier, starting Saturday at 5:00 PM.

### Routing Flow and Surface Pressure

The redirection forces CBD-bound traffic coming off the James Gichuru axis to merge directly into Waiyaki Way's main surface carriageway, raising potential bottlenecks through the Westlands commercial hub. Traffic marshals are being deployed along the route to manage merging streams and direct drivers through the altered traffic pattern.

Moja Expressway advised motorists using the corridor to adjust their travel schedules and allow additional time to navigate the altered traffic setup.

### Vulnerable Corridor

The maintenance directive arrives less than two weeks after similar structural work at the nearby ABC Place–Red Hill Road junction on September 9 triggered extensive gridlock along Waiyaki Way. During that incident, surface traffic stalled for extended periods as vehicles redirected around elevated work zones, highlighting how routine maintenance on expressway connector infrastructure rapidly spills over onto surface arterial roads.

With maintenance crews operating across the 40-hour weekend window, traffic management along Waiyaki Way will test whether surface lanes can absorb the redirected flow ahead of the Monday morning commuter rush.
