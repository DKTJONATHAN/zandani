Statutory boundaries barring retired heads of state from active political management have culminated in the complete institutional exit of former President Uhuru Kenyatta from opposition coalition leadership, creating an immediate vacancy atop the newly minted Ukombozi Alliance.

Uhuru formally tendered his resignation as chairperson of the coalition council in a letter dated October 5, 2026, delivered to coalition party leader Kalonzo Musyoka and copied to the National Steering Committee. The move came barely a fortnight after he relinquished his party leader position in Jubilee, completing a structured withdrawal mandated by law.

"Following the official handover of the Jubilee Party leadership, I am writing to formally resign from my leadership position within the Azimio La Umoja Coalition, effective immediately," Uhuru stated in the resignation letter.

### The Statutory Trigger

![A photo collage of DCP party leader Rigathi Gachagua and Siaya Governor James Orengo, October 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/Kc92dTFK/51f8fc3c1189.webp)

The former head of state's departure traces directly to a September 15 ruling by High Court Justice Lawrence Mugambi, which strictly enforced Section 6 of the Presidential Retirement Benefits Act. The statutory provision prohibits retired presidents from holding executive office in political parties beyond six months after leaving state office.

Complying with that judgment, Uhuru handed the reins of the Jubilee Party to former Interior Cabinet Secretary Fred Matiang'i on September 22, retaining only an informal advisory role within the outfit. His subsequent departure from the Azimio council—which steers broad policy direction and strategic governance—formally terminates his titular leadership of the multi-party alliance he helped establish ahead of the 2022 general election.

Kalonzo confirmed receipt of the resignation on Wednesday, framing Uhuru's exit as a procedural handoff rather than a rupture in political alignment.

"He tells us that his decision is not a departure from our shared political journey, and he reminds us that a coalition is not a temporary alliance of convenience but a covenant of conviction," Kalonzo said in a statement.

### Succession Matrix at the Council

![Uhuru Gachagua](https://i.ibb.co/NdsVGw11/a812e4cec498.webp)

Uhuru's departure has accelerated succession negotiations inside the coalition, which has recently rebranded to The Ukombozi Alliance (TUA) as part of a wider restructuring drive ahead of the 2027 polls.

The alliance is actively considering Democracy for Citizens Party (DCP) leader Rigathi Gachagua and Siaya Governor James Orengo for senior posts on the council. Under the proposals under review, Gachagua is being considered to take over the council chairperson role vacated by Uhuru, with discussions also exploring a co-chair structure alongside Orengo.

The restructuring seeks to bridge legacy opposition parties with newer formations. Currently, the alliance places Kalonzo as party leader, backed by People's Liberation Party leader Martha Karua as deputy coalition leader and Suba South MP Caroli Omondi as secretary-general.

Its expanded council architecture already incorporates Matiang'i as Jubilee's deputy party leader, Party of National Unity (PNU) leader Peter Munya, and Devolution Empowerment Party leader Lenny Kivuti. The steering team has also resolved to extend membership invitations to Meru Governor Kawira Mwangaza’s Umoja na Maendeleo Party, Omingo Magara’s People's Democratic Party, and former National Assembly Speaker Justin Muturi’s Democratic Party.

Whether Gachagua’s DCP and other affiliated factions such as Linda Mwananchi formally deposit coalition instruments with the Registrar of Political Parties remains the next operational hurdle for the expanding bloc.
