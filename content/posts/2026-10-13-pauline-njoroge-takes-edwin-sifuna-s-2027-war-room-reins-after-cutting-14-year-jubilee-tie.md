Pauline Njoroge has officially completed her migration into the core of Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna’s political orbit, taking over as interim Head of Communication for the Linda Mwananchi Movement just months after severing her decades-long ties with the Jubilee Party.

The strategic appointment puts one of Kenya’s most recognizable digital strategists at the steering wheel of Sifuna’s presidential machinery ahead of the 2027 General Election. Her elevation was confirmed on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, coming straight out of an executive conclave chaired by Sifuna to establish the operational framework of his national campaign secretariat.

For Njoroge, the assignment marks a rapid return to the frontline of high-stakes political packaging following her dramatic July 2026 resignation from Jubilee’s National Executive Committee (NEC). That departure abruptly ended a 14-year run inside the former ruling party, where she had risen to Deputy Organising Secretary and stood as one of its fiercest public advocates.

### Taking the War Room Helm

Tuesday’s executive session went beyond customary party pleasantries, focusing heavily on party restructuring, ongoing rebranding initiatives, and assembling the inner circle charged with selling Sifuna’s presidential vision under the Linda Mwananchi banner.

Placing Njoroge in charge of the movement’s messaging brings seasoned air cover to an outfit working to carve out distinct national space. The official announcement pointed directly to her track record across strategic communication, government institutions, intergovernmental organizations, and grueling national campaigns as the primary justification for trusting her with the campaign megaphone.

Her resume carried significant weight during the vetting. Beyond her years inside Jubilee’s administrative engine room, Njoroge was drafted into the dedicated campaign unit that steered the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga’s 2025 bid for the African Union Commission (AUC) chairperson seat, sharpening her operational credentials on a continental stage.

### Cutting Ties to Rebuild Anew

When Njoroge tendered her resignation from Jubilee’s NEC three months ago, it sent clear signals across political corridors that she was seeking unencumbered terrain. Rather than drifting into freelance consultancy or stepping back from the partisan fray, her move into Linda Mwananchi reflects a calculated bet on Sifuna’s youthful, combative political vehicle.

Her appointment also coincided with a broader expansion inside the movement’s creative and technical wings. The executive leadership rolled out fresh appointments across its digital, media, and publicity desks on Tuesday, signaling an aggressive push to build a decentralized apparatus capable of driving agenda-setting conversations online and on the ground.

With the secretariat framework now established and Njoroge holding the communications playbook, Sifuna’s presidential setup shifts from consultative boardroom gatherings into an active, nationwide brand rollout.
