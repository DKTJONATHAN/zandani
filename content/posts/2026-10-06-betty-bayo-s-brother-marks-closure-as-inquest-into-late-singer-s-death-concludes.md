Months after a fierce dispute over medical records and a rushed burial, the family of the late gospel singer Betty Bayo has reached a formal milestone regarding her passing.

Edward Kang’ethe Mbugua, widely known as Ng’esh, announced on October 1, 2026, that the official inquest into his sister's death had officially concluded. According to Mbugua, the legal resolution established the medical cause of death as AMN, bringing a sense of finality after an extended period of unanswered questions.

Betty Bayo—born Beatrice Wairimu Mbugua—passed away on November 10, 2025, while receiving care at Kenyatta National Hospital. Although initial reports attributed her sudden death to leukemia or blood cancer complications, her relatives immediately disputed the narrative and pressed for transparency regarding her final days.

Faced with a lack of access to medical records and postmortem findings following her November 20, 2025, interment, Betty’s mother, Joyce Wairimu Mbugua, engaged legal representation. Through Omenke Andeje and Company Advocates, the family petitioned both the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions and the Inspector-General of Police in December 2025, pointing out that the singer had no history of chronic illness and demanding a thorough review of the circumstances surrounding her death.

The formal conclusion of the inquest brings an administrative halt to a tense chapter that began shortly after the artist's passing at Kenyatta National Hospital nearly a year prior.
