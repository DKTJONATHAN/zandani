The National Land Commission has notified property owners across four counties that they face mandatory valuation and ownership inquiries starting in late October, opening the formal compensation process for supplementary land needed along several national infrastructure corridors.

The statutory directive, published in the Kenya Gazette on Friday, September 25, 2026, places the immediate evidentiary burden on titleholders in Kajiado, Kiambu, Nyeri, and Migori. Landowners seeking compensation for plots targeted by the state must submit verified land records, national identification papers, and banking details either ahead of or directly during the designated inquiry sessions.

### Corridor Additions and Hearing Venues

For residents in Kajiado County, the notices cover supplementary acquisitions tied to the Nairobi–Naivasha Standard Gauge Railway (SGR) Phase 2A corridor. The commission scheduled hearings for three specific parcels—two situated in Ngong and one in Kitengela—on November 19, 2026, at the Scheme 6 Kandisi Chief’s Office beginning at 10:00 a.m. The proceedings will serve as the formal venue for affected residents to contest valuations and raise objections regarding the taking of their property.

![Government officials inspecting the Naivasha-Kisumu-Malaba Standard Gauge Railway Project, connecting Kisumu to Malaba ahead of President William Ruto's launch.](https://i.ibb.co/ym9Nvjs4/071861589542.webp)

In Kiambu County, infrastructure adjustments require additional land across both road and utility sectors. The commission expanded compulsory acquisition lists for the Gatiiguru–Ithanga–Gakungu and Gakungu–Kamahuha–Mbombo road alignment, setting its compensation inquiry for November 26, 2026, at the Kirimiri Chief’s Office.

On that same date, a parallel inquiry will proceed at the Juja Town Chief’s Office to process land takeovers for the Gatundu Water Supply and Sewerage Project. The utility undertaking requires extra acreage for both water distribution networks and wastewater handling, with two parcels formally gazetted for the sewerage component.

### Upcountry Road and Bridge Networks

The commission’s schedule begins earliest in Nyeri County, where an administrative revision prompted fresh proceedings. Following the correction of an earlier gazette entry regarding the Mukurweni–Ini–Gakonya Road Project, the NLC slated an inquiry for an additional parcel on October 22, 2026. Unlike the rural administrative centers utilized for the other projects, that session will take place at Upper Hill Chambers in Nairobi.

![President William Ruto, during the groundbreaking for the Naivasha-Kisumu SGR Extension at Suswa in Narok County on March 19, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/B5XX6S3Y/3be3219bc2ad.webp)

In Western Kenya, the state expanded its acquisition footprint for the Toku Bridge and approach roads project in Migori County. The updated schedule incorporates several parcels needed to secure the river crossing and its feeder links, anchored by a single 1.1925-hectare plot that represents one of the largest continuous tracts gazetted in the latest notice series. The commission set the inquiry for those claims on November 16, 2026, at the Kitere Chief’s Office.

### The Documentation Threshold

Under the statutory rules enforced by the land agency, compensation awards hinge entirely on claimants establishing unencumbered legal interest during these sittings. The NLC instructed all affected individuals, registered occupiers, and legitimate claimants to tender formal written compensation applications supported by original land ownership documents and valid personal identification.

The requirement to produce pre-verified bank details alongside ownership papers indicates an effort to compress the payment pipeline once inquiry panels settle final award values. However, property owners failing to appear or submit formal claims by the close of their respective hearing dates risk having their compensation awards determined in absentia, leaving their restitution tied up in administrative deposits as physical road and rail works proceed.
