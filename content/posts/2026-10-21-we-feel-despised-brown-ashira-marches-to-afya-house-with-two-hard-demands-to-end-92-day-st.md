Taking his union's 92-day grievance directly to the steps of Afya House, Kenya Environmental Health and Public Health Practitioners Union (KEHPHPU) Secretary General and CEO Brown Ashira led hundreds of public health officers through the streets of the Nairobi Central Business District on Wednesday, October 21, 2026.

The capital came to a temporary standstill as marching health workers, waving placards and chanting, targeted top officials over what they term deliberate institutional neglect. Standing outside the Ministry of Health headquarters, Ashira voiced the frustration accumulated across three months of unanswered labor petitions.

“We are here at the Ministry of Health because we feel despised,” Ashira told the crowd gathered outside the ministry. “We have been on a strike for 92 days, and the things we want are very easy.”

### The Monetary Ultimatums

Rather than offering broad compromise language, Ashira narrowed the union's immediate conditions for ending the standoff down to two clear financial actions: paying interns and disbursing risk allowances.

According to the KEHPHPU leadership, public health officers will not resume their duties across county facilities until the national government executes three major steps:

* **Intern Deployment and Compensation:** Immediate nationwide posting and full remuneration for all pending public health interns.
* **Risk Allowance Disbursement:** Immediate payment of risk allowances to active public health practitioners.
* **Formal CBA Execution:** Finalization and formal signing of the union’s pending Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) to anchor long-term service conditions.

Ashira made it clear that public health officers would remain off the job until those baseline financial transfers are completed.

“Once the interns are paid and the public health officers receive their risk allowances, then we will return to work,” Ashira said.

### Policy Friction Over Preventive Care

Beyond immediate remuneration, Ashira directed sharp criticism at the Ministry of Health’s broader policy direction, accusing officials of prioritizing curative, hospital-based interventions while starving preventive and community health structures of attention and budget.

KEHPHPU is demanding official registration and structural recognition as the primary bargaining body for preventive health workers. Union members argue that excluding public health officers from high-level policy decisions leaves disease surveillance and environmental sanitation chronically underfunded.

The 92-day withdrawal of services has severely impacted field operations nationwide, grounding routine public sanitation inspections, disease tracking, and community health oversight.

### Surveillance Pressures Escalate

Ashira's march comes at a precarious moment for national disease control. Public health field officers are usually the first line of defense in managing sanitation hazards associated with El Niño weather patterns and containing active viral threats, including a confirmed Ebola cluster.

Protesting workers emphasized that attempting to manage ongoing public health threats without field officers or deployed interns leaves counties dangerously exposed. The union called for an immediate wave of fresh recruitment to reinforce depleted county health units.

With Afya House leadership yet to sign off on the CBA or release the disputed funds, Ashira and KEHPHPU maintain that their members will remain on the streets until every term is written into a binding agreement.
