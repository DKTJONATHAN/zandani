Wahu Kagwi has singled out Bien-Aimé Baraza’s methodical research habits and studio obsession as the driving force behind his upcoming solo album, *Alusa Continua*, setting the tone at an exclusive weekend listening party where Kenya's music elite sampled the 17-track project.

Addressing creatives and media personalities assembled for the advance preview, Wahu pointed beyond Bien’s stage presence to the work ethic behind his international push.

“One thing about Bien is he gets obsessed with his art. He’s obsessed about getting better. He researches a lot,” Wahu stated, highlighting his deliberate drive to refine his sound for global audiences.

The advance sampling revealed an ambitious, heavily collaborated body of work spanning 17 tracks. Bien’s roster for *Alusa Continua* crosses regional and international borders, featuring American rapper 2 Chainz, Nigerian singer Oxlade, Tanzanian star Alikiba, and Kenyan rapper Femi One.

Veteran singer David Mathenge, popularly known as Nameless, reinforced Wahu’s assessment, framing Bien as an industry trailblazer whose commitment sets a benchmark for local creators targeting overseas markets.

“You are a trailblazer… I know guys are going to be blown away with what you have,” Nameless told Bien during the gathering. “I just can’t wait to see how the world is ready for you, bro. Continue, bro, I am extremely proud of you. You put in the work. Let’s come and enjoy ourselves.”

Beyond the veteran praise, the listening session highlighted the tight creative network that built the record. Singer-songwriter Bensoul, who anchored the project's songwriting, production, and overall creative direction, noted that Bien’s trajectory relies on pulling other artists along.

Bensoul pointed to Bien’s consistency and foresight, describing his leadership style as one focused on looking ahead and bringing other talent into the room.

That collaborative sentiment was echoed by musician Charisma, who acknowledged the operational friction of navigating Kenya’s competitive music ecosystem. Charisma noted that observing Bien’s career provided direct lessons in professional longevity.

“The one thing that I’ve learned… I’d say two things that I’ve learned so much from him. Perseverance and discipline. Being in this journey is not easy,” Charisma said.

Recording artist Coster Ojwang disclosed that his guest appearance on the record developed organically once both artists identified a shared spiritual alignment, explaining that the track came together naturally when the right song presented itself.

The gathering also underscored the lasting ties within Sauti Sol. Bandmate Savara Mudigi joined the listening session, explicitly linking the release to his own current musical rollout and dedicating his latest single to Bien’s milestone.

“My latest single is about celebrating, so it’s a very good time to be alive, the stars have aligned so I’m dedicating it to *Alusa Continua*,” Savara said. “Bien is my brother forever, so I’m celebrating him with my latest single.”

Media personality Talia Oyando added her voice to the room's consensus, calling the singer a "national treasure" as the room sampled the unreleased cuts.

The listening session closed with preview tracks circulating among industry stakeholders, marking the final pre-release milestone for *Alusa Continua* ahead of its commercial debut across streaming platforms.
