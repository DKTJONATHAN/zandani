Pastor Victor Kanyari used his church pulpit to deliver a direct public validation for Morgan Bahati's debut single, *Zoza (Nikifika 18)*, stepping into the online debate surrounding the track at the exact moment the Bahati family shifts its main public focus toward elective politics.

Addressing worshippers at the Salvation Healing Ministry during a Sunday service on November 22, 2026, Kanyari revealed he had personally reviewed the release and urged critics to give the teenager room to develop his craft. Dismissing negative feedback circulating on social media, the preacher insisted that the young artist's musical delivery deserved recognition rather than baseline pushback.

"Morgan Bahati ametoa wimbo mpya inayoitwa *Zoza*. Wangapi wameskiza hiyo wimbo? He has done really well. Hiyo wimbo iko poa," Kanyari told his congregation. "Wale ambao wanamcriticise wanasema wimbo sio poa, waambiwe mimi Pastor Kanyari nimesema hiyo wimbo iko sawa. Morgan Bahati is way ahead than some people."

Kanyari’s altar endorsement followed the official introduction of the project on Friday, November 20, 2026, when veteran musician Kevin Bahati formally launched his firstborn son into the Kenyan entertainment space via his YouTube channel. The unveiling represents the culmination of a multi-year development plan within the household.

According to the elder Bahati, while Morgan had harbored creative ambitions for several years, the decision was taken to deliberately hold back his entry into the commercial market. The deliberate delay was intended to allow the young artist time to mature, refine his vocal presence, and build the personal resilience necessary to handle public scrutiny independently.

During the official unveiling, Bahati highlighted Morgan's growth, composure, and command of language, even pointing out with humor that his son speaks English with a sharper accent than his own. Beyond the personal milestone, however, the debut comes at a structural turning point for the family's public brand.

Morgan's music entry coincides directly with Kevin Bahati’s announcement of a one-year hiatus from recording studio work. The senior artist is stepping back from full-time music production to concentrate his resources and public visibility on his political campaign for the Mathare parliamentary seat ahead of the 2027 General Election.

With Kevin Bahati temporarily vacating the studio to pursue his legislative campaign in Mathare, Morgan’s *Zoza (Nikifika 18)* assumes a primary role in keeping the family's musical footprint active across streaming platforms. Kanyari's pulpit intervention now adds an unexpected layer of visibility to the debut project as online audiences continue to assess the young artist's first official release.
