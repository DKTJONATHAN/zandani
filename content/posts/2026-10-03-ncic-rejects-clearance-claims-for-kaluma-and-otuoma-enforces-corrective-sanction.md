The National Cohesion and Integration Commission (NCIC) has pushed back against narratives framing its regulatory inquiries as exonerations, affirming that adverse speech findings and binding statutory corrective orders remain in force against Homa Bay Town MP Peter Kaluma and Busia Governor Paul Otuoma.

In a press release issued Saturday, October 3, the cohesion watchdog took issue with public characterisations suggesting both elected leaders walked away from recent summonses with clean records. The commission clarified that its administrative interventions resulted in findings of fault, formal cessation orders, and active conciliation terms, rather than an institutional clearance.

“The Commission takes serious exception to reporting in sections of the media that portray its determinations concerning Hon. Peter Kaluma and H.E. Dr. Paul Otuoma as having ‘cleared’ them, or suggest that the Commission summoned them to sanitise their remarks,” the commission stated.

### Seven-Day Deadline for Busia Governor

The formal determination places Governor Otuoma under a strict compliance timeline following remarks he delivered on June 27 during a gathering at Bulanda Junction in Busia. 

Following an evidentiary review under the National Cohesion and Integration Act and the Constitution, the NCIC determined that the governor’s statements were threatening, exclusionary, and capable of inciting hostility against traders and residents perceived as outsiders based on their ethnic or national origins.

Far from dismissing the complaint, the regulator served Otuoma with an immediate cessation notice prohibiting further exclusionary rhetoric. Under the statutory terms:

* The governor must retract or clarify his remarks asserting communal ownership over Busia.
* He is required to issue a formal apology to the affected traders and local residents.
* He must publicly guarantee equal constitutional protection for all individuals lawfully conducting business or living within the county.
* He must submit a signed conciliation agreement to the commission within seven days of receipt and participate in community safety engagements alongside county peace stakeholders.

### Conciliation Terms Restrain Kaluma

The commission also maintained active restrictions on Kaluma, who was summoned over public remarks made ahead of an August Linda Mwananchi rally in Homa Bay.

Investigators reviewed Kaluma’s statements alongside his formal defense, finding the language harsh, ambiguous, and bearing an implicit threat that heightened the risk of political intimidation. Rather than clearing the lawmaker, the watchdog initiated a statutory conciliation procedure while maintaining active orders that bar him from repeating the disputed conduct.

The agency underscored that conciliation is an administrative enforcement tool provided under law to compel behavioral change, not an endorsement or excusal of unlawful rhetoric.

### Statutory Consequences of Non-Compliance

The NCIC directed media platforms to correct reporting that omitted the statutory directives, warning that public mischaracterisation obscures the legal mechanisms used to curb divisive political speech.

The commission warned that failure by either official to adhere to the corrective directives, honor conciliation obligations, or complete the prescribed administrative settlements will void the conciliation process, opening both leaders to immediate statutory enforcement notices and potential prosecution under the Act.
