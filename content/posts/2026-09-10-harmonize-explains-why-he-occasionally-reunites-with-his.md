Tanzanian recording artist Harmonize has addressed the nature of his past romantic relationships, citing a preference for the comfort of familiarity when discussing his history of high-profile reunions with former partners.

**What we know:**
* Harmonize appeared on the Obinna TV Extra podcast to discuss his personal life and romantic history.
* The artist declined to identify a favorite ex-partner, citing a desire to avoid creating friction or disrespecting the current relationships of women he has dated.
* He identified "comfort and familiarity" as primary factors for his past choices to reconcile with previous partners rather than pursuing new relationships.
* Harmonize acknowledged that his past relationship endings were influenced by personal mistakes, which he described as learning experiences.

During the interview, host Oga Obinna questioned the singer regarding his history of returning to past flames, most notably his public reconciliation with actress Frida Kajala. In response, Harmonize explained that the transition to a new partner can be daunting, leading individuals to gravitate toward those they already know.

“Among the reasons that made me go back to a woman who I have separated with before, ni ile feeling ya kujiuliza tena kuanza upya wakati nimemzoea huyu,” the singer stated, noting that the decision was a reflection of his own life experiences.

When pressed on his future romantic intentions, the artist stated that he is not opposed to dating new people. However, he emphasized that he intends to rely on his faith to guide his romantic decisions, rather than relying on a predetermined approach to dating.

Addressing the public scrutiny that often follows his personal life, Harmonize explained his refusal to rank his former partners. He noted that public commentary on past relationships could lead to unnecessary jealousy and complicate the lives of women who have since moved on.

“I don’t want to finish my words,” Harmonize said, suggesting that while he remains reflective about his past, he prefers to focus on the lessons learned rather than the specifics of former unions. He maintained that he holds respect for every woman he has been involved with, including his former spouse, Sarah Michelotti, and actress Jacqueline Wolper.
