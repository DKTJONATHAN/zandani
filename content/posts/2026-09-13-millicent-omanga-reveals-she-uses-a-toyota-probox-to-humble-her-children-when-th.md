NAIROBI - Former Nominated Senator Millicent Omanga has disclosed that she maintains a Toyota Probox within her luxury vehicle fleet specifically as a tool for parental discipline. The politician, known for an extensive collection of high-end automobiles including a Rolls-Royce and a Bentley, detailed the unusual method during an appearance on the Oga Obinna podcast.

During the interview, Omanga explained that the vehicle’s utilitarian reputation serves as a deterrent for her children. She described a process where she instructs her driver to use the Probox for school pickups when she feels her children require a corrective measure. According to Omanga, her daughter, Maya, often goes to great lengths to avoid the vehicle, occasionally hiding for up to an hour to evade being seen in it by peers.

The discussion also revisited Omanga’s transition from a small-scale entrepreneur to a public figure. Omanga, 44, recounted her early career selling clothes from the boot of a car, a venture she pursued before her appointment to the KenGen board in 2013 and her subsequent five-year term in the Senate. She noted that while her affinity for high-performance and luxury vehicles remains a personal priority, the Probox remains the only car in her collection reserved exclusively for household management.
