Talent manager Machabe has drawn a strict financial line amid ongoing digital speculation over the parentage of social media personality Brenda Ochieng’s child, declaring he will only undergo a biological DNA test if KSh 10 million is deposited in advance.

Speaking in an interview with MBC TV Kenya, the former manager of rapper Stephen Otieno Adera, popularly known as Stevo Simple Boy, addressed intense online commentary linking him to the child, who is frequently referenced on digital platforms as Dragon Junior.

Machabe urged social media users to stop making unfounded conclusions based on unverified commentary, expressing frustration at being routinely tagged in public discussions surrounding the child.

"Wakenya please vitu za mtandao msiweke kwa moyoni. You are crucifying me for nothing," Machabe said during the broadcast, describing the targeted comments appearing on his personal social media accounts as unwarranted.

### The KSh 10 Million Condition

![Mkamburi Chigogo Reacts to Roselyn Ashanti’s Ordeal](https://i.ibb.co/YBRB265d/c1f698134ae9.webp)

While maintaining that he is innocent of the allegations, Machabe stated he is fully prepared to take a laboratory paternity test—provided his specific financial demand is met.

Addressing a proposal put forward by an individual identified as Chief Bernard, who offered KSh 1 million to cover the cost of testing, Machabe rejected the figure outright, calling the amount an insult to his current financial status.

"Chief Benard you said you will give out one million, I dare to offer 10 million. One million hiyo ni kuniabuse bro," Machabe stated during the interview.

He challenged Chief Bernard to increase the offer tenfold before any clinical steps are taken.

"If you can give me 10 million bro I'll go for that," Machabe added, instructing the prospective funder to lock in the money. "So wewe weka 10 million na nitokee twende DNA yenye unataka pale unataka."

To justify his stance, Machabe defended his personal finances, claiming that his monthly income makes lower monetary offers irrelevant to his daily operations.

"Per month siwezi kuwa specific but haitakuwa below 5 million," he asserted, claiming his regular earnings fall well clear of five million shillings monthly.

### Origin of the Paternity Controversy

The public scrutiny surrounding Machabe emerged in the wake of the publicized fallout between Stevo Simple Boy and Brenda Ochieng.

Brenda previously opened up about the end of their relationship during an appearance on the *Obinna TV Extra* podcast, hosted by Oga Obinna. That separation followed official biological DNA test results which confirmed that Stevo Simple Boy was not the father of Brenda's child.

Once the rapper was officially excluded as the biological father, public speculation quickly shifted across the Kenyan entertainment ecosystem. Commentary and commentary videos from creator 2Mbili and other online figures increasingly focused on Machabe due to his close professional association with the rapper and Brenda during the relationship, ultimately sparking the demand for a secondary paternity test.

Machabe insists he has no objection to undergoing medical procedure provided his KSh 10 million escrow condition is satisfied by those demanding answers, leaving the next step squarely on the public figures proposing to fund the procedure.
