Karangu Muraya found an unintended souvenir waiting in his driveway following a long workday in Nyandarua: a black SUV sporting fresh dents and scratches across the rear passenger door and fender.

The damage was courtesy of his younger sister, Cess Muraya, who had decided his vehicle made a suitable training ground for her first behind-the-wheel practice session. Rather than keeping the incident private, the content creator took to social media to air the discovery, sharing photographs of the bodywork alongside his commentary.

“Unafika nyumbani after having a long day in Nyandarua alafu unapata sister yako ameamua kujifundisha driving na gari yako na akagongesha,” he posted.

Despite the visible impact on the vehicle's bodywork, Karangu bypassed financial penalties for the household accident, though he made it clear the car had to be brought back to standard. Addressing his sister directly online, he stated that while he would not bill her for the repairs, she bore full responsibility for restoring the vehicle to its original condition.

“Cess Muraya hapa sikulipishi but lazima urudishe gari yangu vile ilikuwa!!!” he wrote.

Rather than leaving the situation to trial-and-error parking lot sessions, Karangu shifted the narrative toward formal instruction. He announced that Cess would check into a structured classroom setting the following morning to learn vehicle operation properly.

The administrative fix involves enrolling her at Gorgeous Technical Institute, a vocational training center owned by the influencer himself, turning an awkward family mishap into an institutional solution. At the same time, Karangu looped in formal channels by tagging his provider, Mellis Insurance, in the post to seek guidance on processing the vehicle damage claim.
