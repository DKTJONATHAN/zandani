Homicide investigators hunting for the perpetrators behind the fatal September 2025 shooting of Nairobi lawyer Kyalo Mbobu have closed in on a key physical asset: the motorcycle allegedly used to transport the gunman to and from the scene of the crime.

Rather than a simple roadside impoundment, the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) unravelled a complex chain of custody, tracing the vehicle through a succession of previous owners before finally seizing it and placing it under forensic lockdown at their headquarters.

The recovery follows a breakthrough during interrogations of a captured boda boda rider, one of three suspects currently in custody over the September 9 attack. According to the DCI, the rider admitted to operating the motorcycle on the night Mbobu was ambushed and subsequently verified the vehicle when detectives presented it to him in custody.

### Tracing the Trail of Ownership

The operation to secure the motorcycle exposed the understated complexity of modern homicide tracking in Kenya's urban transport sector. Once the suspect confessed his involvement and provided details about the vehicle, detectives were faced with a cold trail as the motorcycle had moved rapidly between multiple hands following the shooting.

Investigators systematically backtracked through the transaction history, interviewing consecutive owners to establish how and when the bike changed hands after the killing. The paper trail eventually led homicide officers to its final location, where it was seized without public disclosure of its registration details.

Upon being brought face-to-face with the machine at DCI headquarters, the detained rider positively identified it as the exact motorcycle used during the ambush.

### Forensic Examination at Homicide Headquarters

Now stationed at the DCI Homicide Offices, the motorcycle is undergoing intensive scientific analysis. Detectives are subjecting the machine to latent fingerprint harvesting, DNA sampling, and trace evidence collection to establish direct physical links between the vehicle, the rider, and the suspected gunman.

The recovery forms a vital link in reconstructing the chronology of the crime. Investigators are using the vehicle's history and recorded movements to map out the suspects' timeline before, during, and immediately after the fatal shooting.

While the alleged shooter and rider remain in custody alongside a third suspect, detectives maintain that the forensic audit of the motorcycle could uncover additional co-conspirators or handlers who assisted in concealing the getaway vehicle after the incident.

### The Broader Investigation

The ambush of Kyalo Mbobu on September 9, 2025, sparked a multi-agency manhunt aimed at dissecting both the operational execution and the underlying motive behind the target killing.

DCI officials confirmed that despite reaching this significant evidentiary milestone, the investigation remains actively open across several fronts. Officers are evaluating digital communications, financial trails, and witness statements to determine whether the three held suspects acted independently or on behalf of external instigators.

With the getaway vehicle now physically secured and its owner history documented, investigators are preparing to submit the forensic findings to the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions to solidify the charges as court proceedings approach.
