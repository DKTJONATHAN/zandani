For the past seven years, the head of the late Mzee Nahashon Gachagua Reriani’s household was anchored by its firstborn daughter, who stepped into the matriarchal void left by the passing of their mother, Martha Kirigo Gachagua. 

That advisory post in Naro Moru ended on Thursday, November 5, with the death of 77-year-old Eunice Nyangari Gachagua.

Her passing was confirmed on Friday, November 6, through a formal family statement issued by her younger brother, former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua. The announcement detailed the central position Nyangari held within the extended family structure, acting as a steadying presence for her siblings and younger relatives after their mother died seven years ago.

![Eunice Nyangari Gachagua, elder sister to former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua, who died aged 77 on November 5, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/v6RZfJzX/b826a3930d35.webp)

“The family of the late Mzee Nahashon Gachagua Reriani and the late Martha Kirigo Gachagua is mourning following the passing on of our firstborn sister, Eunice Nyangari Gachagua, in Naro Moru,” Gachagua stated, describing her as a figure whose counsel shaped family decisions in the years following their mother’s death.

### Stewardship of the Reriani Lineage

Nyangari’s role extended across multiple generations of the family, which maintains deep roots in Nyeri County. As the eldest sibling, her home in Naro Moru served as a focal point for family meetings and personal guidance, particularly as the family navigated high-profile public and political transitions over the past decade.

![Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua at State House, Nairobi, on July 19, 2024.](https://i.ibb.co/ymS48Ltj/469bd57080eb.webp)

Her brother noted that her death creates an immediate gap among her surviving siblings, who routinely relied on her as the primary pillar of support within the household.

“We will deeply miss her wise counsel, warmth and the precious moments we shared,” Gachagua said. “Eunice was a happy and warm soul who lit up our hearts.”

Nyangari is survived by her daughter, Waithera, her son, Murimi, along with grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and members of the broader Reriani family network. 

Family representatives have begun gathering in Naro Moru to coordinate burial arrangements, with further updates regarding funeral service schedules expected to be shared with relatives and friends in the coming days.
