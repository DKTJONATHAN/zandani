Nicki Bigfish has stepped forward to unveil the official architectural blueprint and completion timeline for Heha Movers’ upcoming residential home, anchoring the viral dance troupe’s pivot from short-form digital screens to permanent real estate along Nairobi’s Kangundo Road.

Publishing an updated virtual tour across social media, Bigfish confirmed that the proposed three-bedroom residence is scheduled for completion in March 2027. The project, designed to turn online popularity into long-term domestic security for the creator group, relies on structured support from local corporate and foundation entities.

### The Architectural Blueprint

The updated virtual render outlines a contemporary single-family residence designed around the troupe's day-to-day household requirements. The interior layout places a strong focus on shared living spaces, anchoring the home around a central living room connected directly to a dedicated dining zone and a modern kitchen.

Key structural details outlined in the project presentation include:

* **Residential quarters:** A three-bedroom configuration complemented by a centralized bathroom facility.
* **Exterior compound:** A paved yard and parking zone planned to accommodate vehicle parking for residents and visitors.
* **Perimeter security:** A fully enclosed boundary wall featuring a secure main gate for compound access.

"Through the able leadership of @nafmwangi FOUNDATION and @hyssopke, this is the updated artistic view of the Heha mansion that will be built along Kangundo Road and completed by March 2027!" Bigfish wrote in his release statement.

### From Viral Content to Real Estate Assets

Heha Movers initially built their national profile through energetic, humor-infused dance routines that gained traction across Kenyan social media platforms. That digital momentum translated into physical support when well-wishers and commercial sponsors gifted the crew land and building support to establish a permanent base.

By formalizing their partnership with NAFMWANGI Foundation and Hyssop Kenya, the crew has moved the project from an initial land gift into a fully engineered construction pipeline. Placing the residence along Kangundo Road puts the creator group directly inside one of the Nairobi metropolitan area’s fastest-developing suburban belts.

Rather than relying strictly on short-term brand endorsements or ad revenue payouts, the initiative points to a growing trend where creator groups leverage community partnerships to secure long-term physical assets.

With the architectural phase now finalized and public renders open to supporters, work on the Kangundo Road site continues toward the targeted handover in March 2027.
