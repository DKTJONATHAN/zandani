When viewers on a recent livestream pressed Keliah to clarify where she stands with Kenyan content creator Mbavu Destroyer, she chose not to offer a direct explanation. Instead, she turned her screen toward her co-hosts and played footage of Mbavu swimming and traveling alongside environmental activist Truphena Muthoni.

"I don’t know. I will show you the last thing I’ve seen," Keliah told participants on the stream before playing a clip of Mbavu in a swimming pool with Truphena, who gained national prominence after setting a world record by hugging a tree continuously for 72 hours.

The video showed a shirtless Mbavu inviting Truphena over for a hug in the water, a request she accepted while laughing. Mbavu had uploaded the original clip with a caption referencing her environmental feat, writing that the world record holder was "about to break another record by hugging a living stone."

Keliah was not finished using the livestream to address the topic. She proceeded to play a second video featuring Mbavu and Truphena aboard an airplane.

![Mbavu Destroyer // Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/Kp5s6Q1G/b5967b465bab.webp)

That flight video was set to a romantic soundtrack, paired with a caption where Mbavu expressed interest in getting to know the activist better. The audio selection caused a brief pause among the stream's participants, with one male co-host going quiet and stepping out of the frame after having earlier suggested that Truphena might simply be Mbavu's sister.

### Strategic Deflection on Stream

Keliah’s decision to show the footage comes days after she previously addressed public curiosity regarding her connection to Mbavu. In an earlier broadcast, she had clarified that the two had not formally defined their relationship, explaining that she preferred to take time and understand him before making any commitment.

At the time, she emphasized that someone expressing romantic interest was not sufficient reason to rush into a relationship. By broadcasting the new clips rather than answering questions about their current status, Keliah effectively redirected the public interrogation, leaving viewers and co-hosts to examine Mbavu's own public actions.

![Truphena Muthoni // Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/cKrmVnjx/ddcc81658952.webp)

### Audience Reaction and Unanswered Questions

The clips sparked widespread discussion across social media platforms following the livestream. Online commentators offered mixed reactions, with some poking fun at the unexpected pairing of a comedy content creator and an environmental campaigner, while others insisted that Mbavu needed to provide clarification.

Among the comments left by viewers were remarks like, "You don't have to go to Kitengela; Kitengela comes to you," while others noted, "Let's wait and hear Mbavu's feedback."

Neither Mbavu Destroyer nor Truphena Muthoni has publicly clarified whether the flight and pool videos signal a romantic relationship or a joint project. For now, Keliah's livestream broadcast has put the onus entirely on Mbavu to explain the footage.
