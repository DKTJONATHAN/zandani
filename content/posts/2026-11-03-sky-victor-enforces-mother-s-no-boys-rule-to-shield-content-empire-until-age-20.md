Sky Victor is relying on advice inherited from her late mother, gospel singer Betty Bayo, as an active operational rule governing her personal life and digital career.

The young content creator has firmly pushed back against public interest in her personal life, clarifying that romantic relationships sit at the very bottom of her current priority list. Balancing personal responsibilities alongside her online projects, she made it clear that her daily focus remains strictly tied to personal development and professional growth.

“There is so much going on in my life for me to start thinking of having a boyfriend. That is the last thing,” she said.

### A Maternal Rule in Action

Behind that stance lies a specific directive given to her by her mother. Sky disclosed that advice from Bayo continues to guide her choices in public and private spaces, functioning as a guardrail against potential distractions.

“And even my mum has always been telling me boys will waste your time. And I am listening to her even when she is not here,” she explained.

The daughter of Bayo and preacher Victor Kanyari emphasized that her mother's warning about dating being a drain on focus was not something she intended to set aside. Instead, she treats the guidance as an active boundary, maintaining the same discipline in her mother’s absence.

### The June 2026 Moratorium

Her recent statements build on a clear personal timeline she established earlier in the year. During a TikTok Live broadcast in June 2026, the creator addressed questions surrounding her relationship status by setting a firm age milestone before she would consider entering the dating scene.

“Let me tell you, I have never dated in my life, and I will not date until I am 20,” she declared during the live stream.

By confirming that she has remained single throughout her teenage years, Sky drew a distinct line between her online presence and the romantic speculation frequently attached to young digital creators.

### Prioritizing Digital Growth

Rather than dedicating time to romantic pursuits, Sky has chosen to funnel her attention into expanding her digital footprint and building a consistent schedule around content production.

“I will focus on my content,” she stated, outlining a routine that centers her energy on creative projects, practical responsibilities, and individual growth.

As online audiences continue to follow her trajectory, Sky maintains that her professional output and personal growth will remain her sole focus until she reaches her self-imposed milestone.
