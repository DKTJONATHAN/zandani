**NAIROBI, September 14, 2026** - Kenyan content creator Njambi Fever has publicly clarified her marital status, confirming that while she and her husband are currently separated, the pair remain legally married. The digital personality addressed her followers during a TikTok live session, seeking to dispel ongoing speculation regarding the state of her domestic life and potential divorce proceedings.

During the broadcast, Njambi addressed the complexities of their cohabitation, acknowledging that their living situation remains fluid within their three-bedroom home. "Sometimes we live in the same house, sometimes we don't," she stated, noting that the arrangement is dictated by daily circumstances and the couple's moods. She emphasized that while the relationship is under significant strain, the couple has not initiated formal divorce proceedings.

"We are married. We have the certificate," Njambi asserted. "Things are not working out. So right now we are separated, not divorced. We are giving ourselves grace."

The creator described this period as a trial phase for both parties to reassess their lives independently. According to Njambi, the separation serves as a space for reflection, noting in a mix of English and Swahili that both parties are currently testing life outside their union to see if reconciliation or therapy might be viable paths in the future.

When pressed by followers regarding past allegations of infidelity within the marriage, Njambi declined to engage with the topic, shifting the focus to her personal peace. "Honestly, that is not something that can cross my mind. That is not my business," she said.

The influencer, who shares one child with her husband, had previously faced increased scrutiny after posting content that suggested she was single. By clarifying that she is separated rather than divorced, she seeks to resolve the ambiguity surrounding her public and private persona as she continues her career as a digital creator.
