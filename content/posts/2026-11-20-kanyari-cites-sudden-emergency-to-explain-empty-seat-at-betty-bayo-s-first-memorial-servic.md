Pastor Victor Kanyari took to his Facebook page on Friday, November 20, 2026, to account for his empty seat at the first-anniversary memorial service of his late ex-wife, celebrated gospel musician Betty Bayo, attributing his absence to an unexpected personal crisis.

The remembrance ceremony marked exactly one year since Bayo passed away on November 10, 2025. Her passing set off months of bitter public friction between the preacher, the singer’s extended relatives, and her widower, Hiram Gitau, popularly known as Tash. Kanyari’s failure to show up in person immediately drew sharp scrutiny, prompting the preacher to issue a swift public clarification alongside an apology to the singer's household.

"Poleni sana nimepata emergency ya haraka sijaweza kuhudhuria memorial ya late Betty Bayo lakini tuko pamoja na familia Mungu ailaze roho yake mahali pema," Kanyari posted, assuring the bereaved family that he stood in solidarity with them despite staying away from the gathering.

### The Shadow of Dagoretti Custody Verdict

The preacher’s ceremonial absence landed at an exceptionally delicate moment in the domestic dispute surrounding Bayo’s legacy. Just weeks earlier, on October 26, 2026, the Dagoretti Magistrate’s Court delivered a significant ruling in the protracted battle over Bayo’s two children, Sky and Danny.

The court formally awarded custody of the minors to Kanyari. However, the legal victory did not translate into an immediate transfer. The magistrate directed that the children remain temporarily under the care of Gitau, the widower who lived with them until Bayo’s death, to facilitate a structured transition period.

That phased transition required delicate collaboration between two men whose relationship had played out in court filings and strained public encounters. By missing the formal memorial milestone, Kanyari sidestepped an unavoidable face-to-face encounter with Gitau and the late singer’s broader kinship circle, keeping the interaction confined to legal channels and digital posts.

### Frayed Ties with the US Matriarch

Gitau is not the only figure in Bayo’s inner circle with whom Kanyari has traded public friction. The preacher's standing with Bayo’s maternal family collapsed months before the memorial service was planned.

In July 2026, Kanyari publicly acknowledged that his relationship with the late musician’s United States-based mother had completely deteriorated. While the matriarch initially gave her blessing to Kanyari’s petition to take full custody of Sky and Danny, the alliance disintegrated into bitter disagreements over how the minors’ affairs and their mother’s memory should be managed.

Those frayed ties left Kanyari largely isolated from the family committee handling Bayo’s remembrance, turning an anniversary that should have brought closure into an uneasy display of distance.

### An Unresolved Household Transition

While Kanyari used his online statement to seek peace and ask for eternal rest for his former wife, the real test of his standing remains outside the memorial venue. 

With the Dagoretti court order already establishing him as the lawful custodian, the transition protocol that leaves Sky and Danny temporarily under Gitau’s roof remains active. Whether Kanyari’s sudden absence from Friday's memorial service complicates the remaining handover arrangements with Gitau will depend on how smoothly both camps navigate the court-monitored transition timetable in the weeks ahead.
