Lang’ata MP Phelix Odiwuor, widely known as Jalang’o, is leveraging his deep showbiz roots to transform the first anniversary of former Prime Minister Raila Odinga’s passing into a highly curated musical celebration. Rather than a standard political memorial, the veteran entertainer-turned-politician has assembled a distinct lineup of artists to headline a candle-lighting tribute at Uhuru Park on Friday, October 2, 2026.

The lineup bridges the spiritual, the nostalgic, and the secular, reflecting the diverse cultural forces that defined Raila’s political life. 

At the center of this musical curation is Emmanuel Musili, the singer behind "Lero ni Lero." The song, which served as the high-energy soundtrack to Raila’s 2022 presidential campaign, represents a deliberate nod to the late leader's active days on the political trail. By reintroducing Musili's campaign anthem, the memorial shifts from a standard eulogy to a celebration of Raila's political momentum.

To ground the evening in lakeside rhythms, Jalang’o has also billed veteran Ohangla singer Elisha Toto and secular heavyweight Prince Indah. Indah, whom Jalang'o described as the headliner to "crown" the night, is expected to lead a performance dubbed "Doba ya Baba."

“But to crown it all, the king himself, Prince Indah, will also be performing this evening," Jalang’o announced. "Inaitwa *Doba ya Baba*. So make sure that you come.”

The high-octane Ohangla and campaign music will be balanced by a solemn, spiritual segment. Gospel artist Solomon Mkubwa is scheduled to deliver praise and worship performances, while relationship coach and media personality Pastor Robert Burale will guide the congregation in prayer.

The Uhuru Park gathering serves as the opening act of what Jalang’o has branded "Mwezi wa Baba" (The Month of Baba)—a month-long series of national commemorations. The Orange Democratic Movement (ODM) has also rallied its nationwide network to hold parallel candle-lighting exercises on October 2, positioning Uhuru Park as the central hub for Nairobi supporters.

Raila passed away on October 15, 2025, at the age of 80 while undergoing treatment in India, and was laid to rest at his ancestral home in Kang’o Ka Jaramogi, Bondo, Siaya County. 

The commemorative trail will move across various regions throughout October, culminating in a major political and cultural gathering at Nairobi’s Jacaranda Grounds on October 31, 2026.
