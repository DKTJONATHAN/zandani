Musician and politician Samuel Muchoki, popularly known as Samidoh, marked his latest birthday with a public acknowledgment from his wife, Edday Nderitu, that signals a marked shift from the sharp break that defined their domestic relationship three years ago.

Responding directly under an Instagram birthday tribute posted by Edday, the Mugithi singer replied with a pet name that pointedly reclaimed their shared public identity: "Thankyou soo much, Njambi wa Mhesh."

The exchange represents the latest sign of warmth between the two following years of high-profile marital turbulence and physical separation across two continents.

In her original post, Edday shared a tribute framing their bond beyond recent conflict.

“May this new chapter of your life bring you wisdom, peace, success, good health, and countless reasons to smile,” she wrote, before addressing the trajectory of their decade-and-a-half marriage. “Life has a beautiful way of reminding us that some connections are bigger than ourselves, and some roles remain meaningful regardless of where life takes us.”

She concluded the post with a direct salute: “You deserve beautiful things, today and always. Happy Birthday Mhesh.”

Samidoh also took to his main social media profile to post a separate reflective note on the occasion, writing, “The gift of life and good health is never something to take for granted.”

The public warmness stands in contrast to February 2023, when Edday broke her silence on social media following Samidoh’s publicised relationship with Nominated Senator Karen Nyamu. At the time, Edday stated that she had endured 15 years of marriage alongside periods of public humiliation, explicitly declaring that she was unwilling to raise her children in a polygamous arrangement.

Later that year, Edday relocated to the United States with the couple’s children, initiating an extended period of physical separation that played out in full view of their online followings.

However, the tone of their public interactions began shifting during Samidoh’s musical tours in the United States. Reports indicated the two spent time together during his itinerary stops abroad, fueling speculation that they were actively repairing their connection.

By early 2026, that background reconciliation spilled into public view during a joint live video broadcast, where the pair interacted affectionately and shared a kiss for viewers. Edday has also openly referred to Samidoh as her husband during subsequent broadcasts.

Edday’s birthday tribute and Samidoh’s public pet-name reply mark another concrete step in that ongoing reconciliation, leaving the couple’s public narrative anchored less in their 2023 separation and more in their current online co-existence.
