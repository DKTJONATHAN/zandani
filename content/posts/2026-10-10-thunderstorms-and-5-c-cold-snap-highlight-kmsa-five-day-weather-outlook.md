A dual meteorological pattern of heavy afternoon thunderstorms across western Kenya and sharp nighttime drops to 5°C in high-altitude central zones will dominate weather conditions through Tuesday, October 13, according to a five-day outlook from the Kenya Meteorological Service Authority (KMSA).

The bulletin, covering the period from Saturday, October 10, places ten western, lake basin, and Rift Valley counties on alert for lightning hazards and intense afternoon squalls, while high-altitude farming belts face a sharp temperature decline.

### Electrical Storm Corridor

Western region counties—including Kakamega, Vihiga, Bungoma, and Busia—are forecast to receive continuous heavy rain accompanied by thunderstorms throughout the weekend.

Across the Lake Victoria basin, Kisumu and Siaya counties face a distinct daily pattern: clear, sunny mornings yielding to intense afternoon rains, lightning, and thunderstorms through Monday, October 13. Similar rainfall and electrical activity are projected for Homa Bay, Migori, Kisii, and Nyamira, with Kisii and Nyamira expecting moderate showers alongside lightning strikes on Saturday and Sunday.

![Motorists using a flooded highway during cold and rainy weather in Nairobi](https://i.ibb.co/KzjfzqD4/dc07c0752967.webp)

In the Rift Valley ridge, localized afternoon thunderstorms are expected to sweep through West Pokot, Trans Nzoia, Nandi, Elgeyo-Marakwet, Baringo, Kericho, and Bomet counties.

### Single-Digit Thermal Dips

Alongside rainfall hazards, KMSA highlighted a significant plunge in nighttime temperatures concentrated across high-altitude regions.

Nyandarua County is set to record the country's lowest readings during the forecast window. Minimum night temperatures in Nyandarua will fall to 6°C on Saturday evening, before dropping further to 5°C on Sunday and remaining at 5°C on Monday.

Cool nighttime conditions will extend across neighboring highland areas:

![Nairobi residents crossing a flooded road in the CBD (left) and floods in Parklands estate.](https://i.ibb.co/N6Qm6s5T/361af8c8b906.webp)

* **Laikipia:** Nighttime lows of 10°C on Saturday.
* **Samburu:** Minimum temperatures of 11°C on Saturday, rising slightly to 12°C on Sunday.
* **Rift Highlands:** West Pokot, Kericho, Narok, and Nandi will record night minimums between 10°C and 15°C.

### Capital and Coast Conditions

In Nairobi, weather conditions will remain comparatively moderate. The capital is slated for light morning rains and moderate afternoon showers on Saturday, followed by persistent light showers on Sunday and Monday. Temperatures in the city will span from nighttime minimums of 14°C to daytime maximums of 28°C.

Central region counties—Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Murang’a, and Kiambu—will see light morning rains across the weekend, followed by afternoon and night showers. Eastern region counties including Meru, Embu, Tharaka-Nithi, Kitui, Machakos, and Makueni will receive varying precipitation, ranging from light morning rains to weekend afternoon showers.

Along the coast, light to moderate showers are forecast over the weekend for Mombasa, Kwale, Kilifi, and Lamu, while Tana River is expected to experience light daytime rain.

With lightning hazards tracking across key western agricultural belts and cold-snap conditions settling over high-altitude highland zones through October 13, regional transport and municipal services face elevated weather sensitivity over the four-day period.
