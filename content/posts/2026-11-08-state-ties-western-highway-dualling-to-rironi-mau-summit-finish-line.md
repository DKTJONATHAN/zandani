The rollout of a four-lane dual carriageway across western Kenya will remain locked on paper until the ongoing Ksh200 billion Rironi-Mau Summit highway is finished, anchoring a series of mega-corridors to a tight operational timetable.

Speaking at Nyamaiya Stadium in Nyamira County on Sunday, President William Ruto outlined a framework that chains the start of major regional transport works to the formal handover of the central trunk line.

Under this staging plan, construction on the four-lane extension from Mau Summit to Kisumu will not break ground until around April 2027. That milestone depends entirely on contractors delivering the Nairobi-Nakuru-Mau Summit highway by next year.

"The biggest road project we are undertaking is the Nairobi-Nakuru-Mau Summit Highway, which will be ready by next year," Ruto said during the interdenominational service. "However, I want to extend the road from Mau Summit through Kericho, Nyamira and Kisii to Kisumu, where it will connect to the Eldoret-Malaba Highway. That is my assignment for next year."

### Seven Counties, One Arterial Loop

![President William Ruto and Kisii Governor Simba Arati during an interdenominational prayer at Nyamaiya stadium on November 8, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/Pz6rpM4G/b0bc7902d20a.webp)

The planned Mau Summit-to-Kisumu alignment creates a wide logistics loop through southern and western economic hubs. The road is mapped to originate at the Mau Summit junction in Nakuru County, crossing Kericho, Bomet, and Nyamira before reaching Kisii.

From Kisii, the second phase will widen the existing Kisii-Kisumu highway into four lanes, traversing Homa Bay and Kisumu counties. The broader transit ring is designed to link into Nandi and Eldoret, ultimately tying into the primary Northern Corridor toward the Uganda border.

By routing heavy freight away from existing single-carriageway bottlenecks, the loop aims to cut transit friction across the Lake Victoria basin. Yet the engineering schedule leaves little room for contractor delays on the initial Rironi-Mau Summit leg, which serves as the financial and logistical prerequisite for the expansion.

### The 2027 Delivery Bottleneck

Deferring the western extension pushes its groundbreaking into the same compressed window as Kenya's other primary transport investments.

Chief among them is the 243-kilometre Eldoret-Malaba highway, which is being structured under a public-private partnership (PPP) model. That project wrapped up its pre-feasibility study in February 2026 under a joint framework involving the PPP Authority, the Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA), and the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB). It is slated to enter active civil works around early 2027.

Simultaneously, the state intends to begin construction on the long-delayed Nairobi-Mombasa highway expansion in 2027, with preliminary engineering designs and feasibility assessments already concluded.

![Road construction and tarmac laying along the Rironi-Mau Summit highway near the Ngarariga Interchange.](https://i.ibb.co/LdpXgXS9/2ad7b0c8ad0c.webp)

By concentrating the procurement and ground-level execution of these high-capital corridors into the first half of 2027, transport agencies face an unprecedented delivery peak across multiple regions at once.

### County Commitments on Gusii Tour

The infrastructure timeline served as the centrepiece of Ruto's four-day tour through Nyamira and Kisii counties, where regional leadership, including Kisii Governor Simba Arati, met national executive teams.

Alongside the regional trunk road, the administration packaged local capital pledges scheduled across the same horizon. In Nyamira, Ruto pledged the construction of two sports facilities—a Ksh1 billion stadium in Magwagwa alongside upgrades to Nyamaiya Stadium.

The tour also outlined capital allocations to upgrade Nyamira County Referral Hospital to Level 5 status, while putting the operational target for Nyamira University at September 2027, when the institution is slated to admit its first intake of students.

Whether these regional works meet their timelines now depends on KeNHA and private concessionaires hitting their handovers on the eastern approach to Mau Summit over the next twelve months.
