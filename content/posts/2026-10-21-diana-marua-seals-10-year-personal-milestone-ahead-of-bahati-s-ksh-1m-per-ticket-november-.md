Digital content creator and lifestyle influencer Diana Marua marked Tuesday, October 20, 2026, as the precise emotional anchor of her decade-long relationship with musician Kevin Bahati, declaring that the couple had officially hit the 10-year mark ahead of their upcoming formal wedding.

Posting across her social media accounts on the date, Marua designated October 20 as the personal anniversary date that locks in their decade together, creating an emotional baseline just 17 days before their scheduled formal nuptials on November 6, 2026.

"It’s a decade of love with my man. I’m literally over the moon! It’s official… We’ve Clocked 10 Years Today!" Marua wrote in her statement. "20th October will forever be a special date in my heart and in my life. This Man has Loved me Right."

Marua emphasized her personal contentment with the journey, addressing her followers directly while looking ahead to future decades alongside the musician.

"All that matters is that I’m HAPPY! I can’t wait to celebrate 10 more Decades with you, my Love. Happy 10th Anniversary, my King," she wrote.

The October 20 milestone serves as the immediate precursor to a formal wedding ceremony structured around unusually tight guest and financial parameters. On October 7, 2026, Bahati publicly revealed that their November 6 ceremony would abandon conventional large-scale celebrity guest lists in favor of an exclusive gathering capped at exactly 100 people.

Under the terms announced by the singer, entry to the formal wedding requires an invitation ticket priced at Ksh 1 million per guest. Bahati framed the expensive formalization as the ultimate milestone gesture after a decade of continuous partnership.

"Finally, it’s time to mark a decade of love," Bahati explained during the October 7 reveal. "My love, I give you gifts to celebrate you every year, and this year we are celebrating 10 years, and instead of giving you 10 gifts, I have decided to make it official."

Marua welcomed her husband's decision, expressing public gratitude for his move to formalize their union following years of public anticipation and online speculation surrounding their marital status.

Over the last ten years, Marua and Bahati have operated as one of Kenya’s most visible celebrity couples, consistently documenting their personal life, parenting milestones, business ventures, and career evolutions before a massive digital audience. That online visibility has transformed their relationship into a central feature of local pop culture coverage.

With the October 20 personal anniversary officially logged in public, attention now shifts to the final two-week countdown leading into the November 6 invitation-only ceremony.
