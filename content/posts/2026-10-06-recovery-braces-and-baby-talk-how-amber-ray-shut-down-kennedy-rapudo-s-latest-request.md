While Rapudo greeted her at the airport with a bouquet of flowers and stepped into the role of caregiver—assisting with her post-operative garments—he also kept the pressure on for a sibling to young Africanah.

“Baba Africanah, leave me alone. Giving you another child is something that will be hard to convince me to do,” Amber fired back during the exchange. Pointing to the physical toll of her procedures, she noted the contradiction in pushing for a new pregnancy when he was already actively complaining about helping her navigate her daily recovery gear. 

The domestic tug-of-war highlights the immediate physical aftermath of Amber's extensive trip abroad. In a single sitting in Istanbul, the content creator underwent breast augmentation to restore fullness following breastfeeding, 360-degree midsection liposuction, and a calf fat transfer utilizing harvested fat from the procedure. She also underwent a dental makeover involving 21 teeth, with plans for a future double-chin reduction once her current healing process concludes.

Rapudo had initially voiced reservations about the cosmetic journey, arguing that his partner was already striking before the trip. Yet, his reservations gave way to hands-on support at home, even if that assistance came paired with ongoing teasing about future family milestones.

While Rapudo has previously made it clear he would welcome a younger brother for Africanah, Amber's stance on expanding their household has evolved over different seasons and luxury conditions. For now, however, the math of their blended family—two boys and two girls making up a household of four—remains firmly set in stone.

Refusing to let the final word rest with Amber's current recovery constraints, Rapudo left the door open with a grin, telling her to wait and see how she feels once she steps out in her new summer body.
