Kenya Power has scheduled an eight-hour electricity interruption affecting major commercial and residential hubs along Kiambu’s Eastern Bypass corridor on Saturday, November 7, 2026, as the utility company undertakes routine grid maintenance.

The planned service shutdown targets the high-traffic Kamakis and Versity Ville entertainment, retail, and service belt, alongside residential enclaves in Kinoo and agricultural zones in Tinganga. A separate, shorter maintenance blackout is scheduled for parts of Nyeri County on the same day.

According to an official public notice issued on Friday, November 6, the maintenance routine in Kiambu will run from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, cutting power to hundreds of businesses, retail Outlets, fuel stations, and learning institutions during prime Saturday operating hours.

### The Kamakis and Eastern Bypass Corridor Cutoff

The most expansive interruption in Kiambu County targets the Bypass Kamakis and Versity Ville circuit. The eight-hour blackout will affect major commercial centers, leisure facilities, and suburban developments stretching from Exit 11 through Silicon Valley.

Specific commercial hubs and landmarks listed in the utility company's maintenance schedule include:

* **Retail and Commercial Landmarks:** Quickmart 182, Naivas 182, Wisdom Towers, Bypass Corner, Green Valley, and Silicon Valley.
* **Fuel and Transport Hubs:** Galana Petrol Station, Shell Bypass, and Total Karuguru.
* **Leisure and Community Spots:** Green Sport, Exit 11, Bamboo Street, Gatong’ora Kwa Chief, Ebenezer, Key West, Fairview, and parts of Sunrise.

Businesses along the strip relying on continuous refrigeration, electronic POS systems, or high-capacity cooking setups will need to run on private generator power throughout the daytime maintenance window.

### Kinoo and Tinganga Service Disruptions

Beyond the Eastern Bypass belt, Kenya Power announced simultaneous eight-hour outages for two other key Kiambu sectors.

In Kinoo, the maintenance crew will work on lines serving Gaitumbi, Kinco Kwa Chief, and immediate adjacent domestic and commercial customers between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm.

In Tinganga, the interruption impacts both processing infrastructure and community institutions. Facilities listed in the formal notice include:

* **Industrial:** Sasini Factory.
* **Schools:** Tinganga Secondary School and Tinganga Primary School.
* **Shopping and Residential Hubs:** Tinganga Shopping Centre, Kagongo, Ngemwa, Nyautto, and adjacent neighborhood connections.

### Four-Hour Shutdown Scheduled for Nyeri

In Nyeri County, Kenya Power has planned a shorter maintenance window affecting parts of Witima and Ichamama. Unlike the eight-hour Kiambu blackout, the Nyeri interruption is scheduled to last four hours, beginning at 9:00 am and clearing by 1:00 pm.

The Nyeri maintenance work affects both local power generation infrastructure and trading centers, including:

* **Power Infrastructure:** Gikira Hydro Power.
* **Educational Institutions:** Witima Secondary School.
* **Markets and Trading Centers:** Witima Market, Kagumo Market, Gatugi Market, Gaikundo Market, Ichamama, and Ichambugi.

Kenya Power stated that the planned maintenance exercises are essential for routine grid upkeep and network stabilization, with power restoration expected to follow the stated 1:00 pm timeline for Nyeri and 5:00 pm for Kiambu.
