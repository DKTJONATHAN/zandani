**NAIROBI, Kenya** - Socialite Amber Ray has publicly addressed skepticism regarding her recent medical procedures in Turkey, confirming she underwent three distinct surgeries and dental work. The public response follows online discourse questioning her rapid post-operative mobility as captured on her social media platforms.

**What we know:**
* Amber Ray underwent three separate surgical procedures and dental work during her recent trip to Turkey.
* The surgeries included the installation of breast implants and a fat transfer procedure to her lower legs.
* The socialite reports she is currently managing her recovery without oral pain medication, utilizing only prescribed antibiotic creams.
* She attributes her recovery progress to a combination of rest and the use of a health supplement, STC30, which she began taking two weeks prior to the surgeries.

Addressing critics who suggested her activity levels were inconsistent with major surgery, Amber Ray stated, "I've seen some people saying, 'The way Amber is up and about, ni kama hakufanyiwa surgery.’ Mind you, I did three surgeries plus my teeth! This video was actually my very first walk after surgery."

Regarding her post-operative regimen, the socialite noted that she is prioritizing rest while listening to her body’s physical requirements. "The reason I'm able to move around is because I genuinely have the energy, but I'm definitely still resting as much as I can," she added.

The socialite confirmed that the additional fat transfer to her calves was an unplanned decision made while she was already under anesthesia, following a recommendation from her surgeon to achieve a more balanced aesthetic. She remains under follow-up observation as part of her recovery process.
