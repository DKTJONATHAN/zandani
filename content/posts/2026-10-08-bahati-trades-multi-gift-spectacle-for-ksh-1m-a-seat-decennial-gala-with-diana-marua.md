Musician Kevin Bahati is closing the curtain on his trademark public gift-giving marathons, choosing instead to mark a decade of partnership with content creator Diana Marua by locking their anniversary behind a KSh 1 million entry fee.

For years, Bahati’s playbook around personal milestones followed a predictable digital cycle: serialized online reveals where he handed Diana strings of high-end consumer gifts, ranging from vehicles and title deeds to customized jewellery, matched to whatever year of relationship they were celebrating. That decade-long gifting cadence has now been formally retired in favor of an exclusive, black-tie gala targeted at a very small, well-heeled audience.

The pair revealed the shift through a polished, cinematic production set against a lakeside dock. Dressed in formal evening wear, the couple used a scripted exchange to tease the announcement before presenting a black-and-gold motion-graphic invitation branded simply as *The Invite*.

### Capping the Room at 100 Guests

The financial premise of the celebration rests on deliberate scarcity. Rather than hosting an open concert venue or an expansive industry red-carpet reception, the couple has capped the guest list at exactly 100 attendees. 

At a fixed rate of KSh 1 million per seat, the event outlines a gross footprint of KSh 100 million for a single evening. The steep price tag shifts the nature of their relationship content from free-to-view mass digital spectacle to an ultra-private social club for high-net-worth acquaintances, business associates, and select peers capable of meeting the seven-figure threshold.

During the lakeside reveal, Bahati addressed the scale of the transition directly to Diana, reflecting on the milestone and the time it took to arrive at this tier of celebration.

"After 10 years together, I hope I have not taken so long," Bahati said during the video rollout.

Diana brushed off the hesitation, framing the decade not by elapsed time but by deliberate pacing.

"Baby, time doesn’t matter as long as we do it the right way," she replied. "And time doesn’t matter as long as you commit your forever to me."

### Retiring the 10-Gift Format

The pivot represents an intentional break from Bahati’s previous anniversary strategy. The singer had previously established a personal precedent of buying Diana ten separate luxury gifts to commemorate their time together, a content formula that drove millions of views across their digital platforms year after year.

For their tenth anniversary, Bahati noted that the decennial marker required an unprecedented gesture that could not be replicated through store-bought handouts. Moving to a KSh 1 million-a-plate model moves the couple’s brand into a different operational territory, relying on exclusivity and private access rather than the sheer volume of material presents displayed for public admiration.

While the video firmly established the KSh 1 million admission baseline and the 100-seat ceiling, details regarding the specific venue and the itinerary for the black-tie evening were withheld on *The Invite*, maintaining the cloistered tone the couple is attaching to their entry into a second decade together.
