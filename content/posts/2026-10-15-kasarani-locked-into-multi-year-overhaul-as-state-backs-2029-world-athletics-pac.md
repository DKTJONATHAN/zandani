Renovation works at Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani will not conclude with the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, with the government confirming the complex will immediately transition into a second round of structural improvements to stage the 2029 World Athletics Championships.

The extended construction horizon emerged following bilateral talks between President William Ruto and World Athletics President Sebastian Coe at Eldoret State Lodge, where the state committed to meeting a three-point compliance benchmark governing venue technicalities, state funding guarantees, and anti-doping enforcement.

Kasarani is already undergoing comprehensive structural rehabilitation to satisfy Confederation of African Football (CAF) requirements ahead of the 2027 continental football tournament. However, Ruto noted that track-and-field requirements for a senior World Athletics event will necessitate additional engineering and facility installations once the football showcase wraps up.

"The Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani Stadium is already undergoing major improvements for AFCON 2027, and will further be improved for the World Athletics Championships 2029," Ruto stated, pledging that executive machinery will underwrite all logistical requirements.

### The Three-Pillar Hosting Pact

The Eldoret State Lodge meeting established three non-negotiable operational requirements Kenya must fulfill to retain the rights to the global competition:

* **Guaranteed State Financing:** Direct treasury allocations to fund operations, venue readiness, and local organizing committee logistics.
* **Stadium Compliance:** Additional facility-specific modifications at Kasarani beyond standard football retrofits, tailored to premier track-and-field technical regulations.
* **Anti-Doping Backing:** State-backed strengthening of testing, intelligence, and enforcement frameworks to align with international integrity standards.

Coe endorsed the state’s executive assurances, framing Nairobi's selection as a milestone that must catalyze broader athletics development across the continent. Beyond civil works, Coe emphasized that operational success would hinge on local fan mobilization.

"I call upon Kenya to uphold its stellar reputation for turning out in large numbers for our sport by having a sold-out stadium every night to create an electric atmosphere for our athletes," Coe said.

### The Road to 2029

Nairobi won the right to stage the biennial competition on September 15, 2026, when World Athletics selected the Kenyan bid over rival proposals from London, Rome, and Munich. 

The win established Nairobi as the first African host city in the history of the championships. The governing body relied on Kenya's recent track record of staging international tier-one track events, including the 2017 World Under-18 Championships, the 2021 World Under-20 Championships, and the annual Kip Keino Classic circuit meet.

The global hosting calendar will see the championships visit Beijing in 2027 before moving to Nairobi in 2029, with Munich scheduled to stage the 2031 edition.
