Pastor Dorcas Rigathi has spent decades watching the country argue over her husband’s sharp tongue and combative political presence, but inside their home, she insists the man known across Kenya as ‘Riggy G’ is something entirely different.

“He may look very hard out there, but inside, he’s the best man you can ever… He’s a teddy bear,” Dorcas said during a televised interview that aired on Sunday, November 15, 2026. 

Addressing the scrutiny that followed former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua through his rise, his contentious tenure, and his dramatic October 2024 impeachment, Dorcas leaned heavily into the personal mechanics of their 37-year marriage. Rather than presenting a polite, detached portrait of a political spouse, she dismantled the tough-guy caricature and laid out the domestic habits that have sustained the union since their university days.

Chief among those habits is an uncompromising communication baseline. Dorcas revealed that throughout nearly four decades of marriage—stretching from campus activism into the high-stakes corridors of national politics—her husband has never left her hanging on the telephone line.

“For the 37 years, I can tell you, there is no one time I’ve ever called him and he didn’t return my call when he saw it,” she said. “If he’s in a meeting, he will do that. For his children, the same.”

### The Clapback on Tagging Along

Dorcas also turned her sights on public critics who have frequently commented on her constant presence at Gachagua’s side, questioning why she routinely flanks him at public events, church podiums, and travel stops.

Refusing to apologize for stepping into his political slipstream, she framed her visibility as basic spousal loyalty rooted in history rather than optics.

“I love this man very much. I don’t deny it,” Dorcas remarked. “And that is why people are having a problem with me. They even think I go out with him too much. They want him to go with who? He’s my husband.”

Her support dates back to student union corridors, where she watched him take on leadership battles long before national campaigns put a spotlight on their household. That enduring alliance, she explained, made standing beside him during his political turbulence a settled obligation rather than a calculated posture.

### Okanga Takes the Debate to Wamunyoro

The candid sit-down quickly spilled beyond television sets on Monday, November 16, 2026, when political activist Nuru Okanga appeared directly at the Gachagua residence in Wamunyoro. 

Posing alongside Dorcas, Okanga took to Facebook to turn her public defense into a direct comparison with State House, asking whether First Lady Mama Rachel Ruto would ever match such vocal public loyalty for President William Ruto.

“Can Mama Rachael defend Ruto the way Mama Boys defended Riggy G in that interview?” Okanga wrote. 

Okanga, who noted that he had listened closely to her advocacy on the boy child, framed Gachagua’s marriage as a rare asset in Kenyan public life. “Hapo nayo Gachagua alipata bibi na nusu. Full of wisdom! Where is your Dorcas?” he added.

### Two Years of Quiet With State House

Yet for all the outward confidence Dorcas projected about her household, her interview offered a sobering measure of the personal fallout left by the 2024 political rupture between Ruto and Gachagua.

Asked about her current ties to Mama Rachel Ruto—a figure with whom she once shared high-profile prayer breakfasts and joint national platforms—Dorcas revealed the two women have not spoken since before Gachagua’s impeachment proceedings unfolded in October 2024. 

The admission confirmed nearly two years of unbroken personal silence between the former second lady and the sitting First Lady.

Even so, Dorcas avoided personal acrimony when pressed on the freeze. She maintained that the lapse in communication had not erased their underlying history.

“She’s still my friend,” Dorcas said, adding that she remains convinced Rachel would still pick up her call if she dialed, and noting that the distance had not stopped her personal intercessions: “I pray for her.”
