DJ Bonez has stepped away from the digital whisper mill to make one thing crystal clear: he is off the market and staying put.

Relaxing in a tropical-themed lounge during a quickfire Q&A session posted to TikTok by user @graceyuge, the veteran selector addressed swirling speculation regarding his marriage to media personality Kamene Goro just a day after online chatter suggested trouble in paradise.

“I’m married. I’m a married person, so I’m not dating anyone,” Bonez told the camera, firmly shutting down any notion that he was single or looking. 

The impromptu interview offered a rare, grounded look into the mechanics of their high-profile union, which spans six years. Asked how married life has treated him, Bonez offered a pragmatic assessment rather than a polished fairy tale. 

“It’s not that easy, but I’m there walking in the park,” he said, before offering a piece of unvarnished counsel for anyone contemplating a walk down the aisle: “It’s not easy, and you have to believe in yourself, trust yourself.”

When the quickfire format turned to his wider following and the attention that comes with it, Bonez met the subject with equal candor. Admitting that “ladies love me a lot,” he immediately dismissed any implication of impropriety, anchoring his response back to his commitment at home.

The TikTok clip brings a definitive halt to a 24-hour cycle of internet speculation that began after Goro posted remarks hinting at domestic friction. For now, the couple's six-year partnership remains intact as Bonez continues to field the spotlight from the comfort of his lounge chair.
