Private screen habits on handheld devices emerged as the primary driver behind Kenya’s digital traffic shifts in October 2026, pulling adult entertainment platforms ahead of major social networking feeds.

According to web analytics data published by Semrush, smartphones accounted for roughly 7.47 million of the 7.72 million total visits recorded by XVideos in Kenya during the month. That mobile concentration represented 96.81 percent of the site's local traffic, while desktop computers accounted for just 3.19 percent, or approximately 246,100 visits.

The overwhelming reliance on mobile access helped propel XVideos into fifth place among all websites visited nationwide in October, placing it directly ahead of mainstream social media platforms. Over the same period, Facebook recorded 5.36 million web visits, Instagram logged 4.59 million, X (formerly Twitter) drew 4.12 million, and TikTok registered 3.52 million web visits.

At the apex of Kenya’s overall web traffic rankings, search and core communication platforms maintained a dominant lead. Google ranked first with 92.05 million monthly visits, followed by YouTube in second place with 36.56 million. Artificial intelligence platform ChatGPT occupied third position with 9.80 million visits, while WhatsApp held fourth place with 8.02 million visits.

### Double-Digit Spikes and Alternative Sites

The October metrics reflected a sharp month-on-month rebound for adult platforms in Kenya. XVideos posted a 34.37 percent increase in visits compared to September 2026, although its total traffic volume remained 10.54 percent lower than the figures logged in October 2025.

Other international adult platforms registered similar double-digit growth during the same timeframe. Pornhub recorded 5.66 million visits—a 30.92 percent gain from September—while XHamster logged 2.57 million visits, representing a 31.51 percent month-on-month rise. Smaller specialized destinations recorded more modest footprints, with OnlyFans drawing approximately 282,700 visits and RedTube recording 125,390 visits.

### Regional Divergence Across East Africa

The Semrush dataset also highlights contrasting online priorities across neighboring East African markets. While Kenyan web traffic reflected heavy mobile engagement with adult platforms alongside search engines and AI tools, internet users in Tanzania and Uganda demonstrated a stronger preference for digital sports betting hubs.

In Tanzania, Google led with 15.26 million visits and YouTube followed with 8.03 million, but sports betting operator SportyBet secured third place with 5.47 million visits, outranking ChatGPT's 3.50 million visits. In Uganda, Google logged 28.26 million visits and YouTube recorded 10.89 million, while betting platform BetPawa attracted 4.13 million visits.

Semrush compiles its Traffic Analytics estimates through proprietary algorithms and external data streams analyzing over 200 million anonymized internet users worldwide. The metrics track total session visits rather than unique individual users, meaning repeated visits by a single user count toward the monthly aggregate.
