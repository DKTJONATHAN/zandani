NAIROBI - Media personality and preacher Dr. Ofweneke has publicly criticized local church leaders for suspending congregants who attended the recent Rhema Fest at Uhuru Park, characterizing the disciplinary actions as evidence of a fractured Christian community.

In his address, Dr. Ofweneke argued that the internal divisions within the church contrast sharply with the cohesion seen in other religious groups. He contended that the focus on denominational loyalty is antithetical to the concept of the "body of Christ," urging leaders to move away from exclusionary tactics.

"It's not the leg of Christ. It's not the chest of Christ. It is not the kidney of Christ. It's the body of Christ," Dr. Ofweneke stated.

The preacher further suggested that the reliance on prosperity-focused sermons-specifically promises of miraculous financial multiplication-has left many local congregations underserved. He argued that this lack of spiritual depth is the primary reason international ministers, such as Apostle Joshua Selman and Pastor Joshua Oropo, command larger audiences than many local figures.

"If you don't spend enough time with God and in prayer and in the word of God, you will not get enough revelation for this generation," he added.

The Rhema Fest, which featured a mix of international and local speakers including Lady Bishop Kathy Kiuna and Pastor George Masharia, has become a flashpoint for a broader debate on the state of the Kenyan church. Dr. Ofweneke concluded by calling on the clergy to abandon professional jealousy and return to foundational spiritual ministry to remain relevant to contemporary worshippers.
