Embakasi East Member of Parliament Babu Owino has thrown down a public gauntlet to former Nairobi Chief Officer Geoffrey Mosiria, demanding the immediate physical appearance of the woman at the center of recent rumors concerning a secret second family.

Addressing the viral claims during an interview, the lawmaker dismissed the gossip while insisting that his wife, Frida Muthoni Ongili, remains entirely unbothered by the chatter. According to the MP, his spouse's composure stems from a solid understanding of his character.

“My wife is not concerned about that because she knows me; she knows the person she got married to. I am married to Frida Muthoni Ongili, okay? That is my wife,” Babu stated.

Rather than stopping at a verbal denial, the legislator escalated the exchange by challenging Mosiria to put his claims to a public test. He demanded that any purported second wife be brought out into the open for all to see.

“Any other wife I am being given by Mosiria, I want that wife aatolewe in public aletwe,” the MP said.

Shifting from the immediate controversy, Babu linked his stance on family to his childhood. Recalling his formative years, the lawmaker noted that he was raised by a single mother following the death of his father when he was in Class Three. He credited that female-headed household with giving him a firsthand appreciation of the financial and emotional hurdles single mothers navigate to feed and clothe their children.

Reflecting further on parental responsibility, the MP maintained that his background ensures he takes a hard line against child neglect, vowing that he would never walk away from any of his offspring.

“As Babu Owino, I cannot leave any of my children huko nje. How much can a child eat in a day?” he added, underscoring his position on providing for his dependents.

The exchange leaves the narrative resting on whether any tangible evidence will follow the initial online claims.
