**NAIROBI, September 11, 2026** - Comedian Jaymoh Decin has publicly criticized US-based content creator Malcom for failing to promote his upcoming live show, "Sudhee Experience," following a recurring pattern of influencers ignoring local projects while prioritizing death-related content.

**What we know:**
*   **The Conflict:** Jaymoh Decin publicly questioned Malcom after the latter posted a tribute to a deceased TikTok creator, despite Jaymoh having sent multiple outreach DMs over a three-month period requesting promotional support for his show.
*   **The Event:** The "Sudhee Experience" is scheduled for September 12, 2026, at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC).
*   **International Lineup:** The event features acts from Uganda, Rwanda, and Tanzania, including performer Uncle Zala.
*   **Public Reception:** Jaymoh’s initial social media call-out regarding the lack of support for active artists gained significant traction, accumulating over 1,300 likes.

Jaymoh addressed the media today, arguing that the entertainment industry’s tendency to prioritize tragedy over active, living artists is a systemic issue. He stated that his outreach to Malcom was an attempt to establish a reciprocal support system, noting that he frequently supports peers like YY Comedian, Njugush, and Nadia Mukami.

During the briefing, Jaymoh cited Otile Brown as an example of industry solidarity, noting that the artist reached out to him personally for a private engagement.

Reflecting on the financial stakes, Jaymoh admitted that financing major events like the KICC showcase often requires significant personal investment. He maintains that his critique is not born of entitlement, but a call for consistent mutual support among Kenyan creatives.
