Content creator Chuka Kenyan Prince has reignited public discourse surrounding his romantic intentions after posting a fresh declaration of affection for fellow personality Sheryl Gabriella, notwithstanding her prior public rejection of his advances.

In a video disseminated across social media platforms this Friday, Chuka was captured holding a photograph of Ms. Gabriella while affirming that his sentiments remain unchanged despite her previous decision to decline his pursuits. “Sheryl Gabriella nilisema nakupenda ukanikataa… hata unikatae bado nakupenda,” Chuka stated in the clip.

This latest development follows a series of earlier public overtures made by Chuka toward Ms. Gabriella, which have consistently drawn significant attention from online audiences. The dynamic has often been characterized by social media commentators as a one-sided pursuit, with observers frequently employing the term "simp" to describe the nature of his persistent public messaging.

The reaction to the video has been characterized by a mixture of public teasing and critical commentary. While some users treated the declaration as light-hearted entertainment, others challenged the creator to demonstrate the sincerity of his words. One commenter suggested that Chuka should "choraa tatoo ya jina lake kwa kifua ndio tuamini" (tattoo her name on your chest so we can believe you), while others referenced previous, unfulfilled claims regarding his financial status and ability to procure high-end transport.

Ms. Gabriella has yet to issue a formal response to this latest video. As of the time of reporting, the content remains accessible on Chuka’s social media channels, continuing to draw a steady stream of user engagement and debate regarding the boundaries of public romantic gestures in the digital age.
