**NAIROBI, September 13, 2026** - Local tech firm Kwetu eSIM has launched an expanded regional data roaming service, eliminating the need for physical SIM card swaps across more than 200 international destinations. The service, which targets multi-country travellers, allows users to purchase and install a single eSIM profile via QR code, which then automatically switches between partner networks as the user crosses borders.

The user interface centers on a one-time QR code installation. Once the eSIM profile is loaded, it remains dormant until the device detects a supported network within the purchased region. Data allowances are pooled across the regional bundle, meaning a user traversing multiple countries-such as Italy and Croatia-does not need to refresh their settings or purchase new credit upon arrival. Top-ups are handled within the existing profile, ensuring continuity of service without the need for additional technical configuration.

**Confirmation of Costs**
Regional pricing varies by coverage area:
*   **Asia:** Starting at $1.19 (17 destinations).
*   **Europe+:** Starting at $2.39 (36 destinations).
*   **Balkans:** Starting at $4.49 (12 destinations).
*   **Latin America:** Starting at $5.10 (12 destinations).
