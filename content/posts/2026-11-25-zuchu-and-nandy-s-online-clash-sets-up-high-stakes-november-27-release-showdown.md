Zuchu has pulled back the curtain on her strained relationship with Nandy, using an online exchange to air a laundry list of grievances just as both artists prepare for a major career collision on the charts.

The public airing brought forth several specific accusations from Zuchu, who claimed that Nandy has been copying her beauty styles, cultivating relationships with her former manager Doris, and deliberately timing performance schedules to clash with her own events. The digital back-and-forth also dragged up a past controversy surrounding an alcohol endorsement deal that Zuchu previously turned down, a business dispute that first surfaced in audio clips back in 2022.

Nandy appeared to brush off the accusations with a public, sarcastic query questioning why Zuchu chose to turn the simple announcement of her album tracklist into an extensive media tour.

Beneath the social media sparring lies a concrete scheduling conflict that has sharpened the rivalry. Nandy is set to drop her upcoming album, *911*, on November 27. That exact date happens to be the day designated for a major birthday celebration hosted by Zuchu and her partner, Diamond Platnumz, setting up a split in public attention across the Tanzanian entertainment scene.

Following the online barbs, Zuchu challenged her peer to address her directly rather than resorting to veiled social media remarks. The public friction stands in sharp contrast to the warmer optics seen only months earlier. In September, during celebrations marking the 40th day after the birth of Diamond and Zuchu’s daughter, Nandy publicly downplayed any bad blood, stating there was no reason for them to dislike each other and even hinting that a long-awaited collaboration between the two was in active development.

That brief thaw has now given way to renewed scrutiny over a rivalry that dates back nearly a decade. Both singers first crossed paths while navigating the early stages of Tanzania's music industry, competing in a 2016 contest where Nandy finished as the first runner-up. While Nandy secured an earlier career breakthrough, Zuchu eventually found her footing under Diamond Platnumz’s WCB Wasafi stable, intertwining their personal lives—with Nandy married to fellow artist Billnass and Zuchu linked to Diamond—alongside their professional trajectories.

With both camps pushing major projects forward—including Zuchu navigating recent disruptions on stage during the Wasafi Festival pre-party in Mbeya Town and Nandy positioning *911* as a milestone release—the November 27 calendar overlap remains the definitive focal point of their ongoing professional friction.
