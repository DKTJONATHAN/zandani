Zari Hassan marked her 46th birthday weekend by enforcing an unyielding standard of personal control, both at the threshold of her party venue and across her public platforms. The Ugandan entrepreneur and mother of five took a zero-tolerance stance against guests breaking her formal dress code before stepping online to shut down persistent public auditing of her age.

During her birthday celebration on Saturday, September 26, 2026, Zari set a strict visual tone, wearing a white ensemble highlighted with black accents. When attendees arrived failing to meet the event's mandatory formal mandate, the socialite did not hesitate to activate her venue security team, ordering them to turn away anyone attempting to enter in casual wear.

"Jeans and T-shirts? Sorry, you are not my type. Security, please do your job. Thank you very much," she remarked during the event.

### Confronting the Digital Age Auditors

The boundary enforcement extended beyond the party perimeter into her digital spaces the following day. On Sunday, September 27, 2026, Zari published a video addressing commentators who routinely use the ages of her eldest grown sons to challenge her official birth year.

Rejecting assertions that raising adult children makes it mathematically impossible for her to be 46, she explained that her journey into motherhood began in her late teenage years, meaning she effectively matured alongside her firstborns.

"Guys, there is no reason for me to lie about how old I am just because I have big boys. I had babies when I was young, and I have been growing up with them," Zari stated, addressing critics who insist her maternal timeline demands an older age bracket. "So when you look at my kids and say, ‘No, she must be too old,’ just know that I got babies when I was a baby myself. But I hope it makes you feel better if I say I am 100 years old."

### Unbothered at 46

Rather than deflecting or downplaying her age, the business mogul reaffirmed her satisfaction with reaching 46, noting that young motherhood was a factual chapter of her life rather than an issue to conceal. She added that she remains completely confident in her appearance and personal standing.

"I am feeling so gorgeous at 46 years of age. I do not have to lie about my age," she stated.

Her husband, Shakib Lutaaya, backed her presence at the celebration, attending in an all-black suit styled with gold accents. Taking to his public channels, Lutaaya posted a tribute honoring his wife on her milestone day.

"Help me wish my beautiful wife a very happy birthday! May this new year bring you endless happiness, love, blessings, and everything your heart desires. Happy birthday to you, queen! Love you always," Lutaaya wrote.

For Zari, the birthday weekend reaffirmed a long-standing personal policy: whether managing guest lists at high-society venues or setting the record straight on her personal timeline, the entrepreneur continues to control her narrative on her own terms.
