Vehicle owners seeking to comply with Kenya’s transport safety regulations now face an automated countdown clock built directly into the state’s digital licensing portal.

Under operational guidelines issued by the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA), public service vehicle (PSV) and commercial fleet owners who apply for speed limiter fittings on the Intelligent Road Safety Management System (IRSMS) have exactly seven days to physically present their vehicles to an authorized vendor. If an owner misses that window, the digital application automatically expires, forcing the operator to restart the registration process from the beginning.

### Thresholds and Portal Integration

The mandate targets two specific categories of road transport: passenger vehicles carrying seven or more passengers and commercial cargo haulers with a tare weight of 3,048 kilogrammes or more.

![An undated Image of vehicles at an inspection centre in Nairobi during a past exercise.](https://i.ibb.co/JjzSwnQX/bfb2e384a980.webp)

To initiate compliance, vehicle owners must log onto the IRSMS platform (`irsmowners.ntsa.go.ke`) using their Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) PIN and national identification credentials. Account creation requires verification through a one-time password sent to the owner’s registered mobile phone number.

Once authenticated, the system interfaces directly with the Transport Integrated Management System (TIMS) database to pull up eligible vehicles linked to that owner. Operators then select the specific vehicle requiring a speed limiter and assign it to an NTSA-licensed vendor listed on the platform.

### Telemetry Verification and Vendor Transfer Rules

The digital framework moves enforcement away from static physical certificates toward continuous data verification. After fitting the governor, the vendor cannot issue a digital fitting certificate immediately. Instead, the device must continuously transmit speed telemetry data back to the system for at least 15 minutes.

Only after the system confirms uninterrupted data transmission does it release a digital fitting certificate, which remains valid for 12 months. 

![matatu-motorists-ngara-traffic.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/RThf9XSq/57292e759194.webp)

To prevent operators from using non-compliant hardware suppliers, the portal dynamically manages the vendor selection list. Providers whose licenses have been suspended are automatically scrubbed from the active registry, blocking vehicle owners from selecting them during the application phase.

For vehicle owners who need to change their selected vendor after submitting an application, NTSA has embedded a online transfer request system. Once the authority grants approval for a vendor swap, the vehicle owner is given a strict 72-hour window to complete the transfer and take the vehicle to the new installer.

### Operational Support and Compliance Access

NTSA has directed operators needing technical assistance with portal navigation or TIMS record matching to utilize its central help desk channels, supplying support contact through `irsmsqueries@ntsa.go.ke` or the dedicated telephone line `0709932300`.

With the system actively invalidating stale online bookings, transport operators face immediate pressure to coordinate vendor appointments prior to triggering their portal applications, ensuring physical fleets are ready for installation before the seven-day digital window closes.
