Nairobi’s prime hospitality corridor along Mombasa Road—headlined by luxury landmarks Panari Hotel and Ole Sereni—faces an eight-hour electricity grid shutdown on Sunday, November 22, 2026, as Kenya Power undertakes scheduled system maintenance.

The planned interruption will run from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, freezing mains electricity supply across major corporate headquarters, manufacturing plants, health facilities, and dense residential estates in both Nairobi and Uasin Gishu County.

### Mombasa Road and Industrial Corridor Operations Affected

The utility firm confirmed that the Sunday maintenance stretch along Mombasa Road will affect critical commercial facilities, including Airtel headquarters, Diamond Estate, Balozi Estate, Park Side Towers, Express Kenya, DTB, and Equity Bank branches.

Along Likoni Road and Road C, the outage will extend to industrial and commercial operations such as East Africa Spectre, Ideal Paints, Capitol Laundry, Laykish Traders, C&P Shoes, and Park View Apartments.

Further into the capital's Industrial Area, the power cut will cover the full length of Enterprise Road, Likoni Road, and Migwani Road. Heavy manufacturing and processing sites slated for the grid isolation include Tetra Pak, Steel Africa, Styroplast Ltd, Fine Spinners, Hannan Tissue, Paper Converters, Bins Ltd, Rollmill, Panafric Transformers, Mahindra, and Omar Water.

Neighbouring residential and community sites within the sector—including Falcon Estate, Falcon Police Post, and Falcon Catholic Church—will also be off the grid for the eight-hour duration.

### Central Business District and Parklands Healthcare Hubs

In central Nairobi, the scheduled maintenance will touch key trading points around the Commercial Centre, Kirinyaga Road, and OTC. Affected commercial premises include Quickmart, Mathai Supermarket, Nyamakina, and retail outlets along part of Kirinyaga Road.

The interruption will also stretch across Racecourse Road, Salvation Army facilities, and Kamukunji Police Station.

North of the city centre, the maintenance schedule hits the Parklands medical and residential district. The outage will cover 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Parklands Avenue, Masari Road, Mpaka Road, and Ring Road.

Key health institutions and commercial centers listed for the shutoff include Avenue Hospital, Doctor’s Plaza, Jalaram, Baywaters, and Shivaji Road.

### Uasin Gishu Maintenance Window

Outside the capital, Kenya Power's North Rift maintenance team will execute a synchronized outage in Uasin Gishu County.

The regional interruption will affect Kamukunji Estate in Eldoret and adjacent customers from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm on Sunday.

Kenya Power has instructed industrial operators, hospitality management teams, medical centers, and residential consumers across all cited zones to make the necessary operational arrangements ahead of the 9:00 am shutoff.
