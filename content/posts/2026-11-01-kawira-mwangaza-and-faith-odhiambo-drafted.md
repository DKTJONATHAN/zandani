Former Meru Governor Kawira Mwangaza and former Law Society of Kenya (LSK) President Faith Odhiambo have been named as proposed deputy party leaders in Edwin Sifuna’s Linda Mwananchi Movement (LMM) Party, leading a high-profile executive lineup designed to give the outfit immediate public weight.

Unveiling the new framework on Friday, October 30, 2026, during the launch of the party’s central secretariat in Nairobi, Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna detailed a restructured governance hierarchy anchored by one party leader and four deputy party leaders ahead of the party's upcoming National Delegates Conference (NDC).

Under the proposed executive roster, Mwangaza and Odhiambo take up two of the senior deputy slots alongside former Mavoko MP Patrick Makau, with the fourth deputy position explicitly reserved for a representative from the Coast region.

"We will be proposing that we structure the party so that we have a party leader and four deputies. For now, we have resolved that Kawira Mwangaza has decided to be one of the party leaders," Sifuna told supporters and officials gathered at the new Nairobi headquarters.

Mwangaza’s inclusion places the former governor back at the center of national political organization, pairing her grass-roots profile with Odhiambo’s legal background.

The high-profile recruitment extends directly into the party’s operational engine. Suba South MP Caroli Omondi has been fronted for the role of Secretary-General, with political commentator Pauline Njoroge tapped as his deputy secretary-general—bringing one of the digital sphere’s most visible commentators directly into the party's administrative core.

On the organizational side, Vihiga Senator Godfrey Osotsi has been nominated as party chairperson, with former Makadara MP Reuben Ndolo selected to serve as his deputy. Kisii Senator Richard Onyonka is proposed to take charge of the finances as National Treasurer, assisted by Abigael Gikonyo as deputy treasurer.

The unveiling at the Nairobi secretariat marks the transition of the Linda Mwananchi Movement into a structured party with a nationwide executive footprint. The entire proposed lineup now awaits final ratification at the upcoming National Delegates Conference, which will formally seal the executive roster and establish the party's operational command.
