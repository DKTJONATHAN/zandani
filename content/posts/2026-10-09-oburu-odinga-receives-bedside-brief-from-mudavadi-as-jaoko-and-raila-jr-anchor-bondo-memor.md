Senator Oburu Odinga spent the dedication of his brother’s mausoleum under strict medical orders to rest, tracking the high-profile ceremony at Kang’o Ka Jaramogi from a hospital bed before receiving a private bedside debrief from Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi.

Mudavadi, who traveled to the hospital immediately after returning from the Bondo gathering, reported finding the Siaya Senator and ODM party leader making steady progress in his recovery. The face-to-face briefing offered the patriarch an immediate account of the state handover, where President William Ruto officially presented the completed resting place of the late Raila Odinga to the family.

### The Bondo Delegation

Oburu’s physical absence from the podium in Bondo had triggered immediate murmurs across the gathering, given his dual role as the head of the Odinga household and the political leader steering the Orange Democratic Movement. 

To bridge the gap, the family turned to its next generation. His nephew, Raila Odinga Jr., stepped forward to formally convey apologies and greetings to the assembled dignitaries. Minutes later, Oburu’s eldest son, Jaoko Oburu, mounted the stage to deliver his father’s written address directly to President Ruto and the attending public.

In the prepared text, Oburu acknowledged that while clinical directives kept him physically isolated in a hospital room, his thoughts remained anchored at the family homestead. He described the mausoleum as a permanent tribute to his late brother’s lifelong commitment to constitutional governance, human rights, and national cohesion.

### Bedside Acknowledgments and State Reassurance

During their hospital meeting, Mudavadi relayed the scale of the ceremony, which drew political delegations and residents from across the country to Kang’o Ka Jaramogi. 

In response, Oburu issued an expression of gratitude toward President Ruto, national leaders, and Nyanza residents who assembled to commemorate the late Prime Minister. He highlighted the collective goodwill extended toward the family as a meaningful gesture honoring Raila's place in Kenya’s democratic history.

Concerns surrounding Oburu's condition had already prompted a public response earlier in the afternoon. Speaking from the podium in Bondo after members of the crowd raised inquiries about the patriarch's whereabouts, President Ruto assured attendees that the veteran senator was receiving specialized medical care and remained fully engaged with ongoing political and community developments.

With Mudavadi's hospital stop concluded and the family's public representation anchored by Jaoko and Raila Jr., Oburu remains under mandatory rest as he completes his scheduled recovery.
