**MAVOKO, Saturday, September 12, 2026** - Comedian and entrepreneur Sammy Kioko has officially declared his candidacy for the Mavoko parliamentary seat, transitioning from a vocal critic of local government financial management to an active contender for the 2027 General Election.

The declaration took place during a public address where Kioko framed his legislative aspirations as a response to the economic struggles of Mavoko residents. Addressing his supporters, the aspirant detailed his upbringing and the reliance on philanthropic intervention to complete his formal education. He cited the support of missionary John Malloy, who funded his school fees after his parents became unable to meet the costs, as a primary motivation for his "Mbele Pazuri" campaign slogan.

This political pivot arrives months after Kioko utilized social media to challenge the Machakos County administration. In March, he described the receipt of KSh 20,000 as "the highest level of manipulation and intimidation" regarding the KSh 19 million balance he claims is owed for the supply of staff uniforms.

Kioko, who holds qualifications as a teacher, stated that he intends to leverage his experience as a small business owner to address the lack of opportunity for local workers. He maintains that his transition to politics is a direct response to his inability to remain a bystander to the economic difficulties currently impacting the constituency.
