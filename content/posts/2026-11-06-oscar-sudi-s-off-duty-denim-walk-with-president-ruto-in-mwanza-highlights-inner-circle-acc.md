Kapseret MP Oscar Sudi stepped into the role of presidential companion on the shores of Lake Victoria on Thursday, November 5, 2026, pairing a casual white denim jacket and Adidas sneakers with President William Ruto’s structured Kaunda suit during a beachside stroll in Mwanza, Tanzania. The brief video shared by Sudi on Facebook captured a quiet moment of leisure as the Kenyan delegation settled into a luxury hotel ahead of First Daughter Charlene Ruto’s wedding to Tanzanian technology entrepreneur Isaya Yunge.

The contrast in their resort wear highlighted the informal atmosphere of the trip. While Sudi opted for blue jeans, a denim jacket, and sports shoes, President Ruto maintained his traditional ensemble of a Kaunda suit, hat, and ceremonial walking stick. Moving through the hotel grounds, the two legislators paused to converse with hotel guests and pose for photographs, marking a brief interlude in an otherwise packed schedule of family and diplomatic festivities.

### The Road to Mwanza

The Mwanza gathering represents the final leg in a multi-stage marital timeline for Charlene and Yunge, the founder of educational technology platform SomaApps. The cross-border celebrations began months earlier on August 8, 2026, when the two families gathered for a traditional Kalenjin *Koito* ceremony at President Ruto’s Intonna Heritage Farm in Kilgoris, Narok County. That ceremony laid the formal cultural groundwork before activities shifted back to Nairobi and ultimately across the border to the groom's home country.

Prior to departing for Mwanza, the family held a series of private send-off events in Nairobi. On Tuesday, November 3, Charlene hosted a garden-themed bridal shower featuring a white and purple color scheme. The following day, Wednesday, November 4, First Lady Rachel Ruto convened family members and elders for a dedicated blessing and prayer service to anchor the bride before the delegation flew into Tanzania, where Charlene was received with traditional dance troupes and motorcades.

Sudi's presence alongside the President in Tanzania underscores his position within Ruto’s inner political and personal circle, frequently serving as an informal envoy during high-profile family functions. As wedding guests and political allies continue arriving in Mwanza, the cross-border ceremony sets the stage for the formal union of the two families in Tanzania.
