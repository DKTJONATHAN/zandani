Motorists navigating the Westlands corridor face a familiar pattern of congestion this weekend as the Nairobi Expressway shifts its traffic burden onto the lower roads. Starting Saturday, September 19, at 8:00 pm, the elevated overpass deck between James Gichuru Junction and the Central Business District will be closed for scheduled maintenance.

For those accustomed to the speed of the elevated route, the closure-which runs until Monday, September 21, at 12:00 pm-means a mandatory diversion onto Waiyaki Way. While the operator, MOJA Expressway, has maintained that the Westlands entrance will remain functional, the diversion effectively funneling high-volume traffic back into the city’s standard grid is expected to create friction along the busy arterial route.

This closure arrives at a sensitive time for the Westlands traffic network. Just over a week ago, a similar diversion at the ABC Place-Red Hill Road junction resulted in significant snarl-ups, with drivers reporting lengthy delays as the lower road struggled to absorb the unexpected surge of vehicles exiting the toll system.

While the operator has directed motorists to follow the guidance of traffic marshals stationed along the route, the physical capacity of Waiyaki Way remains the primary bottleneck. The decision to restrict access to the overpass deck for 40 hours suggests an intensive maintenance window, but it leaves commuters with little choice but to plan for extended travel times during the weekend period.

MOJA Expressway confirmed that while the overpass will be inaccessible for CBD-bound traffic from James Gichuru, those exiting the expressway toward the overpass are expected to resume normal operations by Saturday at 5:00 pm.

For now, the effectiveness of the traffic management plan depends on how quickly the lower road can accommodate the diverted flow without repeating the gridlock seen earlier this month.
