Public service transport operator ENA Coach has warned passengers arriving from western Kenya to expect substantial transit delays, attributing fleet disruptions to an unresolved traffic snarl-up near St. Mary's Hospital along the Nakuru-Nairobi Highway.

The commercial advisory, published Sunday morning, leaves inbound commuters bearing the cost of long-haul schedule disruptions on Kenya's primary western trunk route, where unmitigated lane congestion forced several motorists to spend the night along the corridor.

According to the long-distance carrier, upcountry services routed through Nakuru encountered severe blockages in the St. Mary's vicinity, throwing scheduled arrival windows into disarray.

"To all our esteemed customers travelling from upcountry via the Nakuru route, please note that we are currently experiencing slight delays due to a traffic snarl-up near St. Mary's Hospital along the Nakuru-Nairobi Highway," the company said in a customer advisory.

![Ena Coach buses trapped in traffic along Nakuru-Nairobi Highway on November 8, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/gbBgmKPk/1dac2d8ee624.webp)

The operator apologized to affected passengers for the disruption, urging patience while assuring travellers that fleet crews were actively working to stabilize trip rotations and maintain passenger safety.

### Bottlenecks on a Fragile Arterial Route

While highway authorities have not established the specific incident that triggered the weekend congestion, the section near St. Mary's Hospital between Gilgil and Elementaita remains an infamous single-carriageway bottleneck. The corridor absorbs heavy mixed traffic, combining cross-border freight with intercity public service vehicles and personal cars returning to the capital.

Any minor breakdown, overlapping manoeuvre, or lane obstruction rapidly cascades into multi-kilometre tailbacks. Sunday's disruption mirrored recurring seasonal snarl-ups that routinely paralyze the corridor during peak travel periods, stranding private motorists and commercial fleets without detour alternatives.

For commercial transport operators like ENA Coach, route blockages ripple across fleet turnaround times. Delays inbound to Nairobi inevitably starve outbound depots of vehicles, forcing terminal managers to reschedule afternoon departures and manage passenger backlogs.

### Dualing Deadlines Approach

The chronic congestion comes against the backdrop of ongoing state-backed infrastructure upgrades intended to eliminate single-lane bottlenecks across the central Rift Valley.

To de-escalate recurrent highway paralysis, the national government commissioned the conversion of the Rironi-Mau Summit corridor into a dual carriageway. The Ministry of Roads and Transport has set December 2026 as the delivery target to open completed dual sections from Rironi to Naivasha to active vehicular traffic, offering immediate capacity relief to the lower segment of the route.

The remainder of the dualing works through to Mau Summit is slated for completion in April 2027, ahead of subsequent development phases planned to extend towards Eldoret and the Malaba border post. 

Until those initial December lanes open to ease high-density weekend passenger flows, long-distance operators remain bound to single-lane transit realities, relying on roadside notices to buffer passengers against volatile trip durations.
