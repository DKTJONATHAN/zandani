Sunday plans across Nairobi’s Parklands commercial and residential corridor, alongside rural outposts in Nyeri County, face an eight-hour utility pause on November 8, 2026, as Kenya Power executes a scheduled grid maintenance shutdown.

The utility operator confirmed that electricity supply will be temporarily turned off from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm to allow technical teams to conduct routine servicing on transmission lines. The planned interruption targets one of Nairobi’s main urban hubs alongside agricultural and residential zones in central Kenya, requiring local residents and business operations to arrange alternative power sources throughout the daytime hours.

### Parklands Corridor Under Eight-Hour Maintenance Window

In Nairobi, the scheduled outage concentrates on parts of Parklands, a major mixed-use sector housing private medical centers, eateries, commercial offices, and dense residential estates. The eight-hour shutdown starting at 9:00 am will affect domestic routines and weekend business activities until targeted supply restoration at 5:00 pm.

Kenya Power stated that the planned work in Parklands is part of ongoing network maintenance intended to reinforce distribution reliability. However, for weekend operations along the commercial stretch, the daytime power pause necessitates advance operational adjustments for establishments that depend on uninterrupted power supply.

### Upcountry Maintenance to Hit Gatunguru and Mioro Outposts

Simultaneously, Kenya Power has designated specific areas in Nyeri County for a parallel maintenance cycle during the exact same eight-hour timeframe.

The upcountry outage will affect customers in Gatunguru, Mioro, and surrounding trading and residential locations. Situated within Nyeri’s agricultural and township network, these areas will experience a complete supply cutoff between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm as utility crews undergo scheduled line servicing.

### Utility Guidelines for Affected Customers

In its official notice, Kenya Power advised both commercial and domestic customers in the affected Parklands enclave and Nyeri locations to plan ahead for the extended power hiatus.

The grid operator urged consumers to put alternative power arrangements in place—such as setting up standby generators or adjusting weekend operational routines—to minimize disruptions to refrigeration, essential services, and business operations during the eight-hour maintenance slot on Sunday, November 8, 2026.
