Across decades of abrasive courtroom battles, constitutional conventions, and national campaigns, Martha Karua’s public posture was rarely associated with compromise. Yet on Sunday evening, the passing of her father, Mwalimu Jackson Karua, prompted a rare convergence of warmth and reverence across Kenya’s fractured political spectrum, honoring the quiet Kirinyaga educator who shaped her resolve.

Jackson Karua died on the evening of Sunday, September 27, at his residence in Kirinyaga County, just hours after his children had gathered at his bedside.

The People’s Liberation Party (PLP) leader had spent the afternoon with her siblings at their rural family home, sharing what turned out to be an unexpected final conversation. The family had sat together singing his favorite gospel hymn before Karua began her drive back to Nairobi. She received the call confirming his death while en route to the capital.

### The Teacher Behind the Advocate

![PLP Leader Martha Karua's father the late Jackson Karua](https://i.ibb.co/zVJkxRQV/0ddb4afbcc33.webp)

In an emotional dispatch released following his death, the former Justice and Constitutional Affairs Minister credited her father’s unbending insistence on schooling and personal ethics as the foundation of her adult trajectory.

"To us, above all, he was a loving father, steady, generous, and present," Karua noted. "He gave us not only his love but also the values by which he lived, and in doing so, left us a legacy far greater than words can capture."

Karua noted that her father remained an immovable backer throughout every chapter of her professional life—advocating for her education during an era when rural girls frequently encountered institutional ceilings, backing her admission to the bar, and standing by her through turbulent tenures in parliament and the cabinet. She characterized his influence as that of a principled village teacher who measured success by character and public rectitude rather than personal gain.

"There is never really an age at which you are prepared to say goodbye to someone who has been part of your life from the very beginning," she said, describing the quiet hours spent beside him earlier in the day as a lasting gift to the family.

### Cross-Factional Tributes

The announcement produced an immediate pause in everyday political feuding, drawing condolences from both the administration and the opposition benches.

President William Ruto issued a condolence dispatch acknowledging the elder Karua’s service to the classroom, framing his decades in teaching as a foundational contribution to generations of Kenyan students. The head of state praised Jackson’s steady presence as a public servant whose influence extended well beyond his household into the broader community.

Leaders across the political divide echoed the sentiment throughout Sunday night and Monday morning. Messages of solidarity were delivered by former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua, Wiper Democratic Movement leader Kalonzo Musyoka, and former Attorney General Justin Muturi, all noting the acute personal weight of losing an anchor figure who had accompanied the family across decades of national scrutiny.

For Karua’s extended household, the immediate focus shifts from public messaging to domestic preparations. The family indicated that formal burial arrangements, service dates, and memorial schedules at their Kirinyaga home will be issued in subsequent communications.
