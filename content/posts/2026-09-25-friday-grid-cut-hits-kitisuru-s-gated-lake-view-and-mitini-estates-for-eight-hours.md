Residents across some of Nairobi's most secluded suburban addresses are preparing for an eight-hour utility freeze this Friday, as Kenya Power moves to carry out scheduled maintenance along the capital's leafy northwestern ridge.

The state utility confirmed that pockets of Kitisuru will go off the grid between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. on September 25, 2026. The interruption is set to cut across high-end gated communities including Lake View Estate and Mitini Estate, alongside properties flanking a section of Ngecha Road and adjacent domestic connections.

For an area dominated by expansive family compounds, diplomatic residences, and home-office setups that depend on steady power for private security infrastructure, the eight-hour working-day window will force a shift to back-up systems and localized generators to sustain daily routines.

### Upscale Estates and Diplomatic Corridors

Kitisuru remains one of Nairobi’s quietest real estate zones, where residential clusters are typically insulated from the bustle of the commercial district. However, routine network overhauls do not bypass its tree-lined avenues.

The utility firm noted that the downtime along Ngecha Road and within the Lake View and Mitini enclaves forms part of routine network reinforcement. Customers within the perimeter have been urged to stage arrangements well before the 9:00 a.m. cut-off, particularly where uninterrupted water pumping, automated gate systems, and surveillance cameras rely directly on standard grid connectivity.

While Nairobi’s impact is concentrated inside these exclusive residential pockets, the utility's maintenance schedule for Friday extends outward into Central Kenya and the Upper Eastern agricultural belt, where daytime shutdowns carry very different stakes.

### Essential Care and Markets in Nyeri

In Nyeri County, a matching eight-hour outage from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. will take Muruguru and Gikunju off the grid. 

Unlike the private compounds of Nairobi, the Nyeri switch-off touches public installations and community health infrastructure. The designated zone covers Muruguru Market, Muruguru Secondary School, and Muruguru Village, extending directly to Gikunju Maternity.

For rural maternity and medical dispensaries, an uninterrupted working day is critical for cold-chain vaccine storage, maternity ward lighting, and essential sterilizing equipment, meaning administrators in Gikunju will need alternative fuel sources running until the 5:00 p.m. restoration mark.

### Telecoms, Dairy, and Water Pumps in Meru

The most expansive interruption of the day is earmarked for Meru County, where technicians will isolate power lines half an hour earlier, maintaining a blackout from 8:30 a.m. through 5:00 p.m.

The Meru circuit spans Githindi, Kunati, and Kinwe, impacting a sprawling mix of telecommunications, agricultural processing, and community utility nodes. Key installations caught in the eight-and-a-half-hour maintenance sweep include the Kunati Safaricom Booster and the Mukongoro Booster, both essential for mobile connectivity and digital payments across the surrounding trading centres.

Agricultural and basic utility facilities will also absorb the downtime. Mikinduri Dairy, which processes fresh milk from local smallholders, falls directly inside the unpowered radius, alongside Theii Borehole, a vital communal water source. 

Several educational institutions in the area are also listed on the maintenance roster, including Kiamikuu Primary School, Mutewa Secondary School, Marega Primary School, Athwana Youth Polytechnic, and Akaiga Secondary School. Village centres and community clinics—specifically Kangethia Market, Amatu Village, Mlango Village, and Mulika Dispensary—will similarly navigate the day without mains electricity.

Kenya Power stated that the regional interruptions remain subject to scheduled network servicing, advising domestic, institutional, and commercial consumers across all three counties to treat power lines as live throughout the maintenance window.
