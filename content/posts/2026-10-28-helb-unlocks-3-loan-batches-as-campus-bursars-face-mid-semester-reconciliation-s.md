The Higher Education Loans Board (HELB) has released three additional tuition and upkeep loan batches directly to university finance offices, shifting the immediate clearance hurdle for thousands of students from state allocation portals to campus bursar desks as the current academic term progresses.

In a public notice issued on Wednesday, October 28, the board confirmed that funds under batches 007125, 007126, and 007197 have been processed and wired. Because tuition disbursements are routed straight to institutional bank accounts rather than individual student wallets, HELB advised affected applicants to confirm payment updates with their respective university or college accounts offices.

The latest release follows five earlier allocations cleared earlier this month—specifically batches 007139, 007145, 007147, 007146, and 007132—as the board works through a backlog that left numerous applicants waiting at the allocation stage for months while continuous assessments and lectures got underway.

### Campus Reconciliations Amid Active Terms

For students on campus, the micro-batch release schedule creates an operational hurdle. While the Ministry of Education urged higher education institutions to admit students while the National Treasury processed state allocations, university finance departments generally maintain strict account clearances before granting full access to course portals and examination registers.

Because the funds land in bulk institutional accounts, campus finance officers must manually or digitally reconcile arriving state deposits against individual student fee ledgers before academic status clearances are updated. Students belonging to batches that are still pending processing remain reliant on institutional bursar leniency to avoid disrupted coursework.

### Expanded Treasury Funding Envelope

This staggered rollout occurs within a significantly larger budgetary allocation for student financing. For the 2026/2027 financial year, the National Treasury allocated Ksh58 billion to HELB, marking a substantial increase from the Ksh41 billion set aside during the previous funding cycle.

Despite the higher funding baseline, the board’s batch-by-batch execution means cash flows into universities in waves, forcing accounts departments to continuously update fee balance sheets throughout the semester.

HELB confirmed that processing for remaining unreleased batches is ongoing, with further updates planned as Treasury releases continue. Students seeking to track their individual application status can access updates by dialing *642# or checking the official HELB online portal while campus finance departments process incoming batch lists.
