The forensic examination into the death of Dorita Macharia, mother to former television presenter Regina Re, has established that she succumbed to severe external bleeding triggered by multiple stab wounds.

Government pathologist Dr Bernard Midia disclosed the postmortem findings following a two-hour examination conducted at the Lee Funeral Home. The procedure took place in the presence of legal representatives for the Macharia family as well as lawyers representing the suspects.

According to Dr Midia, the autopsy uncovered multiple sharp-force injuries across various parts of Macharia’s body, alongside two deep stab wounds to the neck that severely damaged vital blood vessels and resulted in heavy blood loss.

“The injury consisted of multiple stab wounds to the neck with injury to the blood vessels, which led to severe external bleeding. Two deep stab wounds in the neck. There were also other sharp force injuries, cut wounds to various parts of the body,” Dr Midia said on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Macharia passed away on the evening of Friday, October 30, 2026, prompting an active police homicide inquiry into the circumstances of her death.

The case shifted significantly on Monday, November 2, when detectives brought Regina Re and three other relatives before the court seeking extended detention orders to finalize their investigations. Alongside the former television presenter, police presented her brother Antony Jimmy Macharia, her granddaughter Thandie Malaika Wanjiku Gitao, and Thandie’s boyfriend, Immanuel Waigwe Munyeki.

Investigators are continuing to compile forensic and material evidence as the legal proceedings advance toward the next scheduled court appearance.
