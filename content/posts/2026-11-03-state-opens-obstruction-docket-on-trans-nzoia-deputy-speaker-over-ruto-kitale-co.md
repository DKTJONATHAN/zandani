More than a fortnight after an awkward highway bypass between presidential and opposition motorcades in Kitale, state security agencies have escalated the encounter into a formal criminal case against the Trans Nzoia County Assembly leadership.

Trans Nzoia County Deputy Speaker Mwale Obed Mahanga spent Monday night locked inside the Kapenguria Police Station after detectives arrested him over claims that he planned to obstruct President William Ruto’s motorcade during a tour of the county on Friday, October 16.

Trans Nzoia Governor George Natembeya confirmed the detention, revealing that police are pursuing dual dockets against the Matisi Ward representative: obstructing the Head of State’s movement and bankrolling youth groups to disrupt rallies hosted by the ruling Kenya Kwanza coalition.

### The Charges at the Flyover

![An undated image of Trans Nzoia Deputy Speaker, also Matisi Ward MCA, during a public event in Matisi Ward.](https://i.ibb.co/tpTVcLzk/f8ef6e0ebd27.webp)

The formal charges stem from events that unfolded along the Kitale flyover on October 16, where President Ruto’s departure convoy crossed paths with a motorcade carrying Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna and allied politicians arriving under the Linda Mwananchi banner.

Ruto had concluded official engagements in the town, commissioning educational and medical training facilities alongside a student village. As the presidential security detail cleared the transit route out of Kitale, Sifuna’s group entered the same corridor.

While the two motorcades bypassed each other without a physical halt on the highway, the political confrontation spilled over immediately after the President left. Sifuna and Governor Natembeya subsequently addressed a public gathering in Kitale, which anti-riot officers broke up using tear gas. The ensuing skirmishes left two police officers injured and two government-registered vehicles damaged.

Rather than resolving the incident as a logistical and public-order lapse on the day, investigators have reached two weeks back to pin personal criminal culpability on the Deputy Speaker.

### Expanding Administrative Crossfire

![Snippets of the presidential motorcade and that of the Linda Mwananchi Movement passing by each other in Kitale on October 16.](https://i.ibb.co/TN4bt1V/8e19c6e2d22a.webp)

The arrest lands Mahanga in the middle of a prolonged institutional duel between Governor Natembeya and the national executive. Relations between County Hall in Kitale and the central government have deteriorated sharply across several fronts, marked recently by disputes over the control and ownership of the Ksh821 million Masinde Muliro Market project and the subsequent withdrawal of Natembeya’s official security detail.

By Monday evening, supporters and a legal team assembled by the Linda Mwananchi movement converged on Kapenguria Police Station in neighboring West Pokot County, demanding Mahanga’s immediate release on police bond.

Allied opposition figures publicly characterized the detention as targeted intimidation designed to weaken Natembeya’s local political standing and neutralize outspoken grassroots figures in the assembly ahead of future electoral battles.

### Morning Court Deadline

The immediate contest shifts to the courtroom, where state prosecutors face an initial test on their evidentiary threshold. Natembeya confirmed that Mahanga is scheduled for arraignment at 9:00 a.m. on Tuesday, where his legal team intends to challenge both the territorial transfer to a Kapenguria holding facility and the legal foundation of the motorcade disruption accusations.
