Brenda has torn down the dignified, clinical narrative constructed around Stevo Simple Boy’s public breakup, claiming the rapper was never blindsided by Tuesday’s viral DNA findings and had long agreed to raise their young son as his own.

In an explosive Tuesday phone interview with digital creator 2Mbili, Brenda challenged anyone doubting her account to confront the artist directly. Rather than a shocked father blindsided by laboratory paperwork, Brenda described an entertainer who entered the arrangement with full knowledge of the child's true parentage.

“Na Stevo nilikuwa nimeshamwambia kitambo,” Brenda said during the broadcast. “Wacha ukimuliza sasa hivi umulize Stevo, ‘Brenda aliwahi kuambia mtoto si wako?’ Atakuambia.”

### The Alleged Secret Agreement

According to Brenda, the paternity of the child, known publicly as Dragon Junior, was settled between the couple behind closed doors well before formal medical testing entered the picture. She maintained that she had personally confessed the biological reality to Stevo early on, leading to an understanding where the musician consented to act as a father figure.

That private arrangement stood in stark contrast to the events of Tuesday morning, when Stevo released a video seated alongside Dr. Peter Ngumba. The consultation confirmed that DNA samples submitted under Stephen Otieno Senior and Stephen Otieno Junior showed mismatches across 11 tested loci, clinically establishing non-paternity.

While Stevo used that recorded consultation to announce a firm, sorrowful end to the relationship and plead for privacy to rebuild his recording career, Brenda’s remarks paint the subsequent fallout inside their shared house as anything but orderly.

### Paperwork and Thrown Belongings

The most serious claim in Brenda’s phone interview centered on what occurred when the physical DNA certificates were brought to the residence. Brenda alleged that an associate named Oscar arrived at the house carrying the medical paperwork and immediately turned hostile.

“Sasa leo venye Oscar wakakuja hivyo na hiyo karatasi ya results, akaanza kunipiga, kunirushia vitu nje, kuniambia niende, nitoke kwa hiyo nyumba niende,” she told 2Mbili.

Brenda singled out Stevo’s behaviour during the confrontation as the ultimate betrayal, stating that the rapper remained completely passive while his associate hurled her belongings outside and ordered her to leave the premises. For Brenda, Stevo’s refusal to intervene or acknowledge their prior understanding transformed a private domestic arrangement into public humiliation.

### Silence at the Studio Gates

The allegations leave a glaring contradiction at the centre of one of the week’s biggest entertainment stories. Stevo’s official communication on Tuesday presented an artist processing grief with maturity, framing the testing as an unexpected heartbreak that forced a necessary clean break.

Yet Brenda's version frames the public DNA test release not as a moment of painful discovery, but as an orchestrated pretext to force her out.

Stevo Simple Boy has not publicly addressed Brenda’s specific claims regarding their prior discussions, nor has he commented on the alleged altercation involving Oscar. As Brenda's interview circulates across digital platforms, the pressure shifts back to the rapper's camp to clarify whether the laboratory reveal was truly a revelation—or the staged conclusion to an unravelling private pact.
