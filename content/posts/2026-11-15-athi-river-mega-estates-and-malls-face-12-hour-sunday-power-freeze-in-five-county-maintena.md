Athi River is set to bear the brunt of Kenya Power’s Sunday maintenance schedule, with thousands of households and major commercial hubs facing an outage running up to 12 hours while interruptions in Nairobi and upcountry counties remain confined to standard working shifts.

The utility firm scheduled network maintenance across five counties—Machakos, Nairobi, Turkana, Uasin Gishu, and Nyeri—for Sunday, November 15, 2026. While the vast majority of affected feeder lines will drop between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm, the disruption along the Machakos commuter belt features two prolonged, overlapping phases that stretch deep into Sunday evening.

### Athi River’s 12-Hour Operational Pause

The most extensive outage window targets Athi River’s bustling mixed-use corridor, starting at 8:00 am and running straight through to 8:00 pm. The twelve-hour shut-off cuts directly through the high-density residential fabric of Greatwall Gardens Phases 1 and 2, Sabaki Estate, Senior Staff Estates, and Ngwata.

Alongside dense housing clusters, the outage cuts supply to major manufacturing and consumer destinations along Old Mombasa Road. Industrial players including London Distillers, Saj Ceramics, Alpharama, Apex TMD, and Oriental Godowns are slated for the full day-long downtime. Key commercial and services landmarks caught in the same circuit include Signature Mall, Saphire Business Park, Petrocity, Lifecare, Tamarind, and the Athi River Sokoni trading centre, as well as Kenya Power’s own Mlolongo offices.

A secondary Athi River interruption adds pressure starting at noon and lasting until 8:00 pm. This eight-hour afternoon sweep covers Crystal Rivers, Sunset Boulevard, Riverpark Estate, and additional residential blocks across Greatwall Gardens Phases 1, 3, 4, and 5. It also pulls supply from Nova School, Welding Alloys, and Kims Business Park, forcing facilities in the precinct to rely entirely on backup power generators throughout Sunday evening.

### Nairobi’s Retail and Manufacturing Zones

In Nairobi, the utility has isolated distinct residential-commercial strips and industrial production belts for eight-hour daytime interruptions between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm.

In Parklands, maintenance will turn off power along 3rd, 4th, and 5th Parklands avenues, affecting Highridge KPLC Staff Quarters, Diamond 1 and 2, TRV Plaza, and sections of Mpaka Road, Wangapala Road, Jalarain Road, and General Mathenge Road.

Simultaneously, two separate maintenance exercises will interrupt production lines across Industrial Area. Along Migwani Road and sections of Enterprise Road, supply will cut off from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, pulling the plug on Styroplast Ltd, Steel Africa, Hannan Tissue, Tetra Pak, Fine Spinners, and Karatasi Brand.

A concurrent shut-down on Clesoi Road and surrounding sections of Enterprise Road will ground operations at Paper Converters, Bins Ltd, Rollmill, Panafric Transformers, and Mahindra. The same line serves local service infrastructure, including Omar Water, Falcon Police Post, Falcon Catholic Church, and Falcon Estate.

### Upcountry Schedules: Turkana, Eldoret, and Nyeri

Outside the capital and its surrounding metro commuter zone, maintenance teams will isolate specific regional lines to service local distribution equipment.

In Turkana County, the utility will disconnect power between 8:00 am and 5:00 pm across Lokichar, Kasuroi, and Kapese. The interruption covers Lokichar Town, Nalemsekon, Kamarese, and hospitality installations including the Arid Summit Hotel.

In Uasin Gishu County, Eldoret’s Kamukunji Estate and neighbouring connections will experience a seven-hour power stoppage between 9:30 am and 4:30 pm.

Further central, in Nyeri County, rural supply lines servicing Mukurweini and Gaturia will shut down from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. The interruption directly involves key public institutions and utilities, including Mukurweini Boys High School, Gaturia Market, and the South Tetu Girls Water Pump.

Kenya Power advised residents, industrial operators, and commercial tenants across all the mapped zones to make alternative supply arrangements to mitigate downtime ahead of the scheduled Sunday grid work.
