Nana Owiti has outlined what she terms "wivu wa maturity"—a post-separation framework built on emotional detachment and mutual freedom—in response to expanding internet speculation about her estranged partner, King Kaka, finding a new lover.

Speaking during a phone interview with radio presenter Ankali Ray, the media personality pushed back against expectations of public friction or bitterness. Instead, she explicitly endorsed the idea of both parties building separate lives after a relationship ends, framing personal progress as an inevitable part of moving forward.

The conversation was prompted by online chatter after King Kaka posted a video clip on his Instagram page featuring a woman identified as Kalekye. The upload triggered immediate curiosity among followers, with online commentators speculating whether the rapper had officially re-entered the dating scene.

When pressed on the phone call to share her feelings about the clip and the accompanying commentary, Owiti initially laughed off the presenter’s persistent inquiry before delivering a direct response.

![Nana Owiti \\ Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/cKgmTt7m/1eb87a47a526.webp)

"Lakini wewe unakuanga mdaku, hizi ni nini unaniambia? I'm very happy for him kama ivo ndo ukweli. Si iko sawa," she said.

Owiti rejected the premise that former partners should remain anchored to past relationships or expect one another to stay emotionally stagnant long after parting ways.

"Hatuwezi shinda mahali pamoja, ivo ndo maisha iko," she explained.

Rather than framing the situation through conventional drama, Owiti introduced her concept of "mature jealousy." Under this view, individuals acknowledge past emotional bonds without allowing them to turn into controlling behavior, persistent boundary violations, or public rivalry.

"Mimi napenda wivu wa maturity. Kila mtu aishi maisha yake venye inafaa," she stated during the interview.

Her comments highlight a deliberate effort to manage her public profile by steering entertainment coverage away from tabloid confrontation. By endorsing King Kaka's freedom to pursue new relationships, Owiti sought to settle questions about her own emotional stance while setting a clear personal boundary.

She urged observers to focus on individual growth rather than fixating on the private developments of former couples, noting that personal evolution requires accepting shifts that lie outside one's control.

"Tumewekwa hapa kila siku you have to be better than yesterday. Aluta continua," Owiti said, closing the exchange on a note of ongoing self-improvement.
