Terence Creative traded punchlines for domestic diplomacy on stage, dropping to one knee before a live concert crowd with an unscripted petition directed squarely at his wife, digital creator Milly Chebby.

During a live appearance at the Mataha Experience, the comedian commandeered the venue’s microphone to ask Chebby if she would consider expanding their family.

“I want to ask you before these people, will you give me another baby? Thank you,” Terence said into the mic, prompting cheers from the audience as concertgoers reached for their phones to capture the impromptu proposal.

The exchange, which circulated across TikTok shortly after the event, played out with the dramatic staging of a traditional marriage proposal, though the subject was strictly family planning.

### Humor After a Harrowing Year

While the comedic delivery drew laughs across the auditorium, the request carried notable emotional resonance for followers who have tracked the couple’s recent personal milestones.

The public gesture comes months after Chebby disclosed her survival of a life-threatening ectopic pregnancy during a 2025 family vacation in Mombasa.

At the time, Chebby had initially dismissed acute abdominal pain as standard menstrual cramps before a consultation with her gynecologist prompted an emergency diagnosis and rapid medical intervention.

Navigating that medical crisis publicly anchored the couple’s online presence over the past year, shifting their content from routine lifestyle vlogging to candid discussions about maternal health and emergency recovery.

### Expanding a Blended Household

Terence and Chebby, who have long established themselves as one of Nairobi's most visible digital creator pairings, currently share one daughter together.

Beyond their shared household, Terence is also a father to two children from a previous relationship, making any prospective new arrival an addition to an already bustling blended family setup.

The onstage plea at the Mataha Experience bypassed standard private discussions in favor of public theater—a hallmark of how the pair has managed their brand and personal narrative over years in the entertainment spotlight.

Chebby has yet to issue a formal public verdict on Terence's onstage petition, leaving concert attendees and digital followers waiting to see whether the live plea translates into an official family announcement.
