State agencies operating their own air wings face unprecedented legislative scrutiny as Parliament moves to inspect the maintenance paper trail and regulatory compliance of government-run helicopters.

The push, initiated on Wednesday, October 21, targets the operational standards of parastatal fleets following an aborted takeoff that wrecked a Kenya Forest Service (KFS) helicopter at the Kericho Golf Club nine days earlier.

Kericho Woman Representative Beatrice Kemei formally petitioned the Departmental Committee on Transport and Infrastructure to compel civil aviation regulators to produce inspection logs, airworthiness certifications, and pre-flight defect records for public aircraft.

### The Takeoff Failure at Kericho Golf Club

The inquiry stems from the October 12 incident in Kericho, where Environment Principal Secretary Festus Ng’eno and four other occupants narrowly survived after their KFS helicopter crashed during liftoff. While all five walked away without physical harm, eyewitness accounts and field reports pointed to apparent power or mechanical instability immediately before the aircraft came down on the golf course grounds.

![Snippets of a chopper crash in Kericho Glof Club on Mimday, October 12](https://i.ibb.co/gbtPqR0q/f683a7e03470.webp)

Addressing the National Assembly, Kemei argued that the close call cannot be dismissed as an isolated malfunction, pointing to reported technical difficulties during the crew's flight preparations.

"Of particular concern are reports that the helicopter experienced difficulties before the crash, underscoring the need to establish the circumstances surrounding the accident and determine whether any underlying safety deficiency contributed to it," Kemei told the House.

She noted that repeated accidents in recent months require lawmakers to verify whether state-operated birds meet the same civil aviation checks demanded of private charter carriers.

### Scrutiny on Maintenance Logs and Cockpit Approvals

The petition seeks specific evidentiary submissions from transport authorities, including:

* A complete airworthiness and maintenance history of the KFS helicopter leading up to October 12, detailing previous mechanical fault reports and unresolved snag sheets.
* The sequence of takeoff attempts and flight handling decisions executed by the cockpit crew at the Kericho takeoff zone.
* Official records proving the validity of operational approvals and technical clearances issued for the mission.
* Direct verification of pilot flight-hour competency, mandatory simulator recertifications, and procedural compliance.

![Kericho Woman Rep Beatrice Kemei during a funeral service at the county on October 19, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/FbS8w1vL/81c8edecac85.webp)

By focusing on pre-flight documentation, the inquiry aims to establish whether public agencies are deferring essential airframe maintenance or clearing helicopters for VIP transit despite unresolved defect alerts.

### Wider Aviation Oversight Deficits

The parliamentary action expands beyond the KFS fleet to encompass an ongoing wave of aviation disasters on Kenyan soil this year. The lawmaker instructed the committee to obtain comprehensive investigation files from the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) on fatal crashes recorded throughout 2026.

The sector has suffered notable losses in recent months, including a February crash that claimed former Emurua Dikirr MP Johana Ng'eno, and an August 19 incident that killed Ecuador’s intelligence chief, Michele Sensi-Contugi, his wife, and five others.

Kemei wants the Transport Committee to isolate shared technical deficiencies across all recent crash dossiers, demanding that the executive explain what punitive action has been taken against operators or regulatory inspectors who clear unairworthy machinery into Kenyan airspace.

The Departmental Committee on Transport and Infrastructure is expected to summon KCAA officials and management from affected state agencies to present their technical compliance records and address the preliminary findings of the Kericho accident investigation.
