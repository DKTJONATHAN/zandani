Residents across several prime Nairobi suburban stretches alongside industrial operators in five upcountry counties face up to eight hours without electricity on Monday, September 28, 2026, following a widespread maintenance schedule announced by Kenya Power.

The planned service interruptions will knock out power supplies starting as early as 8:00 a.m. in central Kenya and extend through 5:00 p.m. across residential neighborhoods, agro-processing plants, academic institutions, and border towns.

### Nairobi’s Residential and Commuter Corridor Hit

In Nairobi, the utility firm has scheduled an uninterrupted eight-hour blackout spanning from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The outage directly affects the dense residential, business, and leisure belt running through Kilimani, Jamhuri, and adjacent estates.

Key roads and residential arteries listed under the Nairobi shutdown include:

* **Oloitoktok Road** and surrounding courts
* **Parts of Gitanga Road** and **Mararo**
* **Parts of Argwings Kodhek Road**
* **Ole Dume Road** and **Riara Road**
* **Makindi Road**
* **Parts of Ngong Road** and **Joseph Kangethe Road**
* **Parts of Jamhuri Estate** and adjoining customer premises

Commercial establishments, home offices, and apartment complexes along these avenues will need alternative power setups to navigate the working day.

### Agro-Processing Factories Cut Off in Nandi and Vihiga

Outside the capital, the maintenance operation targets critical agricultural and timber processing nodes across western Kenya and the Rift Valley.

In **Nandi County**, electricity will be turned off between 9:30 a.m. and 3:00 p.m., cutting off power to **Tinderet Tea Factory**, **Chepkemel**, **Mbogo Valley**, and neighboring consumers.

In **Vihiga County**, an 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. outage will disrupt operations across **Evojo**, **Mudete**, **Lososi**, **Lusengeli**, and the **Kaimosi Sawmill** industrial zone.

### Cross-Border Hubs and Central Kenya Facilities

In **Migori County**, power lines will go dead from 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., impacting the cross-border trade route and trading centers including:

* **Kiamokebe Centre**
* **Gokeharaka**
* **Ntisinye**
* **Ngugu**
* **Nyamaranya**
* **Parts of Isebania** town

**Nyeri County** features two major shutdown zones. The earliest begins at 8:00 a.m. and runs until 5:00 p.m., cutting supply to **Sagana Falls Local Transformer**, **Ihigaini**, **Gakoe Coffee Factory**, **Mung’aria Coffee Factory**, **Kaigonde**, **Kangubiri Senior School**, **parts of Kanderendu**, **parts of Muthinga**, **Kanyi Transformer**, and **Gititu Market Dispensary**.

A secondary Nyeri interruption between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. will affect **Lady of Lourdes**, **Embassy**, **Njengu**, **Baraka Estate**, and **Eunice Academy**.

In **Kirinyaga County**, the 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. blackout will isolate **Kangaita Tea Factory**, **Old Kangaita Market**, **Kiranja Junction**, **Kiamwangi**, **Mutuma**, and **Kamuruana**.

Kenya Power has advised customers in all listed zones to plan their daily operations and equipment usage accordingly ahead of the Monday morning disconnections.
