As construction machinery falls silent at Nyayo Stadium, parliamentary overseers are attempting to salvage the stalling AFCON 2027 training venue by widening the administrative net to include Nairobi County and the Ministry of Labour.

Operations at the facility ground to a halt after the contractor pulled off-site, citing an unpaid balance of Ksh984 million. Financial disclosures made before the Senate Committee on Labour and Social Welfare revealed that only Ksh200 million has been disbursed against the total Ksh1.184 billion contract.

![An aerial image of an empty Nyayo National Stadium](https://i.ibb.co/zHf7SQVy/ea7f66e10daf.webp)

While foundational upgrades—such as drainage improvements, floodlight erection, and CCTV deployment—have been completed, critical elements remain frozen. The stalled site lacks grid connectivity for its floodlights, LED screens, a functional scoreboard, perimeter fortifications, turnstiles, and essential accessibility provisions for persons with disabilities.

Confronted with the paralysis, Senate Committee Chair and West Pokot County Senator Julius Murgor ordered an immediate expansion of administrative oversight, directing that relevant state and county agencies be formally pulled into the preparations to address safety, accessibility, and emergency coordination.

“If the Ministry of Labour and Nairobi County have not been involved so far, please begin by sending out their invitations today,” Murgor instructed during the committee's Tuesday session.

![Ruto CHAN](https://i.ibb.co/TpVG42R/3aecf0ff7e44.webp)

The standoff at Nyayo forms part of a wider fiscal headache for the State Department for Sports, which is currently grappling with Ksh2.176 billion in cumulative pending bills tied to stadium construction and rehabilitation projects nationwide. Lawmakers have repeatedly warned that these unresolved obligations threaten to saddle the government with escalating liabilities and unfinished infrastructure.

The committee intends to consolidate its findings from Nyayo and the recent Talanta Stadium inspection into a comprehensive report aimed at forcing negotiations with relevant ministries and the National Treasury for the capital required to clear outstanding arrears and restart works.
