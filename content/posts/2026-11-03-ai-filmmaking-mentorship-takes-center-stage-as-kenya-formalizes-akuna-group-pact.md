A new multilateral framework between Nairobi and British actor Idris Elba’s Akuna Group seeks to move government creative support beyond promotional campaigns into technical AI training and commercial production pipelines.

Foreign Affairs Principal Secretary Korir Sing’Oei signed the Statement of Intent with Elba on Tuesday, November 3, on the sidelines of the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM 2026) in Antigua and Barbuda. The agreement embeds digital innovation and artificial intelligence directly into bilateral cooperation goals, linking Kenyan talent with international venture networks.

![Foreign Affairs PS Korir Sing’Oei and Sir Idris Elba sign a Statement of Intent on the sidelines of the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting in Antigua and Barbuda, November 3,](https://i.ibb.co/ZRkqfjPp/2df426f49ccd.webp)

Rather than serving as a standard memorandum of understanding, the pact plugs into ongoing training initiatives designed to equip African filmmakers with generative tools. The Akuna Group has been rolling out an AI filmmaking mentorship program developed alongside Google and Curious Refuge. That curriculum, launched in October, pairs technical instruction with production funding while ensuring that participating creators retain full ownership of their work.

For Nairobi, the agreement provides an external conduit for the government’s Talanta Hela program. State officials are betting that combining foreign investor access with structured skills development will allow young creatives to transition from informal projects to scalable commercial enterprises across Commonwealth markets.

![Actor and Akuna Group Chairperson Sir Idris Elba speaks during an engagement attended by Kenyan officials on the sidelines of the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting in Antigu](https://i.ibb.co/ksGpWRH7/c42c1b252809.webp)

The partnership also formalizes a regional approach under the Commonwealth Creative Economy Partnership, which groups Kenya alongside Ghana, Sierra Leone, and Barbados. Discussions between Sing’Oei and Elba trace back to a virtual consultation in September, where creators flagged persistent industry bottlenecks—chief among them the lack of reliable international payment systems, professional networks, and technical education.

President William Ruto has invited Elba to visit Nairobi during the upcoming Internet Governance Forum, where the partnership's operational roadmap is expected to be unveiled.
