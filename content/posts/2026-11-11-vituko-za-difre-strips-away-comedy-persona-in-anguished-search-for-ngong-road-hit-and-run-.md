Vituko Za Difre set aside the street pranks and lighthearted sketches that built his digital following on Tuesday morning, trading scripted comedy for an unvarnished cry of personal anguish.

The Kenyan creator stepped in front of his camera in acute distress after receiving confirmation that his mother had been killed in a hit-and-run crash along Ngong Road in Nairobi. Faced with an immediate void, his first public reaction was not a crafted press statement but a desperate, repeated question directed into the void of the city's traffic grid: *"Nani amegonga mamangu?"* [Who hit my mother?]

The fatal incident occurred on the morning of Tuesday, November 10, 2026. According to early accounts, a motorist struck her as she navigated the busy thoroughfare before speeding away from the scene without stopping to render assistance or report the impact. By the time the creator addressed his audience, her body had already been transferred to City Mortuary, leaving his family to grapple with funeral arrangements alongside the shock of sudden violence.

### Raw Shock Gives Way to Written Tribute

In the video recording circulated across social platforms shortly after the crash, Vituko Za Difre laid bare the disorientation of the early morning call, telling viewers through visible grief that his mother had been involved in an accident and taken to the morgue.

As the reality settled later in the day, the prankster followed up the initial video with an intimate written tribute, shifting from the frantic demand for accountability to an acknowledgment of his central anchor.

"My heart is broken," he wrote on his social media page. "I never imagined I would have to live in a world without you, Mum."

In the eulogy, the creator reflected on the grounding influence his mother provided behind the camera, away from the public eye that watches his comedic antics.

"You were my blessing, my strength and my home," he stated, acknowledging the weight of conversations left unfinished by the abrupt morning crash. "There are so many things I wish I could tell you, and so many moments I wish I could share with you again. Thank you for everything, Mum. Your love will forever remain in my heart."

### The Search for the Fleeing Motorist

While condolences poured in from fellow entertainers and digital creators across the country, law enforcement officers launched inquiries to track down the vehicle and driver responsible for the crash.

Investigators are working to retrace the sequence of events along the arterial route to establish the motorist's identity. At the time of publication, police had not yet arrested any suspect or identified the registration details of the fleeing car.

The fatal crash on Ngong Road adds to a persistent pattern of pedestrian vulnerability on Nairobi’s primary transport corridors. National transport figures consistently show that people on foot bear the heaviest burden of road casualties in Kenya, with wide, high-speed multilane routes routinely proving treacherous for pedestrians attempting to cross during morning transit hours.

For Vituko Za Difre and his family, the immediate focus remains on funeral preparations at City Mortuary—even as the central question he posed to his audience on Tuesday morning waits for an official answer from traffic investigators.
