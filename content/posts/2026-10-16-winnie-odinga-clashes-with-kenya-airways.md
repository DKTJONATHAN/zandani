East African Legislative Assembly (EALA) MP Winnie Odinga found herself caught in a tense logistical scramble on the eve of her father’s first death anniversary after Kenya Airways abruptly cancelled her flight home.

The unexpected travel snag hit the outspoken legislator just as family members, political leaders, and supporters began converging for the climax of a three-day national program commemorating the late former Prime Minister, Raila Odinga, who passed away in India on October 15, 2025.

Rather than navigating the setback behind closed doors, Winnie took her frustration directly to social media. She posted a screenshot of the official cancellation notice alongside a pointed, unminced remark: “One day we will have a talk about this airline.” Shortly after airing her grievance with the national carrier, she shared a contrasting moment of personal reflection—publishing an image of a lit candle accompanied by the solitary caption, “Dad.”

The timing of the disruption added immediate pressure to an already emotionally charged week. Family and allies were gathering in Siaya County's Bondo region, centered around the Migwena Cultural Grounds, to mark the final stretch of the multi-day commemorations for the veteran opposition leader.

As the cancellation notification flashed across her screen, uncertainty hung over whether the last-born daughter of the late leader would manage to secure alternative travel arrangements in time to join the main memorial service alongside grieving relatives and visiting dignitaries.
