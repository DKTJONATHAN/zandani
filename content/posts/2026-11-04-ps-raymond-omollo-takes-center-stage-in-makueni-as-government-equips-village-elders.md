Internal Security and National Administration Principal Secretary Dr. Raymond Omollo made a major public pitch to grassroots administrators in Makueni County, detailing a modernization package and career progression pipeline for the state's frontline administrative staff.

Speaking during the inauguration of Kikumbulyu Division in Kibwezi West, Dr. Omollo confirmed that the Public Service Commission (PSC) has formally cleared a recruitment drive for additional Assistant County Commissioners (ACCs). The fresh recruitment seeks to staff newly gazetted administrative units across the country.

### Career Elevation for Local Chiefs

Rather than looking exclusively outside the existing administrative corps, Dr. Omollo issued an open invitation to qualified chiefs and assistant chiefs to apply for the upcoming Assistant County Commissioner roles.

The Principal Secretary emphasized that expanding administrative units is part of an ongoing drive to dismantle geographic barriers that previously forced residents to travel long distances to access state services.

### Digital Tools, Healthcare, and Monthly Allowances for Elders

Beyond opening career progression pathways for chiefs, Dr. Omollo turned his focus to the foundation of grassroots governance: village elders.

He confirmed that the Ksh 3,000 monthly stipend introduced under President William Ruto’s administration officially commenced cash disbursements in July. However, the government’s welfare plans for village elders extend beyond cash transfers.

Dr. Omollo announced that plans are currently underway to distribute smartphones to village elders to streamline local communication, while also enrolling them and their immediate families in comprehensive medical insurance under the Social Health Authority (SHA).

### Kikumbulyu Administrative Footprint

The announcement took place during the formal installation of Kikumbulyu Division’s first Assistant County Commissioner, hosted by Kibwezi West Member of Parliament Mwengi Mutuse.

The newly inaugurated Kikumbulyu Division covers 127 villages distributed across four sub-locations: Ngulu, Kathyaka, Ngandani, and Ndetani. Addressing the gathering, Dr. Omollo urged the area’s local administrative team to maintain professional accountability, proactive dispute resolution, and direct community engagement.

### Operational Welfare Fixes and Exam Directives

Addressing administrative officers across National Government Administrative Officers (NGAO) structures, the PS highlighted specific operational improvements aimed at boosting morale:

* Clearing long-standing officer promotion backlogs within the ministry.
* Decentralizing the distribution of official administrative uniforms.
* Expanding access to paramilitary and specialized legal training for field officers.
* Increasing baseline operational funding dispatched to local administrative outposts.

With national examinations currently underway across the country, Dr. Omollo commended security personnel and administrators for maintaining safe test environments for candidates.

He called on administrators and parents to keep close oversight of students during the upcoming school holidays to prevent dropouts before academic institutions reopen in January.

“Education is the great equalizer,” Dr. Omollo stated, reiterating that the national government remains committed to ensuring every Kenyan child transitions without interruption to the next academic level.
