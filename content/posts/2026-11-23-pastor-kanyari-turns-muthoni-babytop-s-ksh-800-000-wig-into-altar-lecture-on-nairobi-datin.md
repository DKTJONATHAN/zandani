Pastor Victor Kanyari brought radio presenter Muthoni Babytop’s viral beauty overhaul to his Sunday pulpit, using the alleged six-figure cost of her new human-hair wig to issue a stern financial reality check to hopeful suitors across Nairobi.

Speaking to his congregation at the Salvation Healing Ministry on November 22, 2026, the preacher singled out the Kameme FM personality, publicly dissecting her updated look and warning men against pursuing relationships beyond their financial reach.

Kanyari claimed the broad-caster’s new brown human-hair piece costs between KSh 700,000 and KSh 800,000. He argued that matching the upkeep of a media figure widely dubbed the "Queen of Mt Kenya" requires economic backing that many casual admirers simply do not possess.

### Financial Realism from the Altar

During the service, Kanyari specifically named specific Nairobi neighborhoods, cautioning men living in lower-income areas against attempting to court Babytop.

“And Babytop, I heard her wig was worth around KSh 700,000 to KSh 800,000,” Kanyari told his congregation. “Babytop, and you are there hoping to date her. Where do you even get that energy? Do you know why she is called the Queen of Mt Kenya? Even if you are handsome, do not think you have a chance there.”

He urged men from estates such as Kayole and Kangemi to align their romantic ambitions with their current living standards rather than chasing high-maintenance media figures.

“Please, if you are from Kayole, be comfortable there, and if you are from Kangemi, be comfortable there,” he added. “You will not manage those standards.”

### Offering a Pulpit Alternative

To drive his point home, the self-proclaimed prophet offered a contrast within his own church setting, suggesting that men pursuing Babytop should redirect their attention toward women with more manageable expectations.

He pointed out Marion Naipei, a woman present at the service, as a practical example of a simpler option for men looking for realistic companionship.

“So leave Babytop, who has high standards, and come here and choose a simple lady like Marion Naipei,” Kanyari said. “Stop disturbing Babytop and let her have peace of mind.”

### The 18-Year Hair Transition

Kanyari’s sermon followed days after Babytop sparked widespread attention on social media by cutting off her signature dreadlocks, a look she had maintained for nearly two decades.

Announcing the transformation online, the radio host joked that her hair had matured enough to enter higher education before she finally decided to part ways with it.

“My dreadlocks have reached a legal age! So I sent them to campus. After 18 years, tumeagana,” she posted, sharing images of herself holding the detached locks while wearing her black trouser suit and the new brown wig.

Babytop framed the makeover as a personal milestone, expressing optimism about entering a new month with a refreshed identity while maintaining that her personal faith remained firm.

Shortly after publishing the photos, Babytop appeared live on her regular Kameme FM broadcast wearing the same human-hair unit, giving listeners and viewers a first look at her updated on-air image.

While Kanyari did not state where he obtained the KSh 800,000 valuation for the hairpiece, his Sunday discourse underscores how quickly high-profile celebrity transformations can move from social media feeds straight into Nairobi pulpit commentary.
