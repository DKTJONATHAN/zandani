Dancehall artiste KRG The Don turned a routine car blessing into an impromptu life-coaching session, laying down a strict code of conduct for content creator Vincent Mboya’s newly acquired vehicle.

Captured in a video shared on Instagram on October 4, 2026, KRG was seen anointing the fresh ride with holy oil. But rather than keeping the prayers strictly spiritual, the artiste used the roadside ritual to issue pointed warnings regarding romance, vehicle passengers, and personal finance.

Top of KRG's decree was a prohibition against using the vehicle to pursue romantic interests. The artiste explicitly warned Mboya against enticing women who might eventually introduce complications into his life, instructing him instead to limit passengers to family members and business associates.

“The biggest thing we are rejecting is that he uses this car to entice ladies who later bring him trouble. May he carry family members or people he is working with only,” KRG declared during the blessing.

The unscripted sermon also ventured into financial territory with an unexpected directive: a complete veto on forex trading. Without elaborating on past experiences or specific market pressures, KRG explicitly commanded the content creator to stay clear of the currency markets.

Beyond the cabin rules, the artiste addressed Mboya's professional trajectory. KRG prayed for the creator's transition into a major entertainment journalist capable of connecting with Kenyan audiences, while explicitly asking for discipline behind the wheel to protect both the driver and pedestrians, specifically noting boda boda riders along local roads.

The vehicle acquisition itself marks a milestone for Mboya, who has previously stated that purchasing the car fulfilled a personal promise made to his late mother.

Whether the content creator intends to heed the dancehall star's specific bans on passenger demographics and currency speculation remains to be seen as he takes his new keys onto the road.
