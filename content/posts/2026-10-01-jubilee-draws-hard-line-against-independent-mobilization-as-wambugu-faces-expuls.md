Jubilee Party headquarters has set a strict seven-day compliance clock for former Nyeri Town Member of Parliament Ngunjiri Wambugu, escalating an internal battle over grassroots organization and digital messaging ahead of the 2027 general elections.

The party's disciplinary committee issued a formal show-cause notice requiring Wambugu to answer nine separate charges by October 8, 2026. At the center of the dispute is the creation and promotion of the 'Jubilee Aspirants Forum 2027,' an independent platform Wambugu announced via his verified Facebook account on March 17, 2026.

![Jubilee party leader Fred Matiang'i addressing the press at Jubilee party Headquarters along Kiambu Road in Nairobi on September 17, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/XrGjt4M4/f406bf0c7651.webp)

According to the committee, the forum operates entirely outside recognized party organs. Investigators pointed to statements describing the platform as independent of any party leadership as a direct breach of the Jubilee Party Constitution, 2025. The charges further allege that Wambugu violated a March 27 directive issued by Secretary General Moitalel Ole Kenta, which ordered members and leaders to halt activities that could exploit or mislead prospective political aspirants.

Beyond unauthorized organizing, the disciplinary framework targets Wambugu's digital footprint and communication methods. The party accuses him of usurping the authority of party leader Fred Matiang'i by issuing unauthorized statements, utilizing party logos, colors, and symbols, and mimicking official communication channels. Investigators also noted that Wambugu allegedly deployed his personal email address to harvest personal information from prospective aspirants seeking to register for party activities.

![Jubilee Party leader Fred Matiang'i (left) talking to former MP Ngunjiri Wambugu on July 9, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/xKTcxxgt/0558c04bc3cf.webp)

The disciplinary committee has directed Wambugu to submit a point-by-point defense—admitting or denying each claim while supplying supporting evidence, witness statements, or mitigating arguments—either electronically or physically at Daraja House in Nairobi by 5:00 pm on Thursday, October 8. The notice underscores that proceedings will advance regardless of whether he chooses to respond.

Wambugu has yet to make a public statement addressing the charges, and attempts to secure his comments prior to publication were unsuccessful as the party prepares to enforce its internal administrative boundaries.
