Two days after handing over residential keys to more than 2,000 families in Uasin Gishu County, the government has set the financial terms for commercial business inside the Ksh5.3 billion Pioneer Affordable Housing Project.

The Affordable Housing Board (AHB) published a competitive tender notice on Tuesday, October 20, inviting private bidders to purchase permanent business retail units across seven residential blocks and bid on long-term commercial leases for the estate’s major recreational and civic complexes.

The tendering framework creates a twin-track commercial model for the newly opened settlement. While retail shop spaces are marked for outright purchase starting at Ksh900,000, prime social infrastructure—including the multi-storey clubhouse and community centre—is being routed through high-value commercial leasing to private operators.

### Retail Price Tiers Across Seven Blocks

The retail footprint covers 104 shops organized across five standard blocks and two smaller segments. Under the AHB pricing structure, shop values scale across five distinct reserve bands based on floor space:

![The Pioneer Affordable Housing Estate in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, October 18, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/j9YyRjr9/7a29385e51dc.webp)

* **Ksh900,000:** Smallest listed commercial shops with a floor area of 7,500 square metres.
* **Ksh1,000,000:** Units measuring 8,300 square metres.
* **Ksh1,100,000:** Units measuring 9,200 square metres.
* **Ksh1,300,000:** Mid-tier commercial shops measuring 10,900 square metres.
* **Ksh1,700,000:** Largest listed retail units measuring 14,200 square metres.

In Blocks A, C, G, H, and N, the board has designated 13 business spaces per block under identical distribution quotas: five units at the Ksh900,000 baseline, two at Ksh1 million, four at Ksh1.1 million, and single allocations for the Ksh1.3 million and Ksh1.7 million spaces.

Blocks L and M house 11 business units each, concentrating entirely on entry and mid-tier allocations: five shops at Ksh900,000, two at Ksh1 million, and four at Ksh1.1 million, excluding the upper-tier units.

### Multi-Million Lease Reserves for Social Facilities

Beyond individual retail ownership, the board is monetizing the project's central gathering amenities through corporate leases with reserve thresholds running into tens of millions of shillings.

At the Pioneer Community Centre, leasing rights are partitioned across three tiers: the ground floor carries a reserve lease price of Ksh27 million, the first floor is set at Ksh22.7 million, and the rooftop hospitality space requires a minimum reserve of Ksh26.9 million.

![President William Ruto pushes a wheelbarrow during the handover of houses at the Pioneer Affordable Housing Estate in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, October 18, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/YBZ6RTS7/a54a7cc681a0.webp)

A parallel structure governs the Pioneer Clubhouse. The board has established a lease benchmark of Ksh21 million each for open floor space across the ground and first levels, while the open-air rooftop zone has been appraised at a Ksh16 million reserve.

### Tender Logistics and Delivery Pipeline

The commercial procurement process requires interested operators to download documentation through the AHB portal or the central government public procurement tenders platform.

Bidders must submit physical, sealed proposals to the Affordable Housing Board's Tender Box on the 21st floor of Prism Towers along 3rd Ngong Avenue in Nairobi. Submissions close at 11:00 am on November 10, with tenders set to be unsealed immediately in the presence of bidders’ representatives.

The commercial release comes directly after President William Ruto presented keys to 2,157 homeowners at Pioneer on Sunday, October 18, operationalizing the first extensive residential section of the development. Built to support an integrated population cluster, the master plan complements its commercial units and clubhouse with a kindergarten, planned junior school, swimming pool, playgrounds, green spaces, two basketball courts, and a football pitch.

The commercial bid evaluation will determine the retail mix serving the estate's incoming population once tenders are opened on November 10.
