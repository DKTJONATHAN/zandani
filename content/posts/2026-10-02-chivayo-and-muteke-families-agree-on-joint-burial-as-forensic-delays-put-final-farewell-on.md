The families of late Zimbabwean businessman Wicknell Chivayo and his wife, Lucy Muteke, have reached an agreement to bury the couple at the same cemetery. However, this moment of unity remains suspended in place as forensic experts in Harare work to formally identify their remains.

The agreement between the two families was confirmed by family spokesperson Herbert Chivayo on Thursday night, October 1, 2026. The announcement came during a sombre vigil at the couple’s residence in the upscale suburb of Borrowdale, Harare, where relatives, church leaders, and close associates gathered to mourn.

Chivayo and his wife died on Wednesday, September 30, when their helicopter crashed in Marondera. The violence of the impact has complicated the standard post-mortem process, leaving the families unable to announce a definitive burial date.

During the Thursday night gathering, the atmosphere inside the Borrowdale residence was defined by low-toned consultations and prayers. Members of the Johanne Masowe EChishanu Church joined the family, reflecting Chivayo’s deep and highly publicised links to religious communities in Zimbabwe. Leaders of the Marondera-based church described the tycoon’s death as a massive blow to both their congregation and the wider community, noting his consistent financial and personal support for their activities.

Despite the collective desire to move forward with the funeral arrangements, the physical reality of the crash has dictated the timeline. Herbert Chivayo explained that the severe nature of the accident requires detailed forensic examinations to ensure formal identification of the victims before any remains can be released to the families.

"The family must first await formal confirmation from forensic experts before finalising the funeral arrangements," Herbert Chivayo stated, adding that the two families will convene to determine the path forward once the medical examiners conclude their work.

For now, the high-walled Borrowdale estate remains a house of waiting. The unified decision by Lucy Muteke's relatives and the Chivayo family to secure a shared final resting place remains firm, pending the scientific clearance required to bring the couple home.
