Musician Kevin Kioko, professionally known as Bahati, has detailed the institutional and personal lineage behind raising his eldest son, Morgan Bahati Kioko, tracing a 15-year arc from a Mathare rescue shelter to elite high school halls.

In a personal reflection shared on Sunday, November 8, 2026, the artist recounted the circumstances under which he took custody of Morgan following the release of his breakthrough gospel single *Mama*. At the time, Bahati was barely 20 years old, unmarried, and lacking stable financial footing.

### The Mathare Connection

Before entering the studio system, Bahati grew up under the care of ABC Children’s Home, an shelter operating in the Mathare informal settlement. When he returned to the facility as a young artist seeking to give back, he met Morgan, an orphan who had been rescued from the street and admitted to the same home.

Despite having no established household or steady income, Bahati elected to take direct responsibility for the child rather than contributing through periodic donations.

“Despite my desire to give back to the community and the burden I had to change Morgan’s life, this wasn’t an easy decision,” Bahati noted.

### Domestic Realities at 20

The transition into immediate single parenthood forced the young musician to absorb all domestic roles without outside help. Without the financial means to hire household staff or secure consistent catering, Bahati managed childcare, daily cooking, and routine housekeeping alongside his early recording schedule.

“To be honest, I was barely 20 years old, still poor, single and struggling to make it as a musician, not even able to afford a house help, so I ended up being a dad, a mum, a nanny, a cook… all at the same time,” he wrote.

He recalled the physical strain of those early domestic setups, noting that their diet relied heavily on basic preparations that did not always turn out as planned.

“Kuna masiku tulikula chakula haijaiva… ilikuwa noma!” he added, pointing to the makeshift conditions of their first apartment.

### From Shelter to Lenana School

Fifteen years after leaving the rescue home, the domestic arrangement has yielded a distinct academic milestone. Now officially named Morgan Bahati Kioko, the young man has advanced into high school, currently attending Lenana School as an active, articulate student.

For Bahati, watching Morgan navigate secondary school marks the completion of a generational loop—taking a child from the very shelter that cushioned his own youth and guiding him into one of the country's prominent national educational institutions.
