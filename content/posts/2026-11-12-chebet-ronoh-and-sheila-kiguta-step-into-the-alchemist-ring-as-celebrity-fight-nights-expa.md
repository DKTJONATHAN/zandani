Digital creator and comedian Chebet Ronoh is trading her regular social media commentary for a pair of boxing gloves, headlining a new roster alongside fellow content creator Sheila Kiguta for the capital's growing entertainment circuit.

The pair have been locked in as the marquee female pairing for Celebrity Fight Nights 3.0, an exhibition boxing series produced by HustleSasa in partnership with rapper and entertainer Barak Jacuzzi. Organisers rolled out the match announcement via social media, framing the bout as an in-ring extension of the creators' online personalities.

The inclusion of a women’s category marks a structural shift for the entertainment boxing franchise, which held its inaugural event in November 2025 with a headline bout between Barak Jacuzzi and creator Lumu at The Alchemist. Following the second edition on June 28, 2026, podcaster Mariah publicly called on event organisers to introduce a dedicated women’s celebrity boxing division. The Ronoh-Kiguta pairing directly answers that pressure, opening the exhibition platform to female content creators for the first time.

Set for Sunday, November 29, the event returns to its usual home at The Alchemist in Westlands, Nairobi. Organisers have built out an expanded entertainment lineup to support the main fixtures, bringing in performers and personalities including Barak Jacuzzi, Alfayo Siwo, Brahim, ActuallyLumu, Peng, and Eddie Grey.

Tickets and advance match cards are already circulating online ahead of the November 29 showdown in Westlands.
