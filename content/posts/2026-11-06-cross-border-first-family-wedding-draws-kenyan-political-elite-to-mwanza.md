A high-profile delegation of Kenyan political leaders crossed the border into Tanzania on Friday, November 6, joining President William Ruto and First Lady Rachel Ruto in Mwanza for the wedding of their daughter, Charlene Ruto, to Tanzanian entrepreneur Isaya Yunge.

The ceremony, held at the Malaika Beach Hotel along the shores of Lake Victoria, brought together government officials, lawmakers, and opposition figures, transforming the First Family's private occasion into a notable cross-border gathering of regional political figures.

### Cross-Party Presence in Mwanza

While church weddings involving presidential families typically draw state dignitaries, the gathering in Mwanza stood out for its broad representation across Kenya's political spectrum.

Key executive and legislative leaders in attendance included National Assembly Speaker Moses Wetang’ula, Prime Cabinet Secretary Musalia Mudavadi, Interior Cabinet Secretary Kipchumba Murkomen, and Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale, all accompanied by their spouses.

![President William Ruto's daughter, Charlene Ruto, and her spouse, Isaya Yunge, during their wedding in Mwanza, Tanzania, on November 6, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/bgg3hPZS/e3447f28ca15.webp)

Alongside government leaders, prominent opposition figures traveled to Tanzania for the event. Suba North MP Millie Odhiambo, Homa Bay Governor Gladys Wanga, and Ambassador Mama Ida Odinga were among the guests present, joining Kapseret MP Oscar Sudi and other parliamentary representatives.

### Church Service and Family Delegation

The religious ceremony was presided over by the Africa Inland Church (AIC) in Tanzania, which conducted the exchange of vows and prayers for the couple.

President Ruto wore a deep burgundy jacket with contrasting lapels, a cream shirt, and a black bow tie, while First Lady Rachel Ruto wore a white dress patterned with burgundy and brown floral designs. The couple escorted their daughter down the aisle during the service.

Charlene wore an elaborate white wedding gown featuring floral detailing across the bodice and skirt, paired with a flowing veil. Her groom, Isaya Yunge, wore a cream suit with a white shirt and patterned black tie.

![President William Ruto (second left), First Lady Rachael Ruto (left), PCS Musalia Mudavadi and his spouse, and Ambassador Mama Ida Odinga.](https://i.ibb.co/3yXmTj7V/fbbcdeac1376.webp)

Members of the First Family formed a central part of the bridal party. Charlene’s sisters—Stephanie, June, Nadia, and Cullie—served as bridesmaids in red gowns, while her brothers Nick and George were also in attendance.

In official portrait sessions following the main service, Charlene changed into a white midi dress with cap sleeves, accessorized with a lace-trimmed veil, gold earrings, a diamond ring, and white heels styled with tulle bows, carrying a bouquet of baby’s breath.

### Continuation of Traditional Ties

Friday’s church service in Mwanza marks the completion of formal wedding ceremonies for the couple, following their traditional Kalenjin engagement ceremony, known as *Koito*, held in August 2026.

That earlier event took place at the Ruto family’s Intonna Heritage Farm in Kilgoris, Narok County, where elders and family members from both Kenya and Tanzania established the cultural foundation for the union.

The transition from the rural engagement in Narok to the church ceremony in Mwanza establishes a formal family connection bridging Kenyan public life and Tanzania's private enterprise sector.
