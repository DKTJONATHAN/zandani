Media personality Oga Obinna refused to let political activist Calvince Okoth, popularly known as Gaucho, cast him as the sole survivor in Embakasi East MP Babu Owino’s political corner. Confronting attempts to isolate his endorsement on social media, the entertainer turned a podcast jab about dwindling support into a sharp counter-claim, asserting that Nairobi's 2027 gubernatorial race suffers not from a shortage of Babu loyalists, but from a total lack of serious opposition.

The online clash traces back to an interview broadcast on Monday, November 16, 2026, when Gaucho sat down with podcaster Mwafreeka to dissect the lawmaker's political trajectory. During the conversation, Gaucho claimed that public momentum behind the MP’s bid for City Hall had utterly evaporated, arguing that Babu's camp could struggle to secure even a Member of County Assembly (MCA) seat despite heavy campaign spending.

Gaucho specifically singled out the former radio host as the final remaining believer in the legislator's vision for the capital. "In fact, mtu anaamini Babu anaweza kuwa Governor imebaki mtu mmoja. Oga Obinna pekee yake," Gaucho declared during the broadcast, framing Obinna as an isolated celebrity voice out of touch with the broader electorate.

By Tuesday, November 17, 2026, Obinna took to his social platforms to dismantle that narrative. Rejecting the suggestion that he was standing alone in the legislator's camp, the creator deployed candidate hashtags alongside an explicit vote of confidence in the MP’s work ethic and constituency record.

"Yes i do. Tuko wengi, btw. #NairobiniBabu #BabuniNairobi na ni Babu kwa Sababu, BABU ni MCHAPA KAZI," Obinna wrote. Shifting the spotlight away from his own endorsement, he added a direct challenge aimed at potential rivals eyeing City Hall: "Kitu hatuna is a proper opponent. Tupatane kwa Debe."

The exchange highlights the increasingly blurred line between digital entertainment figures and capital politics as preparations for the August 2027 General Election gather pace. Babu Owino has consistently declared his intention to challenge incumbent Governor Johnson Sakaja for the county's top job, relying on parliamentary engagements, public appearances, and digital activism to build his citywide profile.

While Gaucho’s remarks underscore ongoing skepticism among some grassroots political commentators regarding campaign conversion, Obinna’s public pushback cements his status as one of the legislator's most visible online champions. With both political operatives and digital creators staking out early positions, the fight for public perception ahead of the 2027 Nairobi gubernatorial race is already well underway.
