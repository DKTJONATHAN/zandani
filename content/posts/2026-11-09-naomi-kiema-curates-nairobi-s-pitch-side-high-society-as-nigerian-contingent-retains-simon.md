Naomi Kiema positioned herself at the center of Jamhuri Park’s weekend social pulse, steering Kenya Breweries Limited’s experiential setup as Nairobi’s fashion-forward crowd turned the Nairobi Polo Club into a vibrant lifestyle runway.

For the Tusker Malt representative, the two-day fixture was designed to merge high-intensity equestrian athleticism with Nairobi's refined afternoon leisure circuit. Pitch-side lounges and curated hospitality setups framed the arena as an international contingent of Nigerian riders outpaced local challengers, successfully defending their crown to lift the Simon Kuseyo Polo Tournament title for the second consecutive year.

"We have had an amazing two-day sporting event that has, as usual, brought out sports fans, the enthusiasts and fashion-forward Nairobians who have enjoyed the pitch-side view of the game and the diverse cultural offerings," Kiema noted, reflecting on the weekend’s turnout. "As KBL, we look forward to maintaining the momentum and elevating the social scene as they enjoy the game."

### Back-to-Back Showdown at Jamhuri Park

![Nigerian Team Retains Simon Kuseyo Polo Tournament Title as Tusker Malt Elevates the Experience](https://i.ibb.co/YwLfpg2/5b09471700cf.webp)

The visiting Nigerian squad entered the tournament bearing the weight of defending champions, facing stiff competition across an intense weekend schedule. Their tactical mastery and speed on the turf kept them ahead in the decisive chukkas, turning the Jamhuri Park encounter into a clinical display of visiting prowess.

Beyond the competitive stakes on the turf, the contest held institutional significance for Kenya's equestrian heritage. The annual tournament serves as an ongoing tribute to the late Simon Kuseyo, a pioneering figure credited with shaping modern Kenyan polo and opening pathways for local players within an internationally connected sport. Retaining the memorial trophy required the Nigerian side to weather sustained local pressure under warm weekend conditions.

### Curating Nairobi’s Pitch-Side Runway

While the contest unfolded at speed on the field, the perimeter developed its own distinct rhythm. Spectators leaned into relaxed lounge settings, trading typical weekend routines for tailored linen, structured millinery, and sharp afternoon cuts that defined Nairobi’s sartorial turnout.

![Nigerian Team Retains Simon Kuseyo Polo Tournament Title as Tusker Malt Elevates the Experience](https://i.ibb.co/xq3FczBq/b3aa688b6fb4.webp)

Beverage stations across the grounds served signature mixtures, including the Tusker Malt Shandy, while corporate and creative professionals utilized the match intervals for relaxed networking. The social layout converted the boundary ropes into an interactive viewing gallery, where live cultural performances and ambient music filled the downtime between chukkas.

### Sustaining the Social Calendar

For Kiema and the sponsoring brand, aligning with the Simon Kuseyo fixture represents a deliberate calendar strategy. The brewer has consistently framed its polo associations around the patience, focus, and measured calculation shared between elite equestrian discipline and slow-brewing methods, translating that philosophy into high-end sporting gatherings.

With the Nigerian contingent carrying the coveted trophy back across the continent for another year, the weekend demonstrated that Nairobi's polo circuit continues to draw its vitality as much from the crowd along the sidelines as from the mallet clashes on the grass. KBL has set its sights on sustaining this intersection of athletics and urban lifestyle across upcoming fixtures on the city’s entertainment calendar.
