Size 8 and DJ Mo have opened the doors to their KSh 46 million suburban home, only to discover that four years of construction discipline cannot insulate a celebrity build from the ruthless judgment of online interior critics.

The gospel singer and her turntablist husband recently published a walkthrough of their finished estate in the Daykio area, located along Kiganjo Road off Thika Road. What was intended as an unveiling of a long-term milestone quickly ran into a polarized public reaction, with commentators fixating on the clean, cream-heavy palette and questioning the couple’s decorative choices.

While some digital onlookers jokingly compared parts of the expansive layout to the clinical corridors of a hospital, others stepped in to defend the couple, noting that personal aesthetic comfort belongs entirely to the homeowner rather than the gallery.

### Behind the Perimeter

Beyond the debate over sofa fabric and lighting tones, the walkthrough laid out the tangible scale of the Daykio project. The living room is anchored by oversized cream-toned seating, decorative accent cushions, and an organized entertainment console designed around broad sightlines.

Adjacent to the lounge, the dining room carries an imposing central table paired with modern upholstered chairs and specialized overhead lighting. The tour also took viewers through the open-plan kitchen, contemporary bathroom layouts, and secondary living quarters spread across the multi-level footprint.

Architecturally, the mansion moves away from purely decorative luxury to functional infrastructure. The property incorporates six bedrooms, a dedicated prayer room, a private cinema, and an in-house sound recording studio tailored to the couple's production work. Everyday convenience is handled through integrated automation, including voice-operated curtain tracks and smart home fittings installed during the final stages of the build.

### A Four-Year Capital Commitment

The finished structure marks the end of a long construction cycle that started in April 2022. The family only took official occupancy four years later, in April 2026, after managing phased contractor timelines along the growing Thika Road residential corridor.

DJ Mo had previously detailed the financial realities behind the undertaking, noting that building the multi-million-shilling home required direct adjustments to their everyday spending. Rather than leaning purely on rapid finishing, the couple spaced out their construction milestones to maintain liquidity without abandoning the original blueprint.

The resulting interior—dominated by neutral wall surfaces, polished tile floors, and uncluttered corners—reflects that deliberate restraint, even as casual viewers mistook the open, low-clutter design for institutional bareness.

### Personal Taste Versus Digital Expectation

The reaction to the Daykio house highlights an enduring dilemma for public figures sharing private real estate: audiences routinely demand opulent, high-density display, yet personal utility often favors streamlined functionality.

For Size 8 and DJ Mo, the KSh 46 million address represents a secured asset containing their primary workspace, family quarters, and creative facilities under a single roof. Whether social media approves of the paint swatches remains secondary to the fact that the keys are in hand, the gates are closed, and the studio is fully wired.
