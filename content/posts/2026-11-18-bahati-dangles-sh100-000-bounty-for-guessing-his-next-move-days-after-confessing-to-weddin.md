Bahati is asking his followers to put a price tag on their curiosity.

Barely a week after appearing on national television to confess that he deliberately misled the public into anticipating a multimillion-shilling wedding to Diana Marua just to hijack attention for his political comeback, the musician has launched another speculative rollout. This time, he is paying his audience to amplify it.

In a directive posted across his social feeds on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, Bahati announced that he would unveil his next move on Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10 am. To ensure maximum comment traffic, he attached a direct financial reward to the riddle, pledging Ksh1,000 to the first 100 followers who accurately deduce the subject of the reveal.

“Sending Ksh1000 to the first 100 comments who guess it right,” he told his followers, setting the collective purse at Ksh100,000. “FRIDAY 10 AM. ANOTHER BIG ANNOUNCEMENT.”

### The Anatomy of the Decoy

The immediate contest mimics the exact promotional machinery the singer weaponised earlier this month. On Friday, November 6, 2026, Bahati and Marua convened high-profile guests at Nairobi’s Argyle Grand Hotel for an ultra-exclusive gathering presented as a celebration of their 10 years together. Invitations were capped at 100 attendees and framed with Ksh1 million access tags, alongside heavily publicised hand-deliveries to prominent personalities.

Online commentary across the country treated the ceremony as the definitive date for their long-delayed formal vows. Instead, Bahati took the podium to proclaim his return to electoral politics.

“I want to announce, and I want to make it official, that I, Bahati Kioko, will be vying as the next Member of Parliament for Mathare Constituency,” he told the room.

Facing blowback for using an implied matrimonial milestone to anchor a campaign launch, Bahati showed no regret during a televised interview on Wednesday, November 11, 2026. Rather than framing the event as a miscommunication, he described the entire buildup as a calculated deception engineered to exploit public appetite.

“I wanted to get the attention of the nation,” he admitted. “Kenyans have been waiting for my wedding for so long.”

### Commercial Yields and Constituency Arithmetic

The singer also used his post-event media round to dismantle rumours regarding the night's earnings, rejecting claims that the invitation structure yielded nine figures. While clarifying that he did not bank Ksh100 million from the night, he confirmed that the staged occasion successfully raised Ksh10 million.

Beyond ticket collections, the evening functioned as a paid promotional vehicle. Bahati detailed how commercial partners and supporting business operators were tied directly into the rollout, securing millions of impressions across digital channels that carried the staged wedding chatter.

That corporate and digital visibility is now feeding into a protracted political rematch. Bahati contested the Mathare parliamentary seat under the Jubilee Party banner during the 2022 General Election, losing the contest to Orange Democratic Movement legislator Anthony Oluoch. With his sights locked on the 2027 ballot, the musician has increasingly treated public intrigue as an operating budget for relevance.

Whether Friday’s 10 am revelation introduces a music release, a family brand expansion, or the next phase of his Mathare mobilization apparatus remains locked behind his comment section. But with 100 micro-payouts dangling for whoever decodes the post first, Bahati has once again ensured that his audience does the heavy promotional lifting.
