In a surprising turn that underscores the complex, often public, relationship between controversial figures, social media personality Manzi wa Mombasa contacted Pastor Victor Kanyari for transport money to attend his church service, despite a history that included Kanyari expelling her for online content he deemed inappropriate.

Preaching during a Sunday service on September 27, 2026, the founder of Salvation Healing Ministry publicly questioned Manzi wa Mombasa’s absence after personally facilitating her journey. Pastor Kanyari openly wondered if she had already spent the fare he sent her, expressing frustration over the situation.

“Yesterday she called me and said she wanted to come to church but was dismayed because she did not have transport money. And I did send her. But now I wonder where she is because she is not here,” Kanyari stated from the pulpit. He added, “She has spent the fare money which I sent her. But also, you may see her coming in later on.”

The preacher’s remarks laid bare an ongoing interaction between the two public figures, despite their previously strained dynamic. Kanyari recounted the interaction, explaining that Manzi wa Mombasa had reached out on Saturday, September 26, indicating her desire to attend the service but citing a lack of funds for transport. He subsequently sent her the money.

This incident provided Kanyari an opportunity to revisit their past disagreements. He reminded his congregants and the public that he had previously expelled Manzi wa Mombasa from his church due to content she was sharing online.

“She had been online doing some bad videos, and that is why I had at some point gotten mad and kicked her out of church,” Kanyari explained, highlighting the past friction that led to her expulsion.

The public questioning and the historical context reveal a persistent, albeit tumultuous, line of communication between Manzi wa Mombasa and Pastor Kanyari. Her decision to seek his financial assistance, despite having been publicly ousted from his ministry over her online persona, suggested a continued, complex engagement between them.

The preacher concluded his remarks by leaving open the possibility of her eventual arrival, but the initial public query cemented the latest chapter in their unfolding and publicly scrutinised relationship.
