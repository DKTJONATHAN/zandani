A violent conflict inside the cockpit of a commercial airliner forced an immediate high-altitude emergency diversion over Saudi Arabia on Wednesday morning, after a co-pilot reportedly stabbed the flight commander mid-flight.

Flydubai Flight FZ1073, a Boeing 737 carrying more than 170 passengers from Dubai to Tel Aviv, departed Dubai International Airport at 6:05 a.m. East Africa Time. Approximately two and a half hours into the scheduled route, flight controllers received distress alerts indicating a severe onboard disturbance and a potential hijacking situation.

Accounts of the incident indicate the first officer stabbed the captain in an apparent attempt to take control of the aircraft. The rapid physical altercation inside the flight deck prompted immediate physical intervention from personnel and passengers in the forward cabin.

### Cockpit Breach and Physical Restraint

![Passengers disembarking a Flydubai plane after an emergency landing at Tabuk Airport on Wednesday, September 30.](https://i.ibb.co/rG6yQc4J/ef4f55246600.webp)

Under international aviation security standards, locked cockpit doors strictly limit access during flight. However, as the struggle unfolded, a passenger alongside a crew member managed to force entry into the flight deck, physical intervention that prevented further control inputs from the rogue pilot.

The combined force restrained the first officer, neutralizing the threat while the damaged aircraft remained stabilized in controlled airspace. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu confirmed the operational sequence, stating that a passenger and crew member breached the flight deck to overpower the pilot responsible for the assault.

While the attacker was restrained, flight attendants moved the injured captain into the main cabin area directly outside the cockpit door to administer emergency medical aid.

### In-Flight Triage and Operational Control

Video footage recorded inside the cabin during the crisis captures flight attendants delivering immediate trauma care while managing passenger movement. Crew members evaluated the captain’s consciousness level, positioned pillows to support the officer’s spine and respiratory alignment, and attempted to stem blood loss.

Conversations captured during the medical evaluation show crew members coordinating in Kiswahili to verify vital signs and coordinate physical aid. One attendant directed another to monitor whether the captain was vocalizing, while systematically instructing helpers to adjust support under the injured officer.

![A close-up of the damage on a Flydubai plane after an emergency landing at Tabuk Airport on Wednesday, September 30.](https://i.ibb.co/W44PpVTN/1ddfdb2553b4.webp)

Simultaneously, crew members enforced standard operational security protocols within the passenger cabin, instructing bystanders to cease filming and maintaining order while the flight crew established communication with regional air traffic control towers.

### Emergency Diversion to Tabuk

With the flight deck secured and the captain receiving first aid, the aircraft executed an expedited emergency descent into Tabuk Airport in northwestern Saudi Arabia. Local ground controllers cleared the flight for immediate arrival, placing emergency medical teams and security forces on standby along the runway.

The Boeing 737 touched down safely at Tabuk without further incident. Ground teams evacuated all 170 passengers onto the tarmac, with zero injuries reported among the passenger complement.

Flydubai, which maintains regular commercial operations across the Middle East, East Africa, and Europe, has not formally released the operational roster or official nationalities of the crew involved. Investigations into the breach and the medical status of the flight crew remain ongoing under aviation safety authorities.
