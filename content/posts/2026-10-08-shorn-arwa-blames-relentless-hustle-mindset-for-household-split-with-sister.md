Kenyan influencer and content creator Shorn Arwa has revealed that her strict, target-driven work ethic directly contributed to a domestic split with her sister, leading the two to end their shared living arrangement.

In a candid, selfie-style video published to her Instagram account, the digital creator addressed the quiet breakdown of their household setup, framing the separation as the unintended consequence of her relentless push for ambition. Rather than pointing to a public disagreement or external conflict, Arwa attributed the domestic shift to the pressure generated when her individual drive for constant output collided with shared family living.

Arwa explained that her personal approach to life is built around structured productivity goals, fueled by a deep-seated motivation to move past earlier personal adversity. However, that constant ambition extended beyond her private planning and into how she expected those in her immediate circle to operate.

“I’m the type of person who wants to do… I have like a list,” Arwa said in the Instagram clip, recounting how she urged her sister to adopt a similar high-intensity approach to work and survival. “I told you my sister, we gotta prove all of them wrong, we need to constantly hustle.”

Arwa acknowledged that her uncompromising standards and persistent insistence on continuous output became overwhelming for her sister. The continuous demand to match her pace and target-oriented focus created a strain on their daily interactions, eventually leading to the decision to move into separate homes.

Shorn Arwa has built her public brand around high-energy commentary and a goal-oriented persona, but her latest admission shows the personal cost that sometimes accompanies that drive within private family spaces. The confession offers a rare glimpse into how the hyper-demanding productivity expectations often required in digital content creation can disrupt personal domestic boundaries.

For Arwa, whose career depends on constant momentum and visibility, projecting those strict expectations onto a family member living under the same roof created an untenable home dynamic.

While Arwa did not disclose whether the two sisters have established new boundaries for their relationship outside of living together, she confirmed that co-habitation is officially over. The decision leaves the content creator focusing on her personal routine while both sisters maintain independent living spaces.
