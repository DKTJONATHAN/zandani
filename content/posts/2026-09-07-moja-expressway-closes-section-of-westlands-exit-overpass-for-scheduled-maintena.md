Moja Expressway Company has announced a 12-day partial closure of the Nairobi Expressway at the Westlands Exit beginning Tuesday, September 8, 2026, to conduct structural maintenance and civil works along the elevated overpass deck.

### What we know

- The infrastructure maintenance will affect the section of the elevated deck situated immediately after the Westlands exit ramp.
- Under the temporary traffic management plan, vehicles leaving the toll route through the Westlands Exit will be diverted directly onto the lower transit corridor.
- "Motorists using the Nairobi Expressway are informed of maintenance and construction works along the elevated overpass deck after the Nairobi Westlands Exit from 8 pm, September 8 2026 to 5 pm, September 20 2026," Moja Expressway stated in its official notice.
- Moja Expressway confirmed that traffic marshals will be stationed along the affected approach ramps and the lower A8 connection point to guide vehicles.

The infrastructure maintenance will affect the section of the elevated deck situated immediately after the Westlands exit ramp. According to a formal notice issued by the toll-road operator on Monday, September 7, 2026, the repair and engineering works will commence at 8:00 pm on Tuesday, September 8, and are scheduled to end at 5:00 pm on Sunday, September 20, 2026.

Under the temporary traffic management plan, vehicles leaving the toll route through the Westlands Exit will be diverted directly onto the lower transit corridor. The operator directed all motorists exiting at that junction to join the lower A8 highway along Waiyaki Way for the entire duration of the maintenance window.

"Motorists using the Nairobi Expressway are informed of maintenance and construction works along the elevated overpass deck after the Nairobi Westlands Exit from 8 pm, September 8 2026 to 5 pm, September 20 2026," Moja Expressway stated in its official notice. "During this period, motorists exiting at the Nairobi Westlands are strictly instructed to join the lower A8 road on Waiyaki Way."

Moja Expressway confirmed that traffic marshals will be stationed along the affected approach ramps and the lower A8 connection point to guide vehicles. Motorists traveling through the Westlands interchange have been instructed to follow the physical directional signage, obey the instructions of the on-site marshals, and reduce their driving speeds when approaching the work zone.

The company stated that the maintenance operations will cause temporary travel adjustments for drivers using the western end of the 27-kilometer expressway. Motorists heading toward Westlands, Kangemi, Uthiru, and adjoining destinations on the western side of Nairobi have been advised to account for traffic adjustments and schedule their trips accordingly.

In tandem with the overpass works, the Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) is executing civil engineering projects on the ground level in the same area. KeNHA’s scope of work involves the construction, extension, and structural enhancement of non-motorized transport infrastructure along the Waiyaki Way corridor.

KeNHA is constructing designated pedestrian walkways and footbridges along the lower A8 highway. The state road authority confirmed that these ground-level works form part of the broader national government project to widen roads, complete urban beautification, and establish dedicated pedestrian facilities alongside Nairobi's major arterial routes.

Normal traffic operations on the elevated overpass deck after the Westlands Exit are scheduled to resume on Sunday, September 20, 2026, at 5:00 pm.
