A formal security guarantee issued by Nairobi Regional Police Commander Issa Mohamud crumbled Saturday afternoon after suspected goons and private security details stormed the main dais at Jacaranda Grounds, forcing two sitting Cabinet Secretaries, a county governor, and the Orange Democratic Movement’s top executive hierarchy to flee the stage.

The disruption at the historic Eastlands venue halted a major political memorial organized to mark the close of the first anniversary since the death of former Prime Minister Raila Odinga, transforming a tightly choreographed gathering into violent running battles.

### Dais Protocol Collapse

The security perimeter disintegrated when several political aspirants attempted to access the main VIP dais accompanied by their personal bodyguards. The resulting friction between rival retinues escalated rapidly into physical scuffles directly in front of the platform.

![ODM RALLY](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/odm-rally.jpg.webp?itok=fXI9BPYE)

Matters reached a boiling point when unidentified individuals breached the stage barrier, pushed into the VIP area, and seized the public address microphone while national party leaders were delivering introductions.

While some leaders attempted to use the remaining public address equipment to appeal for calm—pleading with thousands of attendees across the open field not to stampede—the platform quickly became untenable. When uniformed police moved into the crowd to quell the scuffles, confrontations multiplied, triggering running clashes that dispersed the crowd and compelled the leadership to evacuate.

### Cabinet Secretaries and Party Chiefs Exposed

The security breach placed significant state and county personnel in direct jeopardy. At the dais during the platform invasion were:

* **National Executive Officials:** National Treasury Cabinet Secretary John Mbadi and Mining and Blue Economy Cabinet Secretary Hassan Joho.
* **County Leadership:** Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja and Kisii Governor Simba Arati, who serves as ODM Deputy Party Leader.
* **Party Leadership:** ODM National Chairperson and Homa Bay Governor Gladys Wanga.

![odm.jpg](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/odm_7.jpg.webp?itok=s9-_ZawO)

Both Mbadi and Joho entered President William Ruto's broad-based cabinet as senior ODM representatives, making the security arrangements around their public appearances a dual matter of state VIP protection and political management.

### The Failed Security Assurance

The violent breakdown stood in stark contrast to specific public undertakings made on Friday by Nairobi Regional Police Commander Issa Mohamud. Following planning sessions with ODM organizers, Mohamud announced that multi-agency police units would secure the entire route from Uhuru Park through Jogoo Road to Jacaranda Grounds.

At the time, the regional commander issued an explicit warning that security personnel were under firm instructions to prevent disorder and arrest anyone attempting to disrupt proceedings. Yet on Saturday, the transition from orderly procession to open confrontation took place without effective containment at the VIP dais itself.

Neither the ODM national secretariat nor the Nairobi Regional Police Command had issued an operational debrief or formal statement regarding the breach by late Saturday evening. Authorities have also not confirmed whether any suspects were detained during the scuffles or if casualties were sustained as supporters fled the running battles across the grounds.
