The Kenya National Examinations Council has stripped national examination candidates of all personal timekeeping devices, outlawing even basic analogue wristwatches and transferring full responsibility for tracking exam time to school managers.

Under the directive issued as the 2026 Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) tests enter their third week, field officers must ensure no candidate enters an examination room wearing any form of wristwatch. To replace personal watches, centre managers are required to install functional wall clocks in every active testing room across the country.

The prohibition closes a gap left by earlier security measures. In 2023, the council barred smartwatches and advanced digital timepieces capable of storing data or communicating wirelessly, while continuing to permit traditional quartz and mechanical analogue watches. The complete ban on all wristwear eliminates the need for invigilators to visually inspect individual watch faces for hidden digital capabilities or modified components.

### Operational Strain on Exam Centres

The timing of the enforcement coincides with the transition into papers that involve the largest student populations, placing immediate logistical demands on examination centres. Rather than relying on individual candidates to monitor their own pacing, centre administrators must verify that every classroom used for testing has clear, functional, and accurately set wall clocks visible from every student desk.

The watch prohibition reinforces a broad ban on personal electronic equipment inside national test rooms. Mobile phones, tablets, laptops, Bluetooth earpieces, audio recording tools, and programmable calculators remain barred unless explicitly authorized by the council. Candidates sitting subjects that permit mathematical equipment are restricted to basic non-programmable scientific calculators that cannot store text or software applications. Where digital assessments occur, centres are confined strictly to council-approved computers running authorized software.

### Daily Chain of Custody Protocol

Beyond candidate equipment checks, the council has reiterated strict security steps governing the daily distribution of examination materials. Field teams and centre managers must maintain a synchronized schedule starting at the security distribution points.

* **07:00 AM Container Opening:** Secure metallic storage containers holding national examination papers must be unlocked precisely at 7:00 am each morning in the presence of authorized supervisors.
* **Personnel Verification:** Centre managers must maintain an approved weekly roster of authorized invigilation staff on site.
* **Identity Mandate:** All listed personnel must present original national identity cards at the container site before receiving examination packets.

With high-volume subject papers running throughout the week, the council's strict protocols place total control over exam mechanics—from paper distribution to the clock on the wall—strictly in the hands of designated centre officials.
