EMBAKASI EAST - Embakasi East Member of Parliament Babu Owino has formally declared his intention to contest the Nairobi gubernatorial seat in the 2027 general elections. The 37-year-old legislator disclosed his political ambitions alongside details regarding his academic background and early career earnings during an interview on Saturday.

During the session, the MP confirmed that he holds five university degrees, referencing his transition from his formative years in Kisumu’s Nyalenda estate to his entry into national politics. Owino noted that his professional life began in student governance, a role he stated provided a monthly income of Ksh 40,000.

Discussing his private life, the legislator outlined a strict schedule beginning at 5:30 a.m. and concluding at midnight, emphasizing that prayer remains a staple of his daily routine. He identified his wife as his primary confidant and named the deaths of his father and veteran opposition leader Raila Odinga as the most significant personal losses he has experienced.

The MP’s announcement confirms his entry into the field of prospective contenders for the Nairobi county leadership, an office currently held by Governor Johnson Sakaja.
