Kenya’s energy ministry has completed a top-to-bottom leadership reset across three major state corporations, placing an insider at the helm of the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority (EPRA) as the agency seeks to move past high-profile fuel quality investigations.

Cabinet Secretary Opiyo Wandayi announced the immediate appointment of Engineer Edward Mwirigi Kinyua as EPRA’s substantive Director General on Wednesday, September 30. Kinyua, who previously headed the regulator’s Petroleum and Gas Directorate, steps into the role directly from the technical division responsible for policing oil downstream operations, pricing structures, and fuel specifications.

### Broad Parastatal Reorganization

Kinyua’s elevation was part of a simultaneous C-suite overhaul across the state's strategic energy agencies following competitive recruitment drives by their respective boards:

![Eng. Edward Kinyua, Petroleum and Gas Director at EPRA speaking at a media roundtable](https://i.ibb.co/n8wJV5VQ/704c73554b9d.webp)

* **KETRACO:** CPA Tom Odhiambo Imbo was named Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Kenya Electricity Transmission Company.
* **GDC:** Stephen Kipsang Busieney was appointed Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Geothermal Development Company.
* **EPRA:** Engineer Edward Mwirigi Kinyua takes over as Director General after serving as head of petroleum and gas.

The ministry confirmed that all three executive appointments take effect immediately, concluding a period of transition in agencies that form the backbone of Kenya's power grid and fuel supply chain.

### Succession Following Criminal Probe

Kinyua assumes control of EPRA after months of administrative disruption triggered by the exit of former Director General Daniel Kiptoo. Kiptoo resigned following his arrest by officers from the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI) during an inquiry into allegations that substandard fuel had entered the local market. Kiptoo was subsequently released while police inquiries continued, but the controversy left the regulator facing intense public and commercial questioning over its market oversight.

![Engineer Edward Mwirigi Kinyua](https://i.ibb.co/LXhmzs5s/3d130c060d63.webp)

Prior to Kinyua’s appointment, Dr. Eng. Joseph Oketch—formerly director of EPRA’s Electricity and Renewable Energy Directorate—had been serving as acting Director General. Oketch had guided the authority’s technical standards and grid codes during the interim period.

By selecting Kinyua, the ministry brings an executive directly familiar with oil and gas licensing, depot inspections, and import compliance into the top office. His immediate mandate centers on restoring enforcement rigor across fuel quality checks and maintaining stability in the monthly petroleum pricing cycles.

### A Restructured Board and Executive

The executive changes lock in place a completely new governing structure at EPRA. Nearly two months prior to Wednesday's announcement, President William Ruto appointed former Bumula Member of Parliament Moses Wekesa Mwambu Mabonga as EPRA Board Chairperson.

With both the board chair and Director General seats now substantively filled, the new management faces an immediate test in tightening supply-chain surveillance, enforcing quality benchmarks at transit depots, and rebuilding commercial confidence in the country's energy oversight agencies.
