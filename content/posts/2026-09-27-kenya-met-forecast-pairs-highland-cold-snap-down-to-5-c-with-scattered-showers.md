A sharp drop in overnight temperatures across Kenya’s central highlands will accompany scattered showers over the next five days, placing urban commuters and rural households under a prolonged cold front through Wednesday, September 30.

According to an advisory issued on Saturday by the Kenya Meteorological Service Authority (KMSA), nighttime temperatures are projected to sink into single digits across high-altitude counties. Nyandarua is set to experience the country's steepest thermal plunge, with night lows sinking between 5°C and 9°C.

In Nairobi, where overnight temperatures are expected to touch 12°C, the cold conditions will coincide with light precipitation during key travel windows. The weather agency warned capital residents to prepare for showers concentrated on Sunday night, carrying directly into Monday morning’s rush hour before recurring Monday night.

### The Highland Chill

![Overview of Nairobi cityscape on a cold day.](https://i.ibb.co/n2y8Z6Z/34f1d994061b.webp)

The cold snap extends across the broader central agricultural belt and sections of the northern Rift Valley. Kiambu and Laikipia are forecast to log night lows between 11°C and 14°C, while Samburu faces overnight readings ranging from 7°C to 12°C. In Nyeri and Murang’a, nighttime minimums will oscillate between 13°C and 15°C.

Rainfall in these highland areas is expected to remain light but persistent, with Nyeri, Kirinyaga, Murang’a, and Kiambu forecast to receive showers predominantly between Monday and Tuesday.

Further east, the counties of Meru, Tharaka-Nithi, Embu, Kitui, Machakos, and Makueni are slated for light showers mainly across Sunday and Monday nights.

### Western Corridors and Coastal Showers

Across the Rift Valley and western basins, rainfall activity will be broader in duration. Counties including West Pokot, Trans Nzoia, Nandi, Kakamega, Vihiga, Bungoma, and Busia are marked for light rain running continuously from Saturday through Tuesday.

Kericho is projected to record moderate showers on Monday and Tuesday, while lighter precipitation covers Nakuru, Narok, Bomet, Kisii, and Nyamira. In the Lake Victoria basin, rainfall will encompass Kisumu and Homa Bay, with Siaya expecting light showers extending into Wednesday and Thursday.

![Motorists using a flooded highway during cold and rainy weather in Nairobi](https://i.ibb.co/V0y1wh7Y/29368001111a.webp)

Along the Indian Ocean coastline, maritime showers will remain active. Mombasa, Kwale, Lamu, and Kilifi are forecast to experience light showers predominantly at night through Tuesday.

### Divergent Frontier Heat

The chilly conditions dominating the central and western highlands contrast sharply with conditions in the arid northern frontier, where daytime heat will remain intense.

Mandera and Turkana are forecast to record daytime maximum temperatures reaching up to 38°C, while Wajir will see peaks of up to 35°C over the same five-day window.

With Nairobi and central counties facing the confluence of early-morning moisture and depressed mercury through mid-week, transport operators and public health officials monitor whether the sudden drop in overnight ambient temperatures will accelerate seasonal respiratory complaints across highland settlements.
