A 17.8-kilometre network of feeder and bypass roads encircling Meru's primary educational institutions and suburban connectors has been put up for competitive bidding as national infrastructure planners move to upgrade municipal links to bitumen standards.

The project, issued through formal procurement notices on Tuesday, October 13, is financed by the World Bank under the Horn Africa Gateway Development Project. Rather than a single continuous highway, the road authority has packaged six dispersed arterial roads and two dedicated pedestrian crossings into one urban works contract.

### Targeted Corridors and Student Crossings

The civil works package zeroes in on heavily trafficked municipal arteries that funnel commuter traffic between academic institutions, county referral facilities, and main transit routes. 

![Meru town](https://i.ibb.co/PsRV5Kzn/143b0692a97d.webp)

The six stretches slated for full bitumen upgrading include:

* **Runogone–Kaithe Road**
* **KEMU–Kambakia Road**
* **Three Steers–Kambakia Road**
* **KEMU/Kambakia Link Road to C91**
* **Eastern Bypass–Hospital Road**
* **Western Bypass–Mate Road**

Beyond roadway paving, the design includes dedicated footbridges at Kaaga Boys High School and the Meru National Polytechnic—two nodes characterized by high-volume student pedestrian traffic conflicting with through traffic.

### Tight Procurement and Maintenance Terms

The contracting terms set out by the highway authority enforce a 28-month construction period, followed by a mandatory 12-month defects liability period during which the successful contractor remains financially and operationally responsible for corridor maintenance.

![Meru town](https://i.ibb.co/9HXPmnRY/9542d3dc0dbe.webp)

Procurement conditions bar digital applications, demanding physical submission of bids to curtail submission irregularities.

"The bid documents enclosed in a plain sealed envelope clearly marked with the tender number and description should be deposited in the tender box located at Barabara Plaza, Block C, 2nd Floor, Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA), off Airport South Road, along Mazao Road, so as to be received on or before 26th November 2026 at 11:00 HRS local time," KeNHA stated in the tender documents, adding that late submissions and electronic bids will be rejected.

### The Municipal Transition Benchmark

The 17.8-kilometre multilateral contract directly interfaces with broader state commitments to build municipal capacity in Meru. The national government previously flagged approximately Ksh40 billion in planned urban upgrades to help Meru satisfy the statutory economic, transit, and public works benchmarks required for formal conferment of city status.

Part of that broader blueprint included Ksh5 billion committed to unblocking inner-town gridlock through bypass expansions and 15 kilometres of internal links. The entry of the World Bank-backed Horn Africa Gateway Development Project shifts the immediate operational burden onto pre-construction site handovers and the physical bidding window, which runs through November 26.
