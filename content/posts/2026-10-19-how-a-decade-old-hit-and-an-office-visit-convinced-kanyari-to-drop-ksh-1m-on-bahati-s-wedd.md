Prophet Victor Kanyari’s initial sticker shock at a Ksh 1 million wedding entry fee dissolved the moment pop star Bahati walked into his office with a reminder about where their professional relationship began.

The controversial Nairobi preacher, who had openly questioned why guests were being billed seven figures to attend the upcoming November 6, 2026 ceremony, revealed he personally cleared the million-shilling payment after a face-to-face appeal from the singer on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

Addressing his congregation on Sunday, October 18, 2026, Kanyari pointed to his foundational role in launching Bahati's music career as the decisive factor that overrode his public criticism. According to the preacher, the debt of history tied to the production of Bahati's debut single, 'Mama', made it impossible to stay away from the strictly capped, 100-guest affair.

“Nilikua nimesema sitaenda harusi ya Bahati maana anacharge 1 milioni. Lakini mimi nilitoa hiyo 1 milioni ya Bahati,” Kanyari told his congregants, recounting how the singer visited his office to lobby for his attendance and invoke their years of shared history before mainstream fame.

“Wakati alikuja kwa ofisi yangu akanibembeleza, akaniambia, akanikumbusha, nikaona, ‘Eh, wacha nitoe hiyo pesa kwa sababu… is my friend,’” he added.

Bahati and Diana Marua announced the high-barrier guest policy on October 7, 2026, while celebrating a decade together, setting each seat at the exclusive celebration at Ksh 1 million. Following their private meeting, Bahati published a social media confirmation celebrating Kanyari as the first clergyman to purchase an invitation card, cementing a reconciliation that spans more than ten years of public evolution.

Asked by critics about the abrupt pivot from public mockery to financial backing, Kanyari offered no apologies for his changing positions, noting that those familiar with his temperament understand his pattern of initially rejecting a concept before reconsidering behind closed doors.
