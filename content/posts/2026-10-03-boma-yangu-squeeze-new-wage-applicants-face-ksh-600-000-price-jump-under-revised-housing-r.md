A midnight policy adjustment by the State Department for Housing has created a sharp financial split for Kenyans eyeing government-backed homeownership, raising property acquisition thresholds by up to KSh 600,000 for all incoming applicants.

Under the newly enforced pricing framework released on Thursday, October 1, 2026, prospective buyers submitting applications across the Boma Yangu network now encounter a 20 percent price hike on Affordable Housing units and a 10 percent increase on Market Housing stock.

The revised rates took effect immediately at midnight on October 1, establishing an operational line between early platform adopters and new entrants. According to official directives from the State Department for Housing, the revised cost structure applies exclusively to new applications submitted after the deadline. Existing applicants who lodged their filings under the previous rate card retain their original pricing, effectively shielding early registrants from the financial escalation.

### Breakdown of the Revised Unit Rates

The sharpest percentage shifts hit the core Affordable Housing catalog, where entry-level units absorbed a uniform 20 percent surge across studio and multi-room options.

A basic studio apartment under the affordable classification moved from KSh 1.5 million to KSh 1.8 million, marking an immediate KSh 300,000 adjustment. One-bedroom units jumped from KSh 2 million to KSh 2.4 million, adding KSh 400,000 to the total purchase figure. For buyers targeting two-bedroom affordable units, the price climbed from KSh 3 million to KSh 3.6 million, representing the maximum absolute increase of KSh 600,000 within the affordable category.

Market-rate units, designed for higher-earning tiers, absorbed a 10 percent rate adjustment. Two-bedroom market units rose by KSh 360,000, moving from KSh 3.6 million to KSh 3.96 million. Three-bedroom market properties experienced a KSh 480,000 markup, climbing from KSh 4.8 million to KSh 5.28 million.

### Salary Brackets and Buyer Placement

The revised pricing structure operates directly alongside the government's three-tier income qualification scheme, which categorizes applicants based on verified gross monthly earnings.

Under the state framework, the Social Housing bracket caters to individuals earning up to KSh 19,999 per month. The main Affordable Housing bracket covers wage earners drawing between KSh 20,000 and KSh 149,999 monthly—the demographic most directly impacted by the 20 percent unit increases on studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom layouts. Applicants earning KSh 150,000 and above are funneled into the Affordable Middle-Class and Market Housing tier, where the 10 percent revision applies to larger unit types.

Registration and application processing remain active across designated government digital channels. Prospective homeowners continue to submit files through the eCitizen-integrated Boma Yangu platform, the dedicated web portal, or via the *832# shortcode, with all new submissions processed strictly under the October 1 pricing schedule.
