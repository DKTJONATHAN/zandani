Tech entrepreneur Adam Robinson and his wife Helen Sharp were among the nine people killed when their private Learjet 60 plummeted from high altitude and burst into flames in Palm Beach County, Florida.

The fatal journey on Sunday, November 1, 2026, cut short the lives of an entire Austin family. The aircraft, registered as N722BD, was carrying seven passengers and two crew members from Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas, bound for North Eleuthera Airport in the Bahamas, before it went down near the intersection of Military Trail and Cresthaven Boulevard in Greenacres.

In a joint statement released on Monday, November 2, grieving relatives identified the victims as Robinson, 45, his 40-year-old wife Sharp, and their two young children, Emma, 4, and Charlie, 2. Also on board were family friends Rachael Reed and Charmi Fehr, alongside the family's nanny, Anita Maria Cetina, whom relatives described as having become an integral part of their household through her tender devotion to the children.

Robinson was widely known in business circles as an Austin-based technology entrepreneur who founded marketing technology firms Retention.com and RB2B after earlier stints in finance with Lehman Brothers and Barclays Capital. Sharp operated Helen Sharp Ceramics and founded Influential PR, establishing a prominent profile in Texas public relations and artistic communities.

The flight's cockpit crew comprised captain Jeffrey Gregory, 55, and 20-year-old co-pilot Nathalia Gadelha Filgueiras. Filgueiras had built a reputation in aviation circles after earning her pilot’s licence at just 17 through a high school programme, accumulating notable flying experience before joining the charter.

Air traffic recordings indicate the flight encountered severe icing and moderate turbulence before the crew issued an emergency call as the aircraft dropped rapidly. Dashcam footage captured the final moments of the descent before the jet impacted the ground near Greenacres, leaving a massive crater and scattered debris across the area.
