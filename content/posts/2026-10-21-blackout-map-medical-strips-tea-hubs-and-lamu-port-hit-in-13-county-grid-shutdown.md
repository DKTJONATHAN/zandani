A substantial operational freeze is set for Wednesday, October 21, 2026, as Kenya Power pulls the switch across targeted pockets of 13 counties to carry out network maintenance works.

While maintenance notices from the national distributor are routine, Wednesday’s schedule cuts directly across an unusually dense concentration of public service nerve centres. Major tertiary training colleges, referral facilities, military installations, and industrial tea processing lines will have to run on backup generators or adjust daytime operations.

Most areas on the schedule will be cut off between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm, though sections of Kericho County face an earlier window running from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.

### The Mbagathi and Capital Medical Strip

In Nairobi, the maintenance teams will concentrate on the bustling health, education, and residential belt along Mbagathi Road and its immediate feeder roads. The outage directly touches parts of Kenyatta National Hospital and the Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) main campus, alongside the Kenya Medical Research Institute (KEMRI) and Memorial Hospital.

The same Nairobi feeder also supplies vital support and higher-education infrastructure in the area. Daystar University’s Mbagathi Way Campus, Riara University, Sunshine School, Mbagathi Girls High School, and Shadrack Kimalel School are all slated for the 9:00 am to 5:00 pm shutoff.

Surrounding facilities and estates will similarly spend the working day off the main grid. These include Nairobi West Prison, City Mortuary, Umash Funeral Home, Montezuma Funeral Homes, Akila 2 Estate, Highrise Estate, Highview Estates, Magiwa Estate, Canaan Estate, Ngumo Central, Laini Saba, Undugu, Ngumo, Madaraka, and Geo Map.

### Strategic Coastal Hubs and Ports

At the Coast, the utility's maintenance footprint reaches strategic transport, government, and security installations across Lamu, Kwale, and Kilifi counties.

In Lamu County, power will be turned off across Manda, Magogoni, the Naval Base, and the Lamu LAPSSET Port development, alongside neighbouring supply lines.

Kwale County’s county headquarters and administrative spine will be without mains electricity for eight hours. Affected locations include Kwale Town, Kwale County Offices, Kwale Referral Hospital, Golini, Ziwani, Culture, Kinango, Tiribe, Ndavaya, and Gulanze.

Kilifi’s scheduled outage hits transit and security installations in Mariakani. The circuit disruption takes in Mariakani Town, the local police station, the military barracks, the Air Force facility, Mariakani Health, the VIL weighbridge, Tosha Petrol Station, Msufini, Majengo, Miracle Church, Mariakani Secondary School, Kavuka Primary School, and Mkilo.

### Rift Valley Tea Processing and Farm Belts

In Kericho County, the utility has set aside an eight-hour window from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm that cuts straight into commercial tea processing. The shutdown targets Mau Tea Factory, Kaisugu Tea Factory, the Tea Research Foundation, Chepsir Market, and Brooke Wood.

Neighbouring Nandi County will see outages between 9:00 am and 5:00 pm covering Ndulele, Chemnoet, and Kapchumo.

### Western and Nyanza Circuits

In Kakamega County, the maintenance roster covers Khwisero Market and Khwisero Hospital, running across Emalindi, Mulwanda, Khushiku, Khumsalaba, Ebukwala, Eshibinga, and Eshiluro Village.

Bungoma County consumers along the Nzoia PEFA circuit will be offline from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, affecting Lurare, Lutwindire, St. Paul Nzoia, and Nzoia Market.

Vihiga County’s works will target the areas of Eregi, Malinyo, Kerongo, Chandumba, and Lusiole.

In Migori County, the day-long works cover Lwala Rabuor, Kibuon, Ndiwa Centre, Diruma, Kolanya, and Nyatuoro.

### Eastern and Central Regions

Garissa County will experience outages affecting Maua Millers, Modika CAK, 17KR, Sumeya, Quba, Bulla Hagar, and Duale Farm.

Kitui County customers around Kiusyani Market, St. Luke’s Lower Yatta, Nthilani Market, Ilika Market, Muvitha Market, Syomunyu Girls, Muselele Market, Kalulini Market, and Kalulini Secondary School will similarly be without grid power through 5:00 pm.

In Nyeri County, works will take down supply in two sectors. One covers Muruguru Secondary School, Muruguru Market, Giithierebu Drive, and sections of Kihethu. The second affects Itiati Market, Kiamucheru, Kwa Karoki, and the Itiati Safaricom Booster station.

Kenya Power advised commercial facilities, schools, and medical facilities reliant on steady daytime energy across all affected sectors to adjust their secondary power arrangements ahead of the 9:00 am shutdown.
