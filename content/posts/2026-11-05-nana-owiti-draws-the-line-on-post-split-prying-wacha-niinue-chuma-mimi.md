Nana Owiti is drawing a firm line between her public visibility and her private past. When media presenter Ankali Ray attempted to steer a recent phone conversation toward her split from rapper King Kaka, the media personality shut down the line of inquiry, making it clear that personal gossip holds no space in her current calendar.

The mother of two had answered the call expecting to discuss an unrelated matter, but when asked whether she had moved on from her marriage, she immediately cut through the probe.

"Umenipia juu ya shug(h)li nyingine, achana na hii ingine. Me niko sawa, usijali, niko na watoto," Nana told Ankali, deflecting the attention away from relationship post-mortems and directly onto her daily responsibilities as a parent.

![Nana Owiti \\ Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/PsjN7F5G/d2efdda4ff0f.webp)

The exchange highlighted a deliberate boundary strategy. Rather than leaning into the public appetite for breakup commentary, Nana chose to treat her past relationship as closed territory. She made it plain that public concern over her romantic status was misplaced, assuring followers that her primary focus remains squarely on raising her children.

That boundary extended to her emotional support structure. When pressed further, Nana pointed out that her daily validation comes from an intentionally chosen circle of family and trusted friends, making external speculation irrelevant.

"Shukran, napendwa na familia na marafiki nimewachagua, iyo love inatosha, usijali," she added during the exchange, noting that the affection within her immediate network provides more than enough stability for her current chapter.

Away from the radio inquiries, Nana framed her independence not as a temporary holding pattern, but as an opportunity for physical and personal self-investment. Instead of monitoring narrative commentary around her former union, she indicated that her schedule is now anchored by personal growth targets.

"Wacha niinue chuma mimi, I need to better myself," she stated, choosing to invest her energy in gym routines and individual progress rather than endless public explanations.

By refusing to re-engage the details of her separation, Nana continues to navigate her post-split life on her own terms—keeping her domestic focus intact while directing her energy into self-improvement.
