Nyayo National Stadium transformed into a sea of orange and black this morning as the nation bid a formal state farewell to Raila Amolo Odinga, the veteran opposition leader who died at 80. The ceremony, conducted with military precision, marked the conclusion of a week-long mourning period for the man famously known as "Baba."

The procession commenced at 9:20 a.m. from Parliament Buildings, moving through the city under a heavy escort of Kenya Defence Forces (KDF) outriders. The casket, draped in the national flag, reached the stadium grounds at 9:46 a.m., where it was received by a dense crowd of supporters brandishing palm fronds and signature ODM-branded regalia.

Anglican Bishop David Kodia led the service, characterizing Odinga as a man driven by conviction rather than transactional politics. The ceremony featured a poignant address from Ida Odinga, who challenged the political class to confront the culture of greed that her husband famously opposed.

President William Ruto and international dignitaries, including the Presidents of Somalia and Ethiopia, observed as the military detail managed the solemn proceedings. Security operations across the city remained intensive, with the National Police Service and NYS personnel securing major thoroughfares.

Following the Nyayo Stadium service, the body is scheduled for transport to Kisumu on Saturday for further public viewing. The official interment will take place on Sunday, October 19, at the Odinga family estate in Bondo. A seven-day mourning period remains in effect.
