Geoffrey Mosiria emerged from a high-profile consultative session at Rigathi Gachagua’s Karen residence on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, armed with a bold claim: that the former Deputy President had personally validated his ambitions to run Nairobi County.

Fresh off the sit-down, which also included former Interior Cabinet Secretary Dr. Fred Matiang’i, the former Nairobi County Chief Officer wasted no time deploying Gachagua’s street moniker to bolster his 2027 gubernatorial campaign.

“Nairobi needs a more serious leader, a truthful man,” Mosiria declared following the consultations. “Hadi Riggy G amekubali Nairobi Governor ni Mosiria! Niko ready kufanya kazi ya kufix Nairobi. Nairobi does not need a politician; it needs a worker, who is Mosiria. Siri ni Mosiria! Nairobi ni Mosiria!”

The Karen meeting brings Mosiria into direct orbit with heavyweights shaping the opposition’s political realignments. While he stopped short of claiming a formal coalition endorsement, Mosiria framed the talks as evidence that key figures recognize his administrative credentials.

His core pitch centers on drawing a hard line between seasoned political operatives and administrative fixers. Leveraging his past experience inside City Hall's operational ranks, Mosiria is attempting to brand himself as a pragmatic bureaucrat focused on service delivery rather than party rhetoric.

That positioning strategy comes alongside a broader political outreach. Mosiria has previously signaled an eagerness to collaborate across party lines, naming Wiper Party leader Kalonzo Musyoka and Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna alongside Gachagua and Matiang’i as potential allies in reshaping the capital’s governance.

With the Karen sit-down establishing his high-level political posture, Mosiria is now taking his candidacy directly to the public. He has invited Nairobi residents to a campaign gathering scheduled for Sunday at the KCB Rugby Football Club grounds along Thika Road, adjacent to KCA University.

Sunday's gathering along the busy Thika Road corridor will serve as the first practical test of Mosiria's strategy—specifically whether his transition from county administrative officer to high-stakes political contender can draw genuine public momentum beyond closed-door consultations.
