Kenya Power has slated a nine-hour utility shutdown across designated areas in Nairobi and Kajiado County on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, requiring residents and commercial operators in the affected zones to secure secondary energy sources during routine system maintenance.

The scheduled maintenance window is set to commence at 8:00 AM and run through 5:00 PM, though actual restoration timing will vary across specific distribution lines depending on localized work.

### Geographic Footprint of the Interruption

The planned power cut targets distinct residential and commercial zones across the two counties. In Nairobi, the network downtime directly impacts Spring Valley, an upmarket residential and commercial enclave home to high-end private housing, boutique offices, and diplomatic residences.

Concurrently, the outage extends into southern Kajiado County, affecting customers located in Loitoktok and Njukini. Loitoktok functions as a major cross-border commercial and agricultural hub near the Tanzanian border, while Njukini represents a vital rural trade and farming cluster. Both areas depend on continuous grid electricity to run local retail establishments, agricultural operations, and domestic services.

The simultaneous work across these dispersed locations highlights Kenya Power’s scheduled servicing routines on distribution lines and transformer stations feeding both urban suburban nodes and regional outposts.

### Mitigation and Grid Maintenance Rationale

According to Kenya Power, routine shutdowns are conducted to perform general servicing, repair network infrastructure, and improve overall grid stability across the distribution network.

Because the maintenance window spans core working hours from morning to late afternoon, the utility has advised residents and business operators in Spring Valley, Loitoktok, and Njukini to make alternative energy arrangements. Enterprises and households in the affected sectors are expected to utilize generator backups or auxiliary power systems throughout the nine-hour duration.

Kenya Power noted that power restoration will proceed once technical maintenance on the lines is completed, with full service expected to resume by 5:00 PM on Tuesday.
