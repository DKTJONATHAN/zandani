Emergency surgical and trauma teams at Moi Teaching and Referral Hospital spent Thursday night admitting critically burned casualties following a fuel tanker detonation that killed at least two people in the Juakali area of Uasin Gishu County.

The disaster unfolded along the busy Eldoret-Webuye highway near Paul Boit High School, after a heavy transit hauler loaded with petroleum overturned by the roadside. Within minutes of the crash, dozens of nearby residents and road users converged on the wreckage with containers to collect fuel leaking from the damaged compartments.

The volatile vapor ignited while scores were still crowded around the chassis, triggering a violent explosion that engulfed the crowd and the wreckage in an instant.

### Midnight Evacuations to Regional Trauma Care

![A fuel tanker engulfed in flames along the Eldoret-Webuye highway on Thursday, September 17, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/Jjd3FtS6/4effa62f1e23.webp)

Two victims caught at the center of the blast were killed instantly and burnt beyond recognition, police and humanitarian officials confirmed. At least ten other people suffered extensive thermal injuries, prompting a multi-agency evacuation by county ambulances, Kenya Red Cross personnel, firefighters, and private motorists who stopped to ferry the injured from the scene.

The Kenya Red Cross designated the incident a mass casualty emergency as rescue teams pushed through the intense heat to pull survivors away from the burning wreckage.

Critically injured casualties were redirected to Moi Teaching and Referral Hospital in Eldoret, the main specialized medical facility capable of managing advanced burn trauma in the North Rift region. Medical staff worked through the night to stabilize victims presenting with severe body burns and inhalation injuries.

### Transit Artery Paralyzed

![Traffic along the Eldoret-Webuye highway after a trailer bursts into flames on Thursday, September 17, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/Wv2YWLww/00d36d9076d8.webp)

Beyond the human toll, the explosion severed traffic flow across one of the primary commercial arteries linking the Kenyan interior to western border crossings. Heavy freight trucks and passenger buses bound for Webuye, Bungoma, and the Malaba border were brought to a complete halt for several hours as emergency crews battled the blaze and cleared scorching debris from the carriage.

Uasin Gishu County Police Commander Wilberforce Sichirani said security agencies maintained cordons around the perimeter to deter secondary crowds while forensic and recovery teams combed the wreckage.

"Search and rescue operations continued through the night to ensure all casualties were accounted for, and investigations are actively underway to determine the precise circumstances that triggered the fire," Sichirani stated.

The Juakali disaster echoes a recurring pattern along Kenya’s major transit routes, where overturned tankers carrying flammable hydrocarbons rapidly transform into deadly collection zones before security forces can establish safety cordons. While emergency services succeeded in transferring the immediate survivors to intensive care, police confirmed that specialized identification procedures will be necessary for the two deceased victims as investigations into the hauler’s initial rollover proceed.
