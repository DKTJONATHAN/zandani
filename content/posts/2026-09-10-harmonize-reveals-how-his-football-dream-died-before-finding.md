**NAIROBI, September 10, 2026** - Bongo Flava star Harmonize has disclosed that his career path shifted from professional athletics to music following the collapse of his football aspirations in Dar es Salaam. Speaking during a live broadcast on the *Obinna TV Extra* podcast, the artist confirmed that his entry into the entertainment industry was a pivot necessitated by the demands of his early working life.

The sequence of events leading to his musical success began after the singer relocated to Dar es Salaam to assist his brother-in-law. Harmonize noted that the daily grind of retail work made consistent morning and evening football practice impossible. Recognizing that professional football offered a slow path to financial stability, he chose to step away from the pitch to focus on his vocal talents.

This shift preceded his participation in the Bongo Star Search competition. By 2015, he secured a major recording deal, eventually establishing his own independent label, Konde Gang. As the artist marks his tenth year in the industry, his account confirms that the collapse of his sporting ambitions served as the catalyst for his current status in East African music.
