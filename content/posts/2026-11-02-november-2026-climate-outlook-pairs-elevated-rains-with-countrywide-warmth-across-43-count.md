November 2026 is set to deliver an unusual climate combination across Kenya, with meteorologists projecting elevated rainfall across the majority of the country alongside higher-than-average temperatures nationwide.

According to the latest climate outlook, precipitation levels throughout the month will exceed long-term historical averages in more than 40 counties. Rather than bringing a cooler atmosphere, the rain cycle will coincide with mean monthly temperatures that remain warmer than normal across all 47 counties.

### Heavy Rains Spanning Key Economic Corridors

The forecast maps out significant wet conditions across agricultural, urban, and coastal zones. In the capital and surrounding central highlands, above-average rainfall is expected to cover Nairobi, Kiambu, Murang’a, Nyeri, Nyandarua, and Kirinyaga.

The western agricultural belt and Lake Victoria basin face similar conditions, with above-normal precipitation projected for Kakamega, Bungoma, Busia, Vihiga, Kisumu, Homa Bay, Siaya, Migori, Kisii, and Nyamira.

Across the Rift Valley and South Rift, elevated rainfall is forecast for Nakuru, Narok, Kajiado, Bomet, Kericho, Nandi, Uasin Gishu, Trans Nzoia, Elgeyo Marakwet, and Baringo.

Eastern and coastal counties are also aligned for high precipitation volumes, including Machakos, Makueni, Kitui, Embu, Meru, Tharaka-Nithi, Isiolo, Mombasa, Kilifi, Kwale, Lamu, Taita Taveta, and Tana River.

### Pastoral Northern Belt Expectations

In the northern and northeastern regions, rainfall distributions vary slightly but remain historically elevated. Garissa, Mandera, and Wajir are listed among areas expecting above-average cumulative rainfall for the month.

Further north, major portions of West Pokot, Samburu, and Marsabit are similarly slated for rainfall exceeding seasonal averages.

Specific pockets within Turkana, Samburu, Marsabit, Baringo, and West Pokot will experience rainfall ranging between near-average and above-average thresholds during the 30-day period.

### Nationwide Thermal Elevation

While rainfall will be widespread, atmospheric data indicates no overall cooling trend for the month. Mean temperatures across every region of Kenya are projected to be warmer than long-term November averages.

This persistent thermal pattern means daytime and nighttime temperature baselines will remain elevated even as localized precipitation peaks across the highlands, lowlands, and coastal strips throughout November.
