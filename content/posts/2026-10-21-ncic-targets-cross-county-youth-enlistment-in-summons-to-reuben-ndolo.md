The National Cohesion and Integration Commission has initiated formal proceedings against former Makadara MP Reuben Ndolo, threatening him with arrest warrants if he fails to account for his public admissions regarding cross-county youth mobilisation.

The statutory cohesion agency ordered Ndolo to present himself at its offices on Monday, October 26, at 2:00 pm. The summons follows an investigation into remarks he delivered on Sunday, October 18, during a service at Jesus Teaching Ministry Church in Donholm, within Embakasi East Constituency.

### The Recruitment Network Under Review

Central to the commission's inquiry is Ndolo’s open declaration from the pulpit that he had coordinated youth operations across three western counties. Addressing congregants, the former lawmaker stated that he had personally travelled through Busia, Luanda in Vihiga County, and Bondo in Siaya County to enlist young men ahead of an upcoming political gathering.

![reuben-ndolo.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/5hbsH5PW/c2ad3bcffc20.webp)

Ndolo framed the recruitment drive as an assignment executed on behalf of an unnamed political patron, telling worshippers that he had been dispatched to handle the logistics by an influential figure younger than himself. The mobilisation was tied to preparations for the first anniversary memorial of former Prime Minister Raila Odinga in Bondo.

The agency’s scrutiny centers on whether these organized cross-county youth movements constitute an unlawful paramilitary-style mobilisation or pose an active threat to public order and ethnic coexistence in the staging areas.

### Friction Over Memorial Preparations

Beyond the recruitment disclosures, investigators are reviewing Ndolo’s allegations regarding political infighting surrounding the Odinga family and the logistics of the Bondo service.

During the Donholm address, Ndolo publicly aired sharp disagreements among regional politicians over the treatment of Ida Odinga. He directed specific criticisms at Suna East MP Junet Mohamed, alleging that senior leaders had neglected their obligations during a period of family distress, with one flying to Dubai instead of attending to domestic matters.

![Linda Mwananchi rally on September 13, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/BVrCc8bh/36d53d250abf.webp)

Ndolo also injected national factional politics into the gathering by invoking former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua. The former MP claimed he intended to escort Gachagua on a tour of Nyanza, directly challenging Mohamed over whether local leaders could block such an entry.

### Expanding Enforcement Perimeter

The action against Ndolo signals an intensifying regulatory push by the commission as politicians step up ground operations ahead of the 2027 General Election. Public rallies and religious platforms have increasingly turned into launchpads for unsanctioned political regrouping.

Ndolo is not the sole high-profile figure caught in the agency’s current dragnet. The commission has recently issued parallel summonses to former Deputy President Gachagua, Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale, Busia Governor Paul Otuoma, and Homa Bay Town MP Peter Kaluma over statements being probed for ethnic incitement, hate speech, and national security risks.

In its formal notice, the commission warned that the October 26 appearance serves as Ndolo's sole administrative opportunity to respond to the evidentiary file before a formal determination is made. Should the former MP fail to appear at the designated hour, the agency stated it will initiate contempt proceedings, pursue criminal charges, or secure court-backed arrest warrants to compel his attendance.
