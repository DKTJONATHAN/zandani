Democracy for Citizens Party (DCP) economic adviser Peter Mbae turned a morning broadcast appearance into a dual political salvo on October 5, 2026, declaring that the late Raila Odinga’s reform mantle has settled on Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna, before reviving calls for an official probe into Odinga's death in India.

Speaking live on K24 TV’s *Morning Breakfast Show*, Mbae argued that Odinga’s political impact expanded far beyond the boundary lines of the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM). In his view, Sifuna has stepped forward as the central political figure carrying the reform torch left vacant after Odinga’s passing in October 2025.

“Raila Odinga is a figure who will be studied for generations,” Mbae said during the broadcast. “However, the spirit of Raila Odinga right now rests squarely on Sifuna.”

The studio endorsement comes at a pivotal moment for Sifuna, following his official unveiling as the presidential candidate for The Equitable Party (TEP). The move formally converted his popular “Linda Mwananchi” initiative into a registered political party structure ahead of the 2027 General Election.

During TEP’s launch in Nairobi, Sifuna directly invoked Odinga’s memory, framing his new political outfit as a continuation of the opposition titan's fight for social justice, economic fairness, and institutional integrity.

Yet Mbae’s broadcast commentary did not stop at political inheritances. The DCP strategist used the platform to revisit the unresolved circumstances surrounding Odinga’s fatal hospitalization in India, arguing that public closure remains incomplete.

Mbae asserted that any progressive administration taking office in the post-2027 era would be duty-bound to institute transparent inquiries into the deaths of prominent national leaders, pointing to Odinga’s final days abroad as an issue that still requires clarity for the family and the country.

By formalizing TEP, Sifuna has built an independent platform to organize constituency networks, field legislative candidates, and approach future coalition talks on his own terms. Mbae’s TV appearance underscores how opposition figures are already moving to anchor Sifuna’s new vehicle squarely within Odinga’s historical shadow.
