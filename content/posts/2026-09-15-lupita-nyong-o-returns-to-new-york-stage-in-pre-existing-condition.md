**NEW YORK** - Oscar-winning actress Lupita Nyong’o officially returned to the New York stage on Monday, September 14, 2026, headlining the Off-Broadway production *Pre-Existing Condition* at the Greenwich House Theater.

The play, written by Marin Ireland and directed by Maria Dizzia, explores the complex emotional recovery following an abusive relationship. Nyong’o serves as the first of six actresses scheduled to rotate through the central character, identified as "A," throughout the production’s limited run.

Industry observers note that this return to live theatre reinforces a pattern in Nyong’o’s career, which has consistently balanced high-profile film projects with rigorous stage work. Her previous theatrical milestones include her 2016 Broadway debut in Danai Gurira’s *Eclipsed*-which garnered her a Tony Award nomination-and her 2025 appearance as Viola in *Twelfth Night* for the Public Theater’s Free Shakespeare in the Park.
