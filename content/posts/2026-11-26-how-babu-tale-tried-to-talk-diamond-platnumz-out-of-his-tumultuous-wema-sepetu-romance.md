Diamond Platnumz has pulled back the curtain on the heavy emotional cost of his historical romance with actress Wema Sepetu, revealing how close confidants once urged him to walk away from the relationship.

Reflecting on his past during a candid interview, the Wasafi Records boss acknowledged that while the romance delivered moments of intense happiness, it also brought him to tears. The emotional toll became so visible that his then-manager, Babu Tale, felt compelled to intervene and question why the singer was enduring the turbulence.

Recalling a specific private exchange, Diamond shared how Babu Tale confronted him about the strain of the relationship. According to the singer, his former manager advised him to cut his losses rather than continue struggling.

“Nakumbuka siku moja nilikuwa na boss Tale tulikuwa tunazungumza akaniambia, ‘ndugu yangu kwahivyo unashindwa kabisa si basi kaa pembeni mdogo wangu’,” Diamond recounted, remembering Tale’s direct push for him to step aside.

Despite the managerial advice to protect his peace and career focus, Diamond admitted that logic took a back seat to his feelings. When faced with the option of walking away, he told Tale that being around Wema offered an emotional high that overrode the distress.

“Nikamwambia, boss Tale mimi sikudanganyi nakuambia ukweli huyo mwanamke miki nikiwa naye nijihisi kama niko peponi,” he said, explaining that the moments spent together felt akin to being in paradise.

The candid lookback revisits an era when Diamond and Wema anchored Tanzania's entertainment headlines, drawing relentless public fascination with every public appearance and relationship shift.

Years after parting ways, both stars have established distinct chapters away from their shared past. Wema is currently in a relationship with fellow entertainer Whozu, while Diamond remains linked to singer Zuchu. Both couples navigated notable family milestones earlier in 2026, each welcoming newborn babies into their respective lives.
