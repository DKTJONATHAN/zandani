According to the Directorate of Criminal Investigations, a Nairobi-based advocate dramatically threatened to plunge from the eighth floor of a Westlands office block on Thursday to evade arrest over an alleged Ksh150 million document fraud scheme.

### What we know

- > "The suspect attempted to evade lawful apprehension by scaling a window on the eighth floor and subsequently assaulted our officers during the operation," stated the DCI following the Thursday, September 10 incident.
- * **Incident Location:** The Westery Building, eighth floor, Westlands, Nairobi, on Thursday, September 10, 2026.
- Background to the dramatic confrontation traces back to an ongoing forensic probe initiated after union officials flagged fraudulent legal agreements purportedly binding them to hefty payouts.

> "The suspect attempted to evade lawful apprehension by scaling a window on the eighth floor and subsequently assaulted our officers during the operation," stated the DCI following the Thursday, September 10 incident.

* **Incident Location:** The Westery Building, eighth floor, Westlands, Nairobi, on Thursday, September 10, 2026.
* **Core Allegation:** Presenting forged documents-specifically a disputed Change of Signatory and Fee Note Agreement dated to a 2022 court matter-before the Employment and Labour Relations Court.
* **Complainants:** Three officials from the Kenya County Government Workers Union, Nairobi Branch, who reported unauthorized signatures on a Ksh150 million legal fee claim.
* **Forensic Evidence:** Document examination confirmed the signatures were forged, prompting an ODPP directive on September 2, 2026, to charge the suspect.
* **Additional Offenses:** The suspect allegedly resisted handcuffing and bit two police officers during the apprehension after ignoring a prior police summons.

Background to the dramatic confrontation traces back to an ongoing forensic probe initiated after union officials flagged fraudulent legal agreements purportedly binding them to hefty payouts. The Director of Public Prosecutions subsequently ordered criminal charges, leading to the tactical police operation after the suspect snubbed a directive to report to Parklands Police Station.
