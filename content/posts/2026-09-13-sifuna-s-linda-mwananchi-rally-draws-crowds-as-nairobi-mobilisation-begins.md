Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna is leading a city-wide mobilisation tour today, Sunday, September 13, 2026, under the Linda Mwananchi movement, drawing large crowds to the Central Business District and surrounding markets.

Supporters began gathering in the city centre as early as 8:00 a.m., congregating near Tom Mboya Street and outside All Saints Cathedral. The day’s schedule commenced with a church service at the cathedral, followed by a planned procession through Marikiti, Gikomba, and Burma markets. The movement is expected to traverse Stage 17, B Centre, and Manyanja, eventually proceeding along Jogoo Road to conclude with a rally at Jacaranda Grounds at 2:00 p.m.

The rally, which Sifuna and his allies-including Embakasi East MP Babu Owino and former nominated senator Millicent Omanga-have described as a "mother of all rallies," serves as a platform to project the movement’s influence ahead of the 2027 General Election.

Security has been tightened across Nairobi as the procession moves through high-traffic commercial zones. Local authorities have deployed significant police presence to monitor the movement of supporters and maintain order, noting concerns regarding potential disruptions in densely populated market areas. In anticipation of the event, several businesses within the CBD have opted to remain closed.

"The Nairobi tour serves as a significant test of the movement’s ability to attract supporters in the capital and sustain momentum beyond previous activities," organisers stated regarding the objective of the mobilisation.

The event marks an escalation in local political activity, as the Linda Mwananchi movement seeks to demonstrate its grassroots mobilisation capacity and consolidate its support base within the opposition.
