**KISUMU, September 13, 2026** - A coalition of Christian bishops and clergy representing various denominations across the Nyanza region has officially declared its support for President William Ruto’s 2027 re-election bid.

The endorsement was delivered during an interfaith consultative forum, where the religious leaders expressed their approval of the Kenya Kwanza administration’s current approach to regional development. According to the clergy, the administration’s focus on grassroots inclusion and its collaborative engagement with local elected officials, including governors, senators, and members of parliament, marks a significant shift toward fostering economic progress in the lake basin.

"We commend the government for its commitment to equitable national development, which is a pivotal step toward breaking long-standing political isolation in this region," the clergy stated in a collective address during the forum.

The church leaders further attributed the region's recent shift toward stability to their own proactive interventions during past periods of civil unrest. The bishops noted that throughout Migori, Homa Bay, Kisumu, and Siaya counties, faith groups mobilized to engage the youth, emphasizing the importance of dialogue and peaceful resolution over street demonstrations. They commended the local youth for heeding these calls, stating that prioritizing economic stability over the destruction of property has set a positive trajectory for Nyanza’s future.

Looking ahead, the clergy pledged to use their pulpits and community outreach platforms to promote national unity and support the government’s development agenda. They have formally urged both national and county government leaders to prioritize the expansion of vocational training, agricultural investment, and infrastructure projects to ensure that the region’s youth remain constructively engaged.
