The spiritual heavyweight at the center of Zimbabwe’s most explosive audio leak has traded prophetic circles for a police holding cell in Goromonzi. Christopher Guzha, widely known as Madzibaba DVD of the Johane Masowe eChishanu sect, found himself in handcuffs on Sunday afternoon in an operation that immediately severed his access to the outside world when authorities confiscated his mobile phone.

While law enforcement files place Guzha squarely inside an active armed robbery investigation involving an EcoCash agent who allegedly lost KSh 1.5 million, those closest to him insist the ledgers don't add up. Speaking on condition of anonymity, a family insider dismissed the robbery claims outright, pointing instead to a digital file that has dominated regional timelines: the leaked conversation between Guzha and the late flamboyant businessman Wicknell Chivayo.

The timing of the police action has thrown fuel on an already volatile public debate. Just days before Guzha’s apprehension, Chivayo—alongside members of his family—perished in a tragic helicopter crash in Marondera. In the wake of that sudden loss, a recorded telephone call surfaced online, featuring Chivayo thanking a spiritual leader for lucky charms that purportedly secured him political favor with President Emmerson Mnangagwa. The authenticated recording also outlined plans for a high-stakes ritual at Lake Kariba, involving a clay pot submerged in deep waters, alongside promises of luxury vehicle gifts for the cleric once cash flows cleared.

For observers tracking the intersection of political patronage and spiritual influence in Harare, the rapid transition from a viral internet leak to a formal criminal charge raises uncomfortable questions. Bridget Guzha, the prophet's wife, watched authorities strip away his communication tools the moment the cuffs went on, signaling the severity of the state's interest in his affairs.

Guzha was scheduled to face the bench at the Marondera Regional Court on Monday, setting the stage for a high-stakes legal showdown where the official robbery docket will be weighed against persistent allegations of retaliatory state action over the Chivayo tapes.
