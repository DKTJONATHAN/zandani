Musician and political aspirant Bahati published a detailed five-tier commercial rate card for his son, Morgan Bahati, setting booking and endorsement fees between KSh 1 million and KSh 10 million.

The public rollout on Thursday, November 26, 2026, laid out explicit figures for potential clients looking to contract the young artist across five distinct service categories. According to the published fee schedule, a club appearance by Morgan carries a base price of KSh 1 million, while a corporate performance is set at KSh 2.5 million.

For studio work, artists seeking a feature are quoted KSh 5 million for a song collaboration. Corporate entities aiming to tie their campaigns to the young musician face a KSh 7 million charge for brand endorsements, while political endorsement packages sit at the top of the scale at KSh 10 million.

The commercial pricing aggressive strategy follows less than a week after Morgan formally entered the music industry with the release of his debut track, *Zoza (Nikifika 18)*. The single gathered millions of views shortly after upload, climbing to the top spot on Kenya’s YouTube trending charts and securing an immediate digital foothold.

Bahati accompanied the rate card release with a direct invitation for business inquiries, signaling a deliberate effort to monetize the streaming traction established by the debut track. The rapid move to set multi-million-shilling rates reflects a structured corporate positioning strategy driven by the senior musician, who previously managed his son's entry into the creative space.

According to earlier statements by Bahati, Morgan had expressed a desire to pursue music from an early age. However, the veteran artist chose to hold back his son's official release schedule until he felt the young performer had matured sufficiently to handle the demands of public visibility.

That managerial stance comes at a structural turning point for the family business. Bahati has systematically scaled back his own active recording and performance calendar to focus on his expanding political ambitions, effectively shifting his involvement in the entertainment sector toward executive oversight of his son's brand.

With initial chart figures established and the formal booking terms published, Morgan’s primary operational task now shifts from online streaming metrics to converting social media visibility into executed corporate contracts under the new rate structure.
