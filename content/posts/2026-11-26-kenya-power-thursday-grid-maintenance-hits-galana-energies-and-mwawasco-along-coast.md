Commercial operations along the Kenyan coast face a deliberate seven-hour standstill today as Kenya Power rolls out an extensive grid maintenance schedule across five counties on Thursday, November 26, 2026.

The utility firm's latest round of maintenance spares few sectors, directly cutting power supplies to critical industrial and municipal service providers, including Galana Energies and the Mwawasco water infrastructure network in Kilifi County, where downtime stretches from 9am to 4pm.

Running across a seven-to-ten-hour window, the interruptions target specific high-density commercial zones and residential corridors in Nairobi, Kiambu, Nyeri, Kwale, and Kilifi. In Kilifi's Ganda, Kwa Opanga, and Madunguni areas—spanning adjacent hubs like Kakuyuni Town and Mitangani—businesses dependent on steady electricity must navigate daytime outages that disrupt local supply chains and municipal water pumping schedules.

The capital experiences a similar strain. In Nairobi, businesses and residents in Tassia face the longest single stretch of downtime, with power switched off from 8am to 6pm. Across town, commercial activities in Kilimani face a parallel five-hour blackout running from 9am to 5pm, forcing offices and retail establishments to rely on backup generators or scale back daytime output.

Suburban and agricultural supply lines are equally affected. Kiambu County registers maintenance crews along the Kinoo, Baraniki, and Muthiga circuit, halting operations in Gaitumbi, Kinoo Kwa Chief, and Gathieko between 9am and 5pm. In Nyeri County, rural commercial centres in Karicheni, Gatuamba, and Msafiri face identical five-hour interruptions during peak business hours.

Along the southern coast in Kwale County, routine upgrades lock out businesses in Ng’ombeni, Kombani, Denyenye, and Madibwani from 9am until late afternoon. Kenya Power has directed affected customers in all regions to make prior adjustments ahead of the scheduled restorations, leaving businesses to absorb the cost of lost operating hours as the grid undergoes necessary servicing.
