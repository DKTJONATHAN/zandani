Faustine Lukale, the photographer and digital creator widely known as Baba Talisha, has successfully underwent a therapeutic cornea transplant in Kenya, completing the urgent first phase of a medical effort to save his sight before traveling to India.

The procedure, performed on Saturday, November 21, 2026, served as an immediate intervention to stabilize his condition after an aggressive eye infection severely damaged his vision and left him legally blind. Announcing the surgical outcome on Monday, November 23, 2026, the content creator reflected on the intense anxiety leading up to the operation.

“TO GOD BE THE GLORY! My therapeutic cornea transplant was successfully done on Saturday, and all I can say is THANK YOU, JESUS!” Lukale shared. “It has been a long, painful and uncertain journey, but through it all, God has never left my side. There were moments of fear, tears, questions and uncertainty, but my faith never died.”

While the local surgery represents a major physical milestone, his family has clarified that the operation is part of a broader, two-stage clinical strategy rather than a complete resolution.

### Clarifying the India Medical Plan

Addressing public misconceptions during a recent TikTok Live session, Lukale’s wife, businesswoman Wanjiru C. Wanjiru—known as Miss Wanjey—detailed the exact objective of their scheduled trip abroad. Miss Wanjey noted that the travel to India is not for a secondary transplant surgery, but for comprehensive diagnostic investigations that could not be fully executed locally.

According to Miss Wanjey, the primary target in India is to isolate and identify the specific pathogen driving the aggressive infection. Pinpointing the exact microorganism is essential to establishing a precise, long-term post-operative protocol designed to protect the donor tissue.

While the cornea transplant surgery itself lasted roughly one hour, managing the recovery remains delicate and high-risk. Post-surgical care requires intensive oversight due to the continuous threat of graft rejection and secondary infection if the underlying cause is not completely eradicated.

### Mobilizing Support for Recovery

The creator’s medical emergency triggered a response across his online community, with followers contributing financial support and encouragement to help offset mounting treatment expenses. Extended post-operative management, continuous medication, and international diagnostic travel represent substantial costs for the family.

Acknowledging the public support, Lukale noted that community backing played a vital role during his weeks of treatment in Nairobi.

“As we await medical clearance to fly to India for further treatment, I choose to trust God for complete healing,” Lukale added. “Today, I stand as a testimony of God’s mercy, grace and unfailing love.”

### Next Steps in Clinical Care

With the initial transplant completed locally, Lukale remains under post-operative care in Kenya while doctors monitor the initial recovery of the graft. The immediate focus centers on obtaining formal clinical authorization to travel, ensuring the newly transplanted cornea is sufficiently stable for long-distance flights.

Upon arrival in India, specialists will launch targeted diagnostic testing to lock in an anti-rejection regimen tailored to his specific recovery profile.
