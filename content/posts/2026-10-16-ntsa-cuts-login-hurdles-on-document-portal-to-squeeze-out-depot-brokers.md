Motorists waiting on pending Smart Driving Licences, vehicle logbooks, and reflective plates can now verify processing milestones and pickup points without creating portal user accounts, as the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) moves to dismantle an entrenched cottage industry of depot intermediaries.

Under an operational advisory issued Friday, October 16, the transport regulator instructed drivers whose smart cards have completed production to report directly to the specific biometric enrolment stations where their profiles were captured. Collection requires presenting an original national identity card for physical verification before release.

The administrative push arrives alongside an explicit caution against paying independent middlemen who position themselves around licensing centres promising expedited approvals. NTSA noted that its verification systems have been restructured to eliminate procedural bottlenecks that intermediaries typically exploit for cash.

“Do not deal with brokers,” the authority stated. “We have made our processes completely transparent, giving you full control without any need for brokers or intermediaries.”

![A photo collage of a driving licence (left) and National Identity Cards ready for collection.](https://i.ibb.co/TDcHBFZR/6c7f6d944f41.webp)

### The Mechanics of the Intermediary Trade

For years, informal fixers operating around licensing hubs and cybercafés have monetized bureaucratic uncertainty. Touts regularly claim priority access to driving test bookings, expedited ownership transfers, and backdoor channels to bypass physical collection queues.

Beyond inflated service markups, NTSA warned that routing sensitive transport filings through unaccredited third parties exposes vehicle owners to severe administrative hazards. Motorists risk paying for invalid transaction slips, receiving forged logbooks or counterfeit plates, and yielding national identity data to unauthorized actors who handle physical and digital registration credentials.

To strip leverage from these operations, the agency has decoupled document status verification from the mandatory user sign-ins that previously drove casual applicants toward commercial cybercafés.

### Zero-Login Verification Protocols

Under the public verification portal, document status queries are entirely open and free of charge. Applicants do not need an active profile or password to trace an order.

![Kenyans lining up inside the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) office in Nairobi in 2024.](https://i.ibb.co/21C7kB1N/7800f733e2b4.webp)

* **Driving Licences:** Tracked instantly by entering the applicant’s national identity card number.
* **Logbooks and Reflective Plates:** Verified by inputting the motor vehicle or motorcycle registration plate number.
* **Depot Routing:** Query outputs designate the precise pickup branch holding the physical document, preventing speculative travel to regional headquarters.

By tethering collection strictly to the original biometric enrolment point, the agency is also blocking intermediaries from picking up finished cards on behalf of applicants.

### In-Portal Grievance Escalation

For applicants experiencing prolonged processing stalls, the authority has integrated a direct grievance channel into the public interface. Motorists facing unexplained delays can log non-delivery complaints directly through the web portal without in-person visits to customer care desks.

Resolution notices and tracking logs are transmitted back to the email address entered during the dispute submission, creating a documented audit trail intended to prevent unresolved applications from falling back into the hands of station-gate fixers.
