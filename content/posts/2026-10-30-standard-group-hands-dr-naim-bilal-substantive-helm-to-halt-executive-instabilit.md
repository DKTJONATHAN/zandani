The board of Standard Group Plc has moved to stabilize its executive suite, confirming Dr Naim Bilal Yaseen as substantive Group Chief Executive Officer to head off leadership volatility and steer the company’s commercial recovery.

The appointment, announced on Thursday, October 29, takes formal effect on Sunday, November 1, 2026. It closes an interim window that opened earlier in October when Dr Bilal was thrust into the corner office as acting CEO following the sudden resignation of Chaacha Mwita.

“Following the conclusion of a competitive recruitment process, the Board of Directors of The Standard Group PLC (TSG) has appointed Dr Naim Bilal Yaseen, PhD, to the position, effective 1st November 2026,” the board stated.

### The Mombasa Road Mandate

![Standard Group headquarters along Mombasa Road, Nairobi](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_style/public/images/media/20201014-BOXRAFT-The%20Standard%20Media%20Group%20Mombasa%20Road%20Offices%20.%20Thursday%2C%20October%2014%2C%202019.%20%283%29.jpg.webp?itok=5vHd8Fs7)

Dr Bilal assumes the substantive role at a critical juncture for the Nairobi Securities Exchange-listed media house. In confirming his position, the board explicitly tied his assignment to two operational fronts: accelerating the company's digital transition and shoring up commercial revenue lines, while insulating the newsroom's editorial independence.

The directive hands Dr Bilal immediate oversight of an enterprise balancing modern digital delivery against commercial headwinds in legacy broadcasting and newspaper distribution. 

Unlike an external industry entrant, Dr Bilal brings a unique blend of regulatory, state corporation, and competitive newsroom experience spanning more than three decades. His career footprint includes serving as Managing Director at the state-run Kenya Broadcasting Corporation (KBC) and directing public affairs and communication at the Judiciary. Prior to his institutional public service, he held senior editorial and operational positions across print and broadcast divisions at Nation Media Group (NMG).

His credentials also straddle media operations and corporate jurisprudence. An Advocate of the High Court of Kenya, Dr Bilal holds a Bachelor of Laws, a Master of Business Administration, a master’s degree in Communications Policy, and a PhD in Communication Studies.

### Rapid Turnover at the Top

The quick transition from acting to substantive CEO addresses an uncomfortable period of executive churn along Mombasa Road. His predecessor, Chaacha Mwita, stepped down earlier in October after barely seven months in the substantive post, having himself been appointed to anchor the media house's operational continuity. 

Standard Group did not disclose the grounds for Mwita’s abrupt exit, leaving the commercial and institutional pressures confronting the business as the backdrop to his departure.

By moving through a recruitment process and formalising Dr Bilal's tenure before the month closed, the board has sought to reassure capital markets, advertising partners, and staff of administrative stability. Dr Bilal’s immediate ledger will be tested by how rapidly his hybrid background in law and media management can translate into durable balance-sheet resilience starting November 1.
