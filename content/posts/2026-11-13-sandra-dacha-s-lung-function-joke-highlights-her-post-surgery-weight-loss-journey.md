Sandra Dacha is looking back at her pre-surgery days with a heavy dose of wit, using an old photograph to poke fun at her physical state before undergoing a gastric sleeve procedure.

The Kenyan actress and event organiser shared a throwback image on social media, accompanying it with a lighthearted jab at her breathing capacity during that period. Comparing her respiratory state to someone merely sitting in on a corporate meeting, Dacha joked that even the oxygen around her seemed skeptical of her condition.

“Somebody please confirm if my lungs were really functioning or just attending meetings. I know even oxygen was looking at me like, ‘Madam, tutafute njia ingine ama!’” Dacha wrote.

The humorous reflection offers another look at how the entertainer is processing her physical transformation following a major medical decision. Dacha officially underwent gastric sleeve surgery on September 30, 2026, choosing to document parts of her journey for her followers.

The operation came to light after followers noticed an emotional video showing the actress being wheeled into an operating room. On Monday, November 9, 2026, Dacha addressed the curiosity surrounding the footage, confirming that she had undergone a sleeve gastrectomy to aid in weight loss.

Medically known as a sleeve gastrectomy, the procedure involves the surgical removal of approximately 75 to 80 percent of the stomach. The remaining portion is shaped into a narrow, banana-like tube, permanently reducing the stomach's capacity and limiting the amount of food an individual can consume in a single sitting.

Since opening up about the September operation, Dacha has continued to share glimpses of her transformation, balancing the realities of her medical journey with her trademark humour as she settles into post-surgery life.
