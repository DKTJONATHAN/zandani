Media personality Oga Obinna has trained his sights on social media critics picking apart gospel singer Size 8 and her husband DJ Mo’s newly unveiled residence, demanding to know how individuals who contributed zero financial capital claim the right to audit interior finishes.

Speaking through a video published on his Instagram page on Friday, November 6, 2026, Obinna targeted the unprompted commentary that has trailed the couple’s architectural choices. Online commentators had zeroed in on specific design elements of the multi-million-shilling property, ranging from lighting installations and bathroom greenery to general room temperatures.

“DJ Mo na Size 8, naona mliwajengea Wakenya walikuwa wamewapa contract muwajengee nyumba vile wanaitaka,” Obinna remarked, mocking the persistent entitlement shown by digital audiences who treat private real estate milestones as public public-works projects.

Having toured the construction site on multiple occasions while the project was underway, Obinna pointed to the behind-the-scenes financial discipline required to bring the property to life. He estimated that the sprawling residence could easily carry a price tag upwards of Ksh 50 million, noting that every finished detail matches the personal preferences of the couple and their immediate family rather than the arbitrary standards of internet spectators.

“Kenyans would contribute one bob, not a cent, not a shilling. Hata sumuni mtu hajatoa. Umeenda umebuy shamba yako, ukajenga nyumba yako,” he stated.

Obinna drew a direct parallel to the online reception rapper Khaligraph Jones encountered when he similarly showcased his own mansion, highlighting a recurring pattern where high-profile Kenyan artists face intense digital scrutiny upon completing major capital investments.

Closing his remarks with a direct challenge to digital critics, Obinna advised them to channel that same energy into their own personal development milestones. 

“Build yours. Fagia kwako, jenga yako,” he urged, cautioning that relentless public negativity devalues hard-earned achievements. Addressing the homeowners directly, he added: “Size 8, I love the house. It came out exactly how you guys wanted it. And you guys love the house; that is what matters.”
