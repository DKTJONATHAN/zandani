The Office of the Registrar of Political Parties has received judicial clearance to proceed with the formal gazettement and corporate registration of The Ukombozi Alliance, ending a two-week statutory embargo that stalled the restructuring of Kenya’s main opposition bloc.

Sitting on Thursday, October 29, 2026, the Political Parties Disputes Tribunal (PPDT) discharged its earlier conservatory orders that had suspended the coalition’s registration process. The decision eliminates the primary legal hurdle preventing the newly minted outfit from replacing the Azimio la Umoja One Kenya Coalition as an incorporated political entity.

Wiper Democratic Movement leader Kalonzo Musyoka, who is designated to head the revamped formation, confirmed that the tribunal cleared the path during its Thursday session.

“The Political Parties Disputes Tribunal, sitting today, Thursday, October 29, 2026, has discharged its earlier orders suspending the registration process of The Ukombozi Alliance,” Kalonzo said in an official notice. “No order of the tribunal now stands between the registration of TUA.”

### The Competing Claims at the Registry

The dispute landed before the dispute resolution bench on October 13, 2026, when PPDT Chairperson Innocent Muganda barred the Registrar from approving, registering, or gazetting the name. The freeze followed a petition from rival claimants asserting prior statutory rights over the "Ukombozi" brand through the Ukombozi Peoples Party.

The administrative dispute revealed overlapping applications lodged at the political parties registry over the past 19 months. Records from the Registrar showed that an applicant had reserved "Ukombozi Alliance" as early as July 20, 2026, prompting an official advisory on September 8 that Azimio's proposed name switch had not yet secured formal authorization.

Simultaneously, the Linda Mwananchi outfit, operating under The Equitable Party (TEP), asserted an earlier claim. Figures allied to Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna maintained they had initiated paperwork to rebrand the United Democratic Party (UDP) into Ukombozi in March 2025—predating Azimio’s institutional shift.

### Corporate Succession from Azimio

Thursday’s discharge dismantles that regulatory logjam, granting the Registrar untethered authority to execute the coalition changeover.

Operationally, the ruling enables the formal transfer of coalition instruments from former President Uhuru Kenyatta, who served as Azimio's council chairperson, to Kalonzo. The Wiper leader had steered the coalition through the adoption of the Ukombozi moniker, positioning the reconfigured vehicle to absorb auxiliary political parties ahead of the 2027 general election cycle.

With the conservatory injunction vacated, the procedural onus shifts back to the Registrar of Political Parties to publish the coalition agreement in the Kenya Gazette, establishing the alliance’s legal personality and solidifying its leadership hierarchy.
