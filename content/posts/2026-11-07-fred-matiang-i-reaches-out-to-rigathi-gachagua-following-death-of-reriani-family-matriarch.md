Former Interior Cabinet Secretary Fred Matiang’i has extended a public message of solidarity to former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua following the death of Gachagua’s eldest sister, Eunice Nyangari Gachagua.

Writing in his capacity as Jubilee Party leader, Matiang’i addressed Gachagua directly as a colleague and brother, acknowledging the weight of losing an elder who served as the central pillar for the extended family.

Eunice Nyangari died in Naro Moru at the age of 77. Her passing marks the end of a seven-year period in which she steered the Reriani family as its matriarch, taking over the role following the death of their mother.

### A Message of Personal Solidarity

In his statement, Matiang’i highlighted the deep structural loss for the family, characterizing Eunice as a guiding figure who provided stability across generations.

“I extend my heartfelt condolences to my brother, colleague, former Deputy President and DCP Party Leader, H.E. Rigathi Gachagua, following the passing of his beloved elder sister, Eunice Nyangari Gachagua, aged 77,” Matiang’i stated.

Speaking on behalf of the Jubilee Party leadership and his own family, the former CS offered prayers for Eunice’s children, Waithera and Murimi, alongside her grandchildren and great-grandchildren as they begin period of mourning.

“May Almighty God grant the family strength, comfort and peace as they mourn this profound loss,” Matiang’i added.

### Matriarch of the Reriani Lineage

Gachagua formally announced his sister's passing on Friday, November 6, releasing a statement to the extended Reriani clan to share news of her death in Naro Moru.

Reflecting on her role in the family home, Gachagua noted that Eunice had stepped into their late mother’s leadership shoes seven years ago, maintaining cohesion among siblings, nieces, and nephews.

“We thank God for the 77 years she lived happily here on earth. We will deeply miss her wise counsel, warmth and the precious moments we shared. Eunice was a happy and warm soul who lit up our hearts,” Gachagua said.

Eunice was the firstborn child of the late Mzee Nahashon Gachagua Reriani and the late Martha Kirigo Gachagua. Funeral arrangements and memorial planning for the late family matriarch are expected to be communicated by the family.
