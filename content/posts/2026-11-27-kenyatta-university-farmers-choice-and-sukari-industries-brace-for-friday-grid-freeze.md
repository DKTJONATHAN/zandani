Major institutional and manufacturing operations across Nairobi, Homa Bay, Kiambu, and Kwale are bracing for a severe workday disruption as Kenya Power rolls out an extensive eight-hour grid maintenance schedule on Friday, November 27, 2026.

Unlike routine residential blackouts, Friday’s targeted shutdowns hit critical commercial processing nodes and academic laboratories directly. In Nairobi’s Kiwanja and Kahawa West corridors, the maintenance window—running from 9am to 5pm—will completely sever power supply to Farmers Choice and Kenyatta University, including the institution's specialized School of Engineering and Nyayo Estate surroundings.

The industrial pinch extends far beyond the capital. In Homa Bay County, the grid shutdown sweeps across an expansive commercial zone from 9am to 5pm, directly impacting Sukari Industries alongside regional hubs in Mbita, Rusinga, Sindo, Magunga, Ndhiwa, and Rodi Bay. The prolonged outage cuts off heavy local processing and supply chains across Mirogi, Ratanga, and Kobodo.

Meanwhile, the Mt Kenya circuit will see Gichira and Kihuro locked out of the grid from 8am to 5pm. The sweep encompasses local security installations at Gichira Police, Gichira Market, and adjacent rural enterprise clusters from Hehcti down to Waruru Village.

Along the Coast, industrial and community operations in Waa, Pungu, Magandia, and Kiteje face an identical five-hour operating freeze from 9am. The affected corridor covers Dzombo, Kithadze, Mtsangatifu, Waa Mbvveka, Zibani Primary, Magandia Camp, Nombeni Mavveni, Denyenye, and Kaya Bombo.

Kenya Power states the interruptions form part of its ongoing network enhancement programme. Affected commercial operators and residents have been advised to seek alternative logistical arrangements through the utility's official channels ahead of the 8am and 9am cutoffs.
