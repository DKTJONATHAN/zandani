Kenya’s main opposition formation has formally discarded the Azimio la Umoja identity, replacing the 2022 electoral machinery with a rebranded outfit anchored on a single-term transitional governance pledge.

Wiper Democratic Movement leader Kalonzo Musyoka, flanked by Siaya Governor James Orengo and other coalition principals, unveiled the new entity—the Ukombozi Alliance—during a press briefing in Nairobi on Monday.

Under the new banner, the coalition is organizing its 2027 campaign strategy around a central political doctrine: *'One Term, One Team, One Coalition.'* The framework aims to assemble a broad anti-incumbency front by committing its eventual flagbearer to a single transitional presidential tenure.

"Moving forward, this alliance shall no longer be called the Azimio La Umoja Coalition; we shall be called the Ukombozi Alliance," Kalonzo announced.

### Expanding the Coalition Base

![AZIMIO](https://i.ibb.co/gZ7m1gWY/fd397b6f9cb7.webp)

The rebranding marks a structured effort to broaden the opposition's geographical and institutional reach beyond the core parties that contested the 2022 general election. 

Key among the new entrants formalizing ties with the alliance is former Chief Justice David Maraga, who is mounting a 2027 presidential bid under the United Green Movement (UGM). Maraga’s entry injects an anti-corruption and judicial reform profile into the coalition's leadership ranks.

The alliance has also secured the entry of the Umoja na Maendeleo Party (UMP), led by former Meru Governor Kawira Mwangaza, providing the nascent outfit with an immediate political foothold in Mount Kenya East.

According to Kalonzo, several other political parties are scheduled to sign coalition agreements in the coming days as the alliance finalizes its statutory registration and deposit of instruments with the Registrar of Political Parties.

### Formal Transition and Jubilee Negotiations

The coalition has scheduled a formal handover ceremony to transfer institutional instruments, assets, and Secretariat operations from the defunct Azimio la Umoja structure to the Ukombozi Alliance leadership.

As an immediate operational step, the alliance convenes a joint strategy conference with the Jubilee Party on Tuesday, September 22. The meeting will determine Jubilee's formal integration into the new coalition structure and negotiate power-sharing mechanisms within the governing council.

![AZIMIO](https://i.ibb.co/zVzSWN6Y/945e28288c2e.webp)

The strategic retreat follows months of internal friction across opposition ranks over how to realign following shifting political allegiances within Parliament.

### Backing Uhuru's Political Protections

Addressing concurrent legal developments, Kalonzo endorsed the High Court's Monday ruling that struck down sections of the Presidential Retirement Benefits Act restricting retired heads of state from holding political party offices or engaging in active politics.

The Wiper leader defended former President Uhuru Kenyatta's constitutional right to political association, cautioning President William Ruto against sustained political confrontation with his predecessor.

"The 2027 contest will not be between the two former political allies," Kalonzo stated, arguing that state-directed criticism labeling Kenyatta a 'rebel leader' undermined constitutional protections affirmed by the courts.

Tuesday's consultative session with Jubilee will test the Ukombozi Alliance's cohesion as leadership seats and regional zoning protocols are placed on the negotiating table.
