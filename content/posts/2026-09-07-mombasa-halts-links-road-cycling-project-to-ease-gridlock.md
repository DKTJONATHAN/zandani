Mombasa Governor Abdulswamad Nassir on Monday morning, September 07, 2026, suspended the construction of cycling lanes along Links Road in Nyali to ease severe traffic congestion caused by ongoing diversions from the Mombasa-Malindi Highway works at Bombolulu.

### What we know:

- Governor Abdulswamad Nassir halted cycling track works along Links Road on Monday, September 07, 2026.
- Traffic diversions at the Bombolulu section of the Mombasa-Malindi Road pushed heavy vehicle volumes onto secondary routes across Nyali.
- The Bombolulu corridor serves as a transit link for coastal trade and regional freight between Kenya and Tanzania.
- County engineers and traffic police have reopened all lanes on Links Road to motor vehicles while diversions remain in place.
- The National Disaster Operations Centre and the Kenya Meteorological Service Authority have listed Mombasa, Kilifi, Kwale, Lamu, and Tana River as high-risk flood zones ahead of heavy rains.

Motorists across the North Coast have faced daily delays, with travel times between the Nyali Bridge and Bamburi stretching beyond two hours during peak morning and evening hours. The gridlock followed the partial closure of the Mombasa-Malindi corridor at Bombolulu, where contractors are carrying out major road upgrades.

Governor Nassir said suspending the non-motorised transport project immediately clears the carriageway along Links Road, giving public service vehicles, freight trucks, and private motorists extra room to maneuver through the busy commercial zone.

"Following the recent upsurge in traffic congestion across Mombasa, occasioned largely by the diversion of traffic from the Bombolulu section of the Mombasa-Malindi Road during the ongoing construction works, we have consequently recommended the temporary suspension of the cycling infrastructure works along Links Road," Nassir said in a statement on Monday.

The governor added that opening up the entire corridor is necessary to prevent complete gridlock in the North Coast while the national highway project continues. Links Road functions as the main bypass for traffic heading towards Nyali, City Mall, and surrounding residential estates.

The Bombolulu section remains an economic bottleneck along the Mombasa-Malindi highway. The route forms a segment of the East African Coastal Road Corridor, which links Mombasa to the Lunga Lunga border crossing into Tanzania, carrying regional cargo and local commuter traffic daily.

County officials also based the suspension on flood forecasts issued by the National Disaster Operations Centre and the Kenya Meteorological Service Authority. Both agencies identified Mombasa, Kilifi, Kwale, Lamu, and Tana River as counties facing elevated risks of flash floods and storm surges.

"We have reviewed the prevailing traffic situation and the additional risks posed by the anticipated El Niño rains," Nassir said.

County transport marshals and national traffic police officers have taken up positions along major feeder roads, including Fidel Odinga Road, the Kongowea market access routes, and the Nyali interchange, to manage lane discipline and clear breakdowns.

The County Government of Mombasa and national road agencies will review the Bombolulu diversions and the Links Road project status later this month.
