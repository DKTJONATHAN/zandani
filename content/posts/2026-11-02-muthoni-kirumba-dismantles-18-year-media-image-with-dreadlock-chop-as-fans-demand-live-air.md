Muthoni wa Kirumba has spent twelve years cementing her standing as one of Kenya’s most recognizable vernacular radio personalities, building an on-air identity that grew right alongside her signature 18-year dreadlocks. That visual constant came to a sharp end when the mid-morning broadcaster cut off her entire head of hair to herald what she framed as an absolute personal reset.

The decision to shed nearly two decades of continuous hair growth was unveiled directly to her online audience. Holding onto the severed bundle of locks, the veteran broadcaster marked the transition with a lighthearted jab at the age of her hairstyle.

"My dreadlocks have reached a legal age! So, I sent them to campus. After 18 years, tumeagana! But hello to a New Month, New chapter, new me. SAME GOD!" she announced across her personal handles.

### Digital Authenticity and Synthetic Media Doubts

![Muthoni wa Kirumba // Instagram](https://i.ibb.co/QjpJJkpj/bcd45954d23f.webp)

The sudden disappearance of a trademark that predated her entry into mainstream broadcasting immediately challenged her audience's expectations. In an era where digital visual manipulation is common, a significant portion of her followers initially refused to believe the photos represented a physical reality.

Commentators across digital platforms moved swiftly to question whether the transformation was manufactured through digital editing or artificial intelligence software. User @morine_furaha voiced the widespread disbelief, writing, "Please ikuwe ni AI juu aiiiiii," while another follower, @kadympo, dismissed the update outright as "AI 😂😂😂." The suspicion reached a point where user @theamazingmuthoni put the question directly to the presenter: "Hizi ni AI?"

The knee-jerk presumption of synthetic editing highlighted how deeply the broadcaster's aesthetic was bound to her public identity. For thousands of regular listeners, an unannounced departure from her long-standing appearance felt more plausible as an image-generation filter than a deliberate trip to the salon.

### The Persona Behind the Hairstyle

On Kameme FM, where she co-hosts the flagship mid-morning show *Canjamuka* alongside Kikuyu comedian Muthee Kihenjo, Muthoni wa Kirumba’s long dreadlocks functioned as more than a personal grooming choice. Over her 12-year stint at the station, the look evolved into a core visual anchor for her high-energy, self-assured radio persona.

That deep association triggered a split among her audience between emotional resistance and immediate support. Listeners accustomed to her established image expressed frustration at the shift. User @ndunguannnjoki reacted sharply to the drop, writing, "Nkt umenikasirisha," while @ramseykim93 recalled her long-standing look, noting, "Venye ulikua smart ikiwa kidiva hii ni noma."

Other followers embraced the haircut as an encouraging evolution, with user @waruguru_kanyi posting, "🔥🔥🔥welcome home❤️." Yet despite the praise, the fundamental doubt surrounding the photos persisted across her comment sections.

### The On-Air Reality Test

For regular consumers of Kikuyu radio, the ultimate test of authenticity has moved from social media photos back to the studio. Unwilling to rely entirely on static digital uploads, listeners pledged to use her live studio broadcasts to confirm whether the haircut was real.

User @im_g_authentic captured the audience's intent to audit the change in real-time, noting, "Nangoja live ya canjamuka nione kama ni ukweli, A diva is always a diva."

As the broadcaster prepares for her next turn at the microphone on *Canjamuka*, the physical transformation shifts from a viral social media post into a real-time studio reveal. Whether listeners fully embrace the aesthetic shift or not, her 18-year hairstyle officially moves from a active broadcasting trademark to a concluded era of her media career.
