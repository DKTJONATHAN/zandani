Mombasa politician and East African Legislative Assembly (EALA) MP Suleiman Shahbal has moved to secure an independent electoral safety net for the 2027 contest, with his son, Samir Suleiman Shahbal, taking executive control of The New Democrats (TND) party.

According to a Gazette Notice published on September 18 under Section 20 (1) (c) of the Political Parties Act, Samir assumes the role of interim Party Leader in a sweeping reorganization of the outfit’s national executive. The public has seven days from the publication date to file written objections with the Registrar of Political Parties.

The regulatory filing removes long-serving party head David K. Sudi, reassigning him to Deputy Party Leader in charge of the party. The restructure also ousts Jimmy Kibaki, son of the late former President Mwai Kibaki, from his perch as Deputy Party Leader, installing Francis Kariuki in the position.

### The Mombasa Fallback Strategy

![Samir Suleiman Shahbal, son to Mombasa tycoon Suleiman Shahbal.](https://i.ibb.co/Q7G4F2FX/459feadc0552.webp)

The timing of the corporate overhaul inside TND directly coincides with open political friction between Suleiman Shahbal and the State House administration over the succession race for the Mombasa governorship.

During a September 8 meeting at State House, President William Ruto publicly urged Shahbal to abandon his gubernatorial bid, advising the real estate tycoon to channel his energy into industrialization and job creation instead. Shahbal swiftly broke ranks with that proposal, declaring that external actors were manipulating the presidency to clear the field for rival contenders in the Coast city.

“Let me tell you in no uncertain terms: come 2027, whether they like it or not, there will be no retreat. There will be no turning back. We will face off at the ballot box on August 10, 2027,” the elder Shahbal stated, confirming he would contest the seat regardless of whether his current outfit, the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM), grants him its nomination ticket.

By placing Samir at the helm of an established political outfit, the Shahbal camp eliminates the operational risk of being locked out by party primaries or negotiated consensus arrangements within major political coalitions. TND now offers an immediate, family-anchored vehicle should negotiations inside the Azimio-ODM apparatus or wider broad-based formations collapse.

![President William Ruto (left) pictured with Mombasa tycoon and former EALA MP Suleiman Shahbal (right) at Statehouse on September 10.](https://i.ibb.co/WWPgPQn7/d44ac06d7323.webp)

### Samir’s Expanding Footprint

While Samir has largely operated inside the family's commercial portfolio—directing operations at the flagship Buxton Point affordable housing development in Mombasa—his profile within national politics has sharpened in recent months.

In June, Siaya Governor James Orengo recruited the younger Shahbal into his executive political and technical advisory team. At the time, Orengo cited Samir's strategic capacity, technical skills, and deep civic standing within Mombasa and the broader coastal economy.

The handover inside TND signals that this technical involvement has transitioned into direct party ownership.

With the statutory seven-day objection window now running at the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties, the takeover leaves the Shahbal family in structural command of a registered national party—ensuring that when Mombasa heads to the ballot in August 2027, their candidacy will not depend on the goodwill of party headquarters in Nairobi.
