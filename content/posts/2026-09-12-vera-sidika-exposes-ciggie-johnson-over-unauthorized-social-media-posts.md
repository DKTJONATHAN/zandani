**NAIROBI, September 12, 2026** - Socialite Vera Sidika has publicly denounced nightlife host Ciggie Johnson, releasing private WhatsApp correspondence to substantiate claims of boundary violations regarding the unauthorized use of her image. The dispute stems from Johnson’s circulation of archived video footage featuring the pair, despite an explicit request from Sidika to cease all social media activity involving her likeness.

The conflict escalated after Johnson sought permission to post historical clips of the pair, which Sidika formally declined in a July 15 exchange. Despite his initial agreement to respect her wishes, the videos appeared on his platforms yesterday. Sidika contends the recycling of these visuals is a calculated effort to project an image of a thriving friendship that has been effectively defunct for eight months.

The fallout highlights a deepening rift that began in early 2025. According to courted evidence in the screenshots, Sidika maintains that while she holds no personal animosity, her decision to terminate the professional and social association is final. She has reiterated her stance that the unauthorized publication of their history is a breach of trust that precludes any possibility of a return to their previous rapport.
