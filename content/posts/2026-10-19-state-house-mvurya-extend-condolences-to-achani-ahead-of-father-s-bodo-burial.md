Statements issued by State House and the Ministry of Sports have extended formal condolences to Kwale Governor Fatuma Achani, offering institutional and personal backing to the county chief and her family following the sudden passing of her father, Mzee Mohamed Achani, early Monday morning.

Mzee Achani, 74, died at his rural residence in Bodo Village, located within Msambweni Sub-County. Family and local reports indicate that the elder developed fatal medical complications at dawn while observing Fajr, the mandatory early morning Muslim prayer. In accordance with Islamic rites, the family scheduled his burial for Monday at 4:00 p.m. at his ancestral home in Bodo.

President William Ruto led the national executive in dispatching official condolences to Governor Achani, assuring the devolved administration and her relatives of prayer and solidarity during the immediate mourning period.

![Kwale Governor Fatuma Achani during the official opening of the Digital Community Centre at Busho, Kwale County.](https://i.ibb.co/dwwztSj1/074e3367895e.webp)

“Sincere condolences to Governor Fatuma Achani following the passing of your dear father, Mohamed Achani. Our prayers are with you, your family, and the entire county during this painful time. Rest in peace, Mzee Achani,” President Ruto stated.

The bereavement drew additional institutional weight from Sports Cabinet Secretary Salim Mvurya, who served as Kwale’s pioneer governor for two terms with Achani as his deputy before she succeeded him in the 2022 general election. Mvurya characterized the deceased as a respected pillar whose loss reverberates across the county’s leadership fabric.

"I extend my heartfelt condolences to H.E. Fatuma Achani, Governor of Kwale County, and her entire family following the passing of her beloved father, the late Mzee Mohamed Achani. During this difficult moment of grief, our thoughts and prayers are with the Governor, her family, relatives, friends, and the people of Kwale County as they mourn the loss of their beloved patriarch," Mvurya said.

![Council of governors](https://i.ibb.co/qLy3H29x/efa036970da6.webp)

Prior to his death on Monday, Mzee Achani had experienced recurring health challenges that saw him admitted to medical facilities on several occasions, though his family has kept specific clinical details private.

The death adds to a sorrowful stretch for coastal regional leadership, occurring weeks after neighboring Taita Taveta Senator Danson Mungatana lost his youngest daughter following an illness on September 13.

Relatives, community elders, and local leaders from across Kwale converged at Bodo Village through midday Monday to join Governor Achani for the final prayers and the 4:00 p.m. interment.
