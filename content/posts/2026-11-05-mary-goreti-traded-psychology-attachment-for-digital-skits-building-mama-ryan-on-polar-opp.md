Long before Mary Goreti became a familiar face across Kenyan digital feeds, she was attempting to align her high school passion for acting with the practical realities of earning a living. That search for financial predictability led her into lecture halls to study psychology, a field she briefly put into practice during her professional attachment before fully pivoting to online comedy.

For Goreti, enrolling in a psychology program was less about abandoning creative arts and more about securing a reliable path to earn her keep. The clinical routines of her attachment period, however, ultimately served as a brief interlude before she decided to turn her focus entirely back toward performance and creative self-expression.

That pivot required a distinct public identity. When building her digital presence, the creator anchored her online brand directly to her home life, adopting the name 'Mama Ryan' in honor of her six-year-old son, Ryan. What started as an individual outlet quickly expanded into a structured comedy enterprise across TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube.

Key to that digital traction has been her willingness to switch between sharply contrasting comedic archetypes. Goreti has singled out 'Katonge'—an assertive, take-charge persona who operates with a distinct boss-like authority—as her personal favorite to play. Yet her content routine relies on structural range, frequently shifting from dominant figures like Katonge to understated, submissive wife characters within the same production cycle.

That flexibility on camera is backed by a steady personal foundation. As the firstborn in a family of four children, Goreti credits her parents as her earliest and most consistent supporters, backing her transition from high school theater to full-time content creation.

Today, that transition has reshaped her career footprint. Goreti notes that her independent online comedy work has generated broader visibility and audience engagement than her experiences on mainstream broadcast platforms, powered by a follower base that continues to track her evolving roster of characters.
