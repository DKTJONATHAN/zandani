NAIROBI - A Burundian national operating as a mobile towel vendor in the city center has reported the loss of his entire business capital after he was allegedly coerced into surrendering his mobile money PIN to a group of armed assailants yesterday evening.

**What we know:**
* The victim, a Burundian foreign national, was accosted while conducting street-level trade in the Nairobi Central Business District.
* Perpetrators allegedly used physical threats to force the victim to unlock his mobile device and provide his personal identification number (PIN).
* The victim reports the total theft of his operational savings, leaving him unable to restock his inventory.
* Financial strain is acute; the victim indicates that his family in Burundi is now considering the sale of ancestral land to facilitate his recovery and return.

The incident occurred during peak evening hours, a period that has seen an uptick in reports of petty street robberies targeting small-scale migrant traders. The victim was captured on video in a state of visible distress shortly after the encounter, detailing how the assailants intercepted him before fleeing into the crowded alleys of the CBD.

Preliminary reports confirm that the victim has sought assistance from members of his diaspora community to navigate the process of filing a formal statement with the local authorities. Investigations into the digital trail of the transferred funds are expected to follow, though the victim’s current inability to access his mobile device has complicated the reporting process.

No arrests have been made as of Friday morning.
