Registered applicants within Kenya’s Affordable Housing Programme (AHP) can now adjust their preferred housing typologies and project locations online, allowing prospective buyers to pivot their savings commitments as new regional developments launch.

The State Department of Housing and Urban Planning issued the clarification following mounting inquiries from registered citizens over whether initial selections on the Boma Yangu portal were permanently binding. 

Under the updated digital workflow, individuals registered on the Boma Yangu platform are no longer locked into their initial property choices. Applicants who initially selected smaller configurations—such as a studio apartment—can upgrade to a one-bedroom or three-bedroom unit, while those seeking lower financial commitments can downsize their selections through the portal.

### Digital mobility across projects

![The Pioneer Affordable Housing Estate in Eldoret, Uasin Gishu County, October 18, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/VYS3wBM8/ae4d3eaf7de6.webp)

Beyond unit sizing, the systemic update enables geographic transfers across county projects. Applicants who originally earmarked housing units in Nairobi developments, such as the Southlands housing project, can reallocate their preference to provincial developments, including the Makasembo units in Kisumu County.

The State Department outlined a multi-step procedure for executing unit modifications directly on the user portal:

* Log in to the official Boma Yangu portal.
* Navigate to the **Preferred House** section.
* Select the previously chosen unit, click **View**, and then select **Remove**.
* Browse available inventory across active sites and select a new preferred unit.

The shift accommodates changing economic conditions and household requirements for registered buyers as state-backed housing developments expand beyond primary metropolitan areas.

### Deposit thresholds and procedural gaps

While the technical feature offers fluidity during the accumulation phase, it highlights a crucial administrative distinction within the programme's operational rules. 

To secure formal unit allocation, an applicant must deposit a minimum of 5 percent of the property’s total purchase price, with the remaining balance serviced through structured monthly installments or paid in a single lump-sum settlement for immediate ownership.

However, housing authorities have not yet clarified whether the option to alter unit choices or switch project locations remains accessible after a formal unit allocation has been issued, or whether the digital flexibility applies strictly to active savers who have not yet reached final allocation status. 

As housing handovers and project rollouts scale across counties, the housing ministry faces ongoing pressure to publish explicit guidelines governing buyer rights post-allocation, ensuring transparency for applicants balancing long-term financial obligations against shifting housing needs.
