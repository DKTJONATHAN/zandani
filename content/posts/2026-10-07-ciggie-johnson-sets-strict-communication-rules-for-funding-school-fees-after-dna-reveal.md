Twelve years of raising a child cannot be erased by a laboratory report overnight, but according to content creator Ciggie Johnson, the terms of engagement have fundamentally changed. Speaking on the *Obinna Show Live*, the club host addressed the financial future of the 12-year-old girl he previously believed was his biological daughter, outlining the precise conditions under which he remains willing to clear her school fees.

The conversation follows public disclosures made on Saturday, October 3, 2026, when Ciggie shared an emotional video documenting his phone confrontation with the girl's mother after receiving the DNA results. While the revelation upended a decade of assumed paternity, Ciggie indicated that the emotional bond forged over twelve years persists, even as he draws a hard line against further interaction with the mother.

“If the mother was nice to me, why not?” Ciggie said during the interview, describing the woman as a difficult presence he can no longer bear to share space with. He characterized his past acceptance of the child under false premises as a form of pressure, using the phrase “I was blackmailed to eat” to describe the domestic environment he navigated for years.

Despite his anger toward the mother, Ciggie left the door open for the minor. He stressed that any future financial assistance—including covering tuition—would depend on the child initiating contact independently and maintaining a formal distance from her mother's involvement.

“If the child calls me directly, without even her mother’s consent, I’ll give the child whatever,” he stated. He added a specific protocol for how he expects to be addressed if she reaches out for educational support, noting that hearing her address him as “sir” rather than “dad” would not stop him from assisting.

“I do anything for the child,” he said, while acknowledging the internal conflict sparked by the test results. He admitted that, despite the outcome, a part of him had hoped the science would confirm he was the biological father.

The public exchange has turned attention toward the complex reality of non-biological parenting arrangements when legal and biological certainties collide with long-standing domestic bonds. Ciggie pointed to the psychological weight of the fallout, questioning how emotional attachments survive major relationship betrayals.

For now, the continuation of the girl's education rests on a direct phone call made outside the mother's purview, leaving the future of the 12-year relationship entirely in the hands of the minor and the creator she once called father.
