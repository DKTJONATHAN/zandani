Nairobi’s central commercial, studio, and trading corridors are bracing for an eight-hour daylight shutdown on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, as Kenya Power rolls out extensive scheduled maintenance across four counties.

The state utility has earmarked sections of Westlands and Ngara in Nairobi, alongside peri-urban belts in Kajiado, Kiambu, and Nandi, for power cuts that range from four to nine hours. The deliberate switch-offs, intended to facilitate routine line servicing and network stabilization, will ground daytime grid power during prime operating hours.

### Full-Day Interruption in Westlands and Ngara

In Nairobi, the planned blackout spans from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, cutting directly into standard working hours across both Westlands and Ngara. 

Westlands, one of the capital's busiest hubs for corporate offices, creative studios, co-working spaces, and hospitality venues, will see surrounding customers disconnected for the duration of the eight-hour maintenance cycle. Concurrently, Ngara—a dense node characterized by lively retail enterprises, student hostels, and major transport links—falls under the identical daytime downtime schedule.

Kenya Power confirmed that both primary business clusters and adjacent residential circuits in these zones will lose power until maintenance crews complete their works late in the afternoon.

### Kajiado’s Nine-Hour Stretch in Nkoroi and Matasia

Further south in Kajiado County, customers face an even earlier start and the longest continuous outage on Tuesday’s schedule. 

Power supply in parts of Nkoroi and Matasia will be switched off at 8:00 am, remaining disconnected until 5:00 pm. The nine-hour interruption affects local shopping centres, service workshops, residential estates, and neighbouring consumers along the feeder lines.

With Matasia and Nkoroi serving as fast-growing commuter and peri-urban residential belts, the extended morning-to-evening disruption presents a significant operational hurdle for home offices, neighbourhood bakeries, water pumping facilities, and light commercial operations reliant on uninterrupted grid supply.

### Kiambu Servicing and the Condensed Nandi Window

Kiambu County is scheduled for an eight-hour cut matching Nairobi’s timetable. The utility announced that electricity supply will be interrupted in part of Rumenye from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm, taking out service for local businesses, agricultural smallholders, and adjacent households.

In contrast to the extensive eight- and nine-hour outages in the Nairobi metropolitan region, Nandi County has been assigned a markedly shorter maintenance window.

The planned works in Nandi centre on Olessos Technical and neighbouring institutions, running for four hours from 10:00 am to 2:00 pm. The tighter midday timeframe limits downtime for the technical training institute and nearby trading centres, allowing normal activities to resume well before the evening rush.

### Utility Guidance and Network Upgrades

Kenya Power periodically institutes planned regional blackouts to conduct preventive maintenance, install new lines, replace aged poles, and inspect distribution transformers to minimize unplanned system trips.

The utility has advised affected business owners, educational facilities, and households across all designated circuits in the four counties to take necessary precautions and adjust their power alternatives ahead of the Tuesday interruptions. Grid supply across the affected sectors is scheduled to normalize by 5:00 pm as field maintenance teams conclude their operations.
