Ken Wa Maria survived a early-morning crash along the Nairobi–Mombasa Highway on Friday after his Lexus struck the rear of a commercial lorry and veered off the road near Sabaki.

The veteran Kamba Benga musician—whose legal name is Kennedy Wambua Ngunze—was driving along the busy highway corridor at approximately 4:30 am when the incident occurred near Signature Mall in Athi River, Machakos County.

### The Sequence at Sabaki

Official details provided by law enforcement indicate that the crash occurred while the vehicle was in motion across lanes. Athi River East Sub-County Police Commander John Owuoth confirmed that the musician was attempting to change lanes when his Lexus made contact with the rear right side of an Isuzu lorry.

The impact forced the car out of its lane and off the asphalt, sending it into a roadside ditch. 

Emergency response at the scene ensured the artist was promptly removed from the vehicle and transferred for medical evaluation. He was admitted to Life Care Hospital in Mlolongo, located a short distance up the highway toward Nairobi.

### Medical Status and Immediate Updates

Commander Owuoth clarified that the singer sustained minor injuries during the incident, specifically a cut on his forehead and reported pain in his left leg. Police emphasized that the situation was stabilized at the scene and that there was no cause for alarm regarding his recovery.

Word of the pre-dawn wreck circulated rapidly across social media platforms early Friday morning, triggering immediate concern among his followers. Clarification arrived shortly after through music promoter and digital content creator Rick Be, who was among the first observers to post an update from the medical facility.

Rick Be shared a photo of the musician in his hospital bed, confirming that Ken Wa Maria had undergone preliminary medical checks, was conscious, and remained in stable condition under ongoing care by doctors at the Mlolongo facility.

### Public Response and Musical Legacy

The update brought visible relief to the music community and public figures across the region. Kitui Woman Representative Irene Kasalu was among the early political leaders to extend public well-wishes, issuing a statement hoping for the singer’s quick and complete restoration to health.

Ken Wa Maria remains one of the defining figures in modern Kamba Benga, having built a multi-decade recording career that transformed regional vernacular music into cross-over commercial hits. His extensive catalog includes enduring radio staples such as *Fundamentals*, *Mchagua Jembe Si Mkulima*, *Domitila*, and *Miss Musembi*.

As of Friday afternoon, the artist remained under observation at Life Care Hospital in Mlolongo as doctors monitored his recovery from the minor injuries sustained in the crash.
