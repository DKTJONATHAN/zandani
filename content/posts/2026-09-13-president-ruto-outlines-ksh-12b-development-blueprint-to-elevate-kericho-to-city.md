**KERICHO** - President William Ruto has officially set the machinery in motion to elevate Kericho town to city status, pledging a KSh 12 billion investment package aimed at meeting strict urban development mandates.

The announcement follows a two-day presidential tour of the county concluded on September 11, where the head of state outlined a roadmap to overhaul the municipality’s infrastructure to align with the Urban Areas and Cities Act.

The sequence of planned investments is as follows:

*   **Housing and Industry:** A KSh 5.6 billion allocation to deliver an initial 2,200 affordable housing units and establish a Special Economic Zone (SEZ) to anchor local industrial growth.
*   **Healthcare:** KSh 1 billion committed to the construction of a new medical school and the systematic upgrading of referral facilities in Kericho and Londiani.
*   **Sports:** KSh 2.1 billion earmarked for athletic development, with KSh 1.1 billion dedicated to Kapkatet Stadium and KSh 1 billion for the Kericho Town Stadium.
*   **Infrastructure:** KSh 1.6 billion set aside for rural electrification and the expansion of primary transport routes to support agricultural supply chains.

The national government has confirmed that these projects are designed to satisfy the legal requirements for city status, which include robust revenue-generation capacity, efficient utility management, and a population threshold of 250,000.

Kericho now joins Thika and Meru in the current queue for elevation, following the formal recognition of Eldoret as a city in 2024. Execution of the proposed projects is scheduled to commence shortly as the town prepares its formal application for elevation.
