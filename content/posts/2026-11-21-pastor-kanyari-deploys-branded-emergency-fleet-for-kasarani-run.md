Pastor Victor Kanyari is attempting to convert his pulpit persona into a parliamentary career in Kasarani, but his formal entry into party politics comes with an immediate competitive hurdle: he will not receive an automatic nomination ticket.

Former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua confirmed on Friday, November 20, 2026, that the founder of Salvation Healing Ministry has officially joined the Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) to contest the Kasarani parliamentary seat in the 2027 General Election. Writing on X, Gachagua noted that Kanyari’s decision followed extensive consultations across the constituency. However, Gachagua made it explicit that the preacher will have to fight for the party flag in competitive nomination primaries scheduled for March 2027.

### Branded Fleet and Grassroots Logistics

Ahead of the March nomination battle, Kanyari has already turned his grassroots campaign into a visible presence across Eastlands. The preacher has deployed branded vehicles across Kasarani to provide free transport for medical emergencies and community funerals, using social service logistics to anchor his bid.

That ground movement accompanies a six-point manifesto focused on practical local grievances. Kanyari's platform prioritizes water access, road rehabilitation, youth empowerment programs, and the distribution of educational bursaries. To secure his standing within DCP, Gachagua has tasked the preacher with driving voter registration campaigns throughout Kasarani, as the party positions itself to recruit political defectors and fresh entrants ahead of the 2027 polls.

### From Ruai Service to March 2027 Primaries

The political union between the preacher and the former Deputy President has been building since May 2026. During a joint church service in Ruai, Kanyari publicly endorsed Gachagua’s political movement, signaling his intention to trade pure pulpit commentary for legislative power.

Yet Kanyari’s candidacy carries the weight of a complex public profile. He gained national attention following a 2014 television exposé on alleged fake miracles and his signature "Sh310" seed-planting campaign. Despite continuous public scrutiny, Kanyari has preserved his Kayole church base and maintains that a political career will not displace his spiritual work.

The preacher has insisted that should Kasarani voters choose him to represent them in the National Assembly, he will continue to lead services at Salvation Healing Ministry. His immediate focus, however, rests squarely on surviving the March 2027 primary ballot.
