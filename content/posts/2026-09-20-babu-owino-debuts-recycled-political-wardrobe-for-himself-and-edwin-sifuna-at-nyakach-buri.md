Embakasi East MP Babu Owino knows how to turn a somber village gathering into a stage for grand political theater. 

Attending the burial of Teacher Jeremiah Kisero in Central Nyakach, Kisumu County, Babu did not just offer condolences to his friend, Kenya Methodist University (KeMU) President Kisero Jr. Instead, he used the podium to unveil a double-party makeover designed to solve his ongoing sibling rivalry with Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna.

Rather than building brand-new political vehicles from scratch, Babu has gone shopping in the closets of Kenya's political veterans, dusting off and rebranding legacy parties to suit his 2027 ambitions.

Babu confirmed he has acquired the Muungano Party, a vehicle long associated with former Kitui Governor Kivutha Kibwana. For the 2027 runway, this outfit has been rebranded as The Mwananchi Party (TMP), which Babu intends to use for his Nairobi gubernatorial bid.

But the MP did not stop at styling himself. In a bid to manage the public friction within the Linda Mwananchi movement, Babu offered a matching, custom-fitted political vehicle to his partner-in-ambition, Edwin Sifuna. 

According to Babu, Sifuna will take the wheel of the Ukombozi People’s Party (UPP). This outfit is itself a recycled version of the United Democratic Party (UDP), a legacy brand previously associated with the late former Lugari MP Cyrus Jirongo.

"[Sifuna] will be party leader of Ukombozi People’s Party," Babu told the Nyakach crowd. "Babu, party leader as well, of The Mwananchi Party. Do you agree or not?"

The arrangement allows both politicians to maintain their own independent party structures and headquarters while remaining under the broader umbrella of the Linda Mwananchi movement.

To sweeten the deal, Babu publicly endorsed Sifuna’s presidential ambitions, promising to campaign for the Nairobi senator under the joint platform. 

"We will support Sifuna as the presidential candidate for Linda Mwananchi," Babu declared. "We will form a coalition within Linda Mwananchi, then form another with the broader United Opposition."

This elaborate wardrobe swap comes after weeks of backstage bickering over who would control the movement's primary political ticket. The choice of party had become a sticky point, with Trans Nzoia Governor George Natembeya pushing for Sifuna’s bid to run on the UPP ticket, while Babu quietly secured his own outfit. 

While Vihiga Senator Godfrey Osotsi has dismissed the friction as normal political growing pains exacerbated by "State elements," the rush to secure these recycled parties points to a very real administrative headache.

Political players do not have the luxury of endless bickering. The Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) has set a strict October 15, 2026 deadline for all political parties to submit the names and specimen signatures of their authorized officials. 

By breathing new life into Kibwana’s and Jirongo’s old registrations, Babu has bypassed the tedious process of launching entirely new entities, ensuring both he and Sifuna have fully compliant vehicles ready before the IEBC clock runs out.
