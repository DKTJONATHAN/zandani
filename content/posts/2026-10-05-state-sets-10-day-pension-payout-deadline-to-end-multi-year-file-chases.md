President William Ruto announced on Monday that the National Social Security Fund and allied state pension bodies must disburse retirement benefits within 10 days of an employee leaving service, establishing a reform intended to shield hundreds of thousands of public servants from catastrophic payment delays.

The directive, unveiled during World Teachers’ Day proceedings at the Moi International Sports Centre, Kasarani, targets an immediate transition for educators before expanding across the entire civil service. Under the executive timeline, state pension administrators have three months to align internal systems and clear processing bottlenecks.

“The agencies responsible have assured me that within the next three months, we will be able to pay a teacher’s retirement benefit within 10 days of their retirement,” President Ruto said. “We want to change that culture of retired public servants moving from one public office to another to check if their retirement benefits are in order.”

### Compressing the Legal Processing Window

![Ruto hosted a town hall meeting on the sidelines of the Kenya Health Summit at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre in Nairobi, on August 18, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/XnYWxQS/451bbfb89742.webp)

The planned 10-day turnaround represents a severe contraction of the existing framework. While existing statutory rules allow for a 90-day processing window, real-world execution across state agencies has long collapsed under manual paperwork, audit discrepancies, and missing paper dossiers.

Formal investigations by the Commission on Administrative Justice have previously documented extreme administrative failures where retirees waited between four and 26 years to receive statutory exit packages. In widespread instances across ministries, human resource desks began assembling retiree files only after the officer had already cleared their physical desk, leaving elderly claimants to fund repeated travels to Nairobi to track down lost paperwork.

Under the new mandate, public workers must receive their dues roughly a week and a half after their official exit date, cutting out routine physical clearance follow-ups.

### The Digital Migration Backbone

![Ruto hosted a town hall meeting on the sidelines of the Kenya Health Summit at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre in Nairobi, on August 18, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/twkXCJwF/34af797aa192.webp)

The operational shift hinges on the Electronic Pension Management Information System, an administrative platform deployed jointly on July 1 by the Teachers Service Commission and the National Treasury.

State technicians are currently uploading historical personnel records into the digital portal to eliminate the physical transfer of files between school heads, TSC county directors, the Treasury pensions department, and social security administrators.

By digitizing the verification trail prior to an employee reaching mandatory retirement age, the platform is structured to trigger automatic approvals rather than manual, multi-agency audit rounds.

Whether pension bodies can meet the aggressive 10-day payment standard depends directly on how rapidly agencies complete legacy data ingestion over the next 90 days, ensuring that records reflect correct contribution histories long before a worker’s final day on duty.
