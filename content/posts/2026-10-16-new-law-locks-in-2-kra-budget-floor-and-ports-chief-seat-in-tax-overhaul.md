President William Ruto has formally anchored institutional funding floors and maritime logistics oversight into the tax administration framework under a newly enacted statute that extends beyond employee bonus provisions.

The Kenya Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026, signed at Eldoret State Lodge, establishes a statutory guarantee ensuring the tax collector receives an annual allocation of at least 2 percent of its estimated revenue collection. This baseline provision secures operating capital for the agency as it navigates demanding local revenue mobilization targets.

![President William Ruto assenting to the Tax Laws (Amendment) Bill and the Kenya Revenue Authority (Amendment) Bill 2026 at Eldoret State Lodge, Uasin-Gishu County, October 16, 2026](https://i.ibb.co/Xrkr9srj/b2e67e9ca9ad.webp)

Simultaneously, the legislation introduces a performance-tied incentive structure, granting the authority 3 percent of actual revenue collected in excess of quarterly targets. While the assent framework notes that these additional funds could support employee rewards, the statute stops short of guaranteeing automatic individual payouts or outlining specific employee allocations.

Operating alongside the financial adjustments, the reform alters the composition of the KRA Board. The legislative changes remove previous ex-officio restrictions and introduce the Managing Director of the Kenya Ports Authority as a substantive board member. 

Officials point out that the integration bridges two state bodies whose administrative functions intersect directly at import cargo clearance points. By seating the KPA chief directly within tax governance, the state aims to streamline clearance channels and close revenue leakages tied to international trade and customs declarations.

![KRA Times Tower](https://i.ibb.co/6ctGc9bn/e9c52c57f367.webp)

The statutory adjustments arrive as the authority faces intense fiscal pressure to fund public expenditures domestically while curbing reliance on external debt. KRA collects vital revenue streams including income tax, Value Added Tax, excise duty, and customs levies.

Implementation details regarding individual staff incentive disbursements and the immediate operational impact of the KPA board integration remain pending as administrative guidelines are rolled out across both agencies.
