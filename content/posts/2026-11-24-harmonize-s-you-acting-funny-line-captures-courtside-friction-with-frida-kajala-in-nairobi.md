Harmonize turned away from courtside seating with a five-word remark that quickly eclipsed the basketball action taking place in Nairobi on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. 

"You acting funny," the Tanzanian music star muttered as he broke off an unreciprocated greeting with his former girlfriend, actress Frida Kajala, before immediately redirecting his attention to other fans seated nearby.

The brief exchange, captured on social media footage from the venue, caught the musician attempting to pull Kajala into an embrace as she sat courtside. Instead of leaning in, Kajala stood up, resisted the physical gesture, and turned her gaze away from the singer.

Rather than pausing to press the issue, Harmonize shifted his stance, greeted several other venue guests standing along the perimeter, and walked past Kajala’s seat without making a second attempt to engage her. The short video clip spread rapidly across digital platforms, drawing instant scrutiny to the singer's on-the-spot recovery under the arena lights.

The public encounter unfolded against the backdrop of high-stakes regional athletics. Harmonize was present in Nairobi as part of a high-profile contingent supporting Tanzanian basketball club Dar City in the 2027 Road to the Basketball Africa League (BAL) East Division Elite 16 tournament. The team opened its Nairobi campaign on a high note, securing a decisive 96-76 victory over Mozambique’s Ferroviario da Beira.

Yet the courtside tension temporarily diverted eyes from Dar City's winning start, reopening public focus on the turbulent personal history between the two figures. Harmonize and Kajala first attracted public attention as a couple around 2020, establishing a relationship marked by high-visibility romantic gestures and public reconciliations.

Their trajectory appeared to solidify early in 2026 when Harmonize formally proposed to Kajala. However, the engagement stalled, and by September 2026, the artist publicly confirmed that the couple had separated for a third time.

Neither party has issued a formal statement explaining the courtside interaction captured in Nairobi. The BAL East Division Elite 16 tournament continues in the capital through November 29, 2026, with Dar City pursuing its qualification run while public attention remains firmly fixed on the sidelines.
