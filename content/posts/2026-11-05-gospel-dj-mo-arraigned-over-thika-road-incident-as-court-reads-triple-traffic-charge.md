Samuel Muraya, the gospel entertainer widely known as DJ Mo, stood before a Nairobi court on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, to face three distinct traffic charges resulting from an encounter with officers along Thika Road.

The court appearance shifts a routine highway enforcement stop into a formal legal proceeding for the veteran disc jockey, whose public brand is heavily anchored in Kenya's gospel broadcast and entertainment sector.

### The Three Statutory Counts

The charge sheet presented in court outlines three separate statutory violations under Kenya’s Traffic Act, each tied to vehicle compliance and roadside authority:

* **Lack of valid insurance cover:** Muraya was charged with driving a motor vehicle on a public road without a valid third-party insurance policy, which serves as the statutory minimum requirement for legal vehicle operation in Kenya.
* **Non-display of insurance certificate:** The prosecution entered a second count alleging that the entertainer failed to physically display an official insurance certificate on the vehicle as mandated by road transit regulations.
* **Disobeying police directives:** The third count stems from Muraya's alleged failure to comply with lawful instructions issued by police officers stationed along the highway during the incident.

Together, the counts move the matter beyond a standard roadside administrative caution into a structured judicial review of basic vehicle compliance.

### Highway Enforcement on Thika Road

Thika Road remains one of Nairobi’s heaviest transport corridors, subjected to consistent traffic management controls and spot-checks aimed at enforcing vehicle roadworthiness, licensing, and traffic flow regulations. Enforcement officers along the multi-lane highway routinely check for valid documentation and driver compliance, particularly during peak transit hours.

When drivers fail to produce required legal certificates or contest directions from uniformed officers on duty, standard protocol dictates that the matter escalates from a roadside stop to formal processing at the nearest traffic station and subsequent court arraignment.

### Public Profile and Judicial Process

Muraya has built a long-standing career as a gospel media personality, event host, and disc jockey. Alongside his wife, gospel artist Linet Munyali (Size 8), the couple has maintained a prominent profile across Kenyan broadcast platforms, commercial brand partnerships, and digital lifestyle content.

With the three charges formally read in court, the legal process now requires Muraya to enter his plea and address the statutory compliance obligations cited by traffic authorities. The matter remains active before the court as the judicial system determines the outcome of the charges.
