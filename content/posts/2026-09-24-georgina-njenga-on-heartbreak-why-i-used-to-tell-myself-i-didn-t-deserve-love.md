Long before advising others to walk away from broken unions, creator Georgina Njenga wrestled with the quiet, heavy narrative that she simply was not meant to be loved.

Taking to her Instagram Stories, Georgina peeled back the curtain on the psychological aftermath of a failed relationship. Rather than serving up generic motivational platures, she addressed the specific, damaging internal monologue that often accompanies rejection—the instinctual habit of weaponising a breakup against one's own self-worth.

“To anyone going through heartbreak, yes it’s your turn (don’t tell me you don’t deserve love or that you don’t deserve to be loved). I used to tell myself those things too,” she shared with her followers.

Her commentary focused heavily on the danger of internalising the end of a romance as an indictment of personal value. Navigating the emotional friction of a breakup, she noted, often tricks individuals into believing that rejection equates to a permanent lack of desirability.

Moving past that internal trap requires recognising when a dynamic has completely exhausted its course. Georgina strongly cautioned against the exhausting cycle of trying to force compatibility where mutual effort has died, framing inevitable endings not as total failures, but as necessary space for personal healing and individual growth.

“Don’t force it. It’s for the best,” she concluded, offering a blunt reminder that accepting a relationship's natural expiration is ultimately healthier than clinging to someone who has chosen to leave.
