An eight-hour power shutdown in Nairobi’s Mwihoko area and a concurrent seven-hour maintenance window along Kwale County’s Petrocity Bonje stretch will interrupt electricity supply on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, targeting major institutional and commercial sites across both regions.

In Nairobi County, Kenya Power has designated key residential, academic, and community nodes within Mwihoko for an interruption running from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. The scheduled maintenance directly impacts the district surrounding St. Lucy Kiriri University, a central educational landmark in the area, along with adjoining residential clusters and institutional facilities.

The Nairobi outage boundary covers several established neighborhood pockets. Among the specified sites are Mwithiritia SDA, the Muigai residential area, Discovery, and Mutuya. Technical crews will carry out network line upkeep throughout the eight-hour window, affecting both primary institutional connections and neighboring domestic consumers tied to the local circuit.

Simultaneously, in Kwale County, maintenance teams will isolate the Petrocity Bonje feeder network between 9:00 am and 4:00 pm. In contrast to the academic and residential concentration in Mwihoko, the Kwale interruption focuses on a high-density commercial, manufacturing, and transport corridor.

The Kwale shutdown affects multiple commercial energy suppliers and industrial processing units. Facilities operating within the seven-hour disruption window include Shell Bonje, the Petrocity complex, and Premium Fuels, alongside manufacturing business Kilimanjaro Biscuits and medical service provider Afro Afya. Adjacent commercial premises along the Bonje highway belt are also covered under the maintenance schedule.

Kenya Power maintains that scheduled outages form part of routine infrastructure management intended to maintain grid stability and conduct essential line servicing. Normal power supply across both Mwihoko and Petrocity Bonje is expected to resume progressively as field crews conclude their work at 5:00 pm and 4:00 pm respectively.
