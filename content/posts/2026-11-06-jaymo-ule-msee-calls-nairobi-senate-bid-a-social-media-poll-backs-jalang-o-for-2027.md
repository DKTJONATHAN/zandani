Comedian and political aspirant Rosario Njogu, better known as Jaymo Ule Msee, has written off his exploratory Nairobi Senate campaign as a digital testing exercise, stepping aside to back Lang’ata MP Felix Odiwuor, popularly known as Jalang’o.

The content creator turned politician announced his exit from the capital's upper-house race on Thursday, November 5, 2026, thanking his online followers for participating in what he cheekily described as an intelligent two-day opinion poll.

“As for you my fans Asanteni sana for helping me do an intelligent Opinion Poll for Nairobi in the last 2 Days — Now go back to your businesses Jaymo ako Sawa,” he told his social media audience.

With the Nairobi chapter closed, Jaymo confirmed he is shifting his political focus to Gatundu South, where he intends to contest for the parliamentary seat. He credited President William Ruto with redirecting his energy and campaign strategy toward his home turf.

“Thank you My Boss President Ruto for redirecting my energy, passion and skills to Gatundu South as Charity begins at Home,” Jaymo stated in his address, noting that the constituency demands his full attention and experience.

By stepping back from the Nairobi race, Jaymo has cleared a potential hurdle for Jalang’o, solidifying an alliance within the opposition-to-establishment political matrix. “As for Nairobi I leave and support my Brother Jalang’o for the Senator Seat,” he added.

Jaymo indicated that he will soon make his physical debut in Gatundu South to begin grassroot engagements as he sets up his new campaign machinery for the parliamentary contest.
