A fatal off-campus assault near Kibabii University has culminated in an abrupt, campus-wide displacement order, leaving hundreds of resident learners scrambling for transport and emergency shelter across Bungoma County.

Following a Tuesday special sitting, the Kibabii University Senate resolved to suspend the first semester of the 2026/2027 academic year indefinitely. The university’s Office of the Registrar in charge of Student Affairs subsequently issued a strict notice ordering all students residing in on-campus hostels to clear out by 3:00 PM the same day.

```
KIBABII UNIVERSITY DISRUPTION TIMELINE
• Monday Night: 4 students attacked near campus; 1 killed, 3 injured
• Tuesday Morning: Students boycott classes, protest along local roads; police deploy tear gas
• Tuesday Midday: Senate convenes special meeting, suspends 2026/2027 Semester I
• Tuesday 3:00 PM: Mandatory deadline for all resident students to vacate hostels
```

The flashpoint began on Monday night when unknown assailants ambushed a group of four Kibabii University students along a route near the main campus. The attack left one learner dead and three others hospitalised with serious injuries.

![Undated photo of students outside Kibabii University in Bungoma](https://i.ibb.co/mFBLjfsY/176fefb52058.webp)

By Tuesday morning, grief over the killing transformed into open demonstrations. Protesting students initiated a lecture boycott, spilled onto adjoining roads, and disrupted learning across the campus. Anti-riot police officers intervened with tear gas canisters to scatter crowds, but the friction intensified through midday, prompting university administrators to shut down the institution entirely.

### Eviction into an Unsecured Perimeter

The Senate's emergency closure notice went beyond on-campus residents. The registrar's memo explicitly urged students living in privately rented accommodations in the immediate university neighbourhood to vacate their rooms and seek alternative lodgings outside the zone until teaching resumes.

The directive creates an immediate logistical crisis for learners who must quickly find bus fares and safe transit out of Bungoma, or risk remaining in private hostels within the very peri-urban perimeter where Monday night's lethal ambush occurred.

![An undated image showing university students leaving an institution after being ordered to vacate](https://i.ibb.co/7NLxLnbf/310ed41b0dd6.webp)

### Mounting Turmoil in Public Higher Education

The upheaval in Bungoma mirrors widening friction over off-campus student safety and operational instability across Kenyan public universities. Earlier on Tuesday, students from Multimedia University staged demonstrations along Magadi Road in Ongata Rongai after a colleague was critically injured in a night attack, leading to confrontations and damage to adjacent informal residential structures.

These security crises unfold against a deeply compromised academic calendar. Public university campuses nationwide are already navigating stalled lecture schedules caused by the nationwide academic staff strike that commenced on Friday, October 2.

Kibabii University has not set a tentative resumption date for the suspended semester, leaving the return of academic operations tied to security reviews and the conclusion of investigations into the Monday night killings.
