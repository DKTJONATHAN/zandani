Digital creator Mummie Francie waited five full days before letting the internet in on her delivery room news. When she finally did, she arrived with a custom balloon installation, an exact timestamp, and one of the most distinctive baby monikers of the creator season.

Francie announced on Tuesday that she and her husband, Mbaba, had welcomed their second child—a baby girl delivered in the early hours of Thursday, September 24, 2026. The revelation ended days of private recovery following an early-morning birth that officially expanded the couple's household into a family of four.

Taking to social media, Francie shared curated snapshots from her hospital suite, where a celebratory arrangement dominated by balloon clusters marked the quiet transition. Rather than a rushed live dispatch from the maternity ward, the roll-out was paced deliberately, allowing the creator to reveal the arrival on her own schedule once out of the immediate postpartum haze.

### The 4:14 AM Delivery and a Grand Moniker

In her first public dispatch detailing the delivery mechanics, Francie pinpointed the exact moment her daughter arrived into the world.

“At exactly 4:14am The Goddess was delivered in our Arms,” she noted, pairing the update with heart and celebratory emojis.

The creator soon followed with the official introduction, unveiling the newborn’s full name alongside an expression of religious gratitude: Bijoux Pookie Bubbles.

“Bijoux Pookie Bubbles 🎀🫧💕 24•09•2026, early morning — the Lord delivered her safely into our hands,” Francie wrote in a follow-up message to her followers. “Our answered manifested prayer. Our little princess. She is here.”

The post blended Francie’s signature playful flair with overt spiritual framing, positioning the child's safe arrival as both a divine answer and a personal milestone after months of quiet preparation.

### Curating Family Life on the Feed

For Francie and Mbaba, the arrival of Bijoux represents another chapter in an online presence that has carefully metered their personal milestones. Over their time in Kenya's lifestyle and comedy creator ecosystems, the pair have routinely offered calculated glimpses into their marriage and parenting journey without entirely ceding their private domestic routine to public scrutiny.

Documenting modern parenthood has become a cornerstone for established digital figures, but Francie's approach with her second child favored a controlled buffer. By keeping the September 24 birth date confidential until Tuesday, the couple secured uninterrupted family time before switching on the high-engagement machinery of social media congratulations.

### Digital Peers Queue Up in the Comments

Once the notification alerts cleared, fellow creators and followers immediately flooded Francie's feed, responding as much to the timing and faith-centered language as to the theatrical baby name.

Online creator Karimi_jackie touched directly on the patience underlying the milestone, noting that “God’s time is always the best time and in His own time, He makes everything beautiful.”

Followers echoed the sentiment, with one fan observing, “When ur time comes even baskets will hold water for you. Congratulations mummie.”

Fellow digital heavyweights, including comedy creator Dem Wa Facebook, joined the congratulatory queue alongside hundreds of peers acknowledging the arrival of baby Bijoux.

With the initial hospital-suite rollout complete, Francie and Mbaba now pivot to navigating life as a family of four, leaving fans watching to see how the newest addition will fit into the couple’s ongoing digital landscape.
