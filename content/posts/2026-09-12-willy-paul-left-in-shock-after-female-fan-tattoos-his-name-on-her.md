NAIROBI - Kenyan recording artist Wilson Abubakar Radido, professionally known as Willy Paul, has publicly questioned the intent of a female fan who permanently inked the singer’s name onto her body.

**What we know:**
*   The musician shared a short video on his verified Instagram account showing a fresh tattoo of his name located on the fan’s lower anatomy.
*   Willy Paul, the CEO of Saldido International Entertainment, publicly expressed confusion regarding the gesture, soliciting fan opinions on the meaning behind the placement.
*   The post has drawn significant social media engagement, with multiple users debating whether the fan is displaying a legitimate pregnancy bump or a tattoo.
*   As of Saturday, September 12, 2026, the artist has not provided further updates on the identity of the fan or the circumstances surrounding the ink.

In the caption accompanying the footage, the artist wrote, "A Lady Just Tattooed My Name Kwa Haga Manze, Is This Love Or Something Else?"

The publication of the video prompted a surge of commentary from the singer’s followers. While some users debated the visual authenticity of the body part in the footage, others questioned the nature of such a display. One user, @._twerts.ivan, responded directly to the artist's inquiry, stating, "It’s something else bro."

Other followers noted the unconventional location of the tattoo, with user @papydonn suggesting that the placement may carry unintended derogatory connotations.

The post remains active on the artist’s profile as debate continues among his fanbase regarding the authenticity of the gesture and the motive behind it.
