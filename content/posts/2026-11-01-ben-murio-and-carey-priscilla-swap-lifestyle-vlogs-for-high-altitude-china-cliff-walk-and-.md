Ben Murio and Carey Priscilla found themselves suspended along a sheer, cloud-shrouded rock wall in China, but it was Murio’s quick quip comparing their high-altitude harness setup to Kenyan electricity maintenance crews that turned a nerve-wracking excursion into a viral moment.

High above a deep valley on a via ferrata trail, the content-creating couple traded standard resort vlogs for an extreme cliffside trek. The footage they published shows the pair carefully picking their way across a vertical cliff face, using a system of permanently fixed steel cables, iron rungs, and narrow mountain ladders to move across the terrain while holding recording gear.

With heavy clouds drifting across the backdrop and dramatic vertical drops visible beneath them, the setting carried an undeniable element of danger. Yet as they paused along the rock wall, Murio kept the atmosphere light, comparing their harness-bound resting positions along the cable lines to Kenya Power technicians hanging from wooden electricity poles while executing repairs in Kenyan neighborhoods.

That contrast between high-risk mountain sport and familiar home humor struck an immediate chord with their audience online. Via ferrata routes—an Italian term meaning "iron path"—are specialized climbing trails designed with fixed anchor points that allow travelers to navigate precipitous mountain terrain without traditional free-climbing risks. Throughout the recorded trail, both Murio and Carey remained hooked into the continuous steel safety wire using personal protective lanyards.

Even with visible safety gear keeping them anchored, the sheer height and cloudy atmospheric conditions caused genuine discomfort among many viewers. The clip generated a wave of split reactions, dividing fans who celebrated their willingness to tackle high-adrenaline activities from those who found the exposure too intense to watch comfortably.

Several followers drew direct parallels to the 2022 survival thriller *Fall*, a film centered on two climbers trapped atop an isolated, multi-hundred-foot tower.

"Ni kama nyie ndio mme act Fall 2," wrote commenter @NailerCA, referencing the terrifying perspective in the video.
