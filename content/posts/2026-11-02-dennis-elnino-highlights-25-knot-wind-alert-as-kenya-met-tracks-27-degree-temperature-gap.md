Content developer Dennis Elnino has spotlighted the latest 24-hour weather advisory from the Kenya Meteorological Department, drawing attention to a high-velocity wind alert in northern Kenya alongside a dramatic 27-degree Celsius temperature spread across the country.

The weather bulletin, extending through Monday, November 2, 2026, puts northern pastoral zones on notice for strong atmospheric disturbances even as rain systems move across more than 30 counties.

### High-Wind Alert Hits Northern Counties

The most acute hazard in the 24-hour outlook is concentrated across the northern expanse. Meteorologists issued a specific wind advisory for Marsabit, Samburu, and Turkana counties, warning of sustained southerly to southeasterly winds exceeding 25 knots—or more than 12.5 metres per second.

The high-wind warning coincides with wide thermal disparities across the country. Turkana is projected to record the highest daytime temperature in Kenya at 36°C, closely followed by Wajir and Mandera at 35°C. In sharp contrast, Nyandarua is set to register the coldest night conditions nationwide, with temperatures dropping to 9°C.

### Afternoon Rain to Intensify Across Western Belt

While northern zones face dry skies and heavy wind, rainfall is expected to build up across the western half of the country through Monday afternoon.

After early morning light showers in Nairobi, Kitui, Machakos, and Makueni break into sunny intervals, cloud cover will thicken over the Rift Valley, Nyanza, and Western regions. Counties including Nakuru, Kericho, Bomet, Kakamega, Migori, and Kisii are slated for afternoon light showers accompanied by thunderstorms.

Overnight, Western and Nyanza counties—specifically Siaya, Nyamira, and Kakamega—are forecast to see continued light showers and electrical storms. Conversely, Turkana, West Pokot, and Samburu are expected to remain dry overnight under clear to partly cloudy skies.

### Moderate Showers in South-Eastern Lowlands

In the South-Eastern lowlands and parts of Eastern Kenya, Kitui, Makueni, Taita Taveta, and Garissa are marked for steady moderate showers and thunderstorms, with rainfall totals measured between 5mm and 20mm. Tana River is also mapped for moderate showers across select areas.

Nairobi and the surrounding Central Highlands—including Nyeri, Murang’a, Kiambu, and Kirinyaga—will stay largely partly cloudy, with light showers restricted to a few locations.

Along the coast, towns such as Mombasa, Kwale, and Kilifi are set to clear the precipitation entirely, enjoying sunny weather through both the morning and afternoon hours as the 24-hour advisory window runs its course.
