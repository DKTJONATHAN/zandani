Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja moved on Wednesday to shut down an escalating internal revolt at City Hall, invoking executive powers under the County Governments Act to formally strip Geoffrey Mosiria of his portfolio and reassign key departments across county headquarters.

The administrative intervention, issued through a circular signed by County Secretary and Head of Public Service Machel Waikenda on October 21, severed Mosiria’s oversight as Chief Officer for Citizen Engagement and Customer Service with immediate effect.

Waikenda cited sections 44(3)(a), 45(5), and 64 of the County Governments Act, 2012, confirming that the reallocations carried the direct approval of the governor.

### The Administrative Reorganisation

![Mosiria](https://i.ibb.co/jZMvvFTc/79484efdd7a2.webp)

Rather than an isolated termination, the county circular recalibrated three operational desks through interim designations:

* **Public Participation and Citizen Engagement:** Zipporah Njeri assumes control as Acting Chief Officer, taking over the docket formerly held by Mosiria.
* **Mobility:** Samwel Njogu has been moved into the transport portfolio to serve as Acting Chief Officer.
* **Housing, Urban Renewal, and Lands:** Godfrey Akumali takes substantive charge of Housing and Urban Renewal while additionally absorbing the role of Acting Chief Officer for Lands.

The swift reassignments consolidate administrative control over key service and land management functions under a streamlined group of acting officers answerable directly to the executive office.

### From Enforcer to Challenger

The administrative split follows weeks of deteriorating relations between Sakaja and one of the county's most prominent public faces. Mosiria first gained widespread public attention while heading City Hall’s environment docket, where he spearheaded aggressive, visible crackdowns against illegal waste dumping, commercial noise pollution, and urban environmental infractions.

![nairobi-governor-sakaja.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/0prtSf34/7cbcd8c74276.webp)

His repositioning into customer service marked an initial cooling of relations, culminating on October 11 when Mosiria publicly launched a campaign to unseat Sakaja in the 2027 gubernatorial election. In that declaration, Mosiria cited his tenure across county departments as sufficient grounding to run the capital, even floating former Starehe MP Margaret Wanjiru as a prospective running mate—a proposal that remains formally unconfirmed by the church leader.

### Disputed Books and Whistleblowing Threats

Beyond political posturing, the confrontation has pivoted into public friction over the internal management of county resources. Mosiria previously claimed he had been sidelined following operational disputes over county financial expenditure and service delivery benchmarks, assertions that have not been independently verified.

Hours before his replacement was formalised on Wednesday, Mosiria escalated the standoff, publicly pledging to reveal alleged improprieties within the county government and asserting that administrative pressure was being applied to silence him.

By executing immediate personnel replacements across Citizen Engagement, Mobility, and Lands, City Hall has cut off Mosiria’s operational leverage inside the administration. The dispute now shifts entirely into the political and public arena, where Mosiria’s threatened financial disclosures will test the administration's posture as the newly appointed acting chief officers take over their desks.
