Former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua has detailed the operational trade-off he made upon landing back in the country, ditching his standard high-profile motorcade for a humble Toyota Probox to travel unnoticed from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) to his rural home in Nyeri County.

Speaking during a television interview on Sunday, November 1, 2026, the Democracy for the Citizens Party (DCP) leader explained that the decision to use a basic utility vehicle was part of a strict security protocol aimed at preventing physical and digital tracking by potential security threats.

### The Information Blackout Strategy

To execute the stealth movement, Gachagua intentionally excluded his immediate entourage from his travel itinerary, keeping both his driving crew and close security detail uninformed about his exact arrival time.

"I never told anyone I was coming, even my drivers or even the bodyguard I usually have, because people talk a lot," Gachagua said during the broadcast.

Alongside the operational blackout among his staff, Gachagua placed a strict ban on taking photos while inside the airport terminal. The move was designed to prevent real-time digital tracking or live updates on social media platforms that could betray his location before he cleared airport gates.

### Trading Convoys for Commercial Utility

Addressing the choice of transport, Gachagua noted that traveling in high-end SUVs or a recognizable political convoy would have immediately alerted individuals monitoring airport exits.

"From the airport, I boarded a Probox because I knew even my big cars would be trailed," he stated.

The low-profile vehicle allowed him to blend into late-night transit traffic along the Nairobi-Nyeri highway, completing the journey to his home in Wamunyoro without attracting public attention or security surveillance.

Gachagua linked the extreme precautions to a previous return from the United States, where public announcements led to large crowds at JKIA alongside alleged security threats and organized groups attempting to disrupt his arrival. He credited local youth and divine intervention for safeguarding him during that earlier event.

### Timeline of the US Trip

According to travel details confirmed during the interview, Gachagua had departed Kenya for the United States on August 26, 2026. He completed his unannounced return journey on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, remaining off the public radar until his gradual re-emergence for political hostings at his Wamunyoro estate.

The revelation offers a glimpse into the defensive logistics adopted by the former Deputy President as he continues to navigate his political operations under the DCP banner from his home base in Nyeri.
