Clad in an Inter Milan jersey and flashing a relaxed peace sign, actress Sandra Dacha offered her followers a reassuring glimpse into her recovery this week following an undisclosed health challenge.

The media personality welcomed Kenya Fans Association chairman Shem Okottah to her home in a courtesy call that brought the national football community directly to her doorstep. Dacha, widely recognized for her vocal, unwavering support for Gor Mahia Football Club, shared her appreciation online immediately after the visit concluded.

“Thank you so much, Chairman! Mgonjwa is very happy 😊 May God bless you,” Dacha posted on social media, accompanying her note with a snapshot capturing the pair in high spirits.

The association officially announced the outreach on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, framing the visit as  the solidarity that binds the local sports community beyond stadium terraces and matchdays. While neither Dacha nor the visiting leadership disclosed the specific medical condition keeping her away from public sets, the visit quickly drew a wave of goodwill from colleagues and football supporters alike.

Beyond the get-well wishes, online observers have closely tracked her recent physical transformation and noticeable weight loss, connecting the changes to her ongoing health and wellness journey. As messages continue to pour in across digital platforms, the visit highlights the tight-knit network linking Kenya's entertainment figures with the country's passionate sports fanbases.
