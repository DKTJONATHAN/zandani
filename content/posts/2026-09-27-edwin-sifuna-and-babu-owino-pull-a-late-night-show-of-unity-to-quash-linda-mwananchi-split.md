Nairobi Senator Edwin Sifuna and Embakasi East MP Babu Owino have turned a marathon Saturday leadership retreat into a high-stakes display of political brotherhood, moving quickly to put to rest persistent rumors of an internal split within the Linda Mwananchi movement. 

After weeks of intense public chatter suggesting the reform-oriented lobby was fracturing under the weight of competing ambitions, Sifuna emerged late into the night to signal that his political machinery is fully intact. 

Taking to his channels after the meeting wrapped up, Sifuna acknowledged the grueling hours of the closed-door session but insisted the outcome was exactly what his camp needed.

"Linda Mwananchi retreat imeisha late lakini imeisha mzuri," Sifuna shared. "Always a pleasure being in the company of these great leaders! Mbele kuzuri sana!"

The show of solidarity is a crucial step for Sifuna, who has been searching for a fresh, independent political platform to anchor his 2027 presidential ambitions following his high-profile fallout with the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM). 

Babu Owino, whose own political moves are closely watched by Nairobi’s youth electorate, was equally quick to project absolute alignment. He dismissed any talk of division, framing the Saturday session as a consolidation of power rather than a fire-fighting exercise.

"We are more united than ever," Babu stated. 

According to Babu, the leadership of Linda Mwananchi spent the retreat mapping out a coordinated strategy to mobilize their grassroots bases and prepare for joint political operations ahead of the 2027 general election. He emphasized that the group’s broader goals require a level of discipline and coordination that leaves no room for internal rivalries.

"We agreed that achieving our common political objective requires unity, coordination, and a willingness to work together," Babu added.

The public display of unity comes just days after Babu made a point of reassuring his supporters that he fully backs Sifuna's bid for the top seat. For weeks, political insiders had speculated that the ambitious leaders within Linda Mwananchi were pulling the movement in different directions.

With the endorsements now finalized, the focus shifts to Sifuna’s next big reveal. The Linda Mwananchi leadership confirmed that the Senator is set to unveil the specific political party on whose ticket he will run for the presidency in early October. 

Until that launch, Sifuna and his allies are positioning themselves as a unified alternative to the current administration, with plans to build alliances with other sympathetic political outfits over the coming months. Whether this late-night display of unity can hold as the reality of party building sets in will be the next major test for Nairobi’s most watched political duo.
