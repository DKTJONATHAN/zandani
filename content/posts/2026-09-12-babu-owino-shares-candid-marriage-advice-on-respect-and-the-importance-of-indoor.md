Embakasi East Member of Parliament Babu Owino has offered a candid perspective on marital dynamics, emphasizing the roles of mutual respect and intentionality during a rapid-fire interview conducted this week.

During the exchange, the 37-year-old lawmaker addressed the interviewer’s request for relationship advice by initially posing a question regarding her own marital status. "Get married first," Owino stated, before elaborating on his views regarding partnership longevity.

He underscored that successful relationships rely on the distinct contributions of both parties. According to Owino, a woman’s primary responsibility is to provide respect and submission, which he described as essential pillars of a functioning marriage. Conversely, he noted that men are tasked with providing consistent emotional and social support, punctuated by "indoor games" to maintain quality time within the home.

The MP, who met his wife, Fridah Muthoni Owino, while they were both students at the University of Nairobi, identified her as his closest companion. He revealed that she remains the person he communicates with most frequently throughout his daily schedule.

Outside of his family life, Owino outlined a disciplined personal routine that dictates his daily operations. He maintains a strict schedule of waking at 5:30 a.m. and retiring at midnight, with both the beginning and end of his day marked by prayer.
