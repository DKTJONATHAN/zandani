Plainclothes police officers operating in Nairobi’s central business district bypassed active press credentials on Tuesday, dragging Citizen TV journalist Odee Francis off his assignment and bundling him into a service vehicle as authorities moved to break up public demonstrations against soaring fuel prices.

The confrontation unfolded in broad daylight along Kimathi Street, directly outside the Sarova Stanley Hotel near the Kenyatta Avenue junction, where demonstrators had gathered to register grievances over the escalating cost of living.

### Physical Seizure on Kimathi Street

Francis was tracking the progression of the demonstration when a detail of non-uniformed officers closed in. Eyewitness documentation captured the squad physically subduing the reporter on the pavement before hauling him several metres toward a stationary police Land Cruiser parked along the avenue.

![A File Image of Citizen TV Studios](https://i.ibb.co/V0vrtjMj/e87ba4d1fbca.webp)

The intervention continued unabated despite immediate pushback from surrounding pedestrians and fellow news crews who intervened to formally identify Francis as an accredited broadcast journalist carrying out field duties.

Francis personally appealed to the arresting squad, requesting permission to remain on site to complete his television dispatch. The non-uniformed officers dismissed both his verbal representations and the protestations of working colleagues, forcing him into the vehicle’s rear compartment before driving away from the central commercial artery.

### Disregard for Field Identification

The Tuesday detention highlights an escalating operational hazard for field correspondents covering spontaneous economic unrest within the capital. Rather than establishing safe perimeters or verifying station badges, the responding plainclothes unit treated media personnel on the pavement as active participants in the unsanctioned assembly.

![A File Image of Citizen TV Studios](https://i.ibb.co/rfdpbCNZ/5075515636ca.webp)

Kimathi Street and the intersecting Kenyatta Avenue node have consistently served as focal staging grounds for public picketing, drawing heavy deployments of both regular uniform personnel and covert tactical squads tasked with preventing crowds from massing near government installations.

### Operational Backdrop and Next Steps

The fuel demonstrations had drawn groups into downtown Nairobi early Tuesday to challenge recent price reviews that have squeezed transport operators, small enterprises, and commuter budgets across the metropolitan area.

Authorities had not issued a formal booking station location or specified charges against Francis by midday Tuesday, leaving newsroom representatives and professional media bodies seeking confirmation of his custody status and demanding his unconditional release.
