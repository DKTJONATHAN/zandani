Khaligraph Jones has pulled back the curtain on a surprisingly steady mentorship line in Kenya’s music ecosystem, revealing that he routinely places private phone calls to Willy Paul to keep the singer focused through career turbulence.

Speaking during a social media conversation shared on Monday, November 23, 2026, the veteran rapper stepped away from standard hip-hop bravado to offer a candid assessment of the former gospel star turned secular pop creator. Khaligraph designated Willy Paul—popularly known as Pozee—as one of the most gifted artists of his generation, attributing the singer's longevity not just to raw craft, but to spiritual resilience.

“But mi husemanga Pozee is one of the most talented artists that we have in this generation,” Khaligraph said during the session.

The rap icon drew a sharp distinction between commercial visibility and true industry endurance, arguing that surviving the Kenyan entertainment circuit over several years requires backing that goes beyond routine hit records.

“There’s one thing, kukuwa a big artist na artist mwenye ako na grace ya God. Pozee anakuanga na grace ya God because vitu huyo kijana amepitia hata kukuwa hii industry, na bado mpaka saa hii anasimama,” Khaligraph explained.

The exchange highlighted the contrast between Willy Paul’s public career—frequently shadowed by online feuds, legal drama, and public scrutiny—and the direct encouragement he receives from senior peers. During the discussion, another participant interjected that Willy Paul effectively possessed “nine lives” given the sheer volume of career setbacks he had managed to navigate.

Rather than observing from a distance, Khaligraph disclosed that he actively picks up the phone to offer reassurance whenever public pressure builds.

“Mi humpigia… You know, I call him every now and then, I’m like, ‘Pozee, bro man, whatever you do, just keep on doing it. Mungu anajua kwanini alikuweka hapa hivi. God ako na purpose yako yaani kwa hii life,’” Khaligraph disclosed.

The rapper emphasised that his outreach extends beyond formal industry courtesy, framing himself as a genuine fan of Pozee’s catalogue who takes personal initiative to keep his spirits up.

“I’m a fan of his. I like what he does and I always call him every now and then to big him up,” Khaligraph added.

The public co-sign was acknowledged almost immediately. Shortly after the clip circulated, Willy Paul took to his social media pages to express gratitude for the rapper's public stance, framing the gesture as a rare display of brotherhood in a sector often driven by rivalry.

“Thank You OG @khaligraph_jones. It takes real ome to say such. Bless You,” Willy Paul wrote.

The exchange marks an uncharacteristic moment of clear solidarity between two of Kenya’s biggest music figures, establishing that behind Willy Paul’s headline-heavy career operates a direct line of counsel from the country's top rap figure.
