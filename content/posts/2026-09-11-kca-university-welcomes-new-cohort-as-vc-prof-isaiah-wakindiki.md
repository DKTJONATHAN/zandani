**RUARAKA, NAIROBI** - KCA University formally inducted its latest cohort of students on Friday, September 11, 2026, during a matriculation ceremony held at the institution’s Ruaraka main campus.

**What we know:**
* The university, established in 1989 and sponsored by ICPAK, currently serves a student population exceeding 38,000.
* Prof. Isaiah Wakindiki reported that 96% of the institution’s graduates secure employment or business opportunities within one year of completion.
* The University Council has approved a KES 2 billion investment for infrastructure expansion at the Ruaraka campus, slated for completion by September 2027.
* During the event, the institution received certification from CPA Jacinta Moraa designating it as a KASNEB Centre of Excellence.

Vice Chancellor and CEO Professor Isaiah Wakindiki, an Elder of the Order of the Burning Spear (EBS), presided over the proceedings. Addressing the new students, Prof. Wakindiki emphasized the importance of professional networking and strategic foresight.

"You are sitting next to the next big professor in this republic a few years to come," Prof. Wakindiki stated, noting that the cohort includes future entrepreneurs and political leaders. He further characterized the start of their academic tenure as "crossing the starting line in a marathon."

The university, which currently operates four physical campuses in Ruaraka, Nairobi CBD, Kitangela, and Kisumu, alongside a virtual center, maintains an alumni base of 45,000 individuals across 43 countries.

In response to the institution's growth, Prof. Wakindiki, who also serves as the Vice President of the Association of African Universities, confirmed that the Ruaraka campus is scheduled to host the association’s 60th-anniversary celebrations next year.

Concluding his address, the Vice Chancellor urged the new students to maintain high standards of conduct. "I've never been lucky. I've just been very keen on opportunities that have been placed before me and I exploit them to the maximum. Remember a standard of discipline, a standard of integrity, and a standard of excellence," he said.
