A routine integrity raid on a national examination center in Kisii County turned into an active search-and-rescue mission on Thursday after an enforcement team found itself stranded without armed backup, facing a violent student mob.

The confrontation at Nyabisase Mixed Secondary School began around 10:00 am on Thursday, November 5, when a multi-agency monitoring delegation—comprising officials from the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC), the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI), and the National Intelligence Service (NIS)—entered the compound. They acted on a direct alert from a KNEC invigilator who reported procedural compromises inside the ongoing Kenya Certificate of Secondary Education (KCSE) examination rooms.

Within minutes of entering the administration wing, investigators uncovered a concealed laptop hidden behind the school's offices. The discovery provided immediate physical evidence of unauthorized electronic access at the center. Recognizing that the hardware pointed to a coordinated malpractice scheme, the monitoring officers placed an emergency call to the DCI station in nearby Nyamache, requesting an armed investigative unit to secure the evidence and process the scene.

### Ambushed Before Reinforcements Arrived

![A contingent of police officers standing outside Nyabisase Mixed Secondary School on Thursday, November 5, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/M5N3VnHv/9a6af9c5e924.webp)

The tactical window closed before detectives could arrive.

Alerted to the discovery behind the administration block, students turned on the inspection team. What began as resistance quickly escalated into an organized assault. The learners confronted the officers and examination officials, overpowering the small oversight team and driving them away from the monitoring perimeter.

During the melee, the students commandeered a private vehicle belonging to one of the deployed police officers. They smashed the vehicle, causing extensive bodywork damage, and ransacked the cabin, stealing personal belongings and operational gear kept inside.

As the multi-agency personnel scattered to escape the compound, one police officer sustained severe injuries in the assault. By the time order was partially restored, the injured officer had vanished. His current location remains unknown, making his recovery the top priority for area commanders.

### Widening Multi-Agency Inquiries

The violent pushback at Nyabisase underscores the severe operational hazards confronting mobile examination inspection units during national assessments. Across the country, KNEC and security organs have stepped up unannounced visits to curb early material leakage, unauthorized mobile phones, and external digital syndicates assisting candidates.

However, the events at Nyabisase expose the vulnerability of dispatching monitoring units into potentially hostile environments without immediate tactical escort or quick-reaction support on standby.

DCI detectives from Nyamache have since secured the school compound and launched a four-tier criminal inquiry: locating their missing colleague, identifying the students and any staff members who participated in the physical assault and destruction of the police vehicle, and retrieving the digital chain of custody from the recovered laptop.

With national examinations entering critical stages, security teams in Kisii are now balancing the administration of papers at the center against an active investigation into violent obstruction and an unaccounted-for officer.
