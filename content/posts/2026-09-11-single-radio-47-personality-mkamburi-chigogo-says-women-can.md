NAIROBI - Radio 47 personality Mkamburi Chigogo has sparked fresh debate regarding marital expectations, asserting that excessive verbal communication by men is a primary factor that may lead women to terminate relationships.

In remarks addressing personality dynamics within partnerships, the presenter suggested that men who dominate conversations in private settings risk losing their partners to others who exhibit more reserved temperaments.

“Women despise men who talk a lot. Ukiwa kwa room, you are talking more than me? Utawachwa na mtu ataendea sponsor ambaye ametulia,” Chigogo stated regarding the issue of communication balance.

These comments follow a series of public disclosures made by the host regarding traditional marriage customs. On September 1, 2026, Chigogo addressed the topic of dowry, stating that if she were ever required to finance her own bride price, she would publicly declare the arrangement before both families.

“Mimi nikijilipia mahari, kwenye kikao nitatangaza! Let me assure you, mimi nikawai lipa mahari, wallahi katika kile kikao, uncles zangu, kina nani wako hapo, nitasimama, nitasema tu nataka tu nitangaze na niweke wazi kwamba mna deni langu,” she said during a broadcast earlier this month.

While Chigogo indicated a willingness to contribute financially to marriage negotiations if necessary, she maintained that the expectation remains for the man to fulfill his traditional obligations.

“Kwenye hii familia wanaume, mimi ndio nimejilipia mahari and I expect this man here that is marrying me, kuchangamka,” she added.

The radio host remains a prominent voice on social issues, frequently utilizing her platform to share perspectives on relationship boundaries and modern marriage dynamics. As of Friday, September 11, 2026, there has been no further clarification regarding these statements from Chigogo.
