Kenya Power is cutting electricity supply across key commercial corridors, upscale residential pockets, and regional processing belts on Monday, October 12, 2026, rolling out scheduled network maintenance that will leave affected areas without power for up to nine hours.

The utility firm confirmed that the maintenance drive will run concurrently across sections of Nairobi, Kajiado, Nyeri, Meru, Embu, and West Pokot counties, prompting warnings for households and commercial enterprises to adapt their schedules.

### Nairobi’s Westlands and Spring Valley Belt Unplugged

In the capital, the disruption zeroes in on the affluent residential and hospitality strip spanning Spring Valley and Westlands. Electricity supply will be suspended from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., cutting power to Peponi Road, General Mathenge Road, Donyo Sabuk, Spring Valley Road, and adjacent establishments.

The eight-hour daytime window hits an active precinct lined with high-density residences, boutique commercial spaces, and diplomatic offices that rely heavily on continuous utility supply during Monday business hours.

### Kajiado Commuter and Leisure Nodes Face Nine-Hour Cut

Across the county line in Kajiado, the planned shutdown begins earlier, stretching from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The interruption cuts a broad swathe through Ngong Town and its sprawling residential neighborhoods, taking down supply in Zambia, Scheme 305, Elpaso, Vet Farm, Juanco, Kangawa, and Bulbul.

The outage will also cut through Olepolos, the entire Kerarapon neighborhood, Masai School, sections of Ngong Road, and surrounding properties, leaving peri-urban households and local traders dependent on private backup power throughout the working day.

### Commercial Plants and Institutions Impacted in Nyeri and Embu

In Nyeri County, Kenya Power scheduled an eight-hour interruption between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., affecting a dense mix of industrial, security, and educational facilities. Key installations listed for the outage include the Coca-Cola depot, King’ong’o Prison, Good Shepherd Academy, Nyeri Primary School, and Rware Secondary School.

The cut will also affect Nyeri Baptist, Nyeri Inn, Autospin Garage, and the broader King’ong’o Estate alongside neighboring businesses.

In Embu County, maintenance teams will switch off supply from 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., covering Siakago Market, Siakago Girls, Gatondo, Maramuri, Kiamuringa, Havard, Muchonoke, and Mungau. The outage extends to Minuri, Mwangaza, Kambaru, Makunguru, Gitiburi, Gikuyari, and adjacent consumers.

### Tea Belts in Meru and West Pokot Outposts on the Line

Agricultural output faces a direct daytime pause in Meru County, where Kenya Power has scheduled an eight-hour network overhaul from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The shutdown takes major processing facilities off the grid, including Igembe Tea Factory and Amungeti Tea Factory, alongside Igembe Boys, Kimongoro Market, Athi Market, and Red Canteen.

Other Meru localities affected by the Monday outage include Kilindine, NCME, Antobochiu, Kaweru, Ntherone, Kiancia, Ugoti, Teera, Marega, Amwamba, Kagoma, and Ikuu.

In West Pokot County, the maintenance will run from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., covering far-flung trading centers and administrative settlements. The list includes Pkopoch, Serewo, Kitelakapel, Kacheliba, Orolwo, Kodich, Konyao, Kasei, Kiwawa, Kamketo, Chelopoy, and Alale.

Kenya Power stated that the extensive regional outages are necessary to facilitate scheduled maintenance and repairs on its power distribution lines, advising customers across all six counties to plan accordingly for the prolonged daytime downtime.
