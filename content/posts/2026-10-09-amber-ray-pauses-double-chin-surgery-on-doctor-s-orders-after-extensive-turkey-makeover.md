Faith Makau, known across digital spaces as Amber Ray, ran up against the hard physical limits of cosmetic transformation during her recent surgical run in Turkey, revealing that medical advice forced her to halt an intended double-chin procedure.

While Makau successfully checked off body contouring and an extensive dental overhaul during her September 2026 trip, her surgical itinerary had to be scaled back in the operating room. The influencer wanted to address her chin in the same sweep, but her operating surgeon declined to add the facial procedure to an already crowded roster of interventions.

Makau addressed the boundary publicly while attending media personality Natalie Githinji’s Endometriosis Warriors gathering at the Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC) on Sunday, September 27, 2026. Rather than masking the unfinished aesthetic goal, she laid out the clinical caution that intervened.

“I still want to do my double chin,” Makau said during the event. “I wanted to do it, but the doctor told me he can’t do many surgeries at the same time, so my body needs to be given a break.”

The medical pause arrived after Makau had already committed her body to substantial trauma. Her trip abroad involved breast augmentation alongside Lipo 360, a comprehensive circumferential liposuction procedure targeting the midsection, flanks, and lower back. 

While reviewing options on-site, Makau also consented to expand the surgical scope after her medical team proposed utilizing harvested fat deposits for lower-leg restructuring.

“Go yes… boobs, Lipo 360, and calves,” she noted while listing the operations she cleared for the table.

Beyond the body-sculpting operating theater, Makau underwent a multi-stage oral reconstruction. The dental work covered 21 individual teeth, with the influencer disclosing an estimated price tag of KSh 44,000 per unit. That individual pricing brought her total dental expenditure to roughly KSh 924,000, tipping her cosmetic dentistry bill close to the KSh 1 million mark before tallying airfare, hospital fees, and body contouring.

The volume of simultaneous work explains why her attending physician stepped in. Stacking general anesthesia, extensive soft-tissue trauma from liposuction and grafting, and intensive dental operations pushes bodily endurance to its perimeter. For Makau, that clinical ceiling meant shelving the double-chin correction until her system fully stabilizes.

“So yeah, if I get a chance, I’ll do my double chin. Only that,” she explained regarding her future surgical intentions, insisting she would only revisit the operating table once she had allowed her body sufficient recovery time.

Despite having to postpone the final procedural item on her wishlist, Makau framed the resulting physical transformation around a broader shift in how she approaches aging. In recent reflections shared with her audience, the influencer argued that her perception of her appearance has sharpened rather than diminished with time.

“The older I get, the prettier I look. I don’t know how to explain it, but I feel very womanly,” she said.

For now, Makau’s immediate timeline remains anchored to post-operative recuperation. While the desire to refine her profile remains on the record, any future incision depends on how cleanly her body rebounds from the hefty balance sheet of work completed in Istanbul.
