Raymond Omosa travelled to Mombasa determined to silence rumours that his prized luxury vehicle had been seized by creditors, only to find himself at the centre of fresh controversy after an aggressive driving stunt nearly flattened an onlooker.

The forex trader, known widely across social media as Kenyan Prince, had barely settled into the coastal city before footage of his yellow Lamborghini Urus went viral for the wrong reasons. A clip posted to his TikTok account on Saturday, November 14, 2026, captured the high-performance SUV launching into a sudden, high-speed burst from a petrol station forecourt, narrowly missing a bystander who had stepped forward to record the spectacle.

The near-miss unfolded while Omosa and his entourage stopped to refuel the vehicle. Recognised by service station staff and curious passersby, the Italian performance machine quickly drew a knot of fans and automotive enthusiasts eager to snap photos and record short videos on their phones.

### Sudden Launch at the Pumps

With mobile phone cameras pointed squarely at the Urus, Omosa positioned the vehicle facing an open access corridor next to the station. What followed was a demonstration of the SUV’s raw horsepower that gave little margin for error.

With hazard lights blinking, Omosa stepped on the throttle. The vehicle surged forward with violent acceleration, eating up the short tarmac strip in a matter of seconds. The sudden burst caught one spectator completely flat-footed.

The bystander, who had stationed himself close to the exit path to film the car’s departure, found the SUV bearing down on him at pace. Video captured from adjacent phones showed the individual making an emergency leap sideways, dodging the charging vehicle with split-second timing. The Urus brushed past within inches, exiting the property while the crowd behind continued filming the wake of the stunt.

The clip provoked immediate concern across social platforms, where users questioned the judgment of executing high-torque launches in a crowded commercial forecourt flanked by concrete curbs and fuel pumps.

### The Coastal Rebuttal

The incident came at the tail end of an aggressive pushback by Omosa against days of digital mockery. Rumours had mounted earlier in the week following the circulation of footage showing a Lamborghini Urus loaded onto the back of a flatbed car transporter in Nairobi.

Online critics quickly claimed that the luxury SUV had been impounded or repossessed over unpaid loans, with detractors using the footage to question the legitimacy of the trader's lavish lifestyle displays. Commentators had openly celebrated what they assumed was a humbling financial blow for the creator.

Omosa chose the coastal trip to stage his direct rebuttal. In a follow-up video posted to his YouTube channel on Sunday, November 15, 2026, Omosa appeared alongside his companion, Mushkert, documenting their arrival at their Mombasa accommodation to await the car’s delivery.

He revealed that the transporter seen in the viral clips was not a repossession flatbed, but a private carrier hired to haul the machine safely down the highway to Mombasa. To drive the point home, Omosa shared footage of the vehicle being lowered from the transport truck onto coastal tarmac, directly addressing his critics and asking whether those who claimed the SUV was gone still believed their own claims.

Yet what was framed as a definitive victory lap against online gossip quickly unraveled once the Urus rolled onto active streets. Instead of closing the book on the carrier drama, Omosa's forecourt acceleration test has shifted scrutiny from his car ownership papers directly onto his conduct behind the wheel.
