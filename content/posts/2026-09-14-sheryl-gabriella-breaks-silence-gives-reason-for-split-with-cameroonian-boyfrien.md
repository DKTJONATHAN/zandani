NAIROBI - Kenyan content creator Sheryl Gabriella has confirmed the end of her relationship with her Cameroonian partner, Chapeau, citing "constant opinions and pressure" from the Kenyan online community as a decisive factor in the split.

Speaking on the separation, Gabriella indicated that the weight of public scrutiny became unsustainable, affecting the internal dynamics of the relationship. While the creator has chosen to address the matter directly with her audience, she noted that Chapeau is unlikely to offer a public statement, attributing his reticence to his personal temperament.

The relationship had garnered significant attention on social media platforms, partly due to its international nature. However, Gabriella suggested that the visibility which fueled their online brand eventually contributed to their parting.

This personal disclosure comes during a period of documented volatility for the influencer. in March 2026, Gabriella reported a sudden financial downturn after five major brand contracts were terminated. The loss of revenue forced her to downsize from a residence costing KSh 200,000 per month to more modest accommodations.

The creator has also been forced to defend herself against recent online criticism regarding her late father’s home. Following his burial, followers questioned the humble state of the property, contrasting it with Gabriella’s previous public assertions about funding a house for her parents.

As of Monday, Gabriella continues to manage her digital presence independently while Chapeau remains absent from the public discourse surrounding the breakup.
