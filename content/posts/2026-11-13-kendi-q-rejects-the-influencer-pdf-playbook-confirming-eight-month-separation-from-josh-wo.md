Content creator Kendi Christine, known widely to her online audience as Kendi Q, has set a firm boundary around her personal life by confirming the end of her relationship with dancer Josh Wonder—while revealing that the separation actually took place eight months ago without online drama.

Rather than staging a coordinated public announcement, Kendi addressed the relationship status after a recent post made reference to "Kendi Q’s boyfriend," prompting her to step in and clear up public assumptions about her current life.

In a direct message sent to NGC, Kendi stated unambiguously that the romantic partnership with Josh had ended.

“Saw your post on the ‘Kendi Q’s boyfriend’ statement and just to be clear. Josh and I are not in a relationship. Neither are we dating,” she stated.

### Bypassing the Formal Press Release

Following the direct message, Kendi took to her Instagram Stories to detail the timeline for her followers, confirming that the split occurred early in the year and outlining her intention to remain focused on herself.

“Also. I’ve been single for 8 months now. I will keep it that way till further notice,” she shared, adding a brief “Lovely day squad” to close the update.

Crucially, Kendi explicitly distanced herself from the growing trend among East African digital personalities who release formal, styled press releases when a public romance ends.

“Sina maana ya official pdf statement like other couples,” she remarked in Swahili, poking lighthearted fun at the scripted nature of celebrity breakups before noting she would address her community casually during her regular evening check-ins.

### A Firm Boundary Against Online Harassment

Beyond setting the timeline straight, Kendi addressed members of her audience who expressed disappointment over the end of the long-running relationship. Reflecting on the time she and Josh shared, she maintained that the outcome did not erase the value of the past.

“Those disappointed. Poleni sana. It was a beautiful number of years that I do not regret,” she wrote. “However, darlings, life moves on regardless. I mean it’s been months. One can only take much of what’s done.”

Knowing how online fan bases often mobilize against former partners after a split, Kendi issued a direct warning to her followers, known as the KQ Squad, urging them not to direct online hostility toward Josh.

“Also KQ squad. Kindly let the guy be. Nawajua sana,” she instructed, explicitly cutting off any potential mobbing campaigns against the dancer.

### From Public Milestones to Quiet Separation

The relationship between Kendi Q and Josh Wonder first became a point of public interest in late 2024, following months of speculation that both creators initially denied. The pair eventually went public, documenting shared milestones across social platforms, including Valentine’s Day celebrations and birthday posts throughout 2025 and into early 2026.

By keeping the split private for eight months before addressing it in November 2026, Kendi effectively managed the transition away from the spotlight on her own terms, leaving her followers with a clear message: the relationship is in the past, her focus remains on her path forward, and her audience is expected to respect both sides.
