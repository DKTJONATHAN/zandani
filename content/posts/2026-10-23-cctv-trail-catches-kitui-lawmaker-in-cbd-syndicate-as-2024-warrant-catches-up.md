The Directorate of Criminal Investigations has attributed the apprehension of a sitting Kitui County Assembly lawmaker directly to downtown video surveillance archives, confirming that the ward representative was simultaneously evading an active arrest warrant for jumping court bond.

Detectives based at Kamukunji Police Station intercepted the Member of County Assembly (MCA) for Ikanga/Kyatune Ward along Mfangano Street on Friday, October 23, acting on intelligence that tied him to three separate mobile phone thefts reported across the Central Business District in August and September 2026.

According to investigative briefs released by the agency, retrieved closed-circuit television recordings along the commercial corridors provided the forensic baseline needed to match the lawmaker to the reported street-level syndicate.

“CCTV footage proved instrumental in the investigations, with detectives positively identifying him in connection with the reported incidents,” the DCI stated on Friday.

![Kitui County Assembly](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/kitui-county-assembly-ii.jpg.webp?itok=GWjRdRtq)

### The Makadara Warrant Breach

Beyond the fresh robbery complaints, the downtown sting resolved an open enforcement loophole dating back to early 2024. Processing procedures at Kamukunji revealed that the county lawmaker was already a documented fugitive under Criminal Case No. 112/303/2024 at the Makadara Law Courts.

In that matter, the Ikanga/Kyatune representative had been arraigned on formal stealing charges before securing release on bond. He subsequently failed to attend trial dates, prompting the magistrate’s court to cancel his bond terms and issue an arrest warrant that remained unserved until his downtown interception.

“The MCA is wanted in Criminal Case No. 112/303/2024 after failing to attend court following his release on bond by Makadara Law Courts, where he had been charged with an offence of stealing,” the DCI noted.

![kamukunji-police-station.](https://kenyans.co.ke/files/styles/article_inner/public/images/media/kamukunji-police-station_0.jpg.webp?itok=hOxzu2GY)

### Expanded Downtown Sweeps

The arrest comes amid an ongoing operational clampdown against organised theft networks operating along the capital’s commercial arteries. Nairobi Regional Criminal Investigation Officer Setim Kandie indicated earlier in October that multi-agency teams have intensified targeted operations against syndicates obtaining money through false pretences, street thefts, and electronic device scams spanning Kilimani, Westlands, and the downtown grid.

While several suspects in the broader network have already appeared before trial magistrates, DCI headquarters warned on Friday that detectives are pursuing additional confederates tied to the Mfangano Street ring, maintaining that political rank will not shield co-conspirators from prosecution.

The Ikanga/Kyatune ward representative remains in custody at Kamukunji Police Station pending formal arraignment, where prosecutors are expected to present fresh charges for the recent CBD incidents while moving to enforce the revoked Makadara bond terms.
