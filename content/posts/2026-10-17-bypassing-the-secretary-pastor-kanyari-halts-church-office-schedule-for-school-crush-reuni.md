Pastor Victor Kanyari didn't consult his diary or check his waiting list when his first girlfriend walked into his church office. 

Instead of adhering to the usual administrative protocol governing his daily engagements, the controversial televangelist immediately instructed his secretary to clear the board. “I simply told my secretary that I had to go, regardless of what was on the schedule,” Kanyari recalled of the impromptu visit.

The unscripted drop-in yielded a candid TikTok video filmed against a backdrop of national flags and official office furniture. Rather than discussing ministry matters, the pair spent their time walking down memory lane, blending Swahili and Sheng as they revisited their school days in the 1990s.

During the exchange, Kanyari confessed that his former classmate had completely intimidated him back then. Speaking candidly about the dynamic of their youth, he admitted, “Sikuwa na mpenda sana. Ni kuwa na muogopa, hivyo somehow, uko high class.” In Kanyari’s telling, her perceived social standing left him convinced she was entirely out of his league.

To bridge that teenage gap, the preacher revealed a specific throwback gesture from their youth: he was the person who bought her very first mobile phone.

The lighthearted nostalgia shifted briefly to a darker chapter from their shared past. Kanyari recounted how he grew close to the woman through her brother, one of his closest friends at the time. The conversation touched on the tragic reality of Kenyan politics when Kanyari noted that the brother lost his life during election violence—a fatal outcome the preacher claimed he had tried to warn him against.

Despite the somber memory, the overall tenor of the office visit remained celebratory. The viral clip quickly captured attention across social media platforms, drawing thousands of viewers to the comments section as users reacted to the rare glimpse of the preacher trading pulpit theatrics for nostalgic teenage confessions.

The video continues to circulate on TikTok as users dissect the unexpected window into the televangelist's personal history before his days behind the microphone.
