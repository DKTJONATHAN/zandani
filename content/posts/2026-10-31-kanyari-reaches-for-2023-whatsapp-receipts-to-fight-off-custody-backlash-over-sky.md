Pastor Victor Kanyari has opened his digital archives to defend his record as a provider, publishing screenshots of a three-year-old WhatsApp conversation with his daughter Sky Victor in an effort to silence doubts over his financial commitment to his children.

The screenshot, shared publicly across his social channels, displays an exchange dated October 2023 with a contact saved under the moniker “Sky My D…” In the messages, the teenager expresses relief and gratitude over her education expenses, sending a text that reads: “Thank you baba. You are the best. Now, I can go to school. You’re the best father anybody could ask for.”

Further down the message thread, Sky sends updates regarding her mid-term dates and inquires when Kanyari would be available to pick her up from school. By releasing the exchange, the preacher sought to draw a firm line against recurring public criticism that portrays him as detached from his children's daily care and financial upkeep.

### The Pattern of Public Receipts

The disclosure is not an isolated gesture. Kanyari has made a consistent habit of deploying personal interactions to the public gallery whenever questions surface regarding his domestic responsibilities. He has repeatedly insisted that he shoulders their school expenses, stating that he recently made an educational contribution of KSh 500,000 toward their accounts.

Yet the strategy of turning private father-daughter correspondence into digital ammunition has divided onlookers. While his loyal base seized upon the screenshots as tangible evidence of a father meeting his financial obligations, others questioned the judgment of a parent placing conversations with a minor into the open domain simply to win an online debate.

The public interrogation surrounding the children's welfare took on a sharper edge following the death of their mother, celebrated gospel artist Betty Bayo. Bayo passed away on November 10, 2025, after undergoing treatment for acute myeloid leukemia, leaving behind a complex blended family dynamic that quickly spilled from private grief into institutional corridors.

Bayo was buried on November 20, 2025, in Mugumo Estate, Kiambu, at a funeral service that brought together both Kanyari and her widower, Hiram Gitau, widely known as Tash. While the two men maintained public composure during the burial rites, the legal and domestic arrangements surrounding the children soon came undone.

### The Lingering Custody Standoff

The release of the 2023 messages lands right in the middle of a deeply contested custody arrangement. Following a court decision that granted Kanyari legal custody of the children he had with the late musician, 14-year-old Sky pushed back publicly against the transition.

In an emotional message directed to President William Ruto, Sky openly expressed distress over the prospect of relocating to live with her biological father. During the appeal, she asked for state intervention to allow her to continue residing with her stepfather, Tash, citing her discomfort with Kanyari's household.

By circulating messages from October 2023, Kanyari has leaned on past affection to offset present friction. The chat logs establish that he was actively funding tuition terms before Bayo's passing, but they also expose the deep disconnect between a documented financial transaction years ago and the emotional custody dispute playing out in real time.
