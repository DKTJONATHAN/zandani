Health Cabinet Secretary Aden Duale found himself delivering one of the most sobering press briefings of his tenure on Tuesday, October 6, 2026, as he confirmed Kenya’s first case and subsequent fatality of the Ebola Bundibugyo virus disease (BDBV).

Rather than speaking in broad policy strokes, Duale went on the offensive with highly specific details, launching a public search for passengers of a commercial flight and a relative’s hatchback that transported the terminally ill patient into the heart of Nairobi.

### The Trans-Border Journey

The deceased, a Kenyan citizen, had spent seven years residing in the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC). According to Ministry of Health records, he fell ill about a month ago while still in the DRC, seeking treatment at several local facilities before deciding to make the journey back to Kenya.

His journey home was a multi-stage trek across East African borders. On October 3, the patient traveled by road from the DRC to Kampala, Uganda. From Kampala, he boarded Jambojet flight 8523, landing at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) in Nairobi.

While the patient cleared standard airport public health screenings upon arrival, his condition deteriorated rapidly. A relative met him at the airport, driving him directly to the hospital in a silver Toyota Fielder, registration plate **KDG 699Z**.

### Crisis at Nairobi Hospital

The patient was taken directly to The Nairobi Hospital, where emergency room staff immediately flagged his symptoms. Doctors noted a dangerous combination of high fever, chills, severe muscle aches, sore throat, painful swallowing, and visible cutaneous bleeding at injection sites.

Recognizing the classic signs of a viral hemorrhagic fever, medical teams placed the patient in an emergency room isolation unit and rushed samples for specialized testing.

Within hours, both the National Virology Reference Laboratory at the National Public Health Institute and the Kenya Medical Research Institute (KEMRI) confirmed the presence of the Ebola Bundibugyo strain. Despite receiving specialized supportive care, the patient succumbed to the virus on Monday night at approximately 11:30 PM.

### The Dragnet Begins

With the diagnosis confirmed, Duale’s ministry is now managing a massive trace-and-contain operation. Public health officials are focusing heavily on the immediate environments the patient interacted with during his final journey.

So far, authorities have successfully identified 28 individuals who came into direct contact with the deceased. However, the wider search remains active on the tarmac and the highway.

Teams are currently tracking down 23 passengers and four crew members who flew on Jambojet flight 8523 on October 3, alongside anyone who may have been in close proximity to the Toyota Fielder KDG 699Z during the transit from JKIA.

While offering condolences to the family, CS Duale emphasized that the immediate priority is locking down the transmission chain to prevent a wider outbreak in the capital.
