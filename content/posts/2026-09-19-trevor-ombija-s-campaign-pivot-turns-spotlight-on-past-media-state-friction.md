Broadcaster Trevor Ombija has turned a high-profile television dispute into campaign fodder, telling voters in Nyakach that former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua once sought his dismissal over an on-air confrontation.

Speaking to constituents on Friday, September 18, as he drummed up support for his 2027 parliamentary bid, Ombija placed his editorial record at the centre of his political pitch. The 38-year-old news anchor told the crowd that Gachagua approached the management of Royal Media Services (RMS) seeking his removal after the two clashed during an interview in March 2026.

“As I stand here to address you, the former Deputy President Gachagua once called for my dismissal from work because we confronted each other,” Ombija said, adding that his ethnic background was raised during the fallout surrounding his position at the broadcaster.

The claims could not be independently verified. Neither Gachagua nor the management of RMS had responded to inquiries regarding the alleged dismissal request at the time of publication.

![A photo collage of Citizen TV news anchor Trevor Ombija (left) and former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua.](https://i.ibb.co/32XzJQy/c8c676a3eedc.webp)

The incident cited by Ombija traces back to an interview broadcast in March, during which Gachagua clashed with Ombija and other presenters from Ramogi TV. In the closing minutes of that broadcast, Gachagua publicly accused Ombija of bias, unfair question framing, and unprofessional conduct, saying the journalist was putting words in his mouth.

During that broadcast, Gachagua questioned the premise of his invitation to what he termed a “tribal show” while answering questions on ethnic politics. Ombija countered at the time that the former deputy president had requested the appearance himself.

Ombija, who joined RMS in 2018 as a presenter on *Monday Report* and *Day Break*, is now attempting to convert his media visibility into an elective career in Kisumu County. His entry into the Nyakach race marks a formal exit from broadcasting into partisan politics, where he has declared alignment with President William Ruto’s broad-based administration.

![Citizen TV studio and an insert of former Deputy President Rigathi Gachagua.](https://i.ibb.co/gbRVbpQS/66b3f0216b41.webp)

Addressing the local gathering, Ombija endorsed the current national government framework, stating that he backs Ruto because the president has an agenda for the country. His campaign launch sets the stage for a contested parliamentary primary in Nyakach as the 2027 campaign cycle begins to take shape.
