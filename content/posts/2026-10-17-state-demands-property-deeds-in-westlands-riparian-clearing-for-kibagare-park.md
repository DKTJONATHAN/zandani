Property owners across Westlands and Parklands have been ordered to submit land documents to Ardhi House for technical audit, as state agencies begin clearing structures built directly over the Kibagare River to make way for a continuous public corridor.

The enforcement operation, coordinated by the Nairobi Rivers Commission alongside Nairobi County authorities, targets private residential developments, religious properties, and commercial blocks situated along a designated 30-metre to 60-metre buffer zone.

Rather than a conventional greening initiative, the intervention requires extensive physical de-canalisation—uncovering and restoring open water channels that have for decades been diverted into concrete pipes, culverts, or completely built over by high-density developments.

### Title Scrutiny at Ardhi House

![An aerial image of the proposed plan to build a public park along the Kibarange River in Nairobi's Parklands area.](https://i.ibb.co/zWgM54JD/174c9553c62c.webp)

The Parklands Residents Association has formally advised all affected landholders within the riverway to surrender official ownership paperwork to the commission at Ardhi House. The audits will verify plot boundaries against historical survey records to confirm whether private titles overlap gazetted riparian reserves.

Simultaneously, surveyors and multi-agency inspectors have begun immediate joint re-demarcation exercises on the ground. The teams are focused on re-establishing natural gradients to unclog persistent drainage bottlenecks, most notably along First Parklands Avenue, where undersized culverts frequently back up during heavy downpours.

Notices to vacate and remove encroaching works have already reached properties along Brookside Drive, Grevillea Grove, Ndovu Lane, and Parklands Court.

### Clearing Built-Over Channels

Unlike peripheral river projects, the Kibagare corridor cuts directly through some of the capital's priciest real estate. The proposed path originates west of Ring Road Westlands, close to Sarit Centre and the Waiyaki Way interchange connections.

From that junction, the project layout traces southeast past Muthithi Road, Mpaka Road, and Parklands Road adjacent to The Oval. It runs alongside the Aga Khan Sports Club grounds before advancing toward First Parklands Avenue and terminating near Ojijo Road.

![A building being demolished along the Kibarange River in Nairobi's Parklands area, October 15, 2026.](https://i.ibb.co/bgkF4D3D/f3296c1ee885.webp)

Opening up these sections requires structural realignments by institutional landowners. On Friday, October 16, the Oshwal Religious Centre Committee met with the government task force to review plans touching its complex. Following the session, committee chairman Samir Chandaria pledged the centre’s cooperation in restoring the riverbed and accommodating the public greenway.

### Technical Buffer and Master Plan

Engineering designs under the Westlands Park Scheme show the reclaimed channel will be transformed into an open-air naturalised blue water course. Flanking the water will be a landscaped buffer featuring pedestrian walkways, non-motorised transit trails, and indigenous tree canopies.

While field teams determine plot-specific acreage through ongoing cadastre surveys, state planners are enforcing the 30-to-60-metre baseline control margin from the river’s edges to prevent future encroachment.

The Parklands initiative serves as an active enforcement pilot within the national government's Ksh50 billion Nairobi River Basin regeneration framework. As the Ardhi House technical assessments get underway, the multi-agency team's primary hurdle shifts from design sign-offs to the physical reclamation of paved private property across one of the city's densest urban corridors.
