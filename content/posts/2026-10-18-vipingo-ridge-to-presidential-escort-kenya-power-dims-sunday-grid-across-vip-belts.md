Kenya Power is pulling the plug on some of the country’s most exclusive weekend leisure strips and high-security residential enclaves on Sunday, October 18, 2026, rolling out an extensive eight-hour maintenance shutdown that spans five counties.

While routine utility notices typically read as mundane administrative warnings, Sunday’s published roster cuts directly through prime hospitality properties along the Indian Ocean, VIP installations in Nairobi, and major commercial recreational strips in Kiambu and Machakos.

### The Coastal Luxury and Resort Strip

The most dramatic concentration of leisure disruptions falls on Kilifi’s beachfront corridor, where high-end hospitality landmarks and gated holiday communities will spend the bulk of Sunday off the national grid between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m.

Among the marquee addresses listed for the blackout are Vipingo Ridge, the luxury golf and residential estate, alongside Pazuri Homes, Vipingo Beach, Kuruwitu Beach, and Salama Beach. Iconic coastal hospitality venues, including the Sun N Sand Beach Hotel and the Northcoast Beach Hotel, sit squarely inside the interruption zone.

The shutdown along the coastal artery also encompasses Mtwapa Gardens, Kikambala, Shariani Town, Mnarani, Takaungu, and Kilifi Pearl, reaching deep into the surrounding light-industrial and processing belt that hosts Revital EPZ, Safe Pack, Vipingo Industries, and Vipingo Sisal.

### Nairobi VIP Enclaves and Nightlife Nodes

In the capital, the downtime begins even earlier for key diplomatic and security facilities. Areas around Parklands will lose supply from 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with the interruption taking in 4th Parklands, Masari Road, Kusi Lane, Ngao Road, Suswa Road, Wangapala Road, and the Presidential Escort quarters, alongside Diamond 1 and 2 and KPLC Staff Quarters.

Across Nairobi’s eastern leisure and residential expanse, power drops at 9:00 a.m. through major parts of Kayole and Komarock, taking out prominent social gathering spots including Jawaka Travelers Bar, Kayole 1 Market, and adjacent transport hubs such as Kayole Nyaura and Mihango stages.

A parallel sweep hits the Ruai and Utawala belt, knocking out electricity across Kamulu, Stage 26, Kingoris, Drum Vale, Brookshire, the GSU installations, and the entire stretch covering Njiru, Chokaa, and Sewage.

### Satellite Weekend Hubs in Kiambu and Machakos

In Kiambu County, the Sunday maintenance program strikes the bustling student and weekend social strip along Thika Superhighway. Key commercial and accommodation addresses in Juja—including Centurion Hotel, Ndarugu Hotel, High Point, Kenyatta Road, and the JKUAT University Block—face an all-day outage until 5:00 p.m.

The disruption ripples outward to high-value agricultural estates in the area, catching Karakuta Coffee Estate, Amnia Coffee Farm, Everflora Farm, Diana Farm, and Kiaora Farm.

Across the county line in Machakos, the shutdown paralyses major transit and trading centers that double as weekend retreats. Outages will cover the whole of Joska, Quickmart Joska, Malaa, Kantafu, Koma, and Sunshine, as well as the manufacturing cluster at Kenanie Leather Industries.

Further east, the entire commercial centers of Tala and Kangundo will run on backup generators, with Tala Ridge, Eastman Tala, Tala Market, and Kangundo Hospital scheduled for the uninterrupted eight-hour switch-off.

### Western Outpost Interruption

In western Kenya, Kakamega County’s maintenance envelope targets an eclectic mix of administrative and local recreational points from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Supply will be suspended at Lubao Market, Shikutsa Prison, Shinyalu Bar, Mwiyala, Ebwabwa, and Bukhaiywa.

According to the utility company’s schedule, regular supply across all five counties is projected to resume progressively starting from 5:00 p.m. on Sunday evening, contingent on the completion of the planned network maintenance works.
