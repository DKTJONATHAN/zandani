Comedian Oga Obinna has turned parliamentary security footage into an open-air public courtroom, challenging his Instagram followers to identify a lawmaker captured on CCTV allegedly stealing a mobile phone within the legislative precincts.

In a satirical video shared on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, the media personality didn't hold back as he dissected the absurdity of a high-ranking elected official being cornered by building security cameras yet reportedly denying culpability.

“Huyu ni nani ameiba simu kwa Parliament? Aki ma-members, ni nani hawa watu tulichagua? Hebu kwa comment section below mniambie mnashuku nani,” Obinna posed to his audience, inviting internet detectives to name both the victim and the prime suspect.

The presenter zeroed in on the surreal nature of the confrontation, mocking the audacity required to face clear electronic proof of wrongdoing and still brush it off. 

“Alafu waliiba simu ya nani, na unashuku ni nani? Yaani mpaka anaonyesha CCTV, lakini bado anaruka ngori anasema…” he remarked, leaning into the disbelief surrounding the security breach inside Kenya’s House of Representatives.

Beyond the comedy routine, Obinna’s commentary tapped into growing public fatigue over the standard of integrity expected inside Parliament Buildings. He wrapped up his online segment by questioning the broader implications of electoral choices when legislative chambers begin doubling down on petty property crimes.

“Hebu hapo kwa comment section iwake. Yo! Guy! Wanaiba simu na kura sasa?” he challenged, leaving his followers to debate how security gatekeepers allowed a routine day in the House to descend into a viral sleuth hunt.

The circulating surveillance clip has since fueled intense online banter, shifting public attention away from traditional legislative debates and straight onto the conduct of lawmakers when the chamber's internal cameras start rolling.
